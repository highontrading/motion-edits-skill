## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** community-contest showcase / voting post (one of a numbered series: "FINALIST 03"). The wrapper belongs to earn.edits. The content is a contestant's 13 s motivational photo-composite edit.
- **Narrative beats (inner edit VO):**
  - 0.0–2.4 **Hook / thesis:** a two-sentence antithesis, "pressure doesn't ruin you, it reveals you", told visually by a falling boulder that is then knocked away to reveal a man.
  - 3.0–7.6 **Setup:** everyone talks about what they could make: ideas, plans, a someday project (man tinkering, words stack around a TV).
  - 8.1–10.7 **Turn:** until a timer starts (phone countdown "2:58" in a "PENDING WORKS" drawer, "Do it fast!" notification).
  - 11.2–12.9 **Payoff / CTA:** "This is the timer. Make it count." (the same man, now triumphant at a blackboard, holding up a rolled blueprint).
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** frame 0 already shows the full branded page (headline HAMMAD, the 03 badge, the vote bar). The only moving thing is a boulder dropping into the desert inside the tape window, from mid-sky down to landing in **8 frames (0.27 s)**. Its diff curve rises steadily (3.5→10.9) like gravity ease-in, and the dust puff settles by f11. The green word PRESSURE smears in at f12–20 over the landing spot. A small, single point of motion inside a large static frame pulls the eye straight to the edit.
- **Script → visual mapping (inner edit):** each VO sentence = one composited "set". The nouns become stacked 3D or diegetic words (IDEAS / PLANS / PROJECTS, PENDING WORKS printed on the drawer) and the concept nouns become props (boulder = pressure, phone timer = timer, blueprint = made project).
- **Wrapper rule:** the page is fixed per entry, and only the name, handle, number and vote digit change. It is a template system for an episodic series.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** 3 transitions in 13.5 s (0.22/s). Shots are long (2.4–4.8 s) because the inner edit relies on **in-shot builds** (props and words arriving) rather than cuts. That suits the small on-screen size: fast cutting in a 38%-wide window would be illegible.
- **Wrapper motion:** zero. No idle animation, no blinking PLAY, no hover. The page is a still. This is deliberate: the contrast between a dead-still frame and a moving window makes the window the only focal point.
- **Easing signatures (inner):**
  - Gravity drop: the boulder accelerates over 8 frames (inner diff 3.5 → 4.1 → 4.8 → 5.6 → 6.8 → 10.9), so ease-in (`Easing.in(Easing.quad)`) then an impact puff.
  - Slide-ins of characters and props: the man moves linearly over 12 frames. The TV takes 9 frames with its peak velocity on frame 4 of 9 (an ease-in-out with a quick settle), then a spark.
  - Word rises: 12–18 frames with an ease-out (IDEAS f161–174, PROJECTS f210–227), slow and "heavy" to match the metallic 3D type.
  - Camera: after the flash, a 15-frame ease-out push. Then a 57-frame **ease-in** drift (accelerating into the cut, so the cut feels motivated). After the cut, an 11-frame ease-out pull-back. The alternating in/out acceleration around the cut acts as a speed-ramp handoff.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:** drop-in with impact dust (8 f), linear slide-in from the frame edge (12 f), punch-out with motion blur and dust (10 f), slide-in from the right with a spark (9 f), 3D word rise from behind an object (14–18 f), 90°-rotated vertical words flanking a centre prop, notification bubble pop with rotation (~6 f), paper slide-in from the corner (~15 f).
- **Camera:** the wrapper is locked. Inner net_zoom over the edit: +11 (mostly the push after the flash), net_rot +14.7 (the drawer-shot rotation). No shakes.
- **Secondary motion:** birds flocking, dust particles, sparks, steam, phone UI.
- **Sync:**
  - Hard cut 1 at 3.00 = the first syllable of "Everyone" (0.00 s offset), 0.11 s before beat 3.11.
  - The flash peak (7.93) lands on strong onset 7.94 (Δ 0.00 s), and the new scene is fully revealed at 8.13, +0.03 s after "Until" (8.10). So **the flash peaks on the music hit and resolves on the VO**.
  - Hard cut 2 at 11.03 is 0.15 s before "This" (11.18) and +0.07 s after beat 10.96.
  - Prop hits: the boulder impact at 0.27 is +0.10 after beat 0.16. The TV lands at 5.00 (beat 4.95, VO 5.02). The steam burst peaks at 12.63 on "count" (12.64).
  - Word reveals trail the spoken word by 0.1–0.3 s (IDEAS lands ~0.4 s after "ideas" starts; the slow rise means the word is readable as the next phrase begins).

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Hard cut on the VO onset** (3.00 s): no transition FX, picture changes on the first phoneme. Rebuild: `<Sequence from={wordStartFrame}>`.
2. **White bloom flash** (7.83–8.13 s, 10 frames): inner luma 108 → 137 → 164 → 227 → 248 (peak f238), held 2 frames near white (238–248), then 219 → 192 → 158 → 98 over 4 frames into the new scene. So the timing is 3 frames up, 2 hold, 4 down, with the cut hidden at the white peak. Rebuild: a white `AbsoluteFill` with opacity keyframes `[0,0.25,0.5,0.95,1,1,0.85,0.6,0.3,0]` over 10 frames, plus `filter: brightness()` ramping on the outgoing clip so it blooms rather than just fades. Swap clips at frame 4.
3. **Accelerate-into-cut / decelerate-out-of-cut** (9.1–11.53 s): the outgoing shot's camera speeds up (ease-in, 57 f), and the incoming shot starts with a bright flicker and an ease-out pull-back (11 f). It is a speed-ramp handoff without a whip.
4. **Wrapper-level "transition":** there is none. Each finalist video in the series swaps only the text fields, so the series-level transition is the swipe between posts.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Frame the content in a designed object.** A static, print-quality wrapper (a VHS sleeve, an editorial header, a numbered badge) instantly turns any clip, even user-generated footage, into a "collectible" series entry.
2. **The static shell and the moving core need opposite energy.** 0.8/255 motion outside, all motion inside: the eye never leaves the window.
3. **Encode the series grammar in the page:** NOW PLAYING / FINALIST 03, the 03 badge, SIDE A, the barcode "FW-03". Numbering and catalogue language create a sense of episodes and a reason to watch the others.
4. **A single, literal CTA bar** in a typewriter mono ("COMMENT "3" TO VOTE") turns the post into an engagement mechanic. The digit is repeated in three places (header badge, label, CTA square).
5. **Retro print palette:** cream + ink + oxblood + a tricolour stripe (rust/mustard/bottle green). Strictly limited and warm, so any inner footage colour reads against it.
6. **Inner edit: props are metaphors and words are objects** (metal 3D words standing on the TV, neon text printed on a drawer). Type that lives in the scene feels more expensive than overlaid captions.
7. **Transitions sit in the VO pauses** and peak on music onsets (flash peak = onset exactly; cuts within ±0.15 s of the VO onset).
8. **Accelerating camera into a cut, decelerating out of it** gives a speed-ramp feel without whip-pan blur.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `720×960` (or `1080×1440` for crisper type), 30 fps, duration = inner clip + 0 (loops). An alternative 9:16 export: put the same page at 1080×1920 with the header and CTA scaled up.
- **Layer stack (bottom → top):**
  1. `<PaperBackground color="#e0d6bb" grain={0.04}/>`
  2. `<PageHeader logo="EarnEdits.com" nav="FRAME WAR / VIDEO CLUB" kicker="NOW PLAYING / FINALIST {n}" title={name} handle={handle} badge={n}/>`: two 1.5 px rules, title in Bebas Neue 92 px (at 720 w) tracking 0, kicker 13 px Inter Bold tracking 0.1em `#843129`.
  3. `<MarginRuler ticks every={62} x={16} color="#8a8474"/>` + `<RedMarker x={68} y={236} w={4} h={50}/>` + `<VerticalLabel text="FRAME WAR TOURNAMENT" rotate={-90}/>` + `<VerticalLabel text="ORIGINAL EDIT / SIDE A" rotate={90} tracking="0.3em"/>`.
  4. `<VHSSleeve x={163} y={215} w={400} h={620} shadow={{dx:10,dy:10,color:'#b3ac9c',blur:0}}>`: black case `#2d2a21` with radius 4 → cream insert `#eee4cb` → red header bar `#843129` (name + handle + number) → **`<Video>` or `<OffthreadVideo>` window at 9:16, 272×485**, `objectFit: cover` → spine labels → footer (tricolour 3-segment bar `#a43c23/#c5954b/#3f5f47` 50×4 px each, outlined "VHS" box, barcode SVG, "FW · {n} / SIDE A").
  5. `<PlayMarker label="PLAY" x={614} y={790}/>` (red outline triangle).
  6. `<RetroStripes y={870} colors={['#cd4314','#ffdcb9','#db9637','#110900']} heights={[7,2,6,1]}/>`.
  7. `<VoteBar digit={n} text={'COMMENT "{n}" TO VOTE'} right="YOUR PICK" font="Courier Prime" bg="#191a14" accent="#84312d"/>`.
- **Props schema:** `{name, handle, finalistNumber, clipSrc, accentColor?}` so the whole series renders from a JSON list.
- **Inner-edit helpers (if you build the inner edit too):** `<DropIn>` translateY + scale with `Easing.in(Easing.quad)` over 8 f + `<DustPuff>` sprite at impact; `<SlideInFromEdge>` linear over 12 f; `<PunchOut>` translateX 0→130% over 6 f with `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)` + directional blur 20 px; `<Rise3DWord>` translateY 40→0 px with clip-mask from the prop's top edge, `spring({damping: 200, stiffness: 80})` ≈ 14–18 f; `<WhiteBloomFlash frames={10} peakAt={4}/>`; a camera `<Drift>` that interpolates scale 1→0.9 and rotate 0→5° with `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)` over 57 f into the cut.
- **Tricky bits:** the 3D metal type can be done with layered `text-shadow` extrusion (8 steps, #6b6458→#2b2722) plus a `linear-gradient` background-clip text for the brushed face. Perspective-printed type on a surface: CSS `transform: perspective(600px) rotateX(…)` + `mix-blend-mode: screen` + a green `drop-shadow` glow for the neon.

## 9. Variation ideas
1. **UGC contest / community spotlight** for any brand: swap the VHS sleeve for a vinyl sleeve (music), a cartridge (gaming) or a film can (cinema). Keep the numbered badge and the comment-to-vote bar.
2. **Product drops / "now showing" catalogue**: the window holds a product turntable video, and the header becomes SKU, price and "DROP 03". The CTA becomes "COMMENT "CART"".
3. **Course or tutorial series**: "LESSON 03 / SIDE A", the window holds a screen recording, and the vertical spine text lists the module name. Use a cooler palette (paper grey + cobalt) and keep the static-shell/moving-core contrast.
