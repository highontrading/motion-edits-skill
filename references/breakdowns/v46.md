# v46 — "This transition was made with an AI editing agent" (InVideo sponsored UI-demo promo, 16:9)

**Source creator handle:** earn.edits · **Format:** 1276×718, 16:9 landscape, 30 fps, 27.2 s · **Shots:** 7 (all 6 detected cuts are real; the opening white bloom → scene and the in-shot reframes are camera moves, not cuts) · **Avg shot:** 3.9 s · **Music BPM:** ~129 (light bed) · **VO:** yes, English, casual first-person creator read

**Style tags:** ui-mockup, device-mockup, product-showcase, chat-ui, cursor-ui, typewriter, glassmorphism, depth-of-field, halftone, glow, slow-push, slide-push, cta-endcard, momentum-matched-cut (new), pixel-tile-dissolve (new)

**One-line essence:** a software ad shot as a smooth "virtual camera" gliding over clean UI cards floating on dreamy blurred nature wallpapers. Every cut keeps the lateral camera drift going, and the prompt box types the voice-over as it is spoken, so a static product demo feels like one continuous, expensive tracking shot.

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** sponsored tool promo / tutorial teaser (InVideo AI editing agent). The structure is "proof → how I did it → result in the editor → benefit → CTA".
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–4.8 **Hook/proof:** "You see this entire transition? It was made with an AI editing agent." A phone mockup plays the creator's own ripped-paper car edit, flanked by keyword captions.
  - 4.9–11.7 **How:** uploaded the reference, added clips, told the agent to recreate it. A prompt card: an image is dragged in, a long prompt streams in, the card gets thumbnails, the send button is pressed.
  - 11.8–18.1 **Result:** the agent applied the transition and built the timeline. The full editor window floats on a meadow wallpaper; the camera glides to the agent log, then the timeline filling with clips.
  - 18.1–22.2 **Benefit:** "Instead of building from scratch, I just told the agent what I wanted." A minimal prompt box types the sentence word by word; a cursor clicks send.
  - 22.2–27.2 **CTA:** "InVideo is completely free. Comment 'invideo' and I'll send the link." Logo + "Try Invideo now" pill over a darkened, blurred product homepage.
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** black frame → a **white bloom that blows out the whole frame in 12 frames** (luma 0 → 236 by 0.43 s) → the bloom dissolves through a **grid of glowing square tiles** into the alpine-meadow scene, with the phone already playing a red car clip (0.43–1.4 s). Captions "You see" / "transition" sit either side of the phone by 0.8 s. The visual hook is the result itself (the edit on the phone), and the voice dares you to look at it.
- **Script → visual rule:** each sentence gets one UI "stage". On-screen words are only **2-word keyword pairs** in the hook, and later the **prompt box itself is the caption**: the spoken sentence is typed into the UI.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** UI/product mockup composited onto painterly AI-generated nature wallpapers (alpine meadow, pink cosmos field under blue sky, sunny meadow with trees). The backgrounds are always **defocused (DoF blur ~12–25 px)**, and the UI is always sharp with soft shadows: a macOS-desktop-wallpaper aesthetic.
- **Backgrounds & textures:** wallpapers change per stage: alpine meadow (green/blue `#578cc2`, `#8d9360`) → pink flower field (`#c98c78`, `#cd8b76`, sky `#9cb3cb`) → sunny meadow with a macOS dock → plain light radial gradient `#eae4e9` → `#fefefe` (prompt-only stage) → near-black blurred product homepage `#0a070b`. A **halftone/dot-grid overlay** (small dots or "✱" marks on a ~14 px grid, ~20–40% opacity) sits at the left and right frame edges like a vignette. It is visible on the pink field, on the meadow (yellow dots over the tree) and as a blue dot ring on the grey stage.
- **Colour:** natural saturated pastels for backgrounds, neutral white/black UI on top. Accents are a **cyan-blue `#2a9fe0`** for streaming text and dot bursts, a **lime-yellow `#c4e35a`** for the newest caption word in the hook, and an orange flash on the clicked send button. The CTA flips to dark (`#0a070b` with ~70% black overlay) so the white logo pops.
- **Typography:**
  - **Hook captions:** geometric/neo-grotesk sans (Inter SemiBold / SF Pro Text Semibold), Title Case per word ("You see", "transition", "Made / With ai", "Editing / Agent"), white with a soft dark drop shadow (0 2 8 px, 40%). Small: ~6–8% of frame width per word, cap height ~2.5% of frame height. Set in **two columns either side of the phone** at y ≈ 50–55%, left block ~25% from the left edge and right block ~25% from the right edge. The newest word appears in **lime `#c4e35a`** and turns white ~0.3 s later.
  - **UI text (prompt card, editor):** Inter Regular, `#2b2b2b` on white, ~2.3% of frame height. **Streaming typewriter:** the last ~6–10 characters of the typed text are tinted cyan-blue and fade to dark grey as more text arrives (a glowing "write head").
  - **CTA:** InVideo wordmark (bold rounded sans, white) next to the ghost mascot; "Try Invideo now" in Inter Medium black on a white pill (radius = height/2, ~23% of frame width); "Comment 'invideo' for the link" in Inter Regular grey `#8a8a8a`, ~1.6% of frame height.
- **Layout & composition:** everything is centred. The phone or card occupies 35–60% of frame height. The camera then crops into UI regions (send button, agent log, timeline) at up to ~2× scale, so the UI always fills 70–90% of frame width during the explanation. Text never touches the edges, since this is a landscape edit with no social-UI safe zones.
- **Assets:** an iPhone mockup with a TikTok UI playing the creator's car edit, a **frosted-glass card** behind the phone (inside the card the wallpaper is sharp and more saturated, outside it is blurred: a "focus window"), InVideo's prompt card (thumbnails, Agent/Editor toggle, "Agent Two Ultra" dropdown, send button), a full InVideo editor window (agent log, media bin, timeline), a macOS dock, a hand cursor and an arrow cursor, the ghost mascot logo.
- **Finishing / FX:** depth-of-field on all wallpapers, soft UI drop shadows (0 20 60 px, ~25%), white bloom flash, glowing-square tile dissolves, a blue halftone ring burst, white pixel-square sparkles around the logo, and directional motion blur on the fast slide-ins (~42 px/frame at the peak).

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** slow for a short-form edit: 0.22 cuts/s, shots 1.1–6.4 s. The energy comes from **continuous camera motion inside each shot** (UI reframes, drifts, slide-ins) rather than cut rate. The hook shot is the longest single image (4.9 s) because the product result is the hook.
- **Easing signature (two kinds):**
  1. **Slide-in after a cut = exponential ease-out.** Peak velocity on the first frame after the cut (−6.0 px/frame at 180-px analysis ≈ −42 px/frame at 1276 w), halving every ~4–5 frames, near-rest after ~20 frames, then a residual drift that never fully stops. No overshoot (pan never changes sign). Rebuild: `bezier(0.05, 0.7, 0.1, 1)` over 22 frames, travel ≈ 25% of frame width.
  2. **Camera reframes on the UI = symmetric ease-in-out.** Velocity rises and falls in a bell over 40–48 frames (13.33 s peak, 16.07 s peak); pan and zoom move together. Rebuild: `Easing.inOut(Easing.cubic)` or `bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)` over 42–48 frames.
- **Momentum-matched cuts:** before cuts 2 and 3 the drift **accelerates** (−0.2 → −1.3 px/frame in ~14 frames) and the incoming shot starts at the same or higher speed in the same direction. Across a hard cut the eye feels one continuous leftward camera move.
- **Entrance vocabulary:** white bloom (12 frames, ease-out); glowing tile-grid dissolve (~30 frames, tiles light up in random order, each tile = a 64–80 px outlined square with a white glow); glass card snap (2 frames up, ~6 frames settle); keyword pop with lime highlight (≈3 frames scale 0.9 → 1 + colour fade to white over ~9 frames); streaming text (~5 characters/frame, ≈150 chars/s, cyan leading edge); human-paced VO typing (shot 6); halftone ring burst (8 frames); cursor fly-in (~5 frames, ease-out) + click (button colour flash ~8 frames); pixel-square sparkle burst around the logo (~13 frames); pill button scale-in (~4 frames).
- **Camera:** only virtual moves over 2D UI planes (pan + zoom; rotation is ~0). Net zoom +23 over shot 1 (≈ +5% scale per second), long slow leftward drifts in shots 2–4 (net pan_x −42, −11, −21), three reframes in shot 5. No shake.
- **Secondary motion:** the phone video plays continuously, thumbnails load in, timeline clips populate, cursor movements, and a blinking caret on the prompt box.
- **Sync (VO):** cuts lead the sentence they illustrate by 0.1–0.3 s (11.77 vs "It took" 11.86 = −0.09; 18.13 vs "So" 18.30 = −0.17; 22.23 vs "InVideoEditor" 22.52 = −0.29). The hook captions trail their words slightly: "Made With ai" at 2.93 vs "with" 2.64 / "AI" 3.08; "Editing Agent" at 3.47–3.73 vs "editing" 3.38 / "agent" 3.78 (+0.0…+0.1 s). In shot 6 the typed text **leads the voice** progressively: in step at "So instead" (18.40 vs 18.30), ~0.4 s ahead by "whole thing", and ~0.7–1 s ahead by "told the agent" (on screen by ~20.0 s, spoken 20.96–21.28). It finishes at 20.5 s so the click can land before the VO ends (21.96). The send-button click (21.1–21.3) lands under "wanted" (21.72) within ~0.4 s.
- **Sync (music):** 3 of 6 cuts sit within 0.05 s of a beat (4.87 vs 4.92, 11.77 vs 11.80, 22.23 vs 22.25). The others are 0.13–0.2 s off, so the cuts are loosely beat-aware but driven by the VO.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Momentum cut (slide-through)**: 4.87, 7.50, 10.63 s. The outgoing shot's lateral drift eases in over ~14 frames, a hard cut, and the incoming layer enters at ~42 px/frame and eases out over ~22 frames. Rebuild: both sequences share a `cameraX` driven by one continuous function. The outgoing scene uses `interpolate(f, [end-14, end], [x, x-60], {easing: Easing.in(Easing.quad)})` and the incoming uses `interpolate(f, [0, 22], [+320, 0], {easing: Easing.out(Easing.exp)})` plus a slow −0.5 px/frame drift.
2. **Punch-in cut**: 10.63 s. Cut to the same card at ~2× scale, cropped on the next element of interest (send button), with the same slide-in. Rebuild: duplicate the scene with `scale: 2` and `transformOrigin` on the target element.
3. **White bloom → tile dissolve** (open): 0.0–1.4 s. Rebuild: white full-frame `opacity` 0 → 1 over 12 frames (ease-out), then a grid of ~16×9 tiles, each fading its white overlay to 0 at a random delay in 0–30 frames, with a 2 px glowing outline (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px #fff`) for the first 6 frames of each tile.
4. **Glowing tile-grid wipe** (in-shot, 6.0–6.3 s): a sparse lattice of lit square outlines sweeps across the frame when an asset is dropped into the UI. Same component as #3, applied as an overlay only.
5. **Rise-in window**: 11.77 s. Hard cut and the editor window translates up from +6% frame height to 0 in ~6 frames.
6. **Hard cut to a neutral stage + halftone ring burst**: 18.13 s. An SVG ring of small dots scales from 0.8 to 1.3 with opacity 1 → 0 over 8 frames.
7. **Hard cut to the dark CTA + pixel sparkle burst**: 22.23 s. 10–14 white squares (6–14 px) pop at random positions around the logo and fade over ~13 frames.

## 6. Sound design
A soft electronic bed (~129 BPM, RMS 0.11, quieter than a music-led edit) under a conversational VO. Strong onsets cluster in the hook (1.77–4.41 s: likely the phone clip's own audio/SFX and the glass-card pop), at 10.17–12.24 s (send-button click, whoosh for the punch-in and the window rise), 20.09 s (cursor/click), 22.25 s (logo hit on the CTA cut) and 25.3–26.2 s (typing ticks on the comment line). Expect UI SFX: soft clicks, a "whoomp" on cuts, key-typing during the prompt stages. No silence beats.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Sharp UI on blurred painterly wallpaper.** Any screenshot looks premium on a defocused, colourful backdrop with a soft shadow, the way Apple keynotes do it.
2. **Keep the camera moving across cuts.** Accelerate the drift before the cut and launch the next shot at the same speed and direction. Hard cuts then read as one tracking shot.
3. **Two easing families only:** expo ease-out for things arriving (slides, pops) and symmetric ease-in-out for camera reframes. Never linear, never bouncy for UI.
4. **Make the UI the caption.** Type the spoken sentence into the product's own input box, slightly ahead of the voice, and end with a real click before the line ends.
5. **Stream text like an AI.** A cyan leading edge on typed text instantly reads as "AI generating".
6. **Show the result first.** The hook is the finished edit playing on a phone, with only two keyword pairs as type.
7. **Reframe instead of cut** inside complex UI (log → timeline → whole window) with 1.4–1.6 s ease-in-out moves, so viewers know where to look.
8. **Small texture motifs** (halftone dot edges, glowing tile grids, pixel sparkles) give a flat product demo a designed, branded finish.
9. **The CTA goes dark and simple:** blurred product UI as a texture, logo, one pill button, one line of instruction.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1920×1080`, `30 fps`, 815 frames (27.2 s). A 9:16 variant works by stacking the same cards vertically and scaling to ~85% width.
- **Layer stack per scene:** `<Wallpaper src blur={18} scale={1.05}>` → `<HalftoneEdges color dotSize={2} spacing={14} opacity={0.3}>` (radial mask: visible only near the left and right edges) → `<CameraRig x y scale>` wrapping the UI layer(s) → `<UICard shadow="0 20px 60px rgba(0,0,0,.25)" radius={18}>` → `<Cursor path clickAt>` → overlay FX (`<TileGridDissolve>`, `<DotRingBurst>`, `<PixelSparkles>`).
- **Components & timings:**
  - `CameraRig`: keyframed `{x, y, scale}` with `Easing.bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)` for reframes (42–48 frames) and an additive constant drift `x -= 0.5 * frame`.
  - `MomentumSlideIn`: `translateX: interpolate(f, [0, 22], [width * 0.25, 0], {easing: Easing.bezier(0.05, 0.7, 0.1, 1)})` with `filter: blur()` 6 → 0 px over the first 6 frames. Pair it with an `ExitAccelerate` on the previous scene: last 14 frames `translateX` 0 → −60 px with `Easing.in(Easing.quad)`.
  - `BloomOpen`: white layer opacity 0 → 1 (frames 0–12, `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`) with a scale pulse of 1.0 → 1.05 → 1.0.
  - `TileGridDissolve`: 16×9 grid; each tile has a random delay from 0–30 frames, its white fill opacity goes 1 → 0 over 8 frames, and its outline glow fades out over 10 frames.
  - `KeywordPair`: two text blocks flanking a centre object at x = 25% / 75%. Each word scales 0.9 → 1 over 3 frames, colour `#c4e35a` → `#ffffff` over 9 frames, appears at `wordStart + 3` frames.
  - `StreamingTypewriter`: `chars = Math.floor((f - start) * cps / fps)` with cps = 150 for the "AI" prompt (shot 2) and VO-locked for shot 6 (map each word's whisper timestamp minus 6–20 frames to its character index). The leading 8 characters use `color: mix(#2a9fe0 → #2b2b2b)` by distance from the head. Caret blinks every 16 frames.
  - `Cursor`: SVG arrow or hand; path with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)` over 5–8 frames. Click = scale 0.9 for 3 frames, and the target button's bg flashes `#ff8a1f` for 8 frames.
  - `DotRingBurst`: SVG circle of dots (r = 0.35 × height), scale 0.8 → 1.3, opacity 1 → 0, 8 frames.
  - `PixelSparkles`: 12 white squares, random positions within 300 px of the logo, each 4 frames on with a scale pop, then fading out over 9 frames, staggered 1 frame.
  - `CTAEndCard`: blurred (24 px), darkened (rgba(0,0,0,.7)) screenshot; logo; pill button `spring({damping: 18, stiffness: 220, mass: 0.6})` scale-in; instruction line typed at 25 chars/s.
- **Tricky bits:** the "focus window" glass card (sharp inside, blurred outside) is two copies of the wallpaper: a blurred full-frame copy, plus a sharp copy clipped to the card's rounded rect (`clip-path: inset(... round 24px)`) with a 1 px `rgba(255,255,255,.6)` border and a subtle inner glow. For user footage, the same trick frames a highlight region of a clip.

## 9. Variation ideas
