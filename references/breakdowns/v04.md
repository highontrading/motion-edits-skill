# v04 — "They copied your product? Good." (brand-identity manifesto told through UI mockups and moody 3D props)

**Source creator handle:** earn.edits · **Format:** 720×1280, 9:16, 30 fps, 36.17 s (1085 frames) · **Shots:** 15 real (14 detected; the 5.27 "cut" is an in-shot punch-in, the 24.83 "cut" is a 2-frame invert flash; 3 white-on-white cuts at 15.27, 19.73 and 22.33 were missed) · **Avg shot:** ~2.4 s (range 0.33–5.5 s) · **Music BPM:** 136 (driving bed, VO-led edit) · **VO:** yes, English, punchy motivational delivery with short rhetorical sentences

**Style tags:** ui-mockup, device-mockup, notification-ui, chat-ui, kinetic-type, typewriter, word-by-word-captions, counter-ticker, neon-glow, 3d-render, depth-of-field, glow, grain, motion-blur, frame-in-frame, slow-push, speed-ramp, label-badge, handwritten-accent, cta-endcard, ramp-into-cut*, letterbox-bars*, flash-frame* (*proposed)

**One-line essence:** every sentence of a motivational script is "typed into" a different familiar interface or moody prop scene (Spotify tracklist, neon buttons, Instagram caption, iPhone lock screen, Notes, X composer, film clapper). Each scene is shot like a product ad with shallow depth of field, and every cut is hidden inside a camera move that accelerates into the cut and decelerates out of it.

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** motivational brand-strategy short ("copycats can't copy you") with a creator-product CTA (AE project file).
- **Beats:**
  - 0.00–1.78 **Hook:** "They copied your product? … Good." A provocative question, then a one-word reversal.
  - 2.06–7.26 **Escalating permission list:** three "Let them…" sentences (copy your logo / mimic your colours / repackage your offer).
  - 7.50–11.06 **Turn:** "So what?" → they can copy the product but not *you*.
  - 11.28–18.96 **Proof by intimacy:** the 2 AM idea, your energy, your story, your vision (things that can't be stolen).
  - 19.54–21.74 **Thesis:** "Products can be copied, but brands can't."
  - 22.08–30.04 **Chant/payoff:** antithesis pairs "Let them replicate / You'll keep innovating / Let them watch / You'll keep moving / they'll never match your fire".
  - 30.32–35.90 **CTA:** "After Effects users, this project file is on earnedits.com. Visit now and download it."
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** frame 0 is already mid-move. A dark 3D space holds an orange book-poster reading THEY COPIED YOUR PRODUCT, plus floating "GOODBYE" print scraps and a phone. The camera is pushing in at its fastest speed (zoom velocity 1.4 at frame 1, decaying to 0.1 by frame 17, an ease-out). At 0.77 s (frame 23) a **diagonal yellow-black caution-tape bar slams across the frame** with a 1-frame zoom kick (zoom 3.24, decaying ×0.65 per frame). From 1.40 s the whole layer stack accelerates left (pan_x −0.37 → −7.0 px/frame over 7 frames) and the edit cuts at peak speed into a tilted Spotify album page reading "Good".
- **Script → visual rule:** each sentence gets its own *interface* and the words of the sentence become the content of that interface: track titles, button labels, a social caption, a lock-screen notification, a text bubble, a note, timeline list items, a tweet draft. The only "pure type" scenes are the thesis and chant lines.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** UI-mockup / device-mockup montage mixed with photoreal 3D prop still-lifes (books, alarm clock, CD, hanging telephone handset, clapperboard, vintage camera). Every scene is lit like a product ad: one warm key light, deep shadows and heavy depth-of-field falloff. The UI scenes use real app chrome (Spotify, Instagram, iOS lock screen, iMessage, Apple Notes, X composer, iOS camera) so the script feels "found in your phone".
- **Backgrounds & textures:** alternates **dark/moody** (near-black teal `#031114`, `#0c181e`, black void) with **bright/clean** (IG white `#ededed`, Notes white `#fefefe`, light grey `#efefef`). Light-dark polarity flips on almost every cut: dark → dark-tilted UI → neon dark → white IG → warm wood → dark shelf → white Notes → grey card → grey type → grey type wall → dark teal props → pure white → black letterbox → black → dark camera UI. The wooden-desk scene has **window-blind gobo shadows** (diagonal light bars). The props scenes have floating dust/bokeh particles. The CTA has fine film grain and dust specks on a black clapper.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** 15 shots in 36.2 s ≈ 0.41 cuts/s. Shots are 1.6–2.4 s in the hook and permission list, 2–4.5 s in the "can't steal" block, 1.0–1.4 s in the chant (fastest section, 23.7–26.9), and 5.5 s on the CTA. There are no sub-0.5 s shots except the flare.
- **Easing signature: "ramp-into-cut".** At 9 of the 14 real cuts the outgoing shot **accelerates exponentially into the cut** and the incoming shot **starts at speed and decelerates**:
  - Velocity before the cut roughly ×1.4–1.5 per frame over the last 6–8 frames (e.g. pan_x −0.37, −0.65, −0.98, −1.31, −2.11, −3.77, −7.02 → cut; zoom −0.20, −0.32, −0.44, −0.58, −0.74, −0.97, −1.27, −3.16 → cut).
  - After the cut the decay is either fast (×0.5 per frame, settled in 3–4 frames: 11.27, 23.73) or long (×0.88 per frame, ≈12 frames: 7.33).
  - Net effect: the viewer never sees a static cut; the energy is carried across it.
- **In-shot punches:** bell-shaped zoom pulses with an **asymmetric 4-frame attack / 6–8-frame release** (8.27, 8.9, 9.8, 5.27). Peak velocity 5–8 units/frame at analysis scale, i.e. a 6–10% scale change. These land on notification arrivals and key words.
- **Entrance / exit vocab:**
  - *Ease-out slide-in with blur* ("Good", INNOVATING, LET THEM): 4–6 fr.
  - *Neon light-up* (opacity/glow ramp with scale 0.9→1): 3–4 fr.
  - *Grey→black word reveal* (colour interpolation): ~6 fr per word.
  - *UI typewriter:* 1 char per frame (~30 cps) or word-chunked to the VO, with a blinking caret (≈16 fr period).
  - *Notification drop-in:* slide down 20 px + fade, 6 fr, with a camera punch.
  - *iMessage bubble pop:* scale 0.6→1.05→1, ≈6 fr.
  - *Sparkle spring:* scale 0→1.15→1, ≈8 fr.
  - *Stamp* (WATCH): scale 1.4→1, 3 fr, no fade.
  - *Write-on doodles:* stroke dash, 6–15 fr.
  - *Film-frame pop-on:* translate + rotate settle, 5 fr.
  - *Fade-in UI chrome:* 5 fr.
- **Camera:** almost every shot carries a slow constant drift or push (net zoom +13…+22 in shots 1, 2, 4, 14; net pan −103 px at analysis scale ≈ −410 px real in the phone shot). Combined with the ramp-into-cut, the camera never truly stops. DOF and 3D tilt (Spotify page ≈ −25°, phone ≈ 15°) make flat UIs feel like physical objects.
- **Secondary motion:** pendulum sway of the hanging handset; bokeh specks drifting; the LED counter; the caret blink; scrolling type-wall rows in alternating directions; the fire video playing inside the composer.
- **Sync:**
  - **VO-driven, not beat-driven.** Mean |cut − nearest VO word onset| = 0.07 s; mean |cut − nearest beat| = 0.10 s. With a beat period of 0.44 s, 0.10 s is chance level.
  - Cuts land on the **first word of a sentence** within ±0.03 s in 8 of 14 cases (3.33 vs "Let" 3.40; 4.93 vs 4.90; 11.27 vs 11.28; 23.73 vs 23.70; 25.93 vs 25.92; 26.93 vs 26.92; 30.30 vs 30.32).
  - In-scene reveals are word-locked: neon buttons within 0.03 s, typewriters track the VO in real time, the LED counter lands on "2 AM", the squiggle lands on "you". Plain-type scenes run ~0.25–0.3 s behind the VO, which reads as an echo.
  - The only beat-ish hits are SFX impacts on pops (strong onsets at 8.87, 9.96, 10.82, 20.71, 24.03, 25.19).

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Ramp-into-cut (momentum cut)** at 1.57, 3.33, 4.93, 7.33, 11.27, 13.20, 15.27, 19.73, 22.33 and 23.73. The outgoing camera move (pan, zoom, tilt or rotation) accelerates over 6–8 frames with growing motion blur; the cut lands on the peak-velocity frame; the incoming shot starts with matching-direction velocity and eases out over 4–12 frames. Rebuild: wrap both scenes in a `<CameraRig>`. Outgoing `interpolate(f, [end-8, end], [0, 1], {easing: Easing.in(Easing.exp)})` drives translate/scale. Incoming `interpolate(f, [0, 10], [1, 0], {easing: Easing.out(Easing.exp)})` drives the residual. Add `<CameraMotionBlur shutterAngle={180} samples={8}>` during the last and first 4 frames.
2. **Pull-back reveal into frame-in-frame** at 4.93: the neon "screen" zooms out to become an Instagram post. Rebuild: render scene A inside a `<PostCard>` and animate the card scale from frame-filling (≈2.4×) to 1× over 8 frames with an ease-out.
3. **Invert flash** at 24.83: 2 frames of a white/inverted frame mark a new chant line. Rebuild: `filter: invert(1)` or a white solid at 85% for 2 frames.
4. **Slide-out to blank** at 26.9: the type exits left, accelerating, followed by a hard cut to black.
5. **Flash-out + TV-off flare + fade-up** at 30.30–30.97, separating story from CTA:
   - 1 black frame, 1 zoom-blurred frame, black;
   - a white anamorphic starburst (radial gradient + 2 px horizontal streak + 4 diagonal rays) collapses scaleY→0.02 then scaleX→0 over 4 frames;
   - 3 black frames;
   - opacity 0→1 over 10 frames into the CTA.
6. **Card push** at ≈17.5 and ≈19.2: duplicate cards side by side translate left as a unit over 2–3 frames (a slide-push between list states).

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Put the script inside familiar interfaces.** Track names, button labels, captions, notifications and drafts make the message feel native to the viewer's phone and give every line a fresh "set" for free.
2. **Never cut on a still frame.** Accelerate into every cut (×1.45 per frame over 6–8 frames) and decelerate out of it, so the edit feels like one continuous camera flight.
3. **Light the UI like a product.** Tilt screens in 3D, add depth of field, gobo shadows, bokeh and warm keys. Flat screenshots become premium objects.
4. **Alternate dark and light on every cut.** The luma polarity flip (≈35 ↔ 235) is a free rhythm device that keeps the viewer's eye refreshing.
5. **Reveal words when the VO says them, in the UI's own grammar:** a button lights up, a track highlights, a caret types, a counter lands on "2 AM".
6. **One accent colour per scene, with semantic consistency:** green = you/winning, red/orange = the copycat.
7. **Use a size hierarchy inside a phrase.** The verb is huge and the connective is tiny ("They / can't / steal", "Visit now / & download it").
8. **Bookend with a "power-off" moment** (flare collapse + black + fade-up) to signal the CTA without breaking the mood.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, duration = VO length + ~0.3 s (≈1090 frames here). Mark VO sentence onsets from Whisper timestamps and place scene boundaries exactly on them (0 to +1 frame).
- **Typical scene stack (bottom → top):**
  - `<SceneBackground>` (solid/gradient or a 3D-prop still with `filter: blur()` on far layers)
  - `<GoboShadows>` (optional SVG diagonal bars at 25% multiply)
  - `<CameraRig>` (3D tilt `rotateX/rotateY`, slow push, ramp-in/out)
  - `<UIMockup type="spotify|instagram|lockscreen|notes|timeline|xcompose">` built from DOM
  - `<WordSyncedReveal>`, then foreground props with DOF
  - `<LetterboxBars ratio={0.226}>` when needed
  - `<Bokeh>`, `<GrainOverlay opacity={0.06}>`
- **Key components:**
  - `RampCut({outMove: {x?: px, scale?: n, rotate?: deg}, inResidual, outFrames: 8, inFrames: 10})`. Easing: out = `Easing.bezier(0.7, 0, 0.84, 0)`, in = `Easing.bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`. Motion blur via `@remotion/motion-blur` `CameraMotionBlur` (shutterAngle 180, samples 8).
  - `PunchZoom({at, amount: 0.08})`: scale bump over 12 frames, attack 4 (`Easing.out(Easing.quad)`), release 8 (`Easing.inOut(Easing.cubic)`).
  - `NeonButton({label, color: '#6cb903', litAt})`: `box-shadow: 0 0 6px color, 0 0 24px color, 0 0 48px color55`, opacity and glow 0→1 over 4 frames, `spring({damping: 14, stiffness: 220, mass: 0.6})` scale 0.9→1.
  - `TypewriterField({text, startFrame, cps: 30 | words: whisperWords, caretColor})`: caret blink `Math.floor(f/8)%2`.
  - `TrackHighlight`: the list row whose `startFrame <= f` animates color `#7a7a7a → #ffffff` over 5 frames.
  - `CounterTicker({from: 1, to: 120, format: 'mm:ss-ish', frames: 45, easing: Easing.inOut(Easing.cubic)})`, rendered in a DSEG7 / LED font in `#ff3b1f` with a glow.
  - `GreyToBlackWords`: each word `color: interpolateColors(f - wordStart, [0, 6], ['#9a9a9a', '#1a1a1a'])`.
  - `SparkleBurst`: SVG 4-point stars, spring `{damping: 10, stiffness: 180}` scale 0→1, a ±8% idle twinkle every 20 frames, `#00ff6a`.
  - `WavyUnderline`: SVG path with `strokeDashoffset` over 6 frames.
  - `TypeWall({phrase, rows: 14, accentRows: [5..9], speed: 2})`: rows translateX alternating ± direction with an ease-out start.
  - `StampWord`: scale 1.4→1 over 3 frames, `WebkitTextStroke: '3px #000'`.
  - `InvertFlash({frames: 2})`.
  - `TVOffFlare({frames: 8})`: radial-gradient star; `scaleY 1→0.02` (frames 0–3), `scaleX 1→0` (frames 3–6), with an additive streak; then black and a `FadeIn({frames: 10})`.
  - `HandwriteOn`: a script font converted to SVG outlines (or Vara.js-style) with a stroke-dash reveal at ≈1 letter per 2 frames.
  - `FilmFramePop`: a polaroid/film-strip image, translate + rotate from ±8°, spring `{damping: 16, stiffness: 160}`.
- **Tricky bits:**
  - Tilted 3D UI with DOF: put the UI in a `transform-style: preserve-3d` wrapper (`rotateX(18deg) rotateZ(-25deg)`, `perspective: 1200px`) and render a duplicate blurred copy masked by a linear gradient for focus falloff.
  - The caution-tape slam is a large PNG with motion blur, entering in 1 frame plus a 0.03 scale kick on the camera.
