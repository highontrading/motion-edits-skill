## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** a character-comparison micro-essay (Batman vs Joker) used as a **process/BTS showcase** for an editing-template business. The edit and the tool are shown together: top = the work, bottom = proof it was made in AE.
- **Beats:**

  - 4.0–6.6 **Batman's assets:** "money, power, respect", with one tarot card per noun.
  - 7.3–13.9 **Batman's cage:** "trapped by his past" → "follows rules" → "still believes the world can be saved" (gallery of framed posters → chained mirror portrait → globe rising from waves).
  - 14.6–18.9 **The turn:** "The Joker has no rules, no hope, nothing to lose" (mask on a newspaper desk → Phoenix-Joker stills).
  - 19.3–23.2 **Antithesis payoff:** "Batman fights the darkness. / The Joker became it." The giant JOKER title sits behind the Ledger figure.
  - 23.6–28.8 **CTA (no VO):** EDITORS + three AE icons on a grid-paper desk → yellow card with EARNEDITS → EARNEDITS.COM.
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** frame 0 is already mid-move. The Joker card fills the comp and the camera is pulling back at top speed (flow 1.12 at f1, decaying to 0.33 by f10). The sun-face, swirl rays and clouds are revealed around it. Nothing on screen is static, and two kinds of visual noise grab attention at once: the coloured gizmos and the scrolling timeline.
- **Script → visual rule:** each VO sentence gets its own 3D "set" (a backdrop plane + 3–8 cut-out layers + a camera move). List nouns become separate physical objects (MONEY / POWER / RESPECT tarot cards). Only the punchline phrases become type (BY HIS PAST, HE STILL BELIEVES, JOKER BECAME IT).

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** 12 inner scene changes in 23.6 s of VO = one per ~2.0 s; range 0.95–3.35 s. The longest holds go to scenes with a title reveal (scene 7 HE STILL BELIEVES 3.35 s, scene 13 CTA 3.1 s). The Joker-turn section (17.3–19.3) is the fastest, with two ~1 s Phoenix stills. The outer frame never changes, so even at this pace the reel feels calm.
- **Easing signatures (per_frame.csv, 15 fps):**
  - *Opening pull-back:* starts at peak velocity (flow 1.12 at f1) and decays monotonically to 0.33 at f10. That is an ease-out with no ease-in (`Easing.out(Easing.exp)`), ~10 frames at 15 fps ≈ 20 frames at 30 fps.
  - *Zoom-through 1.53–2.27:* flow 0.44 → 0.85 → 1.33 → 1.54 → **2.18 (1.80)** → 1.21 → 0.96 → 0.74 → 0.54 → 0.43 → 0.31 → 0.20. That is 4 frames up and 7 frames down, an asymmetric ease-in-out weighted to the out side (≈ `bezier(0.5,0,0.15,1)`). Zoom is positive the whole way (no overshoot).
  - *Tarot card fly-ins:* POWER 5.40→5.67 peak (4 frames) → 6.00 settle (5 frames); RESPECT 6.07→6.27 (3 frames) → 6.93 (10-frame tail). Fast attack, long ease-out. Rotation flips sign (−1.4 → +0.12) at 6.80, a tiny settle overshoot of ~1–2°.
  - *Scene arrivals after a hard cut* keep moving: 4.00 (zoom −0.95 → settle in 5 frames), 19.27 (zoom +0.68 decaying over 8 frames), 21.53 (zoom +1.15 → +0.13 in 3 frames). The new scene always enters "already moving", like a camera that never stops.
  - *Real-px scale:* flow ×4 for full-frame px, and ×~4 again within the comp window. Peak in-comp velocities are ~40–55 px/frame at 720 px wide.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:**
  - camera pull-back reveal (10 frames)
  - zoom-through into the next set (11 frames)
  - tarot card fly-in with 3D Y-tilt and a small rotation settle (3–4 frames attack, 5–10 frames settle)
  - prop rising from below the frame edge (Joker card 2.4 s, globe 12.5 s; ~5–8 frames, ease-out)
  - pendulum swing on hanging stars and lamps (period ~1 s, ±5°)
  - chains swinging in (~6 frames)
  - L→R glyph reveal on BY HIS PAST (~1 glyph/frame)
  - word-by-word pop on HE STILL BELIEVES and BECAME IT (pop ≈ 2–3 frames)
  - 3D Y-flip per glyph on ".COM" (~3 frames each, staggered 2 frames)
  - staggered icon pop (3 icons, 3-frame stagger)
  - hand-drawn ellipse write-on (~5 frames)
  - Exits are almost never animated: the next scene cuts in mid-motion.
- **Camera:** an AE 3D camera on every set. The camera is never locked off: slow pushes of ~+0.1–0.3 zoom/frame, a 1 s dolly-out reveal on the poster wall, and a spin-push (rotation ~+1° per frame for 3 frames) into the Phoenix still. Parallax comes from 3–5 depth planes per set (backdrop, mid props, hero, foreground clouds/chains).
- **Secondary motion:** swinging hanging stars and lamps, drifting clouds, bobbing waves, a gently tilting card, the live AE playhead and scrolling layer bars in the bottom panel (constant low-level motion), and the gizmo arcs that move with their layers.
- **Sync:**
  - *VO-driven cuts* land on the sentence's first word: 4.00 vs "Batman" 4.00 (0.00); 7.40 vs "But" 7.32 (+0.08); 11.27 vs "He" 11.44 (−0.17); 14.60 vs "The" 14.58 (+0.02); 19.27 vs "Batman" 19.34 (−0.07); 21.47 vs "The" 21.56 (−0.09). The mean is ≈ −0.04 s, essentially on the word.
  - *Noun props* land on their word: card peaks at +0.0 to +0.3 s after word onset; the globe starts 0.09 s before "world".
  - *Type* appears 0.1–0.3 s after its word starts (JOKER +0.23, BECAME +0.73 is the exception, IT +0.13).
  - *Music-locked cuts* in the no-VO CTA: 23.60 (beat 23.75, −0.15), 26.73 (beat 26.75, −0.02), 28.00 (beat 28.03, −0.03). After the VO ends, the editor switches from word-sync to beat-sync.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Zoom-through push** (1.53–2.27): the camera accelerates forward through the current set (4 frames), the next set's curtain frame is already behind it in Z, and it decelerates into the new composition (7 frames). Rebuild: two scene layers in 3D space (`translateZ` 0 and −1500 px) and a camera `translateZ` driven by `interpolate(frame,[0,11],[0,1500],{easing: Easing.bezier(0.5,0,0.15,1)})`, with the outgoing scene fading out over the last 3 frames.
2. **Hard cut into continuing motion** (4.00, 7.40, 19.27, 21.47): a clean 1-frame cut where the incoming scene already has a camera push/pull at ~70% speed that decays over 3–8 frames. Rebuild: start the incoming scene's camera spring at `frame = -4` (pre-roll) so frame 0 is mid-ease.
3. **Dark fly-through** (9.53–9.80): a dark glossy object (cape/cowl surface) fills the comp for ~3 frames and wipes into the next set. Rebuild: a full-frame dark PNG with motion blur scaling 1→4 and translating through the camera over 4 frames; switch scenes on frame 2.
4. **Spin-push + dissolve** (17.13–17.47): rotation +0.7 → +1.1 → +0.4 per frame and zoom +1.4 while luma drops 59→44 (warm desk → dark still). It runs ~5 frames with a cross-dissolve inside. Rebuild: outgoing scene `rotate(0→8deg) scale(1→1.25)`, incoming `rotate(-6→0deg) scale(1.2→1)`, opacity crossfade over 4 frames.
5. **Prop occlusion** (12.47–12.8): the rising globe covers the title instead of the title animating out. It works as a transition *within* the scene and hands focus from type to object.
6. **Backdrop drop** (28.00, on beat): the outer painting cuts to black while the comp keeps playing, a "curtain falls" ending. Rebuild: `<Sequence>` end of the backdrop layer at the beat frame.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Show the machinery.** Leaving 3D gizmos, motion paths and the live timeline visible turns an ordinary edit into a credibility piece ("this is real 3D comp work"). It suits template sellers, course creators and agencies.
2. **Frame the work like art.** Hanging the comp window as a canvas inside a themed oil painting (a clown painting for a Joker story) makes the raw UI part of the theme.
3. **Every sentence is a built 3D set** with 3–5 depth planes and a camera that never stops. The new scene arrives already moving.
4. **List nouns become physical objects** (one tarot card per word) that fly in on the word with a 3–4 frame attack and a 5–10 frame ease-out.
5. **Colour-code the two sides:** cool blues for Batman and warm wood/lamp/red checker for Joker, so the antithesis reads without captions.
6. **Use type sparingly:** only 3 punchlines become text, set huge (85–90% of comp width), and the hero stands *in front* of the title for depth.
7. **Objects replace text exits:** the globe rises over HE STILL BELIEVES instead of the text animating out.
8. **Switch sync source when the VO ends:** word-locked cuts during VO (mean offset ≈ 0 s), beat-locked cuts (≤ 0.15 s early) for the CTA.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, duration = VO + ~5.5 s CTA tail (≈ 870 frames for this script).
- **Outer layout (BTS mode):** `<PaintingBackdrop src>` filling the top 58% (y 0–1110 at 1080×1920) → `<CompWindow x={264} y={82} w={552} h={980}>` holding the actual scene → a `<FakeEditorUI>` panel at y 1110–1920 (dark `#1d1d1d`, a mini preview of the same scene at 0.4× scale, a timeline of 30–40 coloured layer bars with a moving playhead `left = frame/total`). Use the same `<Scene>` component twice (main + mini preview).
- **Gizmo overlay (optional):** an SVG layer per animated prop that draws red/green/blue axis arrows (40–60 px), a blue quarter-circle rotation arc and a dotted motion path following the prop's position. 1.5 px strokes, `#ff3b3b` / `#35d24a` / `#3b6bff`, 80% opacity.
- **Scene stack (bottom → top):** `<Backdrop texture>` (z −800) → `<Cutout depth={-400}>` mid props → `<Hero depth={0}>` → `<TitleBehindHero>` (rendered between backdrop and hero) → `<Foreground depth={200}>` (clouds/chains) → `<Grain opacity={0.07}>` → `<Vignette strength={0.35}>`. Wrap in a CSS `perspective: 1200px` container. Camera = transform on the container: `translateZ` and `rotateZ`.
- **Components & timings (30 fps values):**
  - `CameraPush`: `scale` 1→1.08 over the scene, linear plus an initial ease-out kick. Incoming scenes start with `spring({frame: frame+8, config:{damping: 26, stiffness: 90, mass: 1}})` so frame 0 is already mid-motion.
  - `ZoomThrough`: 22 frames, `Easing.bezier(0.5,0,0.15,1)`, the outgoing scene `scale 1→3` + opacity 1→0 over the last 6 frames, the incoming scene `scale 0.6→1`.
  - `CardFlyIn`: from `translateY(60%) rotateY(35deg) rotateZ(-8deg) scale(0.7)` to rest, `spring({damping: 14, stiffness: 160, mass: 0.8})` (≈ 7 frames attack, ~2° overshoot), triggered at `wordStart(noun)` from the Whisper JSON. Previously landed cards `translate(-20%,-25%) scale(0.75)` on the same spring.
  - `RiseFromBelow` (globe/card): `translateY(110%→0)` over 12 frames, `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`, starting 3 frames before the word.
  - `Pendulum` (hanging stars/lamps): `rotate = 5*sin(2π·frame/30 + phase)`, transform-origin at the top.
  - `GlyphReveal` (BY HIS PAST): per-glyph opacity/`clipPath inset(0 100% 0 0 → 0)` with a 2-frame stagger.
  - `WordPop` (HE STILL BELIEVES): per word `scale 1.3→1` + opacity, 5 frames, `Easing.out(Easing.back(1.4))`.
  - `GlyphFlip3D` (.COM): per glyph `rotateY(90→0deg)`, 6 frames, 4-frame stagger.
  - `IconStagger`: 3 icons, `spring({damping: 10, stiffness: 220})`, 6-frame stagger.
  - `EllipseWriteOn`: SVG path `strokeDashoffset` length→0 over 10 frames, `#d62828`, 5 px, with a slight hand-jitter path.
  - `SpinPushDissolve`: 10 frames, as in §5.4.
- **Text behind subject:** render the title layer *under* the hero cut-out PNG (alpha). No roto is needed when the hero is already a cut-out. For live footage, use a segmentation matte (e.g. pre-computed person mask video) as the hero layer.
- **CTA:** a yellow radial gradient `radial-gradient(circle at 50% 50%, #f4f40c 0%, #cecc0e 45%, #8c8a09 100%)` + grain 0.12. Brand text Montserrat Black white with a `-webkit-text-stroke: 3px #9a9a9a`.
- **Ending:** cut the `<PaintingBackdrop>` to black on the last strong beat (frame of beat 28.03 s).

## 9. Variation ideas
1. **Template/course promo:** show *any* finished edit inside a mock editor UI (Remotion code panel, Premiere, Figma) framed in a themed painting or photo. Gizmos and a moving playhead prove craft; end on the product URL.
2. **Character comparison series (anime, sport rivals, brands):** keep the 3D-set-per-sentence grammar and colour-code each side (cool vs warm), one object per listed trait, with the title behind the hero for the payoff line.
3. **Founder or brand story for an agency reel:** cut-out team photos on tarot cards ("VISION / CRAFT / SPEED"), paper-cut backdrops per chapter, and a beat-cut yellow logo card. Drop the BTS frame for the client version and keep it for the social teaser.
