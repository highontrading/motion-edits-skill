**One-line essence:** a business-news story told as a chain of surreal product "stages" (picnic table, floating island, CRT, weight-loss machine, clipboard, newspaper) that each settle in with a soft 1-second scale ease, held together by one constant caption bar. The hero keyword type changes style per scene (3D extrude, orange badge, handwriting, marker underline), so every clause feels freshly art-directed.

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** business/market-disruption micro-documentary ("famous brands threatened → by a non-food thing → origin story → consequences list → analyst number → thesis → CTA").
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–4.4 **Hook:** three of the world's biggest food brands are named and then told they "just got a new competitor". Brand recognition plus a mystery.
  - 4.8–7.6 **Tease by negation:** "it doesn't sell" burgers / soda / snacks, with the product swapping on each noun.
  - 8.2–9.2 **Reveal:** "a weekly injection". A cut to black with an orange badge is the first tonal break.
  - 9.6–14.2 **Origin:** Ozempic was designed for diabetes → "something unexpected happened".
  - 14.5–22.6 **Mechanism:** rapid weight loss, no junk-food cravings, "not because of discipline" but chemistry.
  - 22.9–31.2 **Consequences montage:** snack sales weaker, fast-food targets missed, alcohol brands warn. Nine real-footage clips in 8.6 s.
  - 31.5–37.4 **Authority number:** a Morgan Stanley estimate of $30B a year, shown as a newspaper clipping.
  - 37.6–43.9 **Thesis:** one drug disrupted every industry "built on human overconsumption" (black → white flash title).
  - 44.1–48.7 **CTA:** "Editors, project file on earnedits.com. Visit now and download it."
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** frame 0 is an empty picnic table in a formal garden. By 0.23 s a Coca-Cola bottle, a burger with fries and a Pepsi can fly in from the left, bottom and right, oversized and motion-blurred, and land by 0.9 s while the camera eases right. The caption THE COCA-COLA COMPANY is already on screen at frame 0. Recognisable logos plus a physical "arrival" stop the scroll.
- **Script → visual rule:** each sentence gets one "stage", and each named noun inside the sentence gets a camera move, a product swap or a light sweep on that prop. The scene's on-screen hero type is the sentence's verb phrase (IT DOESN'T SELL, IT'S A WEEKLY INJECTION, PEOPLE STARTED LOSING WEIGHT RAPIDLY, NOT BECAUSE OF DISCIPLINE). The caption bar carries the full VO in 2–4-word chunks.

Cut-out product photos and AI-/stock-generated sets (formal garden, purple sky with a floating grass island, a cluttered 80s office with a beige CRT, a factory hall with a weight-loss "machine" on a conveyor, school lockers) are mixed with a 9-clip **real-footage montage** (supermarket shelves, fast-food kitchen, Heineken commercial) and a fake newspaper clipping. It ends with pure kinetic type. The look is "magazine collage", not flat vector.
- **Backgrounds & textures:** every stage is a full-bleed photographic set with shallow depth of field (background soft, hero sharp). ## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** 0.41 real cuts/s overall, in three distinct tempos. (1) **Stage scenes** 2.9–5.5 s each (0–22.9 s), where the variety comes from in-scene events (pans, swaps, light sweeps, write-ons) every 0.5–1 s rather than cuts. (2) **Footage montage** 22.96–31.53: 9 clips at a 0.95 s average (min 0.33 s), the fastest part. It reads as an evidence list. (3) **Slow finale** with 6.1 s newspaper, 2.9 s and 3.6 s type cards and a 4.7 s CTA.
- **Stepped animation:** all graphic motion updates on a 2-2-3-frame rhythm (≈12 fps inside 30 fps), so moves look slightly "stop-motion"/collage-like. Footage clips run at native frame rate. Rebuild with `frame - (frame % 2.5)` style quantisation (or render graphics at 12 fps and conform).
- **Easing signatures (from per_frame.csv):**
  - **Scene settle (signature move):** nearly every collage stage enters scaled ~108–115% and decays to 100%. Per-step zoom −3.9 → −2.4 → −1.8 → −1.5 → −1.2 → −0.9 (ratio ≈0.78 per 12 fps step) gives ~0.6 s to 90% settled and ~1.2 s to rest. There is **no overshoot** (no sign flip in zoom) and a long linear-ish tail after. Equivalent: `Easing.out(Easing.exp)` over 30 frames, or `spring({damping: 200, stiffness: 60, mass: 1})` (critically damped).
  - **Camera pans in shot 1:** symmetric ease-in-out bells. Pan 1: 0.13 → peak 0.53 → 0.97 (~26 frames). Pan 2: 1.03 → peak 2.20 → 2.54 (~45 frames, longer acceleration). Pan 3 starts at peak velocity right after the hold (2.97) and decays over 17 frames (ease-out only). Use `Easing.inOut(Easing.cubic)` for travel pans and `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)` for "return" pans.
  - **Newspaper spin-in:** rotation and scale decay with ratio ~0.8 per step over ~1.0 s, a textbook expo-out. Use `interpolate(f,[0,30],[1,0],{easing: Easing.bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)})` driving scale 1.3→1 and rotate −15°→−2°.
  - **Punch-in at 34.2:** a 1-step jump followed by 0.5 s ease-out. **Pull-out at 36.3:** ease-in over 0.4 s (zoom −1.0 → −3.9), then a cut to the settled frame and a 0.5 s ease-out. Overall `Easing.inOut(Easing.quad)` ~27 frames.
  - **Placard rise:** ~0.7 s, a small pop (+zoom), then a settle with residual rotation decay, at most a light overshoot. `spring({damping: 14, stiffness: 120, mass: 0.8})` on translateY from +60% to 0 and rotate −10°→−3°.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:**
  - Product fly-in from off-frame with motion blur (~0.8 s).
  - Light/specular sweep across a prop (~7 frames).
  - In-place prop swap on the noun (hard swap, shadow persists).
  - Ghost-fade title lines/words (opacity 0.3 → 1 over ~4 steps / ~10 frames, 3-frame stagger).
  - Twin-bar wipe badge (two bars from opposite sides, ~7 frames).
  - Word pop on a screen.
  - Hand-drawn ellipse draw-on (~7 frames).
  - Handwriting write-on, word-synced (~0.25 s per word).
  - Marker underline draw-on (~0.4 s).
  - Placard rise from the bottom.
  - Black → white flash with text inversion (10 frames).
  - Outline-box draw-on + typewriter in the CTA.
  - Exits are almost always hard cuts; nothing animates out.
- **Camera:** collage stages always carry a slow pull-out after the settle (net_zoom −10 to −18 per shot), so no frame is ever static. In-shot pans are used to "point" at named items. Footage clips keep their own native moves (push-ins on the Heineken, handheld on shelves).
- **Secondary motion:** props bob on the island; the before/after men move along the conveyor in a loop; the clipboard sways slightly; the CTA backdrop video keeps playing.
- **Sync (measured):**
  - Cuts land **on the VO word**, not ahead of it: offsets from the first word of the new phrase are 0.00 ("And"), +0.03 ("It's"), +0.08 ("Ozempic"), +0.03 ("People"), +0.05 ("because"), +0.04 ("Snack"), +0.02 ("Fast"), +0.03 ("According"), +0.02 ("One"), −0.01 ("Editors"). The median is +0.03 s, i.e. the cut sits 0–3 frames after the word starts.
  - Beat alignment is loose (cut-to-nearest-beat offsets range −0.26…+0.25 s at 117.5 BPM). **VO drives the edit and the music is a bed.**
  - Write-ons and word pops start 0–0.1 s after each spoken word.
  - Prop swaps lead their noun by ~0.05–0.15 s.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Hard cut on the phrase start** (most cuts): the new stage is already settling (scaled ~110%) on frame 1, so even a hard cut feels like a soft "arrival".
2. **Punch-in cut + continuing pan** at 12.55: cut to the same image at ~150%, offset right, with a pan continuing in the same direction that eases out over 0.8 s. It reads as a camera "dive" toward a detail (the sticky note). Rebuild: same `<Img>` with a scale/translate jump, then `interpolate` translateX with expo-out.
3. **In-place object swap** at ~6.2 and ~7.15: the prop is replaced on the noun with no transition; the island, title and shadow remain. The cheapest possible "new visual per noun".
4. **Black → white flash** at 40.37–40.71: background luma ramps over ~10 frames (ease-in), the white text fades out and the black text starts building. It works as a mid-sentence tonal flip ("didn't just X — it Y").
5. **Cut to black statement card** at 8.21 and 37.64: tonal breaks before reveals.
6. **Footage montage hard cuts** at 0.33–1.2 s intervals, timed to word starts (montage = list of evidence).
7. **Spin-in settle** (newspaper at 31.53): the scene arrives with rotation and scale and decays (described above).

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **One stage per sentence, one event per noun.** Hold a scene 3–5 s but trigger a pan, swap, light sweep or write-on on every named thing, so attention resets every ~0.7 s without cutting.
2. **Settle-in instead of static.** Every graphic scene enters at ~110% and eases to rest over ~1 s with an expo-out, then keeps drifting. It reads as "camera" and hides hard cuts.
3. **Change the type treatment per scene, keep the caption constant.** Six different hero-type looks (3D extrude, knock-out badge, CRT pop, placard print, three handwriting styles, newspaper underline) sit over one rock-solid caption bar at y 75%. The variety stays readable.
4. **Handwriting for the "human/organic" clauses.** Written-on script appears exactly where the VO is about feelings, surprise or biology ("unexpected", "chemically changed their appetite").
5. **Use real footage as evidence.** The consequences section switches from staged collage to fast real B-roll, which signals that this is really happening.
6. **Authority as a physical artefact.** Numbers come from a newspaper clipping with marker underlines, not from a chart.
7. **Statement cards on void with a flash flip** for the thesis, so the moral lands with zero visual noise.
8. **Stepped 12 fps animation + halftone + grain** gives everything a printed-collage tactility.
9. **Cut exactly on the first syllable** of each new sentence (median +0.03 s), with music as a bed.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, duration = VO + 0.5 s. Quantise graphic animation to 12 fps: `const stepFrame = Math.floor(frame / 2.5) * 2.5;` and feed `stepFrame` into all `interpolate`/`spring` calls for collage layers. Footage uses the real `frame`.
- **Typical scene stack (bottom → top):** `<StageBackground src blur={0..6}>` → `<HalftoneOverlay dot={3} opacity={0.12}>` → props (`<Cutout>` with contact shadow) → `<HeroTitle variant>` → `<SettleCamera>` wrapping all of the above → `<CaptionBar>` (outside the camera so it never moves) → `<GrainOverlay opacity={0.06}>`.
- **Components & timings:**
  - `SettleCamera`: `scale = interpolate(stepFrame, [0, 30], [1.12, 1.0], {easing: Easing.out(Easing.exp), extrapolateRight: 'clamp'}) - 0.0015 * Math.max(0, stepFrame - 30)` (post-settle drift ~ −4.5% per second).
  - `PanTo({targetX, start, dur})`: `Easing.inOut(Easing.cubic)`, 26–45 frames, max speed ≈ 0.55 frame-widths/s.
  - `PropFlyIn({from: 'left'|'right'|'bottom', delay})`: translate from 120% off-frame, scale 1.4→1, `filter: blur()` from 8px→0 over 24 frames, `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`.
  - `LightSweep({angle: 60, dur: 7})`: a white linear-gradient band (20% width, opacity 0.7, `mix-blend-mode: screen`) translating across the prop, masked to the prop alpha.
  - `PropSwap({items:[{src, at}]})`: hard swap at word start − 3 frames.
  - `GhostWord({at})`: opacity 0.3→1 and `blur(2px)`→0 over 10 frames, `Easing.out(Easing.quad)`; stagger by VO word times.
  - `TwinBarBadge`: bar A `scaleX` 0→1 from the left, bar B from the right, 7 frames each with a 4px horizontal motion-blur edge, then the text clip-reveal over 4 frames.
  - `Extrude3DText`: 8–10 stacked `text-shadow` layers `1px 1px 0 #393835`, plus a `0 12px 18px rgba(0,0,0,.35)` drop.
  - `HandwritingWriteOn({font, words})`: per-word `clip-path: inset(0 X% 0 0)` from 100→0 over `(wordDur)` frames. For true stroke animation use an SVG single-line font with `strokeDashoffset`.
  - `DrawEllipse` / `MarkerUnderline`: SVG path, `strokeDashoffset` over 7–12 frames, stroke 5px, round caps (`#f39a1e` / `#6cc93a`).
  - `SpinInCard`: rotate −15°→−2°, scale 1.3→1, 30 frames `Easing.bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)`.
  - `PunchIn({scale: 1.7, originX: '80%'})`: a 1-frame jump of 40% of the delta, then the remainder over 15 frames ease-out. The reverse pull-out uses `Easing.inOut(Easing.quad)` over 27 frames.
  - `FlashInvert`: background `interpolateColors(f,[0,10],['#000','#fff'])` with `Easing.in(Easing.quad)`; text colour flips at frame 5.
  - `CaptionBar({chunks})`: Oswald 600, 44 px at 1080 w, `background: rgba(0,0,0,.72)`, `padding: 6px 10px`, centred at y 75%. Hard switch per chunk at its first word's start (no animation).
  - `CTA`: `<OffthreadVideo>` backdrop with `filter: grayscale(1) contrast(.5) brightness(1.5) blur(1px)`, a script write-on, a `SettleCamera`-style icon pop (1.15→1, 18 frames), and `TypewriterInBox` (outline `strokeDashoffset` + caret).
