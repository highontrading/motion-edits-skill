## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** news-mystery micro-documentary ("death → panic → evidence → twist → open question → CTA"). Note that the CTA is a cliff-hanger: it never answers the question.
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–6.1 **Hook:** a scientist died mysteriously, and plague fears resurfaced. Lab → coffin → grave → masked crowd.
  - 6.5–14.0 **Setup / evidence 1:** the date card "OCTOBER, 2026" → the named worker (red name tag) → a newspaper clipping with a highlighted headline.
  - 14.7–20.7 **Escalation:** "Rumors of possible plague infection spread" as one-word blueprint cards → NBC clipping with green highlight and a circled "200".
  - 21.4–27.0 **Context:** a family watching TV news → a wall of archival B/W plague photos.
  - 27.0–33.6 **Turn:** "But there was a twist." (stacked italic words) → a Russian official at a press conference → B/W close-up on "no outbreak".
  - 34.1–36.3 **Open question:** "So what actually killed the scientist?" A burn iris opens into a red-lit portrait, seen through a keyhole.
  - 37.0–42.6 **CTA:** "Some editors rewatch this. Others open the project file on earnedits.com." 3D type on grid paper → retro room with CRT, AEP tag and a URL pill.
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** 0.00–0.20 a defocused lab window (bright, calm). At 0.20 the scientist **drops in from the lens**: she starts ~3× scale and blurred, then scales down to her mark in 4 frames with a long ease-out. At ~1.0 s (VO "died" 1.16) a **coffin box grows up behind her** while she stands in it. At 1.50 a rough-edged circle of earth opens around the coffin (grave seen top-down). Three escalating literal visual puns land in 1.5 s, and the calm-lab → coffin reversal is the scroll-stopper.
- **Script → visual rule:** each VO sentence gets one *location* (lab, grave, sanatorium, desk, living room, wall, ministry). Within it, the noun is shown literally (coffin for "died", masked crowd for "plague", a newspaper for "reported"). Typography appears only for dates, names, headlines and the rhetorical pivot ("But there was a twist").

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** footage-led cinematic stills. Photoreal AI-generated plates (consistent "character" across lab, newspaper photo and red-room scenes) animated with 2.5D pushes, layer drop-ins and short image-to-video motion (the official gestures, mic forest). Print-prop inserts (two newspapers, a photo wall) act as "evidence" interludes, and pure type cards act as narrator interludes.
- **Backgrounds & textures:** every scene is a different physical world. Bright clinical lab (#e4edf1 / #86b5cf) → B/W top-down grave on black soil (#151619) → golden-hour brick sanatorium (#513c2b + peach sky) → **black scratched-film card** (#1a1b1e with white dust/scratch particles) → blue lab → dark plank wall with a held newspaper → **blueprint grid** (#1c3667 / #294478) → mahogany desk with noir props (rotary phone, stamps, camera, folders) → teal 1970s living room (#2d433e / #4d7366) → **slate concrete wall with film damage and light beams** (#2f3e41) → dark teal-black gradient (#161e22) for the "twist" words → grey Moscow ministry → red darkroom (#762e2b / #bd3934) → grey **grid paper with window-shadow gobo** → green poster-covered retro room (#21432b).

Film dust/scratches on dark cards. Volumetric light beams on the photo wall. Heavy vignette on dark scenes. **Fake depth of field** (foreground crowd, mics and chair backs blurred; lower stacked words blurred). Directional motion blur on every move. Red bloom/glow around the burn iris. A white flash frame at 24.57.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** 16 shots in 42.6 s (0.38 cuts/s); shots 1.3–4.3 s, median ~2.3 s. The pace is almost flat. Energy comes from in-shot motion rather than cut rate. The fastest stretch is the hook (1.67 and 1.33 s shots). Evidence shots (newspapers, photo wall) get the longest holds (3.8–4.3 s) so the viewer can read highlights.
- **Signature, the momentum cut:** shots don't end on a still frame. They end on an **ease-in acceleration** that is still speeding up when the hard cut hits: tilt-down at 5.8–6.5 (flow ×5 over 21 frames), newspaper fall at 13.5–14.67 (×3 over 35 frames), zoom-out at 10.9–11.4 (×11 over 15 frames), keyhole push at 36.7–37.0 (×4 over 10 frames). The next shot then usually *opens* with an ease-out decay (drop-in, pull-back, slide-in), so the energy carries across the cut.
- **Easing signature:** entrances are a short ramp (4–8 frames to peak) followed by a **long exponential decay** (15–40 frames). Examples: the scientist drop-in peaks at frame 4 and drops to 25% by frame 10. The TV pull-back decays from 5.3 to 0.2 px/f over 39 frames. The plate slide-up takes 8 frames to peak and 14 to settle. There is no overshoot anywhere (no sign flips in pan/zoom), so the feel is cinematic and weighted rather than bouncy. Rebuild with `Easing.bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (expo-out) for entrances and `Easing.bezier(0.7, 0, 0.84, 0)` (expo-in) for exits into cuts.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:** drop-in-from-lens (scale 3→1 + blur, 4 + 12 frames); grow-from-floor prop (coffin, 8 f); rough-edged circle reveal (5 f); lid slide (10 f); plate slide-up (22 f total); walk-in subject with motion blur (10 f); name-tag bar-extend + text wipe (16 f); typewriter with cursor (2 f/char) + growing underline; highlighter sweep word by word (~3–4 f/word); hand-drawn ellipse (4 f); fly-in-from-corners photos (10 f) + white flash (2 f); rotated-blur settle for single words (4–5 f); stacked word build with DOF; foreground mic slide-in (6 f); burn iris (6–12 f); pendulum rope drop (~30 f, damped); URL type-on (~2 f/char).
- **Camera:** almost every shot has a slow 2.5D push or pull: pulls dominate (negative net_zoom in 12 of 15 detected shots: −12 to −152), and the CTA room is the one strong push-in (+49). Rotation is used twice as a style beat (blueprint grid net rot 38°; photo-wall rotate-out). The handheld shake appears only once, on the B/W "no outbreak" close-up, to mark doubt.
- **Frame-rate texture:** the TV room, photo wall and CTA type (21.4–28.0 s, 36–38.5 s) are **animated on 2s** (flow alternates ~3 px/f and 0.0 every frame, i.e. 15 fps inside a 30 fps timeline). Combined with per-frame jitter on the photos, this gives an archival/stop-motion feel. Everything else is smooth 30 fps.
- **Secondary motion:** subject sway in the AI plates, scrolling website on the CRT, swinging AEP tag, drifting date line, light beams sliding across the wall, the ghosted double-exposure on the B/W close-up.
- **Sync:**
  - **Music:** 9 of 17 cut/transition points sit within ±0.08 s of a beat (117.5 BPM, 0.51 s grid). All the big section changes are on-beat: 3.00 (−0.02), 6.50 (+0.02), 14.67 (+0.04), 17.17 (−0.06), 21.43 (−0.05), 24.57 flash (−0.04), 37.00 (−0.01).

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Momentum cut (accelerate into hard cut)**: 6.50, 14.67, 37.00 (and 11.50 as a plate swap). The outgoing shot's camera move ramps up with ease-in over 10–35 frames and is cut at peak velocity, often onto black or a contrasting bright frame. Rebuild: animate the outgoing camera with `Easing.in(Easing.exp)` over the last 15–20 frames, add directional blur proportional to velocity, then hard-cut. Start the next shot with an ease-out move.
2. **Drop-in from lens**: 0.20. A cut-out subject starts at ~300% scale, defocused, and scales to 100% in ~16 frames (peak velocity at frame 4) over a background that is simultaneously pulling back. Rebuild: `scale: interpolate(f,[0,16],[3,1],{easing: expoOut})` + `blur(24px → 0)`.
3. **Rough-edged circle reveal (earth iris)**: 1.50–1.67. A circular matte with a torn-dirt edge grows from centre to cover the frame in 5 frames, revealing the top-down grave, then a 1-frame bloom flash and desaturation. Rebuild: SVG `clipPath` circle with a turbulence-displaced edge (`feTurbulence` + `feDisplacementMap`, scale 20), r 0 → 110% over 5 frames.
4. **Plate slide-up**: 8.67 and 39.0–39.4. The new full-frame plate slides up from the bottom over the old one (8 frames ease-in, 10–14 frames decay, peak 30–52 px/frame real) with vertical motion blur. At 39.0 the old card (grid paper) slides up and away to reveal the room beneath.
5. **Desaturate → zoom-out into print (zoom-through to evidence)**: 10.80–11.90. Saturation drops 84 → 35 in 6 frames, a red coffin-shaped frame strokes on around the subject, and the camera pulls back so the plate becomes the photo inside a newspaper. The plate is swapped for the full newspaper composite during the fastest, blurriest frames (11.50). Rebuild: one `<Sequence>` where the photo is nested inside the newspaper comp at the same screen position; scale the newspaper comp from `1/photoScale` → 1.
6. **Rotate-and-shrink → rotated word settle (rotation match)**: 27.1–27.6. The photo grid rotates ~−1°/frame and shrinks with blur. Next frame "But" appears at a similar tilt and settles flat in ~4 frames.
7. **Vertical slide-push**: 29.2–29.6. The text card and the press-conference plate move up together (peak 69 px/frame, 19 frames total, ease-in-out with a sharper in) with heavy vertical blur. The "twist." word is still visible at the top while the flag scene fills the bottom.
8. **Burn iris**: 34.03–34.5. A small red-hot spot appears at the speaker's mouth, glows (bloom), then opens as a circle with soft pink-red edges revealing the next scene (0 → ~60% width in 6 frames, full by ~12 frames). Rebuild: radial `clipPath` + an outer ring of `radial-gradient(#ff3b3b → transparent)` with `mix-blend-mode: screen`; animate r with expo-out.
9. **Keyhole pull-back / push-in**: 35.47 and 36.7–37.0. A keyhole matte (black with a thin warm rim light) enters as the camera pulls back with a defocus pulse, then pushes back in, accelerating into the cut.
10. **White flash on impact**: 24.57–24.60, a 2-frame full white overexposure when the photos land (an "impact" frame).

## 6. Sound design
A dark, pulsing cinematic bed at ~117.5 BPM sits under a calm, close-mic'd narrator. Strong onsets cluster on the typing (6.99–7.64: four clicks under the date type-on), on whoosh-style transitions (9.01 plate slide-up, 14.88 first blueprint word, 29.47 vertical whip) and on CTA ticks (39.78 slide-up, 41.24/41.82 URL typing). Tension is carried by grade (B/W, red) rather than by audio stops.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Cut at peak velocity.** End shots on an accelerating move and start the next on a decelerating one. The cut then feels like motion continues through it, even across unrelated locations.
2. **Literal visual puns in the hook:** "died" → a coffin grows behind the living subject while she stands there. Make the first verb physically happen to the subject within 1 s.
3. **Evidence interludes as tactile print.** News facts become physical newspapers (held by hands, lying on a prop-dressed desk) with highlighter and marker annotations. Clipping, highlight and circle reads as proof.
4. **Desaturate to mean death or denial.** Grade shifts (colour → B/W) are tied to semantic beats, not used decoratively.
5. **Two type voices only:** a cinematic italic serif for narrator interludes and a functional grotesk for data (date, name). The loud 3D display face is saved for the CTA.
6. **Weighted expo-out easing, no bounce.** Keep it serious: 4-frame ramps, 15–40-frame decays.
7. **Mix frame rates:** archival or retro sections on 2s (15 fps) with jitter, and the rest smooth at 30 fps.
8. **Lens-warp and fake DOF on flat backgrounds** make graphic cards (grid, blueprint, planks) look photographed.
9. **Cliff-hanger CTA:** ask the question, then route the curious to the product.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, duration = VO + ~0.4 s (≈1278 frames for this script).
- **Layer stack per typical scene (bottom → top):** `<Plate src parallax={{bgScale:[1.0,1.08]}}>` (AI still or clip, with a `useCurrentFrame` slow push) → optional `<CutoutSubject>` layer (separate PNG for 2.5D) → `<ForegroundBlurLayer>` (mics, chair backs, crowd heads at `blur(8px)`) → props/annotations → `<Typography>` → `<FilmFX>` (grain + dust/scratches) → `<PixelMeshOverlay opacity={0.05} pitch={3}>` → `<Vignette strength={0.35}>`.
- **Components and timings:**
  - `MomentumCut`: wraps the last N=15–20 frames of a scene, applies `translate/scale` with `Easing.in(Easing.exp)` and blur = |velocity| × 0.4 px; next scene's first 15–40 frames use `Easing.out(Easing.exp)`.
  - `DropInFromLens`: scale 3 → 1, blur 24 → 0 px over 16 frames, expo-out.
  - `GrowProp` (coffin): `scaleY 0 → 1` from the bottom anchor over 8 frames, expo-out.
  - `EarthIris`: SVG clipPath circle with `feTurbulence baseFrequency=0.04` displacement on its edge; r 0 → 1.1 × diag over 5 frames.
  - `TypewriterDate`: 2 frames/char, cursor `|` blinking every 8 frames while typing, `<Underline>` width = measured typed width (2 px, #d9232b), whole group `translateY(-15px)` over 45 frames, linear.
  - `NameTag`: red rect `scaleX 0 → 1` (origin left) over 6 frames, text `clipPath inset(0 100% 0 0) → inset(0 0 0 0)` over 10 frames, expo-out. Track position from a keyframed anchor array (or a manual 3–5 key track) to follow the subject.
  - `PrintZoomOut`: desaturate via `filter: saturate(1 → 0.35)` over 6 frames, then the parent newspaper comp scales from `photoScale⁻¹` to 1 over 20 frames (ease-in to frame 15, then expo-out after the plate swap).
  - `HighlighterSweep`: per-line `<div>` behind the text with `scaleX` 0 → 1 (origin left), 3–4 frames/word, colour #f2e23c or #5fe82f at 85% opacity, `mix-blend-mode: multiply`.
  - `MarkerEllipse`: SVG ellipse path, `strokeDashoffset` len → 0 over 4 frames, stroke #e0402a 4 px, slight rotation −8°.
  - `SingleWordCard`: italic serif word, first word enters `rotate(-10deg) scale(1.15)` → 0/1 over 5 frames; subsequent words hard-swap on `wordStart` frames from the Whisper JSON. `text-shadow: 5px 7px 6px rgba(0,0,0,.6)`.
  - `StackedWords`: each new word inserted at the top; container `translateY` += lineHeight per word with a 5-frame expo-out; `filter: blur(n × 1.5px)` for the word n lines below the newest.
  - `PhotoFlyIn`: 4 cards from the 4 corners (±700 px) to a 2×2 grid over 10 frames, ±3° rotation; `WhiteFlash` 2 frames at landing; render the group on 2s (`Math.floor(frame/2)*2`) and add ±0.6° per-step jitter.
  - `BurnIris`: circle clip r 0 → 1.2 × diag over 12 frames (expo-out), with a ring glow `box-shadow: 0 0 60px 30px #ff3b3b` and a 4-frame pre-glow spot.
  - `KeyholeMatte`: SVG keyhole mask scaled 3 → 1 (pull back) with a `blur(10px)` pulse for 6 frames.
  - `Type3DStack` (CTA): per-letter pop (scale 0.6 → 1, opacity 0 → 1, 2 frames) timed from word timestamps; extrude = 10 stacked `text-shadow` layers 1 px apart in a 35%-darker shade, plus `drop-shadow(0 12px 10px rgba(0,0,0,.25))`.
  - `RopeTag`: pendulum `rotate = A·e^(−t/τ)·sin(2πt/T)`, A = 18°, T = 0.7 s, τ = 0.6 s, pivot at the top of the rope; enters by translateY −400 → 0 over 8 frames.
  - `URLPill`: glass pill (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`, 1.5 px white 60% border, radius 999), typed at 2 frames/char.
- **Lens warp:** apply `feDisplacementMap` with a radial-gradient displacement map (or a WebGL barrel shader, k ≈ 0.08) to flat backgrounds only.
- **On-2s sections:** wrap in a `<Freeze>`-per-2-frames helper: `const f = Math.floor(frame/2)*2` passed down via context.

## 9. Variation ideas
1. **True-crime / unsolved-case Shorts:** same evidence grammar (case-file folders on a desk, polaroids flying onto a corkboard with a flash, red marker circles), with B/W for victims and a keyhole or peephole matte for the question.
