## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** SaaS/product promo for an AE project-file marketplace (earnedits.com), as a problem → solution → 3-step benefit list → comment-for-link CTA.
- **Beats:**

  - 1.17–3.17 **Pain 1:** "are you still starting every edit from zero?" A counter physically counts down to 0 behind the bust.
  - 3.27–4.60 **Pain 2:** "Deadlines are tight" (a sticky note of clients being scribbled out, the grade turns red).
  - 4.70–5.83 **Pain 3:** "and ideas take time" (a clock drops in, red highlight on "time").
  - 5.83–11.10 **Solution:** earnedits.com gives you real open AEP projects, not templates (website card + orbiting 3D AEP icons).

  - 13.97–17.07 **Benefits as a numbered list:** 01 Take proven ideas · 02 Re-edit them · 03 Finish your projects faster.
  - 17.20–20.00 **CTA:** "Comment "LINK" and we'll send it to you." A 2.8 s hold.
- **Hook mechanics (0–1.5 s):** frame 0 is already typeset: a centred grey "editors" with **outlined echo copies** bleeding off both sides, like a marquee. A small right-aligned quote types in at the top-right (0.0 → 0.8 s), and a thick grey stroke draws itself across the frame (0.27 → 0.80 s). At 0.9 s the frame rotates and defocuses (a rack-focus whip) into the next scene. Three layers of motion in 1 s on an almost-empty white page: calm, but clearly designed.
- **Script → visual rule:** each VO sentence gets one prop-driven scene whose prop is a **visual pun on the sentence** ("from zero" → a countdown to 0; "deadlines" → a crossed-out client list; "take time" → a clock; "open AEP projects" → physical AEP file icons; "real edits" → real reels playing in a phone). Captions show a paraphrase of the line (2–8 words), not the full transcript.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** ~12 scenes in 20 s (~0.6/s). Scene lengths go 1.2 / 1.5 / 0.5 / 1.3 / 1.1 / 5.0 / 2.1 / 3.1 / 2.8 s: fast through the three pains (0.5–1.5 s each), long holds for the product (5 s) and the list (3 s). The **0.1-second flicker montage** at 12.0–12.6 is the single burst of speed, placed under "already went viral".
- **Easing signatures (measured):**
  - **Ease-in into every transition:** rotation 0.38→0.57→0.72→0.94→1.34 over 5 frames into the 1.17 cut (≈1.3× per frame); the push before 4.50 rises 0.12→2.16 over 6 frames; the icon fly-through rises 1.86→2.40→3.60→4.89→6.75→6.94→12.83 over 7 frames. Use `Easing.in(Easing.exp)` or `bezier(0.7,0,0.84,0)` for 6–8 frames.
  - **Settle after the cut:** scenes either re-focus from blur over ~13 frames (1.17→1.60) or snap in with a short 5–6 frame scale-down (6.13–6.33; clock 4.73–4.90) and no overshoot (zoom never changes sign).
  - **Exposure ramps:** bleach to white over 7–11 frames (luma +90–100), hold white for 3–7 frames, then the new scene fades or glitches in within 1–3 frames.
- **Entrance vocab:**
  1. **Blur-to-sharp word reveal** (the signature caption): each word goes from ~8 px blur at 30 % opacity to sharp at 100 % over ~6 frames, with no position change.
  2. Typewriter by word groups (the quote).
  3. SVG stroke draw-on (thick hook stroke 16 frames; hairlines ~12 frames each).
  4. Rapid counter tick-down with deceleration.
  5. Red caret → highlight-box wipe (6 frames).
  6. Marker scribble-out write-on.
  7. Slide-in list cards (~12-frame ease-out, alternating sides).
  8. Prop slide-up + scale-down (clock).
  9. Y-axis spin of the 3D icon.
- **Camera:** mostly locked-off, with slow pushes (shot 4: +15.6 zoom over 0.8 s) and slow drifts. The big moves are all transitions (rotational defocus, punch-in, fly-through). Layered DOF gives depth without moving the camera.
- **Secondary motion:** blue drips animate on the bust, the dotted orbit arc rotates, the HUD stays still, the clock hands turn, the icons float and spin, the light streaks glide, the green ribbon undulates, and real video plays inside the phone card.
- **Sync:**
  - **Cuts on words and onsets:** the cut to "deadlines" at 3.27 is +0.03 s after the word (3.24), on the onset at 3.251. The flash to the product (5.83) is 0.05 s before "Earnedits" (5.88) and on the onset at 5.898. The "real edits" scene (11.20) is +0.08 s after "Real" (11.12). The clock entrance (4.70) sits on the onset at 4.737, and the red "time" box (5.33) on the onset at 5.317.
  - **Captions:** they generally trail the word by 0.25–0.35 s ("ideas" 4.44 → 4.73, "re-edit" 14.68 → 02 card 14.93, "Comment" 17.32 → 17.60). They lag more (~0.7 s) when a transition is in the way ("are you still" 0.78 → 1.60; "Take proven ideas" 13.28 → 13.97). "finish your projects faster" is in sync (15.76 vs 15.73).
  - **Beats:** at 144 BPM (0.418 s), the flicker swaps (12.00, 12.10, 12.20, 12.27, 12.37, 12.47, 12.53, 12.63) run at ~3 frames, i.e. 1/4-beat subdivisions starting on the beat at 11.958.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Rotational defocus whip** (0.87–1.60): the outgoing frame rotates (~5°) and blurs with an ease-in over 6 frames. Cut at maximum blur, and the incoming scene starts blurred and racks to sharp over ~13 frames. Rebuild: `rotate(0→-5deg)` + `blur(0→14px)` over 8 frames with `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)`; next scene `blur(14→0)` over 13 frames with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`.
2. **Punch-in on the same subject** (2.67): a hard cut to ×2.3 scale of the same composition, used as emphasis on "zero".
3. **Burn-to-white + light leak + blinds glitch** (2.93–3.30): overexpose to pure white over ~5 frames with pink/blue fringes, a 1-frame warm light leak (orange/pink gradient blob), 1 frame of horizontal venetian-blind lines over the new image, then a clean new scene.
4. **Push + bleach** (4.33–4.70): the push accelerates while exposure ramps to white over 8 frames; the next scene emerges from the same white.
5. **RGB-split flash** (5.83–6.13): a 1-frame white burn with chromatic offset, then interlaced scanline frames, 4 frames of pure white, and scanline glitch-in of the new scene with a scale-down snap.
6. **Object fly-through wipe** (10.87–11.20): a 3D prop flies at the camera (7-frame ease-in), and its dark face fills the frame for 2 frames (the matte). The next scene is revealed as it passes (luma 70 → 244 in 2 frames).
7. **Light-leak burn** (13.33–13.97): a warm flare sweeps from a corner, the frame goes to white over 11 frames, holds ~7 frames, and the next scene fades up from white.
8. **RGB-fringe glitch dissolve** (17.07–17.23): content splits into red/blue ghost copies with dotted pixel dropout and dissolves to white in 5 frames.
9. **In-frame flicker montage** (12.00–12.63): not a scene transition. The phone card's content hard-cuts every 2–3 frames while the surrounding layout stays still.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **White as a luxury colour:** an off-white base, 60–80 % negative space and small precise props read as "Apple". Rotate one accent colour per scene against the constant white.
2. **Visual puns for every line:** countdown → "from zero", clock → "take time", crossed-out note → "deadlines". The prop does the explaining, so the type can stay quiet.
3. **Quiet type, loud transitions:** captions are small, grey Poppins with blur-in, and all the energy goes into flashes, leaks and fly-throughs between scenes.
4. **Exposure is the transition glue:** every major cut passes through white (burn, bleach, leak). Because the base is white, the flashes feel organic rather than like effects.
5. **Ease in hard into the cut** (6–8 frames, ~1.3× velocity per frame), then settle softly (blur rack or 5-frame snap, no overshoot).
6. **Real UI and real work in frames:** the website card and the phone card playing actual projects provide proof. The 0.1 s flicker montage says "lots of content" in 0.6 s.
7. **Layered depth on a flat page:** DOF-blurred numerals, outline echoes, HUD text and soft shadows make a white page feel three-dimensional.
8. **Numbered glass cards** for benefits: oversized gradient-masked numerals plus a 2-line label is instantly scannable.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, 30 fps, 600 frames.
- **Global layers:** `<PaperBG color="#f6f7fc" grain={0.04}>` → `<GlowBlob color={sectionAccent} position="bottom" blur={200} opacity={0.6}>` → scene content → `<FlashTransitions>` → `<Grain>`.
- **Components:**
  - `<BlurWord text start>`: `filter: blur(interpolate(f-start,[0,6],[8,0])px)`, `opacity: interpolate(...,[0.3,1])`, `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`. Poppins 400, `#4a4a4f`, `letter-spacing: -0.02em`, `line-height: 0.88`.
  - `<EchoWord text>`: a central filled word plus 2–4 copies with `color: transparent; -webkit-text-stroke: 1px rgba(0,0,0,.25)` offset by one word-width each side. Optional slow marquee of 1 px/frame.
  - `<StrokeDrawOn path width={22} gradient>`: SVG path with `strokeDasharray` = length and `strokeDashoffset` interpolated over 16 frames with `Easing.inOut(Easing.cubic)`, plus a linear gradient #333→#ccc for a tube look.
  - `<CountdownTicker from={822} to={0} frames={50}>`: value = `Math.round(from * (1 - Easing.out(Easing.cubic)(p)))`, Poppins 600, blue gradient text (`background-clip:text`), `blur(4px)` for DOF.
  - `<HUDOverlay>`: absolutely-positioned mono labels (ISO, WB, 1/60, F2.8, REC, RAW 4K) rotated 90° on both edges at 35 % grey, plus small audio-meter bars.
  - `<RackFocusWhip>`: outgoing `rotate` 0→−5° + `blur` 0→14 px over 8 frames (`Easing.in(Easing.exp)`); incoming `blur` 14→0 over 13 frames.
  - `<BurnToWhite frames={8} hold={4} fringe>`: a white overlay ramp plus an RGB split (three copies of the scene with `mix-blend-mode: screen`, tinted R/B, offset ±6 px), and an optional `<LightLeak>` (radial gradient `#ff9a5c → transparent`, sweeping from a corner over 10 frames).
  - `<BlindsGlitch frames={1}>`: a repeating-linear-gradient horizontal-stripe mask (4 px on / 4 px off) over the incoming scene.
  - `<HighlightBox color="#d12e29">`: a 3 px caret at the word's left edge, then `scaleX` 0→1 with `transform-origin:left` over 6 frames, behind the italic serif word.
  - `<ScribbleOut>`: SVG zig-zag path draw-on over 8 frames in marker purple at 80 % opacity.
  - `<FlyThroughWipe prop="aep-icon">`: 3D icon (Three.js or PNG) `scale` 1→8 with `rotateZ` 0→15° over 7 frames (`Easing.in(Easing.exp)`); swap the scene when its face covers 100 % of the frame (hold 2 frames).
  - `<FloatingIcon>`: `@remotion/three` extruded rounded rectangle (navy face `#2e2c5f`, lilac bevel `#9a8cf0`) with `rotateY = f * 3°` and `y = sin(f/30)*10`.
  - `<FlickerMontage clips frameEach={3}>`: a `<Sequence>` per clip of 3 frames each inside a rounded phone mask.
  - `<NumberedGlassCard n="01" text>`: `background: linear-gradient(#fafafa,#e9e9ec)`, `border: 2px solid #c9c9cc`, `border-radius: 28px`, `box-shadow: 0 6px 18px rgba(0,0,0,.08)`. The numeral is ~2.2× text size with `mask-image: linear-gradient(to bottom, #000 65%, transparent)`. Entrance `translateX(±110% → 0)` with `spring({damping: 22, stiffness: 140})`; stack `translateY` −90 px per new card with the same spring.
  - `<GlowRibbon>`: an SVG sine path, `stroke:#3ddc6a`, `stroke-width:14`, `filter: blur(6px)` copy under a sharp copy; animate the phase at 0.02 rad/frame.
- **Fonts:** Poppins 400/500/600; Helvetica Neue / Inter 800–900 for "deadlines are tight"; Playfair Display Italic for "time"; a mono font (JetBrains Mono) for the HUD.
