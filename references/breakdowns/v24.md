## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** business case-study micro-documentary ("crisis → numbers → question → strategy → proof → verdict → CTA").
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–5.1 **Hook:** in 2022 Netflix lost a million subscribers in a single quarter. A date, a famous brand and a loss number in the first sentence.
  - 5.1–7.4 **Stakes:** people thought it was the beginning of the end.
  - 7.4–14.3 **Turn by numbers:** three years later: 325 million subscribers and $45 billion in revenue.
  - 14.3–17.4 **Status:** still the biggest streaming platform (Netflix "N" logo moment).
  - 17.4–18.4 **The question:** "What changed." on a blank paper card, a full 1-s pause.
  - 18.4–23.1 **Answer:** stopped competing on price, started creating cultural moments.
  - 23.1–28.2 **Proof ×3 + summary:** Squid Game, Wednesday, Stranger Things, then "Shows that took over the internet".
  - 28.2–32.6 **Verdict:** didn't just survive the streaming wars, "They WON them."
  - 32.6–37.3 **CTA:** editors, grab the After Effects project file on earnedits.com (phone mockup).
- **Hook mechanics:** frame 1 is already an image with a question in it: a small gilded portrait frame on a grey wall with venetian-blind light stripes, captioned "Netflix / In 2022". The camera pushes in continuously (net_zoom +53.5) and, at 1.3–2.1 s, **flies through the picture frame**. The B/W photo becomes full-colour (sat 23 → 72) as the words "Netflix lost" land. That is a museum-to-reality move in under 2 s.
- **Script → visual rule:** one sentence = one AI-generated still of the protagonist (or a real show clip for proof). Every spoken word appears as a caption at the moment it's spoken. Numbers and the verdict get a size jump and a yellow hand-drawn mark.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** footage-led editorial. The CEO scenes are photoreal **AI-generated stills**: the same face in different sets (Netflix HQ, a street, a library desk with cash stacks, an office with posters, in front of a glass tower). They are animated with 2.5D push/pan, rack-focus and, in the last scene, image-to-video gestures (arms open at 30.4). Real footage is used only for the three show clips. Text-only "breath" cards are set on paper.
- **Backgrounds & textures:**
  - A light-grey paper texture (#c5c5c3 / #d5d5d5) with a **fine vertical ribbed-line pattern** (scanline-like, visible in every frame) and faint grain.
  - "Three years later" shows a **halftone dot screen** over the B/W photo.
  - A brown wood desk (#51352a / #614234) is the set for the newspapers and the CTA.
- **Colour:**
  - The world is mostly desaturated or B/W (mean sat on several shots is ≈0).
  - **Selective colour:** only the protagonist's navy suit and blue tie (#1d2a4a-ish) stay coloured against grey streets and buildings.
  - Accents: mustard yellow for the torn-paper tag (#c9a227), a lime-yellow hand-drawn ellipse and underline (#d8d23a), lime-recoloured money stacks (#c6d21a) and Netflix red (#e50914 on black #040404).
  - Full colour is reserved for the hook payoff (blue sky #527d9d) and the show clips (Squid Game pink #812348 / teal #3d5c69).
- **Typography:**
  - **Primary caption:** a high-contrast condensed **italic serif** (Instrument Serif Italic / Editorial New Italic / Playfair Display Italic class), sentence case.
    - On photos it is white with a soft outer glow (≈8–12 px, 40%) and a faint drop shadow.
    - On paper cards it is black.
    - Size: ~6–7% of frame width per x-height for normal words. "Netflix lost" spans ~50% of width; "What changed." ~45%.
  - **Emphasis by scale:** a key word is set 2–2.5× larger in the same italic, with the line before and after small. "Forty five" (small) / **billion** (≈55% of width) / "dollars in revenue" (small); "Three years" / **later**; "and started creating" / **cultural moments.**
  - **Hand-drawn marks:**
    - a lime-yellow loose **ellipse** circling "billion", written on;
    - a yellow **underline swoosh** under "of the end.";
    - a white hand-drawn **lasso loop** over the newspaper stack.
  - **Label badge:** "Three years later" sits on a **mustard torn-paper tag** (irregular polygon, slight rotation), white italic serif on top.
  - **Secondary face** for the verdict: a white **rounded heavy geometric sans** (Nunito Black / Quicksand Bold / Comfortaa Bold class), lowercase "the streaming **wars**" with "wars" ~2× bigger, a soft drop shadow, and mixed case "They WON **them.**".
  - **CTA:** white ultra-heavy grotesk (Inter Black / Helvetica Now Display Black), "EDITORS" at ~100% width with a step-and-repeat echo trail, and "GRAB THE PROJECTFILE" below at ~60% weight.
- **Layout & composition:**
  - Captions sit on the centre axis at ~45–55% height, over the subject's chest or face (they never fight the bottom IG UI).
  - Stacked word-builds are left-aligned at ~x 8–10% when the subject stands right of centre ("People / thought / it was / the / beginning").
  - The subject is either a centred full-body figure (shots 2, 13) or a three-quarter seated figure (shot 5). The face is always in the top 30–45%.
- **Assets:**
  - AI portraits of the CEO;
  - a gilded picture frame;
  - vintage newspapers ("Financial Herald: From DVDs to 25 Million", "Streaming Giant") with red headlines and bar charts;
  - cash stacks;
  - real Netflix posters ("The Kitchen", "Delete");
  - real show clips (one still carries a burned-in source subtitle at the bottom);
  - the Netflix N logo;
  - an iPhone mockup showing the earnedits.com site with looping template previews.
- **Finishing / FX:**
  - grain plus a vertical line screen everywhere;
  - halftone on one shot;
  - **constant shallow depth-of-field**: backgrounds soft, edges blurred, and every cut begins defocused;
  - vignettes on dark scenes (luma ~11 on the logo scene);
  - glow on captions and on the "streaming wars" sans.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** ~0.38 cuts/s, a deliberate, slow, premium pace. Shots run 0.9–5.1 s, median ~2.1 s. The **proof montage** speeds up to ~1.0 s per shot (23.1–26.2) and the **question card** holds for 1 s of near-silence. Visual change is carried by caption replacement every ~0.3–0.6 s rather than by cuts.
- **Easing signatures:**
  - **Camera pushes:** ease-out from the cut (velocity highest in the first 3–6 frames, decaying over 24–36 frames). This "lands" each new image.
  - **Lateral caption-carrying slides:** symmetric ease-in-out, 21–36 frames, peak velocity at mid-move.
  - **Fly-through:** ease-in-out over ~40 frames.
  - **Snaps** (What changed, EDITORS, logo build) are 2–3 frames.
  - **Overshoot** appears only on the CTA phone (pan_y sign flip).
- **Entrance / exit vocab:**
  - word-by-word **replace** captions (one chunk at a time, centred);
  - word-by-word **build** captions (accumulating stack, left-aligned or two-line);
  - **scale-hierarchy build** (small, BIG, small);
  - scale-down snap + blur-to-focus (What changed., ~12 frames);
  - torn-tag pop then text fill (~8 frames);
  - SVG write-on ellipse, underline and lasso (~6–10 frames each);
  - colour-toggle flash (money);
  - rise-with-overshoot (phone);
  - step-repeat echo slide (EDITORS);
  - caption exits by **travelling with the camera move** (pushed off-frame with motion blur), never by fading.
- **Camera:** virtual 2.5D on stills:
  - push-ins (net_zoom +53 hook, +20 street);
  - pull-outs (−29 newspapers, −37 CTA);
  - lateral slides (pan_x −15 to −22 street/kiosk, +32 office whips);
  - micro-rotation ±1–6°.
  
  Real depth comes from **rack focus**: subject blur vs caption sharp at 31.5; whole-frame defocus pulses at 10.6 and 13.5.
- **Secondary motion:** blind-light stripes, a flickering lamp glow, the CEO's AI-animated gesture, money colour flicker, and looping UI inside the phone.
- **Sync:**
  - **Cuts land on the first syllable of the new sentence:** 5.13 vs "People" 5.16 (−0.03), 7.37 vs "Three" 7.44 (−0.07), 8.63 vs "325" 8.74 (−0.11), 11.80 vs "45" 11.84 (−0.04), 14.27 vs "still" 14.38 (−0.11), 18.40 vs "Netflix" 18.40 (0.00), 23.07 vs "Squid" 23.14 (−0.07), 24.13 vs "Wednesday" 24.04 (+0.09), 25.07 vs "Stranger" 25.08 (−0.01), 26.17 vs "Shows" 26.20 (−0.03), 32.63 vs "Editors" 32.62 (+0.01). The median lead is ~0.04 s.
  - The VO is also laid onto the 129 BPM grid, so the same cuts hit beats: 5.13 (beat 5.108), 7.37 (7.384), 11.80 (11.865), 18.40 (18.437), 23.07 (23.081), 25.07 (25.078), 32.63 (32.694); |offset| ≤0.07 s.
  - Captions appear on the spoken word (chunk changes every 0.3–0.6 s). Emphasis marks (ellipse, underline) write on ~0.3 s after their word, while the word is still on screen.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Fly-through picture frame** (1.1–2.4): scale the framed-photo scene ~5× with ease-in-out until the frame edge leaves the canvas. The photo inside becomes the full-bleed scene and its saturation blooms 0.3→1 over ~45 frames.
2. **Focus-pull cut** (nearly every cut: 7.37, 8.63, 11.80, 17.40, 23.07, 24.13, 25.07): the outgoing shot softens over 3–6 frames, there is a hard cut, and the incoming shot starts at ~8–12 px blur and sharpens over 3–12 frames. It reads as one continuous lens focusing on the next idea.
3. **Whip pan within one set** (19.0, 19.6, 20.5): 3–12-frame lateral moves with heavy horizontal motion blur across one extended AI-generated office, so the shot can cover three "angles" without a cut.
4. **Logo light-streak zoom-through + whiteout** (16.2–17.4): the N ribbon breaks into vertical light streaks, the streaks multiply into a full-frame spectrum, then a white flash leads to the paper card. Rebuild: ~40 vertical rects with random hue/width, scaleX and opacity animated with staggered `interpolate`, plus a 2-frame white solid.
5. **Dim-out / cut to black** (7.2–7.37, 14.27): vignette closes and luma drops before a sentence change.
6. **Caption-carrying slide** (6.2–6.9, 29.0–30.2): the camera slides sideways, the old caption leaves with the background (parallax ×1.3) and the new caption is already in place on the other side.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Selective colour on the protagonist:** desaturate the world, keep the hero's suit in colour. Instant focus with a premium print feel.
2. **One editorial italic serif, used with scale contrast** (small / HUGE / small) instead of many fonts. Glow on photos, black on paper.
3. **Hand-drawn yellow marks are the only accent colour.** Use them for numbers and the key phrase (ellipse, underline, lasso, torn tag).
4. **Every cut is a focus pull.** Start incoming shots soft and rack in, so hard cuts feel like one continuous camera.
5. **Paper "breath" cards** for the question and the summary (dead-static, black serif) reset attention between busy scenes.
6. **AI stills + 2.5D:** one consistent protagonist across many invented sets, animated with ease-out pushes, lateral slides and rack-focus; add image-to-video only for the verdict gesture.
7. **Cuts on the first syllable** of each sentence (≤0.1 s lead), with the VO itself placed on the music grid.
8. **The brand moment uses the brand's own motion language** (the Netflix N ribbon streaks) for the "biggest in the world" line.
9. **The CTA reuses the set** (the wood desk from the newspapers) with a physical phone that overshoots into place.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, duration = VO + 0.3 s (~1120 frames).
- **Typical scene stack:**
  1. `<KenBurnsStill src push={{from:1, to:1.08}} ease="out" focusIn={8}>`
  2. `<SelectiveColor mask="subject.png">` (desaturate the bg layer, overlay the colour subject cut-out)
  3. `<Vignette>`
  4. `<SerifCaption>`
  5. `<MarkerDoodle>`
  6. `<HalftoneOverlay|LineScreen>`
  7. `<Grain opacity={0.08}>`
- **Components:**
  - `KenBurnsStill`: scale `interpolate(frame,[0,30],[1.0,1.06],{easing: Easing.out(Easing.cubic)})` then linear +0.01/s; `filter: blur()` from 10 px → 0 over 6–12 frames at the start and 0 → 8 px over the last 4 frames (focus-pull cut).
  - `SerifCaption({words, mode:"replace"|"stack"|"tiered", emphasis})`:
    - words from the VO word-timestamp JSON; each word appears at `wordStart − 1 frame` with opacity 0→1 and blur 6→0 px over 4 frames;
    - emphasis words at 2.2× font size;
    - white `#fff` + `text-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(255,255,255,.45), 0 2px 6px rgba(0,0,0,.4)`;
    - font: Instrument Serif Italic.
  - `MarkerDoodle({shape:"ellipse"|"underline"|"lasso", color:"#d8d23a", strokeWidth:4})`: SVG path with `strokeDashoffset` from length → 0 over 8 frames, `Easing.inOut(Easing.quad)`, starting ~9 frames after its word.
  - `TornTag({color:"#c9a227", rotate:-3})`: polygon `scale` 0→1 with `spring({damping:14, stiffness:200, mass:0.6})`, text 4 frames later.
  - `FlyThroughFrame`: parent scale 1→5 over 40 frames, `Easing.bezier(0.55,0,0.35,1)`; inner photo `saturate()` 0.3→1 over 45 frames.
  - `WhipPan`: translateX of an extra-wide plate by 35–60% over 4–10 frames, `Easing.bezier(0.7,0,0.3,1)`, plus SVG `feGaussianBlur stdDeviation="24 0"` scaled by velocity.
  - `ColorToggle`: CSS `hue-rotate`/`sepia` + `saturate(4)` filter on a masked money layer, toggled on syllable frames.
  - `LightStreakTransition` (see §5);
  - `QuestionCard` (paper bg, scale 1.4→1 in 3 frames + blur 10→0 over 12 frames);
  - `PhoneMockupCTA`: translateY 60%→0 with `spring({damping:11, stiffness:120, mass:0.9})` for the visible overshoot; screen = `<OffthreadVideo>` loop; `EchoTitle` = 5 copies at 20% steps of opacity, offset 6% width, collapsing over 4 frames.
- **Tricky effects:**
  - Selective colour without roto: generate the still with the subject on a separate layer (or segment it once with a matting model), then render the bg with `filter: grayscale(1) contrast(1.1)` and the subject on top in colour.
  - The vertical line screen: `repeating-linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(0,0,0,.06) 0 1px, transparent 1px 3px)` at `mix-blend-mode: multiply`.
  - Halftone: an SVG pattern of dots with `mix-blend-mode: overlay`.

## 9. Variation ideas
1. **Company comeback stories** (Apple 1997, Lego 2004, Marvel 2009): same structure (crisis → numbers → "What changed." → strategy → three proofs → verdict) with AI stills of the founder, selective colour on a brand-colour garment, and the brand's own logo animation for the "biggest" beat.
2. **Personal-finance explainer** ("How index funds beat 90% of pros"): newspapers and cash stacks on the wood desk, yellow ellipses around the percentages, paper question cards, a calculator/phone mockup CTA.
3. **Creator/coach testimonial:** real client footage in place of AI stills, still desaturated with the client kept in colour, italic-serif word captions, whip pans across a single wide location, and a focus-pull cut to a paper card asking the hook question.
