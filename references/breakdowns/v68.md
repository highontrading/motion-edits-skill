## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** 17 shots in 40.4 s (0.42 cuts/s). The AI story section (0–31 s) runs 1.25–5.4 s shots (median ~2.5 s). The footage payoff (31.1–35.2) **quadruples the pace** to 0.5–1.07 s per clip (≈1.2 cuts/s) for a montage rush. The CTA then holds 5.1 s.
- **VO-locked cuts:** 13 of 16 cuts land within ±0.08 s of a sentence or phrase start, typically **1 frame (0.06 s) before the word**: 2.81 "it" −0.05, 5.31 "In" −0.05, 10.69 "He" +0.01, 12.31 "inspired" −0.05, 15.25 "But" −0.03, 18.75 "the" −0.07, 20.69 "Then" −0.05, 25.06 "the" −0.02, 31.62 "Lego" −0.08, 33.31 "most" −0.01, 35.19 "Smart" −0.03. Footage cuts follow single words.
- **Music:** cuts also sit 0.04–0.19 s *before* beats (103 BPM, 0.58 s grid). The VO is paced to the music, so phrase starts and beats coincide, and the edit follows the VO. Strong onsets coincide with cuts at 2.86, 5.34, 10.73, 12.40, 15.35, 25.10 (whoosh/hit SFX on cuts).
- **Type reveal timing:** each title line starts forming within −0.2…+0.15 s of its spoken word and is solid **4 frames (0.25 s) later**. Lines stack top-down, one per VO word group. Titles exit by dissolving back into fragments (~4 frames) or leave with the plate.
- **Easing signature:** camera moves are slow, continuous pushes (+0.5 to +1.0 zoom/frame) on almost every AI set. The one long lateral move (shot 9) has a 1-second ease-in and near-linear cruise. Entrances are short ease-out (tower rise: peak at frame 5, 50% decay by frame 9). The corkboard swing is a damped oscillation (~3 half-cycles, 18 frames). Exit into the amber card is an **accelerating slide** (×5 velocity in 7 frames), the same momentum-cut idea as v67. No bouncy overshoot on type; the only overshoot is the physical pendulum.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:** rise-from-behind (tower, 8 f); pixel/ember dissolve-in for titles (4 f per line) with char-spot clean-up; pencil write-on (~20 f for 4 letters); sticker pop (2 f); morph crossfade between AI loops (~3 f); accelerating slide-out with blur (7 f); top-down colour-card wipe (4 f); fire ignition and spread (~10 f); foreground giant-arm entrance (10 f); lateral truck (ease-in 16 f); pendulum swing-in (18 f); staggered brick rise with saturation ramp (21 f); shatter-assemble icons (10 f); invert flash (2 f).

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Hard cut 1 frame before the sentence** (default, 11×). There is no transition graphic; continuity comes from the shared amber grade, vignette and dot mesh. Rebuild: `cutFrame = round(sentenceStart × fps) − 1`.
2. **Momentum slide into a colour card** (13.75–14.0): the plate accelerates down-right with motion blur (flow 1.6 → 7.7 px/f over 7 frames), cut at peak velocity, then the amber card wipes down from the top in 4 frames.
3. **Title smear cut** (25.0): one frame where the "IN 1958" title and plate are smeared horizontally (directional blur ~60 px), then a hard cut to the swinging corkboard. A cheap whip.
4. **Invert flash** (31.00–31.12 and 35.19–35.31): frame 1 = colour-inverted last frame of the outgoing shot (yellow title → blue on white), frame 2 = colour-inverted first frame of the incoming shot, then normal. Rebuild: `filter: invert(1)` on the last outgoing and first incoming frame, optionally with a +1 zoom jolt. It marks a **chapter change** (story → present-day footage, footage → CTA).
5. **Same-location revisit** (2.81 → 15.25): the desert shop returns at night to burn. A state-change match that the viewer reads as "the same shop, later".
6. **Pendulum entrance as transition** (25.06): the new shot opens with its hero prop in motion (swinging), which absorbs the hard cut's jolt.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Depth-sort your titles:** put the keyword *behind* the hero object (BILLION behind the tower) or interleave lines with the subject. It instantly reads as composited in 3D, not as captions.
2. **Materialise, don't fade:** titles assemble from glowing fragments in 4 frames on the exact spoken word. Reuse the same reveal for every title, so the effect becomes the series signature.
3. **Two-colour title logic:** setup word in white/grey, payoff words in glowing yellow-orange. Charcoal or black only on light or amber cards.
4. **One AI set per sentence, one grade for all:** wildly different sets (desert, workshop, classroom) feel like one film thanks to the shared amber grade, heavy vignette and dot-mesh overlay.
5. **Turn points get pure type cards:** flat amber or black background with the key phrase only, for the name meaning and the crisis.
6. **Switch medium for "today":** move from AI story-world to real footage for the present-day proof, and speed the cutting up ×3–4 with one-word captions.
7. **Invert flashes as chapter markers:** 2 frames, no extra graphics, and the change of section is unmistakable.
8. **Physical micro-stories inside shots:** a pencil writes the name, a sticker pops, the shop catches fire, a giant arm scoops the miniature, bricks build up. Each shot has an event, not just a camera move.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, render at `30 fps` (the reference is 16 fps; keep motion durations in seconds), duration = VO + 0.3 s (≈1213 frames at 30 fps).
- **Layer stack per AI scene (bottom → top):** `<Plate src>` (AI still/clip, `scale: 1 + 0.0015·frame` push) → `<TitleStack depth="behind">` → `<ForegroundMatte src="tower_cutout.png">` (a cut-out of the hero object, so titles can sit behind it) → `<TitleStack depth="front">` → `<EmberParticles>` → `<Vignette strength={0.6}>` → `<DotMesh pitch={3} opacity={0.06}>` → `<Grain opacity={0.05}>`.
- **Components and timings (30 fps values):**
  - `PixelDissolveTitle`: text rendered to a mask, revealed by a blocky noise threshold. Use an SVG `<filter>` with `feTurbulence type="fractalNoise" baseFrequency="0.08"` + `feComponentTransfer` step function, or a canvas of 8×8 px blocks whose opacity threshold animates 0 → 1 over **8 frames** (expo-out), starting at `wordStart − 2 frames`. Add a char-spot overlay (dark noise blobs, multiply) that fades over 12 frames, plus 20–40 ember particles drifting up 30–60 px.
  - `GlowType`: `color: linear-gradient(#ffd23a, #e08a00)` via `background-clip: text`, `text-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(255,154,0,.6), 0 4px 0 #6b3e04`.
  - `RiseFromBehind`: hero cut-out `translateY(40% → 0)` over 15 frames, `Easing.bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)`, masked by the foreground rooftops layer.
  - `WriteOn`: SVG path of the script word, `strokeDashoffset` len → 0 over 40 frames (linear), with a hand+pencil PNG whose position follows `getPointAtLength`.
  - `StickerPop`: scale 0 → 1.15 → 1 over 6 frames, `spring({damping: 10, stiffness: 220, mass: 0.5})`, rotate −15° → 0.
  - `MomentumExit`: last 12 frames `translate(0 → 25%, 0 → 40%)` with `Easing.in(Easing.exp)` and motion blur ~0.5 × velocity.
  - `ColorCardWipe`: amber radial-gradient card, `clipPath: inset(0 0 100% 0) → inset(0)` over 6 frames.
  - `Pendulum`: `rotate = −35·e^(−t/0.35)·cos(2π·t/0.6)` degrees, pivot at the top string point, t in seconds.
  - `BrickStackRise`: 12–16 brick PNGs, each `translateY(120% → 0)` over 6 frames with a 1.5-frame stagger by column, and `filter: saturate(0.2 → 1)` across the 40-frame group.
  - `LateralTruck`: background `translateX` with an ease-in over 30 frames, then linear (~9 px/frame at 1080 w).
  - `InvertFlash`: `<Sequence>` of 2 frames with `filter: invert(1)` on the outgoing last frame and the incoming first frame.
  - `FootageCaption`: one or two words, centred at y = 50%, heavy rounded face, yellow #f5c518, `-webkit-text-stroke: 3px #3a2400` + `drop-shadow(0 3px 0 #000a)`, hard-swapped on word starts with no animation.
  - `IconShatterAssemble`: 3 icons built from ~30 polygon shards each, flying in from ±80 px with random rotation, converging over 12 frames (expo-out), then a `drop-shadow(0 0 16px #8b6cff)` glow.
- **Footage integration:** grade real clips to match (warm, +saturation, heavy vignette, slight chromatic aberration via 2 offset RGB copies at ±2 px, the same dot-mesh overlay). Keep captions in screen space at centre (no tracking needed). Cut on every 1–2 VO words.
