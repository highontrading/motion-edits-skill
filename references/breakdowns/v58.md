## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** ~20 shots in 32.2 s (0.62/s). Hook shots are 0.1–2.0 s, the prompt/result ping-pong runs 0.7–3.1 s per shot, and the last result holds 4.4 s. Rhythm: **prompt (1.2–3.1 s) → result (0.7–1.0 s)** for three rounds, then one long result and one long prompt.
- **Easing signatures (measured):**
  - **Camera sway / layout slides are ease-in-out bells:** the pan at 9.0–9.5 rises 9 frames and falls 8 frames, peak 5.4 (≈38 px/frame), shaped like `Easing.inOut(Easing.cubic)`. The split-screen slide at 14.0–14.5 is asymmetric (11 frames accelerating, 5–6 decelerating), so it reads as an ease-in with a quick catch, like `Easing.bezier(0.6, 0, 0.2, 1)`.
  - **Zoom-settle on every result cut:** the cut lands with a +1.7 to +2.9 zoom kick, then decays at ×0.6–0.8 per frame (−1.8, −0.8, −0.7, −0.4, −0.3). The incoming frame starts ~3–4% oversized and settles in 6–8 frames. End card: −1.6 → −0.1 over 20 frames (×0.87 per frame).
  - **Pixel dissolves are ease-in builds** (20 frames, diff 1 → 13) that end on the cut, so the energy peaks exactly at the transition. The highlight burst at 4.35–4.95 is a symmetric bell (8 up / 4 hold / 10 down).

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Colour-wash bloom open** (0.00–0.23): frame 0 is a soft white-to-blue sky gradient. A blue overlay at ~70% opacity then lifts while the wallpaper sharpens (7 frames). Rebuild: a solid `#2b91f5` layer with opacity 0.9 → 0 and a backdrop blur of 20 → 0 px over 7 frames, `Easing.out(Easing.quad)`.
2. **Feathered colour sweep** (1.97–2.10 blue → white; 3.03–3.20 white → orange → black): a full-frame flat brand colour floods in along a diagonal with a very soft (~25% of the frame) gradient edge in 2 frames, then the next background sweeps over it the same way in 2–3 frames. Rebuild: two stacked layers, each masked by a `linear-gradient(135deg, black 40%, transparent 65%)` whose position animates from −100% → 0% over 2–3 frames.
3. **Punch-in cut** (4.10, 15.43, 27.60): a hard cut from a wide UI to a ~2.2× crop of the element the VO names (the Claude chip, the prompt box). The crop keeps the same screen position bias, so the eye doesn't search.
4. **Motion-blurred card whip** (5.30–5.57): an orange slab (the panel's border glow) shoots up 400+ px in 3 frames with vertical blur, decelerates and resolves into the panel. Rebuild: translateY 80% → 0 with `Easing.out(Easing.exp)` over 8 frames plus an SVG `feGaussianBlur stdDeviation="0 24"` → 0.
5. **Warm light wash** (7.47): luma 25 → 145 in 2 frames as an orange haze sweeps up from the bottom over the dark panel and hands over to the sunset wallpaper. In effect it is a light-leak cross-dissolve, 4–6 frames.
6. **Pixel-block wipe reveal** (12.97–13.33): bright yellow square tiles (~3.5% of frame width) grow from the right edge in a ragged column. The editor window appears behind the column as it retreats. Rebuild: a grid of N×M tiles, each with a reveal frame = `baseDelay(x) + random(0..4)`, colour `#f5d000`, each tile visible for 3 frames before switching to transparent over the new content.
7. **Pixel-tile dissolve on send** (15.7–16.2, 22.1, 28.5–29.17): orange/peach tiles (~4% of width) multiply across the prompt box with an ease-in build, then a hard cut to the result. It is the "message sent / processing" signifier.
8. **Split-screen layout slide** (14.0–14.5): the whole desktop translates right ~39% of the frame width while the dark Claude panel is revealed underneath on the left.
9. **Zoom-settle cut** (16.23, 18.47, 22.27, 29.20): a hard cut where the incoming shot starts 103–104% scale and settles to 100% in 6–8 frames.
10. **Blur-library end card** (29.20): a hard cut to a 20 px blurred, darkened product library with the logo popping on top.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Prompt → result ping-pong.** Alternate "instruction typed" and "change applied" shots, and put the results in VO silences. It is the clearest way to prove an AI or automation feature.
2. **Stage UI on a believable desktop with a beautiful wallpaper.** The real-world framing (dock, windows, cursor) makes a demo feel genuine, and the wallpaper's palette can be chosen to match the brand.
3. **Use the brand colour as a transition, not just an accent.** Full-frame feathered sweeps in blue, then orange, also signal the narrative switch (hook → product).
4. **Punch in on whatever the VO names** (the Claude chip, the prompt box), then cut back wide for the result.
5. **One "processing" signifier, used every time** (the pixel-tile dissolve on send), so the viewer learns the grammar after one use.
6. **A feedback glow on the changed object** (the monitor glow pulse) tells the eye where the change happened in a busy NLE.
7. **Zoom-settle on hard cuts** (3–4% scale decaying in 6–8 frames) makes static screen captures feel alive without camera shake.
8. **Keep the authenticity marks:** typos in prompts, a real model chip ("Opus 5.5 · Medium"), real timeline track colours.
9. **CTA = brand lockup on a blurred product library** plus a comment keyword, with word-by-word fade.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1920×1080`, `30 fps`, duration = VO + 0.4 s (≈ 966 frames for 32.2 s).
- **Layer stack per scene:**
  - Desktop scene (bottom → top): `<Wallpaper src="sunset.jpg" kenBurns={{from:1.0,to:1.03}}>` → `<AppWindow src={screenCapture|OffthreadVideo} radius={10} shadow="0 30px 60px rgba(0,0,0,.45)">` → `<Dock icons tooltip>` → `<Cursor path clicks>` → transition layers.
  - Split scene: `<SplitLayout leftWidth="39%" left={<ChatPanel prompt/>} right={<DesktopScene/>}>`.
- **Components:**
  - `ColourSweep({color, angle: 135, feather: 0.25, frames: 3})`: a full-frame div with `mask-image: linear-gradient(135deg, #000 calc(p - 12%), transparent calc(p + 12%))` where `p = interpolate(frame, [0, frames], [-30, 130])`. Chain two sweeps (colour in, next bg in).
  - `TypedPrompt({text, startFrame, charsPerFrame: 1.2, leadColor: '#e8622c', leadLength: 14})`: render `text.slice(0, n - leadLength)` white plus `text.slice(n - leadLength, n)` orange, with a caret blinking every 15 frames. Start typing ≈ VO word time + 0.3–1.2 s.
  - `PixelTileDissolve({colors: ['#f99500','#f2a96d','#e8622c'], tile: 0.04, frames: 18, easing: Easing.in(Easing.quad)})`: a grid of tiles over the target rect; a tile's visible frame = `threshold(rand(i), progress)`; tiles flash for 3 frames. Use `mode="reveal"` with yellow `#f5d000` for the window wipe (column sweeping from the right).
  - `PunchIn({rect, scale: 2.2})`: a hard cut to a pre-scaled crop.
  - `ZoomSettle({from: 1.035, frames: 8})`: `interpolate(frame, [0, 8], [1.035, 1], {easing: Easing.out(Easing.exp)})`, applied to every incoming result shot.
  - `LayoutSlide`: right-pane `translateX(-39% → 0)` over 16 frames, `Easing.bezier(0.6, 0, 0.2, 1)`.
  - `CardWhip`: translateY 80% → 0 over 8 frames, `Easing.out(Easing.exp)`, with a motion blur from `<Trail layers={5} lagInFrames={0.5}>` or `@remotion/motion-blur`.
  - `GlowPulse({rect, color: '#ffd1e0'})`: box-shadow `0 0 0 → 0 0 28px 6px` and back over 10 frames (sine).
  - `ChecklistWriteOn({lines, framesPerWord: 3, ghostOpacity: 0.25})`.
  - `EndCardLockup({logo, wordmark, cta})`: a blurred library background (`filter: blur(20px) brightness(.35)`), the logo `spring({damping: 14, stiffness: 200})` scale 0.6 → 1, wordmark lines staggered 8 frames, CTA words fading + `translateY(4px → 0)` every ~4 frames synced to the VO word timestamps.
- **Data-driven sync:** feed the Whisper word JSON in, and place each result shot at `max(prevVOEnd + 0.5 s, promptTypedEnd + 0.3 s)` so it always falls in the silence.
- **User footage integration:** put the user's own clip inside `<AppWindow>`'s monitor rect (a vertical 9:16 `<OffthreadVideo>` at ~16% of frame width). The "edit" states (new background, subtitles) can be separate renders swapped on the result cut, with the GlowPulse marking the change.

## 9. Variation ideas
1. **AI-agent / automation SaaS** (e.g. "my agent updates the CRM"): the same ping-pong. The prompt is typed in a chat panel, then a cut to the CRM table with a glow pulse on the changed row, pixel dissolve on send, and a sweep in the brand colour.

3. **Phone-app feature promo (9:16):** the wallpaper becomes a phone home screen, the split becomes a top/bottom split (prompt top, result bottom), colour sweeps run vertically, and the CTA is a comment keyword on a blurred app gallery.
