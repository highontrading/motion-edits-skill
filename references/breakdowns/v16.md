**One-line essence:** a history narrative told as halftone sports-poster collages (pitch, map, wood desk, stadium tunnel). Each composite opens with a fast zoom settle and leaves on an accelerating whip. A burst of archival trophy footage, cut faster and faster, carries the "century later" turn. A bottom subtitle bar sits under the hero type the whole time.

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** sports-history mini-documentary / brand-hype piece ("humble origin → obstacles → time jump → scale today → title drop → CTA").
- **Beats:**
  - 0–4.3 **Hook:** in 1930 FIFA launched an experiment few thought would last.
  - 4.8–13.3 **Origin story:** 13 teams flew to Uruguay; European nations declined the trip across the Atlantic.
  - 13.7–18.6 **Time jump:** "Nearly a century later" (black void), then archival trophy montage as it becomes "the biggest event in football".
  - 18.6–26.0 **Scale today:** nations spend years qualifying, billions watch, no trophy weighs more.
  - 26.3–30.0 **Title drop:** "This is the FIFA World Cup" (slow VO, black void, rotating trophy, "26" logo build).
  - 30.0–35.7 **CTA:** "Editors, project file on earnedits.com, visit now and download it".
- **Hook mechanics (0–1.5 s):** frame 1 is a zoom settle (zoom 4.8/frame decaying) on a pitch with a vertical column of ghosted years 1926→1930. The highlighted year rolls like a slot counter (1927 → 1929 → 1930) while a ball rolls up the touchline beside it, so the date literally "arrives". At 1.4 s a cut-out pair of striding legs sweeps in and kicks.
- **Script → visual rule:** one composite per clause. The key noun or number becomes the hero graphic (1930 counter, giant red 13, map with planes, DECLINED stamps, FIRST WORLD CUP tiles). A second, smaller layer of plain subtitles carries the full sentence.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** sports-poster collage. Cut-out athletes, balls, trophies, planes and hands are composited onto photographic grounds: a striped pitch, a stadium net, an aerial centre circle, a sky, a green cutting mat with a vintage map, a wood desk flat-lay, a night stadium, a players' tunnel, space with a globe, and a blue grid-paper desk. A real archival-footage section (15.4–18.7 s) breaks the collage world.
- **Backgrounds & textures:** a **coarse halftone screen over everything** (very visible on the 1930 numerals and the CTA desk), grain, dark vignette, and a thin grey film-gate edge on the void shot. Clouds/smoke PNGs are a recurring connective material (shots 3→4, 5, globe).
- **Colour:** each set has one dominant field: pitch green `#67974b`/`#486d34`, sky blue `#1f8ee9`, kraft map + green mat `#30451a`, wood `#3d241c`, near-black `#1f1d1d`, night stadium `#080c0e`, navy desk `#3a4865`, CTA yellow `#eccc3f`. The accents are **red `#e0181c`/`#e02f37`** (13, CENTURY, DECLINED, EXPERIMENT box) and **gold/yellow `#f2c200`/`#c5a548`** (YEARS, FIRST WORLD CUP tiles, the yellow cards).
- **Typography:**
  - Hero type: heavy grotesk caps (Archivo Black / Gotham Black / Montserrat Black-like), white or yellow with a **dark bevel/drop shadow** (2–4 px). "NOTION SPEND / YEARS" and "EDITORS" ≈ 75–80% width. "MORE / WIEGHT" is split either side of the trophy at ~38% width each.
  - **Painted-on-pitch type:** "FEW BELIEVED / WOULD LAST" in white condensed italic-ish caps, laid in perspective on the centre circle so it reads like pitch markings.
  - **Giant numeral:** "13" in red heavy rounded sans, ~50% width, with halftone fill, a plane flying in front of it and "TEAMS" white below.
  - **Pixel/bitmap display face** (Pixelify Sans / Silkscreen-like) for the time jump: "NEARLY A" white, **"CENTURY" red `#e0181c` at ~1.4× size and 75% width**, "LATER" white. The same face returns, outlined white with a black stroke, on the CTA "VIST NOW AND DOWNLOAD IT".
  - **Label badges:**
    - yellow cards (`#f2c80f`, 3 px dark outline, square corners) with white caps and a black stroke ("COMPETE IN THE", "THE LONG JOURNEY ACCROSS THE ATLANTIC", ~78% width);
    - a red box with yellow condensed caps ("EXPERIMENT");
    - olive/gold word tiles, one per word ("FIRST" / "WORLD" "CUP");
    - a charcoal rounded box with grain and a 2 px white border ("TRYING TO QUALIFY");
    - paper strips "0.1 SPAIN / FIFA WORLD CUP 1930" in black heavy type with red "DECLINED" rubber stamps (double-rule border, ~10° tilt).
  - **Subtitle bar (always on):** white sentence-case sans (Inter/SF Pro Semibold-like), cap height ~2.5% of frame height, on a **dark translucent rectangle** (black ~50%, no radius) centred at **y ≈ 68%**. It shows 2–4 word chunks synced to the VO and persists across cuts.
  - Logo lockup: the official "26" mark with the trophy plus "FIFA / WORLD / CUP 26" and small "EARNEDITS X FIFA".
  - **Quality warning:** the source has several on-screen typos (NOTION for Nations, ACCROSS, WIEGHT, VIST, ENGIAND). Spell-check every string in a rebuild.
- **Layout:** the hero type sits in the upper-middle band (y 15–55%) and the subtitle is locked at y ≈ 68%, which keeps the bottom 25% clear for the IG UI. Hero objects are centred (trophy, globe, ball). Flat-lay sets keep the props at the corners (trophy cup, Champions League ball, stopwatch, camera, controller, Game Boy).
- **Assets:** football boots, balls (classic, Adidas match balls), cut-out players (Ronaldo bicycle kick, Neymar, Portugal/Argentina players), plane cut-outs (side and top-down with drop shadows), a vintage Uruguay map, flag stickers (rounded squares with white borders), B/W cut-out hands holding rubber stamps, the World Cup trophy (photo and a 3D-looking render), a globe with landmarks (Liberty, Pisa, Arc de Triomphe, Taj Mahal), a CRT TV in a cluttered room, a stadium LED board with "EARNEDITS" (brand placement inside the scene), and archival broadcast clips.
- **Finishing:** halftone, grain, vignette, soft drop shadows on all cut-outs, **bloom/glow** on the CTA words, an **RGB-split glitch** on the "26" logo build, and handheld jitter on the archival clips.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** 0.73 detected cuts/s. The structure accelerates: 1.0–2.2 s collage shots (0–15 s) → a 1.6 s void "breath" → **an archival montage of 1.33, 0.45, 0.55, 0.33, 0.33, 0.17, 0.11 s** (each clip shorter than the last, into the kick) → 1.4–3 s shots for the scale-today section → long, slow title drop (3.1 s, with VO pauses "This… is… the") → 3.8 s CTA.
- **Easing signatures (18 fps data):**
  - *Settles* are critically damped exponential decays with no overshoot. Zoom pops decay ~0.72 per frame (card pops, 8 frames to <10%). Scene settles ~0.81–0.86 per frame: the 8.44 drop goes 9.4→1.5 in 9 frames; the 3.17 pull-out goes −5.1→−1.0 in 11 frames.
  - *Exits* are ease-in ramps of 6–10 frames that peak on the last frame (slides 8.39, 13.72, 26.83; zooms 3.11, 21.11).
  - *Big in-shot pushes* are symmetric ease-in-out over ~14 frames (25.0–25.7).
  - Peak real speeds: flow ×4. Slides ~28–38 px/frame; the archival whip ~36 px/frame.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:** slot-reel year counter; perspective pitch type writing on letter by letter; giant numeral pop behind a passing object; word-tile stacking; paper-strip + flag-sticker pops; stamp hands sliding in and stamping; yellow-card pop with word-fill; letter type-on split around a hero object; landmark pop-ups on the globe; RGB-glitch logo assembly; bloom-flash word reveal. Exits are slides, cloud wipes or hard cuts. Nothing fades out.
- **Camera:** a virtual camera that is never still in the collage shots: an entrance settle (always) → an optional mid-shot push on the key reveal → an accelerating exit. Net per-shot zoom ranges from −44 to +30. Rotation is used as an accent (−3.6° kick at 3.22; a slowly rotating sky in shot 4; the rotating trophy).
- **Secondary motion:** planes gliding, a bird flock, rolling balls, a rotating sky, landmarks growing, grade flicker, handheld jitter on archival clips, the preview video playing inside the framed phone.
- **Sync:**
  - **Cuts land on clause onsets, +0.0 to +0.17 s after the first word:** 2.17 vs "experiment" 2.14; 3.17 vs "few" 3.00; 4.89 vs "Just" 4.80; 6.67 vs "Uruguay" 6.66; 8.44 vs "first" 8.28; 13.78 vs "Nearly" 13.74; 15.39 vs "the (tournament)" 15.36; 18.67 vs "Nations" 18.58; 21.17 vs "Billions" 21.18; 24.06 vs "No" 24.04.
  - The title-drop cut (26.89) lands inside the narrator's pause between "This" (26.30) and "is" (27.32).
  - Hero words land between 0.4 s early and 0.4 s late. Headline type leads on the void card (CENTURY −0.41, LATER −0.50) and on THE LONG JOURNEY card (−0.3). It trails on typed pieces (DECLINED stamps +0.5 after "declined"; MORE WIEGHT +0.25–0.4).
  - The "26" logo lands exactly on "FIFA" (28.22).
  - Subtitle chunks switch on chunk onsets (±1 frame).
  - Beats (92 BPM) are secondary. The 30.0 CTA cut sits 0.12 s before the beat at 30.12, and onsets cluster on the stamps (11.31), the whip to black (13.65), the montage start (15.42) and the finale build (26.3–35.4).

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Velocity-matched zoom cut** (3.17, 22.61, 24.06): the outgoing zoom ramps for 6–8 frames and the incoming shot continues the direction, decaying over 8–18 frames. At 3.17 the incoming shot also gets a −3.6° rotation kick.
2. **Velocity-matched vertical slide** (8.44): the outgoing set accelerates downward (ease-in, 8 frames), the incoming stadium enters already moving downward from the top (the cut sheet shows "FIRST" cropped at the top edge and a black gap at the bottom on frame 1), then decelerates over 14 frames.
3. **Whip into a hard cut to black** (13.72→13.78, 26.83→26.89): the same accelerating vertical slide, but the destination is a static void. The motion stops dead, which works as a dramatic "full stop" before a time-jump or title line.
4. **Cloud wipe** (4.6–4.89, 6.67–7.11): cloud/smoke PNG layers scale and drift up to cover the frame (luma 67→204 in 5 frames); the scene switches under the white; the clouds part over ~8 frames on the new scene.
5. **Frame-in-frame zoom-out** (21.17): the composite shrinks with an accelerating zoom-out, then a hard cut to a CRT TV whose screen shows that same composite, with the zoom-out decaying. It connects "years of qualifying" to "billions watch".
6. **Accelerating archival montage** (15.39–18.67): seven hard cuts with shot lengths shrinking from 1.33 s to 0.11 s, each clip with handheld jitter, which builds energy into the modern composite.
7. **Grade flicker** (23.39, 23.89): a 1-frame switch between two exposures of the same composite, used as a visual beat.
8. **RGB-split logo build** (28.22–28.67) and **bloom-flash words** (33.78, 34.67).

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Every collage shot has a three-part camera life:** an entrance settle (exponential, ~10–15 frames), an optional mid-shot push on the key reveal, and an accelerating exit that hands its velocity to the next shot.
2. **Turn dates and numbers into objects:** a slot-reel year counter, a giant numeral with a plane flying in front of it, digit/word tiles.
3. **Put type in the world** (painted on the pitch in perspective, split around the trophy, inside a TV) instead of floating it on top.
4. **Two text layers:** a big art-directed keyword plus a constant small subtitle bar at y ≈ 68%. The viewer gets both the vibe and the full sentence.
5. **Switch medium for the emotional beat:** a black void card for the time jump, then real archival footage cut on a shrinking rhythm (1.3 s → 0.1 s) before returning to collage.
6. **Halftone + grain + soft shadows** unify stock cut-outs of wildly different sources (photos, renders, archival).
7. **Hard full stop before the title:** whip into a black void, slow VO, a rotating hero object, then a glitch logo build on the brand word.
8. **Spell-check.** The reference ships typos (NOTION, WIEGHT, VIST) that undercut the premium feel.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `fps={30}` (the reference delivers 18 fps; render 30 and keep the motion curves), duration = VO + 0.5 s.
- **Layer stack:**
  1. `<SetBackground />`
  2. `<CornerProps />`
  3. `<CutoutLayer shadow />` (players, trophy, planes)
  4. `<WorldType />` (perspective / occluded)
  5. `<Badge />` cards
  6. `<SubtitleBar y="68%" />`
  7. `<HalftoneOverlay opacity={0.2} />`
  8. `<Grain />`
  9. `<Vignette />`

  Wrap the whole stack in `<ShotCamera enter exit push />`.
- **Components & timings:**
  - `ShotCamera({enter:{zoom:1.08, rot:-2}, push:{at, amount:0.06, frames:14}, exit:{type:'zoomOut'|'slideUp'|'slideDown', frames:8}})`. Enter: `spring({damping: 28, stiffness: 160, mass: 1})` (critically damped, ~12 frames at 30 fps). Push: `Easing.inOut(Easing.cubic)`. Exit: `Easing.in(Easing.exp)` with the peak on the last frame; the next shot's enter starts at the same velocity sign.
  - `SlotYearCounter({from:1926, to:1930, rowHeight:0.1*H})`: translateY the column; the active row is opacity 1, the rest 0.3; ~16 frames ease-out.
  - `PerspectiveType`: CSS `transform: perspective(900px) rotateX(55deg) rotateZ(-12deg)`, letter type-on at 1 char per 1–2 frames, white at 90% with `mix-blend-mode: screen`.
  - `GiantNumeral` behind an `<ObjectFlyBy>` (plane `translateX(-120%→120%)` over ~40 frames, z-index above the numeral).
  - `WordTiles` (stagger 2–3 frames, scale 0.7→1 with `spring({damping:18, stiffness:220})`).
  - `PaperStrip` + `FlagSticker` (zoom pop 1.25→1 in 8 frames, no overshoot).
  - `StampHand` (B/W PNG slides in 10 frames, presses at 1.0→0.95 scale for 2 frames, leaves the `DeclinedStamp` behind with 10° rotation).
  - `YellowCard` (pop 1.12→1 over 8 frames; words fill on VO timestamps −0.3 s).
  - `CloudWipe` (3–4 cloud PNGs, scale 1→2.5 and translateY 30%→−10% over 6 frames; cut under; reverse on the new scene over 8 frames).
  - `ScreenInsertZoomOut` (render the previous composition into a `<div>` mapped onto the CRT screen quad; animate the parent scale 1/screenScale→1).
  - `ArchivalMontage({durations:[40,14,17,10,10,5,3]})` with `<HandheldJitter amp={6px} rot={1deg} every={2} />`.
  - `GradeFlicker` (filter swap for 1 frame).
  - `RGBGlitchLogo` (three tinted copies offset ±12 px → 0 over 8 frames with `mix-blend-mode: screen`).
  - `BloomWord` (`filter: brightness(3) blur(8px)` → `brightness(1) blur(0)` over 3 frames, plus `text-shadow` glow).
  - `SubtitleBar` (Inter SemiBold 40 px at 1080 width, `background: rgba(0,0,0,.5)`, padding 4 px 10 px, chunk switch at word start).
- **Tricky bits:**
  - The rotating sky behind a fixed plane: put the background on its own layer with `rotate(${frame*1.2}deg) scale(1.6)` so the corners never show.
  - Splitting text around the trophy: render the trophy PNG above the text layer with a soft contact shadow.
