**One-line essence:** a 10-second preset ad in which the same "FFX" file icon is dragged by a hand cursor onto four very different collage scenes (flat-lay product, sky + plane, football years, walking businessman). Every drop instantly "applies" a finished animation, so the product's promise ("one click") is shown four times in one bar each before the CTA.

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** template/preset-pack advertisement (a showreel of before→after with a CTA). No narration. The copy is a single end card: "ONE CLICK. THAT'S ALL IT TAKES / COMMENT "PRESETS" to get it on your dms".
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–2.0 **Demo 1 (hook):** a flat-lay of Listerine + Marvis toothpaste on white tile. The bottle cycles through 5 colourways (blue → pink → orange → purple → white) every half-beat. The FFX icon is dragged onto the bottle and the drop lands the final white "Sensitive" bottle with a regrade.
  - 2.0–3.73 **Demo 2:** a whip into the sky. An airliner bursts through the clouds, a giant red "13" sits behind it and a bird flock crosses; the FFX drop adds "TEAMS".
  - 3.73–5.8 **Demo 3:** a top-down football pitch. A column of years (1925–1930) as ghosted type; the ball rolls up the touchline and the highlighted year ticks 1927→1930. The FFX drop locks "1930" bold white, then a player's legs stride in and kick the ball.
  - 5.8–8.0 **Demo 4:** a cut-out businessman doing a stiff walk cycle on a street; the background scrolls. The FFX drop adds foreground lamp-post parallax and a darker grade.
  - 8.0–10.07 **CTA:** a dark grain card. A 3D "EARN EDITS PRESET" SD card flips upright; headline words pop in; a final FFX drop "applies" the end card; hold 1 s.
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):**
  - The frame opens mid-push-in (zoom +7 on frame 1, decaying).
  - Props pop into place in the first 4 frames.
  - The hero product swaps colour every 4 frames (0.27 s), so there is visible change 4× per second before anything is explained.
  - The hand-cursor + FFX icon appears at 0.53 s. It is the thread the viewer follows through the rest of the video.
- **Script → visual rule:** with no VO, the **FFX drop** is the narrative unit. Every scene = establish (≈0.7 s) → drag-in (≈0.5 s) → drop = visible upgrade (new element or regrade) → exit on the next bar.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** a cut-out collage / "realistic sticker" world. Each scene is a different stock-photo set built from cut-outs with soft contact shadows: a top-down product flat-lay, a sky plate with a cut-out plane, a top-down grass pitch with cut-out boots, a ball and a halftone player's legs, and a full-body cut-out man over a building plate. The UI layer (hand cursor + FFX file badge) is flat white vector on top of everything, and it is the only consistent element.
- **Backgrounds & textures:**
  - White/grey square bathroom-tile grid (`#e2e0e0` with `#c5c5c5` grout) carrying a soft paper/concrete texture.
  - Saturated cyan sky `#1e9dfc` with real cloud photos and light grain.
  - Mown-stripe grass `#46742d`/`#6b9253` with a white touchline.
  - A brick façade plate (`#564c4c`) with a road strip.
  - The CTA is charcoal `#171717` with heavy film grain and dust specks.
  - Each scene has a different hue family; nothing repeats.
- **Colour:** one dominant field per scene + one saturated accent object: tile grey + the colour-cycling bottle; sky blue + **signal red** `#e72b2c` "13"; grass green + **cobalt** boots and white type; brown brick + black suit; charcoal + **orange** `#e8762e` ("PRESETS", the SD-card contacts `#f08a24`). The grade shifts on each FFX drop: drop 1 darkens the tile by ~10% (luma 203→185); drop 2 deepens the sky (152→134); drop 3 brightens the pitch (118→134); drop 4 desaturates the street (sat 51→40). The drop is visible even without a new layer.
- **Typography:**
  - **"13"**: ultra-heavy grotesk numerals (Druk Heavy / Anton-wide-like), signal red with grain and a soft white outer glow. ~55% of frame width, placed behind the plane (depth sandwich: number behind the plane, clouds in front).
  - **"TEAMS"**: heavy grotesk caps with a white→ice-blue vertical gradient and a subtle bevel/inner shadow (`3d-extrude-type` lite), ~45% W. It types on letter by letter (T, E, A… visible at 2.93 s).
  - **Years**: extra-bold grotesk (Montserrat Black / Gotham Black-like). The active year is solid white at ~58% W; the others are a stack of the same size in white at ~20% opacity on a 125 px row pitch (at 720 w), cropped by the frame edges.
  - **"Mouth Freshner"**: hand-lettered marker script (Caveat Brush / Permanent Marker-like), black on a torn yellow tape label `#f5d330` with a drop shadow, ~38% W.
  - **CTA**: heavy rounded display sans (Lilita One / Gilroy Black-like), ALL CAPS. "ONE CLICK. / THAT'S ALL IT TAKES" in white is ~76% W on the long line, with a 2–3 px soft grey drop shadow. "COMMENT" is white; **"“PRESETS”"** is orange `#e8762e` at the same size, with curly quotes as part of the design. "to get it on your dms" is lowercase, ~35% of the headline size, and types word by word.
- **Layout & composition:** the hero is always centred on the vertical axis at y ≈ 45–55% and fills 40–60% of the frame width. Secondary props sit diagonally around it (toothbrush top-left, petri dish top-right, tubes bottom-left/right), like a classic flat-lay "X". Type sits on the centre line. The CTA stacks headline / product / COMMENT block in thirds (y 28%, 50%, 68%).
- **Assets:** product pack shots (Listerine ×5 colourways, Marvis tubes), a petri dish with water droplets, an A380 cut-out with an orange exhaust glow, a bird-flock PNG sequence, Nike boots, a white football, halftone footballer legs, a cut-out businessman (puppet-rigged limbs), street lamp-post cut-outs, a 3D SD-card render branded EARN EDITS, and a **white "fx / FFX" file-badge icon** with a white glove "grab" cursor.
- **Finishing / FX:** soft contact shadows under every cut-out (offset ~8 px down-right, 40% black, 12 px blur); film grain everywhere (strongest on the CTA); motion blur on whips; halftone dot texture on the player's legs; a soft white glow around the "13".

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** 4 cuts in 10 s (0.4/s), but each scene contains 3–6 discrete events (pops, swaps, drag, drop, regrade), so the visual change rate is ~3 events/s. Scenes last 1.73–2.2 s, which is **≈ one bar each** at 112 BPM (one bar = 2.14 s).
- **Easing signatures (per_frame.csv):**
  - **Entrance push-ins decay exponentially with no ease-in.** Shot 1: zoom +7.07, +3.52, +2.89, +1.96, +1.94, +0.98 … +0.15 (13 frames ≈ 0.87 s). Shot 3: +3.42, +1.76, +1.24, +1.00 (settles in 4–5 frames). In Remotion that is `Easing.out(Easing.exp)` or a spring with damping ≈ 26, stiffness 120.
  - **Exit whip = pure ease-in.** Flow 0.45 → 0.84 → 1.39 → 2.32 → 3.44 → 5.44 → 8.58 → 11.3 over 7 frames (roughly ×1.5 per frame). The cut lands on the fastest frame. Use `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)` over 7–8 frames at 15 fps (≈14–16 frames at 30 fps).
  - **Drop impacts:** a 1-frame scale kick (z +2.7 at 1.00; −1.4 at 8.67–8.73) with a 2–3 frame settle. Sign flips at 8.67 → 8.73 (rot −0.72 → +1.23) give a small wobble/overshoot on the end card.
  - **Constant-velocity layers:** the walk background (pan_x steady at −1.6 to −1.9 every frame from 5.87 to 7.93) moves at linear speed while the puppet's limbs carry the "life".
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:**
  - **Colourway swap**: an instant substitution every 4 frames, stop-motion style, no tween.
  - **Prop pop-in**: 1–2 frames.
  - **Number behind object**: a fade+scale of 2 frames.
  - **Letter-by-letter type-on** (TEAMS ≈1 letter/frame).
  - **Object-driven counter** (the year ticks as the ball passes each row).
  - **Cut-out stride-in** (the legs enter from frame-left over ~5 frames).
  - **3D card flip-up** (3 frames, slight overshoot).
  - **Word pop stagger** (≈1 word every 2 frames = 0.13 s).
  - **Tagline typewriter** by word.
  - **FFX badge drag**: it enters from frame-left (always the left edge, x ≈ 0–10%), travels ~250–300 px to the hero in 5–6 frames with a slight rotation (−10°→0°) and is released over the hero. On release it vanishes in 1 frame and the effect lands the same frame.
- **Camera:** small digital moves only. Push-ins at shot heads (+3…+7 zoom/frame decaying), one big diagonal whip (shot 1 → 2), a push-through-clouds (2.0–2.33), a pull-back for the kick (5.13–5.47), and a constant lateral scroll for the walk. No 3D camera.
- **Secondary motion:** bird-flock flapping, drifting clouds, the plane's exhaust flicker, the puppet's walk bob, and the cursor's glove "grab" pose (closed fingers while dragging).
- **Sync (music only, beats every 0.534 s):**
  - **Colour swaps sit on the eighth-note grid** (0.00/0.27/0.53/0.80/1.07 vs. beats 0.07/0.60/1.16 + half-beats).
  - **Cuts land on strong onsets:** 3.73 vs onset 3.62 (+0.11); 5.80 vs onset 5.805 (0.00); 8.00 vs onset 7.988 (+0.01). The 2.00 whip arrives 0.25 s before the 2.25 onset, so the sky reveal (2.0–2.33) completes on the hit.
  - **FFX drops sit on or near beats:** 1.00 (beat 1.16, −0.16), 2.93 (beat 2.79, +0.15), 4.80 (beat 4.99, −0.19), 6.73 (beat 6.62, +0.11), 8.73 (beat 8.80, −0.07). Rule: **cuts on downbeat onsets (≤0.1 s), drops within ±0.2 s of a beat in the middle of the bar.**

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Ease-in diagonal whip** (1.47–2.00): the outgoing flat-lay accelerates up-left over 7 frames (≈×1.5 velocity per frame) with growing motion blur and a +2.5° roll on the last frame. The cut lands at peak speed onto a white cloud plate that is itself mid-motion. Rebuild: `translate` with `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)` over 14 frames at 30 fps (to −60% X, −60% Y), directional blur proportional to velocity, `rotate 0→3°`; the next scene starts with 2–3 frames of residual blur.
2. **Push-through clouds** (2.00–2.33): the incoming scene starts as near-white cloud (luma 236) and the camera scales through the cloud layer to reveal saturated sky in 5 frames. Rebuild: a foreground cloud PNG scaled 1→2.5 with opacity 1→0 over 10 frames (30 fps) over the sky plate.
3. **Zoom-snap cut** (3.73): the outgoing scene is zooming out (z −3.8) and the incoming one is zooming in (+3.4), so the cut feels like a punch. Rebuild: last 3 frames scale 1→0.94, first 6 frames of the new scene scale 1.12→1 with `Easing.out(Easing.exp)`.
4. **Hard cut on onset** (5.80, 8.00): a straight cut, with the energy carried by the action inside the new shot (the walk starts immediately; the SD card is already mid-flip).
5. **"FFX drop" in-scene transformation** (1.00, 2.93, 4.80, 6.73, 8.73): the signature device. The badge is released, disappears in 1 frame, and the same frame introduces a new element or regrade plus a 1-frame scale kick (±2–3%). Rebuild: `<FfxDrop at={f}>` triggers `{ swapLayer, gradeTo, kick: [1.025, 1] over 3 frames }`.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **One recurring UI actor sells the product.** The same cursor + file badge "applies" a result in every scene, so the viewer learns the product's promise by repetition without any VO.
2. **One scene = one bar.** Lock scene length to the musical bar (~2.1 s here) and put the "drop" mid-bar, so every scene has a setup and a payoff.
3. **Make the drop visible twice:** add a new element and a regrade (±10–15% luma or saturation) on the same frame, plus a 1-frame scale kick.
4. **Radical variety across scenes** (bathroom flat-lay → sky → pitch → street → black card) shows range, which is the selling point of a preset pack.
5. **Depth sandwiches with cut-outs:** big type behind the hero object, with clouds or props in front. This costs almost nothing and reads as 3D.
6. **Hook with stop-motion substitution:** swapping the hero's colourway on eighth notes creates motion with zero animation.
7. **Whip exits are ease-in; entrances are ease-out.** The cut always lands at peak velocity.
8. **Grainy charcoal CTA** with a rounded heavy font, one orange keyword in quotes, and a held final second.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, 300 frames (10 s). Scene length = `Math.round(60/BPM*4*fps)` = 64 frames at 112 BPM.
- **Layer stack per scene (bottom → top):**
  1. `<Plate src texture>`
  2. `<Cutout src shadow={{x:8,y:10,blur:12,opacity:.4}}>` props
  3. `<HeroType>` (behind or in front of the hero via z-order)
  4. `<Hero>`
  5. foreground `<Cutout>` (clouds / lamp-post)
  6. `<FfxCursor>`
  7. `<GradeLayer>` (CSS `filter: brightness() saturate()`)
  8. `<GrainOverlay opacity={0.06–0.15}>`
- **Components:**
  - `FfxCursor`: props `{from:[-0.05,0.55], to:[0.5,0.5], dragStart, dropAt}`; travel 10–12 frames with `Easing.inOut(Easing.cubic)`; rotation −10°→0°; glove sprite switches open→grab at `dragStart`; unmount at `dropAt`.
  - `FfxDrop`: at `dropAt`, swap or add a layer and animate `scale: interpolate(f-dropAt, [0,1,4], [1,1.03,1])`; tween the grade over 2 frames.
  - `SwapCycle`: `srcs[]`, `everyFrames={8}` (eighth notes at 30 fps/112 BPM), hard substitution.
  - `PushInEntrance`: `scale: 1 + 0.12*Math.exp(-0.25*f)`.
  - `WhipExit`: last 14 frames, `translate = Easing.in(Easing.cubic)` to −60%/−60%, with blur `stdDeviation = 0.8*velocity`.
  - `CloudPushThrough`: a foreground cloud scaled 1→2.5, fading out over 10 frames.
  - `ObjectDrivenCounter`: rows of years at fixed y; the active row = the row the ball's y has passed; the active row switches from 20% white to 100% white in 1 frame; the ball's y animates linearly.
  - `PuppetWalk`: a cut-out with separate limbs; rotate thighs/shins/arms with `Math.sin(f*2π/16)` at ±22°; the background translates linearly at −14 px/frame; the foreground is 2× faster.
  - `TypeOnLetters` (1 letter / 2 frames).
  - `WordPop`: scale 0.8→1 + opacity over 4 frames, 4-frame stagger, `spring({damping: 14, stiffness: 200})`.
  - `Card3DFlip`: `rotateY 25°→0`, `rotateZ −8°→0`, `spring({damping: 10, stiffness: 180, mass: 0.6})`.
- **Tricky bits:** the "13" glow is `text-shadow: 0 0 24px #ffffff80` plus a grain mask (`mix-blend-mode: multiply` noise PNG clipped to the text). TEAMS uses `background: linear-gradient(#fff, #bfe3ff); -webkit-background-clip: text` with a 2 px darker `text-shadow` below for the bevel. Halftone legs use an SVG `feTurbulence`-free halftone: a radial-dot pattern used as a mask over a high-contrast cut-out.
