## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** quote-driven business-lesson Reel ("famous person said → list of things you *don't* need → the one thing you do need → 3-step method → reframe → CTA").
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–1.7 **Hook (authority):** "Alex Hormozi once said". A real-looking X post with his avatar, a blue check and 120M views; a cursor types the attribution.
  - 2.0–12.2 **List of negations (8 items):** a book a week, making my bed, journaling, a 2-hour morning routine, alcohol, Netflix, marriage, leverage. Each "without" is a new container, about 1.3 s each.
  - 12.5–18.8 **The one rule:** "find shit people want, sell it for more than it costs you, many times." This runs Spotify lyrics → IG caption → falling-bitcoin kinetic type.
  - 19.3–20.0 **Turn:** "That's the lesson." A match lights in the dark behind giant green type.
  - 20.5–30.5 **Method (3 steps):** find a PAIN → AGITATE it, make it IMPOSSIBLE to IGNORE → make your solution the natural next step by claiming a unique POSITION in the market.
  - 31.0–33.8 **Reframe triad:** "That's not motivation. That's positioning. That's helping."
  - 34.5–39.2 **CTA:** "Editors, comment 'project'… access to the AEP file". This is the identical end-card template used in v76 (palette hex values match exactly).
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** frame 1 already shows a tweet card, and the camera is pushing in (zoom +3.5 analysis units on frame 1, decaying over ~20 frames). By 0.27 s a cursor is typing **"Alex Hormozi" once said :** into the tweet body with a caret, and a play-button video thumbnail of Hormozi sits below. A famous face, a famous name and an active cursor are all on screen before 0.5 s, and by 1.6 s the hand cursor clicks play.
- **Script → visual rule:** the *repeated word* ("without") is the anchor and the *noun* chooses the container. Journaling → a Notion journal, Netflix → the Netflix app icon, marry → a wedding video in an X compose box, alcohol → a wine-bottle sticker. In the method section each key noun becomes a giant single word (PAIN, IMPOSSIBLE, Ignore, POSITION).

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** ~0.49 cuts/s; shots run 0.93–4.6 s. The *list* section (3–12 s) is metronomic at ~1.3 s per "without" item, and each item lands within 0.05 s of the spoken "without" (7.87/7.88, 9.17/9.22, 5.53/5.58). The method section uses **in-shot hard word swaps** (shot 11: 6 swaps in 2.6 s) instead of cuts, so the perceived cut rate stays high (~1 change per 0.45 s) while the template stays fixed.
- **Cut ↔ VO sync:** only 8/21 cuts are within ±0.1 s of a beat (chance level at 117.5 BPM), so the edit follows the VO. Median cut-to-word offset is ~0.05 s early. Examples: Editors 34.47/34.46 (0.0), make 23.97/23.94 (+0.03), journaling 5.53/5.58 (−0.05), Find 20.40/20.46 (−0.06), ignore 25.03/25.12 (−0.09), Agitate 23.03/23.16 (−0.13), sell 16.43/16.54 (−0.11), "That's the lesson" 19.10/19.34 (−0.24, a fade-up from black that needs lead time). **Word-swap timing inside templates is +0.0 to +0.17 s late** (PAIN +0.10, PEOPLE +0.17, POSITIONING +0.13). It is a deliberate "word lands, then the type confirms it" feel, versus the cuts, which lead.
- **Easing signatures (from per_frame.csv, 30 fps):**
  - **Opening push-in:** a long exponential ease-out (z 3.5 → 2.1 → 1.8 → 1.6 → 1.4 → 1.2 → 1.1 → 0.94 → 0.83 → 0.76 → 0.66 → 0.59…, decay ≈ 0.9/frame, ~20 frames). This is a softer, more "documentary" settle than the scene cuts.
  - **Scene-cut settle:** a faster ease-out (bottle drop pan_y 8.4 → 6.0 → 4.4 → 3.2 → 2.5, decay ≈ 0.73/frame, ~8 frames). No overshoot (no sign flips) anywhere, which corresponds to `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)` or `exp`.
  - **Nested zoom-out:** extremely front-loaded (z −13 → −5 → −2.4 → −0.8 → −0.4: ~60% of the move in frame 1, done in 5 frames). It reads as a "snap back" rather than a dolly. Use `Easing.out(Easing.expo)` over 6 frames.
  - **Fly-through type:** an accelerating zoom (1.9 → 3.5 → … → 5.2 per frame) that ends in a whip (−5.7), i.e. ease-in to a cut.
  - **Rotate-whip re-frame** (15.2 s): rotation −5° → −11° over 2 frames, then settled. A quick handheld "camera grab".
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:**
  - cursor typing with caret, 12–18 chars/s
  - karaoke colour fill per word (green or white)
  - burned per-word pop captions (Hormozi style), 1 frame
  - per-word stamp with a slight scale-down (110% → 100%, 3 frames)
  - letter-by-letter type-on on footage (~2 frames per letter)
  - outline → fill reveal (IMPOSSIBLE, ~8 frames)
  - opacity fade-in with overlay blend (Ignore, ~6 frames)
  - hard word swap (template), with a full-frame colour inversion for the hero word
  - deblur + grey → black darken (film template, ~4 frames)
  - out-of-order letter pop (EDITORS)
  - handwriting write-on plus a hand-drawn ellipse or underline (~8–12 frames)
  - paper slide-in with rotation (CLAIMING note, ~6 frames)
  - sticker drop-in with rotation (bottle)
  - letterbox open (band → full frame, ~10 frames)
- **Camera:** a constant virtual camera over UIs: push-ins (tweet, compose, lyrics), 3D-perspective tilts (Notion, Spotify, kinetic type), trucks through an icon field, and a stepped tilt-up over the desk. Footage shots have no added camera move; they are kept static so the type composited into depth stays readable.
- **Frame-rate as texture:** the first half (UI / list) runs at a smooth 30 fps; from the lesson onwards everything is on 2s (45–75% duplicate frames). This gives the method section a tactile, hand-made feel and visually separates "the list" from "the lesson".
- **Secondary motion:** falling and spinning bitcoins at 3 depth layers, the flickering match flame, ghost words scrolling behind the film template, floating icons with independent drift, the Ae icon wobble, and a cursor hovering and clicking.

Rebuild: a radial-gradient overlay `screen` blend, opacity 0 → 1 over 3 frames, a white solid for 3 frames, then the next scene with brightness 1.4 → 1 over 5 frames.
6. **Defocus + vignette out** at 19.0 s: 2 frames of blur with a grey radial vignette closing in, then a cut to near-black and a fade-up (6 frames).
7. **Hard word-swap on a fixed template** at 20.4–22.5 s, plus a **frame inversion** for the hero word (PAIN). Rebuild: the template is one component; the centre word comes from the VO-word array; `invert` is a prop for one word.

## 6. Sound design
- A quiet ~117.5 BPM bed (rms 0.13). The Spotify card shows a Beatles track title, but the bed is not clearly identifiable, so treat the card text as set dressing. The VO is continuous with gaps ≤ 0.6 s.
- Strong onsets cluster in the list section (1.97, 2.23, 3.71, 4.20, 4.43, 4.99, 5.16, 5.74, 9.29, 9.80, 11.61, 11.82, 13.63, 15.14, 17.07–17.18 s), which suggests clicks, pops and whooshes on each new "without" item and the kinetic-type hits. They then go sparse in the lesson (only 20.50, 23.15 and 26.26 s), so the method section plays almost dry under the voice. They return as a dense CTA cluster (34.64–37.15 s: pops for EDITORS letters, folder, icon, whoosh).
- Implied UI SFX: keyboard clicks under the cursor typing, a mouse click on the play button, a match-strike scrape at 19.3 s, and a whoosh on each light burn.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **For a list, keep the anchor word and change the container every item.** The repeated "without" gives rhythm, and the eight different native UIs give novelty. Each container is chosen by the item's noun.
2. **Make captions part of the world:** app-icon labels, lyric lines, a tweet body, a compose box, an IG caption. Words feel discovered, not subtitled.
3. **Use a fixed branded template for the core lesson** (poster with logo, barcode and tagline) and only swap the centre word on the beat of the voice. It is cheap to produce, instantly on-brand, and reads as "important".
4. **Composite type into the footage's depth.** Put the hero word *behind* the actor (roto), blend a word into skin, mask footage into a porthole. It turns stock or movie clips into designed frames.
5. **Separate the sections with texture changes:** dark-mode UI at 30 fps for the setup, then the lesson on 2s with grain, fisheye and spotlight. The viewer feels the gear change without a title card.
6. **Cuts lead the voice by ~0.05 s; template word-swaps trail it by ~0.1 s.** Picture changes anticipate, and keyword confirmations echo.
7. **Light burns as chapter breaks:** two orange-to-white burns frame the method section.
8. **Reuse one CTA end card across the series** (identical to v76). It builds recognition and saves production time.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, ~1180 frames for 39.2 s. To get the "on 2s" look in later sections, wrap them in a `<StepTime fps={15}>` that floors `useCurrentFrame()` to even frames (`const f = Math.floor(frame/2)*2`).
- **Timing source:** whisper word timestamps. Cuts happen at `wordStart − 2 frames`; template swaps happen at `wordStart + 3 frames`; karaoke highlights at `wordStart`.
- **Layer stack (UI scene):** `<Backdrop color|wallpaper>` → `<Camera3D perspective={1400} rotateX rotateZ scale>` → `<UIMock type="tweet|notion|spotify|igPost|xCompose|homeScreen">` → `<Cursor path clickAt>` → `<DepthBlur planes>` → `<Grain opacity={0.06}>`.
- **Components:**
  - `TweetCard({avatar, name, handle, verified, body, typeFrom, media, stats})` with a `Typewriter` body (`cps: 12`, caret blink every 16 frames) and an embedded `<OffthreadVideo>` that carries `BurnedCaption` (yellow `#f2c12e`, Anton, `WebkitTextStroke: '2px #1a1a1a'`, one word per VO word).
  - `KaraokeQuote({words, active: '#2fd06a', idle: '#9aa5a8'})` with lines that auto-push upward with `spring({damping: 20, stiffness: 120})`.
  - `NotionPage({title, rows})` inside a `LetterboxBand({aspect: 16/9})`.
  - `StickerPoster({bg: 'bliss', halftone: true, cutout, lines: ['WITHOUT','GIVING UP','ALCOHOL']})`. The extrude is 8 stacked `text-shadow` layers 1 px apart in `#4a3a10`. The cutout drops in with `interpolate(f,[0,8],[-300,0],{easing: Easing.out(Easing.cubic)})` and rotates −12° → −6°.
  - `HomeScreenIcons({icons: [{src, label, depth}]})`: labels bound to VO words; depth → blur `[0, 6, 14]px` and parallax factor `[1, 0.7, 0.4]`.
  - `LyricsView({lines, activeIndex})`: `rotateX(18deg) rotateZ(-6deg)`, inactive lines at 40% opacity with blur proportional to distance from the active line.
  - `NestedZoomOut({inner, targetRect, frames: 6})`.
  - `KineticStack3D({words})`: each word a plane in a `<ThreeCanvas>` or CSS 3D with `translateZ` spacing of 400 px, and a camera z-path with `Easing.in(Easing.quad)` ending in a whip. Falling coins are instanced sprites with random spin.
  - `PosterTemplate({brand, cornerLabel: 'PAIN IS GAIN', barcode, tagline, word, invert, fisheye})`. Fisheye: an SVG `feDisplacementMap` with a radial map, or a pre-rendered bulged grid PNG plus a `radial-gradient` vignette.
  - `FilmSheetTemplate({line, ghost})` with ghost text `translateX` 0 → −6% over the hold.
  - `RotoTextBehind({footage, matte, text})`: a three-layer sandwich (footage → text → footage masked by the alpha matte from a roto pass, e.g. a pre-rendered matte video via `<OffthreadVideo>` with `mask-image`).
  - `OverlayWord({blend: 'overlay', opacity: 0.45})`.
  - `PortholeMask({ring: 6})` with `clipPath: circle(42%)` plus a white ring div.
  - `SpotlightDesk` (a radial-gradient cone mask over a paper texture) with `HandwriteOn` (SVG path dash), `EllipseDraw` (red stroke, 10 frames) and `TornNoteSlideIn`.
  - `LightBurn({from: 'right'|'top', frames: 3, hold: 3})`.
  - `CTAEndCard` (shared with v76).
- **Tricky bits:** keep the UI mocks pixel-faithful (real spacing, icons, verified badge) because that fidelity is what sells the "native container" effect. Rotoscoped text needs a matte, so supply user footage with a matte from an external segmentation step, or fall back to placing the word behind a soft luma key of the subject.
