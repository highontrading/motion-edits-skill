## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** educational "design principle" explainer (teach → demonstrate → reframe the common mistake → moral) that funnels into a comment-keyword CTA for a tutorial library and project files.
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–5.4 **Hook / reframe:** "Your animations might not be boring because they lack effects. They might be boring because they lack contrast." A false belief, then the real cause.
  - 5.9–8.7 **Definition:** "Contrast is simply the difference between two things." Three pairs of photo cards (small/BIG, slow/FAST, muted/BRIGHT).
  - 9.1–17.1 **Demo 1 (size, brightness):** everything the same size, so cartoon eyes look everywhere. Then a dark field of muted eyes, where one eyeball "suddenly becomes bright".
  - 17.3–25.0 **Escalation (motion-specific contrast):** speed (space shuttles + "SPEED" callout), shape (irregular hexagon → circle among rectangles), density ("one of my favourites": a crowded globe still life).
  - 25.2–34.5 **The mistake:** "editors think more interesting = adding more". Shown inside an editing-app frame with a timeline, then a collage that keeps piling up, then "don't immediately add another effect".
  - 34.8–39.1 **Moral:** "Good motion design doesn't make everything interesting. It makes the right thing interesting." One hero object (headphones) with converging arrows.
  - 39.5–49.2 **CTA:** a pull back into After Effects ("Want to see how these animations are actually made?"), then "Comment EDIT", the full tutorial library, the project files, "to study and recreate it yourself".
- **Hook mechanics:** frame 0 is a dark grunge texture with a torn-newspaper hole holding a crumpled paper ball. Within 0.27 s the hole tears wider (3 unique frames) to reveal grid paper and a blue-framed card. The crumpled ball "unfolds" into an astronaut collage (0.5–1.1 s) while "your ANIMATION" drops in letter by letter. Paper tearing, unfolding and type all move inside the first second.
- **Script → visual rule:** every VO clause gets a small lowercase/sentence-case lead word plus a giant uppercase keyword ("your / ANIMATION", "might not be / BORING", "They / LACK / EFFECTS", "is simply / THE DIFFERENT"). Each abstract principle is acted out literally on screen at the moment it is named (the eyeball brightens on "bright", the shuttle streaks on "speed", the hexagon morphs to a circle on "shapes", the canvas fills with stuff on "adding more").

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** only ~0.35 hard cuts/s, but the camera or a transition moves every 1.5–3 s, so the felt pace is ~0.6 scene changes/s. Shots run 1–6.6 s. The middle "demo" section (14–25 s) changes worlds fastest (~1.6–2.3 s each); the CTA is the longest hold (6.6 s), kept alive by stacking elements.
- **Frame rate:** the whole piece is animated on 2s (15 unique fps in a 30 fps file). Every move reads slightly "stepped", which matches the paper-collage look. To reproduce it, render at 30 fps and hold every other frame (`Math.floor(frame/2)*2`) on collage layers.
- **Easing signatures:**
  - *Zoom-through* (3.9–4.47): **ease-in** over 8 unique frames (zoom rate ×10 from start to end), then a hard settle on the new framing. Bezier `(0.55, 0, 1, 0.45)`.
  - *Camera tilt* (45.5–46.3): **ease-in-out**, with peak velocity mid-move (~56 px/frame real at 720 w), ~24 frames total. Bezier `(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)`.
  - *Pull-backs* to UI (25.6–26.3, 39.7–40.5): fast start, then a long ease-out settle (zoom/frame −3.2 → −2.9 → −2.8 sustained, then decaying over ~1.5 s). `spring({damping: 26, stiffness: 120})` on scale.
  - *Slow pushes:* nearly every scene carries a linear-ish push of +10…+43 net zoom over 2–4 s (≈ 3–8% scale growth per scene).
  - *Pops* (cards, eyes, bricks, letters): ~3–4 unique frames (≈ 0.2–0.27 s) with a visible overshoot (letters land below/above baseline, then settle). `spring({damping: 10, stiffness: 200, mass: 0.6})`.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:** per-letter drop with baseline jitter (stagger ~1 unique frame = 2 frames per letter); white label wipe L→R; type-on per letter; card pop with overshoot (pair-staggered ~4 unique frames); sticker scale-up; brick pop stagger; eye-sticker pop + pupil dart; shape morph (hexagon → circle, ~5 unique frames); flame sprite loop; whip-off with motion blur (shuttle, car); paper-strip slide; scribble circle write-on (~6 unique frames); desaturate → colour "bright" switch (2–3 unique frames); exits are mostly **fades of ~8 frames** or the scene being carried away by the transition.
- **Camera:** a continuous virtual camera with push-ins on almost every scene, two big pull-backs to reveal "the screen it lives on" (NLE timeline at 25.7, AE UI at 39.7), one zoom-through into a frame (3.9) and one vertical tilt in the CTA. No shake.
- **Secondary motion:** the playhead scrubbing the timeline, flame loops on the shuttles, eye pupils darting, sparkle twinkles, arrows pulsing inward, and the AE viewer cycling through other projects.
- **Sync:**
  - **Cuts ↔ beats (99 BPM, 0.6 s grid):** the hard cuts sit almost exactly on beats: 21.57 (beat 21.525, +0.04), 31.13 (31.068, +0.06), 39.70 (39.683, +0.02), 42.60 (42.585, +0.015), 0.20 (0.093, +0.1). The 35.07 cut is 0.18 s before beat 35.25. The CONTRAST shape wipe starts on onset 6.04, the newsprint tear on onset 23.41 and THE FULL TUTORIAL on onset 43.65.
  - **Text ↔ VO:** keywords appear **0.15–0.5 s after** the spoken word (median ≈ +0.3 s): EFFECTS +0.1, CONTRAST +0.17, SAME SIZE +0.25, IS MUTED +0.25, BRIGHT +0.25, DENSITY +0.47, COMMENT EDIT +0.35, PROJECT FILE +0.5. The text *confirms* the word just heard rather than anticipating it. The one tight hit is the **eyeball brightening exactly on "bright" (16.80 vs 16.82)**: the demo event is frame-synced and the caption follows.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Paper-hole tear-open** (0.0–0.53): a torn-edge hole in a dark texture scales from ~40% to >150% of the frame in ~8 unique frames, revealing the scene underneath. Rebuild: an SVG mask with a jagged torn path, scale `spring({damping: 18, stiffness: 120})`, with a newspaper-texture ring around the edge.
2. **Zoom-through into a framed card** (3.9–4.47): ease-in scale ~1 → 1.9 centred on the card, until the card's white interior fills the frame, then new elements populate it. 16 frames.
3. **Badge-expand shape wipe** (6.47–7.0): the blue CONTRAST badge band grows vertically from ~10% to 100% of the frame height (16 frames, ease-in-out) and becomes the next background.
4. **Rising crumple/newsprint underlay** (9.6–10.1): a crumpled-paper photo slides up from the bottom *behind* the foreground cards, replacing the background while the foreground stays.
5. **Hand-pull wipe** (13.3–14.0): a cut-out hand (with a white scribble outline) grabs the top edge of the scene and drags it down out of frame, exposing the next scene above. ~20 frames, ease-in. Rebuild: translateY of the outgoing scene with a hand PNG pinned to its top edge.
6. **Vertical push** (17.9–18.5): the incoming scene slides down from the top while the outgoing one is pushed down. ~18 frames, with a slight zoom pulse.
7. **Torn-paper drop** (23.2–23.5): a dark newsprint sheet with a torn bottom edge drops from the top and becomes the next card. ~8 frames.
8. **Pull-back to the UI** (25.6–26.3 NLE, 39.7–40.5 AE): the current full-frame canvas scales to ~55–60% and is revealed as the preview inside an editor (timeline/UI fades up around it). It is a meta "it's just a timeline" move.
9. **Blur-out cut** (30.9–31.13): a Gaussian blur ramps 0 → ~15 px over 4 unique frames, then a hard cut to the next scene, which starts blurred and racks to sharp over ~6 frames.
10. **Cursor-dragged page wipe + 1 black frame** (42.43–42.63): a grid-paper sheet is dragged in diagonally by a cursor, then a single black frame acts as a "blink" before the CTA world.
11. **Slide-over black card** (47.6–48.0): a black rectangle slides in from the left over the stack and carries the last line.
- Avoid: the **1-frame stray flash of the CTA scene at 32.87** looks like an export/edit error rather than a technique.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Demonstrate the principle as you name it:** the eyeball brightens on "bright", the shuttle streaks on "speed", the shape morphs on "shapes" and the canvas piles up on "adding more". For abstract topics, show each noun as a literal event.
2. **A new physical surface per idea** (grid paper → blue felt → newsprint → grunge → cutting mat → green felt → NLE UI) with a single saturated field colour, and the hero as the only contrasting element.
3. **Small lead word + giant keyword** stacked on every line; two keyword widths (extended for nouns, condensed for verbs and adjectives) keep the rhythm without changing family style.
4. **Join scenes with camera and paper, not cuts:** zoom-through, hand-pull, tear, push and pull-back. Save hard cuts for beats (all within ±0.06 s).
5. **Meta pull-backs** (into a timeline, then into AE) prove craft and set up the "learn how" CTA naturally.
6. **Animate on 2s** for collage material, since a stepped cadence feels handmade and premium; keep pops at 3–4 unique frames with overshoot.
7. **Let the caption trail the VO by ~0.3 s** in a teaching voice: the ear gets the word first and the eye confirms it. The demo event itself, however, should land on the word.
8. **The CTA as a growing stack:** each VO noun (tutorial, library, project file) adds one card while the camera tilts up, so the offer visibly accumulates.
9. **Proofread keyword cards.** Giant type magnifies typos.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, with collage layers stepped on 2s (`const f2 = Math.floor(frame/2)*2`); duration = VO + ~0.8 s.
- **Scene stack (bottom → top):** `<SurfaceBg texture="grid|felt|newsprint|grunge|cuttingMat|nleUI" tint>` → `<Vignette>` → `<CutoutSticker src border={6} shadow>` props → `<Doodle type="scribbleCircle|arrow|dashedArc">` → `<LeadKeywordStack lead="your" keyword="ANIMATION" width="extended|condensed" treatment="plain|badge|tape|outline|extrude">` → `<GrainOverlay opacity={0.06}>`, all wrapped in a `<VirtualCamera>` with `scale`/`translate` keyframes.
- **Components:**
  - `LetterDrop({text, staggerFrames: 2, from: {y: -40, scale: 1.3}, spring: {damping: 10, stiffness: 200, mass: 0.6}, baselineJitter: 8})`.
  - `LabelWipe` (clip-path inset from right 100% → 0 over 8 frames, `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`).
  - `CardPopGrid({pairs, staggerFrames: 8})` with scale 0 → 1 springs.
  - `EyeSticker({lookTargets})` (pupil translate keyed every 6–10 frames).
  - `DesatToBright({at})`: `filter: grayscale(1) → grayscale(0) saturate(1.3) brightness(1.15)` over 4 frames, plus a `ScribbleCircle` (SVG path `strokeDashoffset` over 12 frames).
  - `ShapeMorph` (flubber-interpolated SVG path, hexagon → circle, 10 frames).
  - `ZoomThrough({target, frames: 16, easing: Easing.bezier(0.55, 0, 1, 0.45)})`.
  - `BadgeExpandWipe` (scaleY of a rect from 0.1 → 1 over 16 frames).
  - `HandPullWipe({handSrc, frames: 20})`.
  - `TornPaperDrop` (mask with jagged-edge SVG).
  - `PullBackToUI({ui: 'nle'|'ae', targetScale: 0.58, spring: {damping: 26, stiffness: 120}})`, where the UI chrome fades in at 0 → 1 over 10 frames.
  - `BlurCut` (blur 0 → 15 px over 8 frames out, 15 → 0 over 12 frames in).
  - `CursorDragWipe` + `BlackBlink(1 frame)`.
