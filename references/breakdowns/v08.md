**Source creator handle:** earn.edits · **Format:** 720×1280, 9:16, 16 fps (delivered; the motion has a slightly stepped, near stop-motion cadence), 47.1 s · **Shots:** ~22 scenes (24 detected. **Style tags:** collage-explainer, cutout-photo, paper-texture, grid-paper, grain, halftone, vignette, kinetic-type, stacked-type, giant-keyword, label-badge, handwritten-accent, serif-editorial, counter-ticker, character-face-stickers, retro-computer-mockup, device-mockup, masking-reveal, spring-overshoot, slow-push, documentary-vo, cta-endcard, subtitle-pill, lens-iris-wipe, pixel-type

**One-line essence:** a news-finance story told as ~22 distinct tabletop "sets" (grid paper, Greek flag, felt stage, chalkboard, wood desk, sky). Each one is built around a single fact, with a different display typeface per scene and the VO sentence always running underneath as a neutral subtitle pill. Variety of type and set is what keeps a 47 s numbers story watchable.

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** finance/news micro-explainer ("big number → comparison → twist → history → current data → open question → CTA").
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–4.4 **Hook (big number):** "Warren Buffett has $381 billion in cash." Caricature, then a hypnotic ring-burst, then the 300 → 381 sticker correction.
  - 4.4–8.6 **Scale comparison:** the cash is more than Greece's whole economy (Greek-flag tablecloth, map sheet slides in).
  - 8.6–17.6 **Twist + evidence:** he isn't spending it; he has sold for ~3 years (countdown cards) while the S&P 500 rose 80% (chalkboard).
  - 17.6–22.6 **Contrast + Why:** everyone else is buying (hands grabbing bills), he's waiting (hand on bills); "Why?" (magnifier on a palm with a WHY badge); "he's done this before".
  - 22.6–29.5 **History:** 1999 and 2007 (serif + pink years), "he waited" (gold portrait), "then bought big" (money sunburst), "when prices crashed" (pixel type on black).
  - 29.5–36.3 **Now:** market cap at 230% of the US economy (pixel digits flip 200 → 230, US stamp + Trump cut-out), "That's very high" (red/yellow card), "sitting on cash" (head-lid gag).
  - 36.3–41.6 **Open question:** "waiting", then "do prices fall first or does he start buying?" (clipboard handwriting, cupped hands with coins, yellow card slam).
  - 41.6–47.1 **CTA:** editors get the project file on earnedits.com: sky collage, phone with the site, green URL, "VISIT NOW & DOWNLOAD IT".
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** frame 0 is already a rich top-down desk (grid paper, an old map, a red book, $ bills) with a dark tablet showing a **big-head caricature of Buffett** on a sky/grass background. The camera **crash-pushes in from frame 1** (zoom 6.25 → 0.84 over 9 fr, expo decay ×0.8/frame). A speech bubble "I'm Warren Buffett" pops in at 0.25 s. At 1.125 s there is a hard cut to white, where the cut-out Buffett with a red sticker outline sits above a "300" paper strip. At 1.25 s **concentric red wavy rings burst outward** from his silhouette (5 fr, with overshoot). So there are three distinct visual events before the number is even spoken.
- **Script → visual rule:** **one sentence = one set** (background + props + typeface). Inside a set, the key phrase is rebuilt as large kinetic type word by word, while the *full* VO runs as a phrase-chunked subtitle pill at y ≈ 76%. The kinetic type is allowed to paraphrase (e.g. "BUT HE IS NOT SPENDING IT" for "But he's not spending it"; "THEN THE WHOLE COUNTRY OF GREECE" for "than the whole country…").

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** tabletop/flat-lay **photo collage**: real photographic props shot top-down (bills, coins, pens, alarm clock, clipboard, headphones, olives) + cut-out people (Buffett photos with white/red sticker borders, cut-out hands, a Trump head) + graphic type cards. Between the collage sets there are pure **typographic cards** (countdown, red "THAT'S VERY HIGH", black "WAITING"), which act as palate cleansers.
- **Backgrounds & textures (one per scene, never repeated twice in a row):** grid paper with an old map (hook) → white with red op-art rings → greyscale fanned $100 bills → Greek flag tablecloth → dark bank-vault wall (warm brown) → countdown cards (red / charcoal / black / white-vignette) → blue felt stage with potted plants and floating coins → chalkboard on a nail → green cutting-mat grid → blue speckled paper → red damask wallpaper + gold frame → radial $100 sunburst → charcoal grain → green grid paper → saturated red card → royal-blue portrait backdrop → charcoal → walnut desk → sky with clouds. Almost every surface carries **paper grain/speckle**, and the white and red cards carry a strong **vignette** (corners −30–40% luma).
- **Colour:** every scene uses one dominant hue + neutral + an accent: red `#b32e14`/`#d23416` + black; royal blue `#2543a7`/`#2856d0` + white + pink `#f0a0d8`; green grid `#3e6e3e`/`#5b7546` + red/white tiles; saturated red `#d71718` + mustard `#e8b820`; walnut `#462719`/`#6b432f` + yellow `#f0b818`; sky `#6daad7`/`#9bb1c2` + red `#e83030` + green `#2ec440`. The palette swings hard between adjacent scenes (luma 20 ↔ 180 within 1 s at 10.75–11.0) to make each cut feel like a page turn.
- **Typography:** many display faces, one per scene, all ALL CAPS except the serif and handwriting:
  - **Subtitle pill (constant):** Helvetica Neue / Arial Bold, white, sentence case, ~4% frame height, centred at y ≈ 76%, on a rounded rectangle (radius ~6 px) of **semi-transparent black (~55% alpha)**, so it picks up the scene colour (blue pill on blue, maroon on red). It changes per 2–5-word chunk exactly on the word start.
  - Numbers: **Helvetica/Arial Black** "300" on a white paper strip; the "81" correction is a **red sticker with a white numeral, tilted ~8°**, slapped over the zeros.
  - Yellow **brush handwriting** ("billion dollars"; Caveat Brush / Mansalva-like, `#e0c020`), written on letter by letter.
  - **Distressed heavy grotesk** (white, grunge texture knocked out), stacked 5 lines and centred, for "THEN THE WHOLE COUNTRY OF GREECE".
  - **Mustard ultra-bold extended sans** (`#d4a017`; Impact/Anton Wide/"Bebas Kai"-like), 2 lines, ~90% frame width, with a dark soft shadow and motion-blurred entry: "BUT HE IS NOT SPENDING IT".
  - **Countdown leader:** lowercase Helvetica Bold "one/two/three" between red rules + a giant Helvetica Bold numeral (~35% width) + a tally column + a growing square-grid icon.
  - **Overlapping numerals** "1 3 2" (black / red `#e34124` / light grey) above "THREE YEARS" in black condensed heavy.
  - **Rounded heavy sans with drop shadow** (Montserrat Black / Gotham Black-like, white, offset grey shadow): "HE'S BEEN SELLING", "S&P 500", "WENT UP"; "80%" in red with a white inner stroke and a dark outline.
  - **Label badge:** "WHY" in orange-yellow on a red rectangle with a 3 px black border (sticker look).
  - **Old-style serif** (Times / Garamond-like, light, white): "In … And …", with the years in **pink set inside dark-green boxes** (`#1a4a1a` fill, 2 px black stroke), a retro-broadcast look.
  - **Blocky pixel/square font** (Silkscreen / "Press Start"-like but heavier, white with a black 1-px outline): "HE WAITED", "THEN BOUGHT BIG" (on black label rectangles), "WHEN PRICES CRASHED" (CRASHED in red), "& NOW THE MARKET" (with a red underline); **pixel digits** in red tiles with white fill for "200 → 230".
  - **Yellow `#e8b820` heavy soft-serif/grotesk** (Cooper Black-ish weight) stacked, "THAT'S VERY HIGH", ~85% width; orange `#f0a020` heavy sans "BUT HE'S SITTING ON CASH" on a photo.
  - **Red heavy italic-free sans** "WAITING" with per-letter drop.
  - **Handwriting** (Caveat / Gochi Hand, black) on the clipboard: "The real question is".
  - **White heavy with drop shadow** on a yellow card: "OR DOES HE START BUYING"; CTA "EDITORS" in red heavy with white offset, "PROJECT FILE" white, **"EARNEDITS.COM" in green `#2ec440` with a dark-green outline**, "VISIT NOW & DOWNLOAD IT" white.
  - Size rule: hero type 70–95% of frame width, stacked 2–5 lines, centred at y ≈ 40–55% (above the subtitle pill).
- **Layout & composition:** centred symmetry in every scene. The hero subject (Buffett, the chalkboard, the clipboard, the phone) sits on the vertical axis in the upper 60%, the kinetic type sits over or just below the subject, and the subtitle pill is locked at 76%. Props frame the edges in flat-lays (pens as a border around the Greek type, coins in the corners).
- **Assets:** cut-out press photos of Buffett (several), a big-head caricature, cut-out hands (grabbing, flat, cupped), a Trump head cut-out, real $100 bills, coins, a Greece relief map, Greek stamps, a chalkboard, an alarm clock, a keyboard, a manila folder, a clipboard, headphones, a Polaroid camera and a beige retro Mac (both **halftone-dotted**), an iPhone mockup with the earnedits.com site, cloud PNGs.
- **Finishing / FX:** paper grain on all flat colours, heavy vignettes on cards, soft drop shadows under all cut-outs (~8–12 px blur, 40% black), halftone dots on the retro objects, motion blur on fast entrances, and a dark **circular lens/porthole mask** for zooms.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** ~0.47 scene changes/s; scenes run 0.8–4.5 s, median ~1.7 s. There are two bursts: the **countdown (4 cards in 1.0 s)** at 10.25–11.0, and 33–37 s (three 0.8–1.75 s cards). The longest holds go to the dense data scenes (chalkboard 4.5 s, 230% 3.7 s, Greece 3.4 s).
- **Easing signatures (measured at 16 fps):**
  - **Expo-out settles everywhere, ratio ~0.7–0.8 per frame:** opening push-in (×0.8, 9 fr), US stamp fly-in (×0.8, 10 fr), card slam (×0.7, 7 fr), phone rise (×0.7, 7 fr), money sunburst zoom-out (×0.75, 9 fr).
  - **Overshoot on pops:** the ring burst (one sign flip after 5 fr), the chalkboard pendulum (one swing past zero, 8 fr), the map sheet rotation and the WAITING letters show a small bounce. These correspond to springs with damping ≈ 10–14.
  - **Stepped/held animation:** hands, coins and some type hold 2 frames per pose at 16 fps (≈ 8 poses/s), which gives a paper-cut stop-motion feel.
  - **Camera trucks are ease-in-out** (the final pan is a symmetric bell over 12 fr).
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:** crash push-in (9 fr); offset-ring burst (6 fr); sticker slam (2+3 fr); handwriting write-on (~1 fr/letter); word-by-word pop (2 fr); word slide-in with motion blur (2–3 fr); stack-push (new line pushes the block up, 3 fr); per-letter drop with rotation (1-fr stagger); digit tile add + flip-hop; sheet slide-in with rotation (5 fr); pendulum swing-in (8 fr); fly-in from the bottom with rotation (10 fr); head-lid lift (4 fr); grey → white word fill (3 fr).
- **Camera:** the default is a slow push (zoom +0.3 to +2.5/fr analysis), with occasional pull-backs at scene ends (Greece −7.5; 1 3 2 card −3.7). Big camera moves are reserved for reveals: the opening crash-in, the sunburst zoom-out, the lens iris and the final CTA truck. Net zoom per detected shot: +29.6 (hook), +33.7 (hands), −27.7 (sunburst), −10.3 (clipboard → card).
- **Secondary motion:** floating coins on the blue stage, bobbing Polaroid/Mac/mouse in the sky, the phone screen scrolling, the clouds drifting across the phone.
- **Sync:**
  - **Cuts land on the first word of the new phrase:** 17 of 25 scene changes/re-frames are within ±0.12 s of a word onset (e.g. "In" −0.02, "That's" +0.01, "So" −0.04, "The" −0.02, "Or" +0.04, "Editors" −0.10, "then" 0.00, "Why" +0.04). This is the main sync rule.
  - **Kinetic type trails the VO by ~0.0–0.4 s** (median ≈ +0.3 s: "HE'S" +0.34, "SELLING" +0.36, "WENT" +0.5, "500" 0.0). The subtitle pill changes exactly on the word start, and the hero type lands just after it, so the eye reads the pill first and then gets the slam.
  - **Music:** at 143.6 BPM (0.418 s period) the cut-to-beat offsets are spread evenly (±0.2 s), so the cuts are **not beat-locked**; 7 cuts coincide with strong onsets within 0.06 s (15.31, 22.62, 33.19, 34.94, 37.13, 40.0, 41.63), which suggests SFX hits are placed on the cuts rather than the cuts on the music.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Hard cut on phrase onset** (most cuts): the cleanest and most common. A full set change on the first syllable of the sentence.
2. **Offset-ring burst** (1.19–1.56 s): 5–6 concentric wavy offset outlines of the cut-out silhouette scale out from 0.6 → 1.0 with a stagger of 1 frame each and slight overshoot. Rebuild: an SVG path of the silhouette, `feMorphology dilate` or pre-made offset paths at radii 20/45/75/110/150 px, alternating red `#a81516`/white, each with `spring({damping: 12})` delayed 1 frame.
3. **Push-down scene replace** (5.0–5.2 s): the new flat-lay enters from the top while the old one exits at the bottom, ~4 fr, with motion blur.
4. **Countdown card flicker** (10.25–11.0 s): four 4-frame full-colour cards (red → charcoal → black → white), each a hard cut, functioning as a "film leader" interstitial for the duration "three years".
5. **Pendulum swing-in** (13.06 s): the outgoing subject drops down while the new object swings in on a pivot (rotate from ~35° to 0° with a damped spring, `transform-origin` at the nail).
6. **Lens/iris re-frame** (15.31 and 20.50 s): a thick black ring (porthole or magnifying glass) sweeps in from one side and the area inside is shown scaled ×1.6–2. At 21.19 the lens slides again to re-frame. Rebuild: a mask `radial-gradient(circle at x y, transparent R, black R+40px)` + the inner scene scaled, with ring position animated over 4 fr.
7. **Slide-left exit** (17.56 s): the board leaves to the left over ~3 fr as the next set cuts in.
8. **Zoom-out reveal** (26.0 s): a fast pull-back (9 fr, expo) where the new environment (the money sunburst) appears around the held subject, so the background is replaced while the subject stays.
9. **Card slam over the scene** (40.0 s): a solid-colour card scales from ~1.3 to 1.0 (7 fr expo) over the live set and becomes the type plate.
10. **Object fly-in + truck** (43.19 / 44.75 s): the phone rises with rotation and clouds composited in front, then the camera trucks right.

## 6. Sound design
A driving electronic/hip-hop bed at ~144 BPM under fast VO (rms 0.126). The strong onsets coincide with visual hits rather than with the grid: 1.99 (81 slap), 3.46 (handwriting), 12.28/14.51/15.37 (type pops, lens), 18.92/19.99 (hands), 22.1–24.4 (year pops), 33.18/33.79/34.39/34.88 (THAT'S / VERY / HIGH / cut), 37.15, 39.99, 41.63 (CTA cut). This implies per-pop SFX (whooshes, paper slaps, stamp thuds, ticks) laid on each kinetic hit. The VO barely pauses (max gap ~0.5 s at 7.68–8.22 and 9.78–10.24), and both gaps are filled by a visual reveal (map sheet, countdown).

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **A new set per sentence.** 22 backgrounds in 47 s, each a different material (paper, felt, chalkboard, wood, sky, damask), so the eye never settles.
2. **Two text layers with separate jobs:** a neutral, always-on subtitle pill for comprehension and a stylised, paraphrased hero phrase for emphasis. The pill switches on the word; the hero lands ~0.3 s later.
3. **Typeface as a scene property.** Change the display font with the set (serif for history, pixel for the market, handwriting for the question), keeping it ALL CAPS, stacked and 70–95% of frame width.
4. **Numbers get gags.** Correct 300 to 381 with a slapped sticker, flip a pixel digit (200 → 230), count one-two-three on film-leader cards. A statistic becomes an event.
5. **Literal visual metaphors:** a head opening to show cash, hands grabbing versus a hand resting, a magnifier for "why", a WHY badge on a palm.
6. **Cut on the first word of the sentence** (±0.1 s), not on the beat. Put SFX on the cuts and pops so the music bed can run freely.
7. **Expo-out with a ~0.75 per-frame decay** for nearly every entrance, plus a little overshoot on physical objects (pendulum, ring burst, letters).
8. **Grain, vignette and drop shadow on everything**, with halftone dots on retro objects, so the collage reads as printed matter.
9. **CTA in the same collage language** (floating retro objects, a phone sandwiched between cloud layers, a green URL) rather than a flat end card.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps` (multiply the 16-fps frame counts ×1.875; keep "on 2s" stepped animation for hands/coins by quantising frame → `Math.floor(f/4)*4` at 30 fps ≈ 7.5 poses/s). Duration = VO + 0.3 s (≈ 1420 frames for 47 s).
- **Layer stack per scene (bottom → top):** `<SetBackground src texture grain={0.08} vignette={0.35}>` → `<Prop>` cut-outs with `<DropShadow blur={10} opacity={0.4}>` → `<Subject>` (cut-out person with a sticker outline) → `<HeroType font words>` (centre, y 40–55%) → `<SubtitlePill chunks>` (y 76%) → `<CameraRig>` wrapping everything (push/pull/truck).
- **Components & timings (30 fps):**
  - `SubtitlePill({chunks: [{text, start, end}]})`: Inter/Helvetica Bold 40 px, white, padding 8×16, `background: rgba(0,0,0,0.55)`, radius 8; hard switch on chunk start, no animation. Chunk the whisper words into ≤5-word groups split at punctuation.
  - `HeroWordStack({words: [{w, start}], lag: 9, font, color, lines})`: each word appears at `start + 9` fr (~0.3 s) with `scale: spring({damping: 14, stiffness: 220, mass: 0.6})` from 1.25 → 1 and opacity 0 → 1 over 3 fr. When a new line is added, the whole stack `translateY` springs up to stay centred (`StackPush`).
  - `CrashPushIn({from: 1.0, to: 1.18, frames: 17})`: `interpolate` with `Easing.out(Easing.exp)`.
  - `RingBurst({paths, colors: ['#a81516','#f2ede6'], stagger: 2, spring: {damping: 11, stiffness: 160}})` for the hypnotic offset rings; add a slow 0.5% scale breathing afterwards.
  - `StickerSlam({text: '81', rotate: -8, from: 1.4, frames: 9})`: scale 1.4 → 1 with `spring({damping: 12, stiffness: 300})`, plus a 2-frame white flash on the strip underneath; SFX "slap".
  - `HandwritingWriteOn({text, font: 'Caveat Brush', framesPerChar: 2})`: an SVG stroke-dashoffset if you have vector strokes; otherwise a per-character clip reveal.
  - `CountdownLeader({steps: ['one','two','three'], cardFrames: 8, bgs: ['#d23416','#2d2c2d','#0a090a'], numColors: ['#111','#e8e8e8','#e34124']})`, ending on `OverlapNumerals('1','3','2')` on a white vignette card.
  - `PendulumSwingIn({pivot: [0.5, 0.05], fromDeg: 35})`: `rotate = 35 * spring({damping: 8, stiffness: 120})` inverted (`35 * (1 - s)`), so it swings past 0 once.
  - `LensReframe({ringWidth: 60, radius: '46%', scale: 1.8, from: 'right', frames: 8})`: an outer black mask + scaled inner scene.
  - `DigitTiles({sequence: ['2','20','200','230'], flipIndex: 1})`: tiles in a pixel font (`Silkscreen` or `Press Start 2P`) in white on red `#c8202a` tiles with a 3 px dark stroke; the flip is `translateY -60% → 0` with a char swap at the apex (6 fr).
  - `LetterDrop({text: 'WAITING', stagger: 2, fromY: -80, rot: ±12, spring: {damping: 10, stiffness: 200}})`.
  - `HeadLid({topMaskPath, liftPx: 120, rotate: -6, frames: 8})`: split the cut-out into two images (top of head and body) and lift the top with a spring; reveal a prop between them.
  - `CardSlam({color: '#f0b818', from: 1.3, frames: 13, easing: Easing.out(Easing.exp)})`.
  - `PhoneFlyIn({from: {y: '60%', rot: 18}, to: {y: 0, rot: 8}, frames: 13})` with `<Cloud>` layers at z above and below the phone; then `CameraTruck({dx: -18%, frames: 22, easing: Easing.inOut(Easing.cubic)})`.
- **Fonts via `@remotion/google-fonts`:** Inter (pill), Anton / Archivo Black (mustard heavy), Montserrat Black (rounded heavy), Silkscreen (pixel), Cormorant / EB Garamond (serif), Caveat Brush (yellow script), Caveat (clipboard), Bowlby One / Rubik Mono One (THAT'S VERY HIGH).
- **Tricky bits:** the halftone on retro objects is an SVG `feTurbulence`-free pattern (`<pattern>` of dots multiplied over the image via `mix-blend-mode: multiply`). The grain is a looping 4-frame noise PNG at 6–8% opacity with `mix-blend-mode: overlay`. Distressed white type uses `mask-image` with a grunge texture.
