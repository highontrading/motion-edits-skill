**One-line essence:** a 20-second "secret resource" promo that never shows a flat screenshot. The website is always a physical object in a lit space: an iMac in a sunlit room, a phone on a wooden desk with blind shadows, a curved 3D wall of project cards, printouts scattered on a green desk. The camera keeps moving through these spaces, and the VO's keywords float in the room as bold-sans + thin-handwritten word pairs.

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** creator-economy resource promo / soft ad with a comment-to-get-link CTA (sister account of v82's earnera/earnedits brand; same visual system).
- **Beats:**
  - 0.00–5.2 **Hook / social proof:** "I barely see editors talk about this site, but it helped me understand how better edits are really put together." Shown as a dim room → iMac showing the site → frosted terminal window typing the second half.
  - 5.4–10.9 **What it is:** "This site offers you fully open After Effects projects, all designed in styles that actually help you go viral." Phone on a desk → black void → curved carousel of project cards.
  - 11.1–14.5 **Bonus:** "What's even better? They upload new projects every single week." Zoom into one card with "what's even better?" → pull back to a 3-card row with green pagination dots 1-2-3-4.
  - 14.9–19.6 **CTA:** "So if you want access to this site, just comment 'link' and we'll send it to you." Green desk of scattered printouts and screenshots → IG comment sheet typing "Comment link and will send it to you" → 3 black frames.
- **Hook mechanics:** frame 0 is a nearly black wall corner with a warm light slit (luma 45). Within 4 frames a dark website screen slides in from the bottom-right corner and the camera pulls back and rotates (net_zoom −35.8, rot +20 across the shot). "i barely" blur-focuses in at f10–f15. Its blurred offset ghost copy sits below it, and the motion is stepped (18 fps). The first 0.5 s already shows a moving camera, real UI and the first words on screen.
- **Script → visual rule:** the product is literally on screen in every scene (iMac, terminal window, phone, carousel cards, printouts, comment UI). Each VO clause becomes a 2–3-word on-screen phrase, and its last noun is set in the handwritten face ("Editors", "Site", "After effects Projects", "go viral", "projects", "week", "Site").

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** 2.5D/3D product staging of UI. Website screens are mapped onto devices and cards inside lit 3D sets, with a moving virtual camera. Compared with v82 it relies far less on metaphor props and far more on product-in-space.
- **Backgrounds & textures:**
  - 0–3.8 s: beige plaster wall `#c8c3ab`/`#554e43` with a hard window-light patch and shadow, a framed "S-TIER CONTENT" logo poster, a green 3D sticker badge (clapper/play icon), a dark wooden desk, an iMac.
  - 3.8–5.4 s: dissolve to a **green-tinted, dark wall of pinned printouts** (earnera "e" logo poster, "Everyone" carousel post, "every Entrepreneur" sheet) with a warm lens-flare hotspot top-left.
  - 5.4–8.2 s: top-down **dark walnut desk with orange venetian-blind light stripes** (`#7a411e`/`#a35629` stripes on `#341e15`) and a phone, plus a rainbow lens-flare arc at the bottom.
  - 8.2–15.3 s: **black void** with sparse green four-point "twinkle" stars and a green top-glow (`#1b291d`), floor reflections under the cards.
  - 15.3–17.7 s: **flat-lay green desk** `#1e2b17` covered in screenshots, printouts and phone mockups, with soft shadows.
  - 17.7–19.55 s: white/grey IG comment sheet with a strong vignette.
- **Colour:** warm neutrals + black, with **brand green as the only saturated hue** (`#425b45`, `#465a3e`, UI buttons ≈ `#3cb54a`, pagination dots, twinkles). The orange blind-stripes scene is the one warm-saturated outlier and sits mid-video for contrast. No other accents.
- **Typography:**
  - **Bold sans** (Roboto Bold / Helvetica Neue Bold-like, tight tracking ≈ −3 %), all lowercase: "i barely see", "talk about this", "this site offers you", "fully opened", "if you want acces to this" (sic), "just comment". Size: 35–45 % of frame width per line (~260–320 px of 720). Black `#151515` on the light wall; light grey `#d0d0d0` / white on dark scenes. Each sans line has a **blurred offset ghost copy** directly below it (same text, ~35 % opacity, ~12 px blur, +0.6 em down), the same treatment as in v82.
  - **Handwritten accent:** the same thin, tall, condensed marker hand as v82 ("Shadows Into Light"/"Reenie Beanie" family), mixed case: "Editors", "Site", "After effects / Projects", "actually help you go viral", "upload new projects", "every single week". Size 30–60 % of frame width. White or near-black, sometimes with a soft grey drop/ghost.
  - **Mono terminal:** a frosted-glass macOS window (red/yellow/green traffic lights, 1 px white 30 % border, `backdrop-filter: blur`, rounded 16 px) with `>> ` prompt and a light monospace (SF Mono / JetBrains Mono Light-like), ~2.5 % of frame height, centred lines, a typewriter caret.
  - **Card headline:** "what's even / **better?**" in Helvetica/Roboto Bold black on an off-white card; the second line is one weight heavier and ~1.2× larger, indented right (stepped two-line layout).
  - Words not yet spoken can appear first as grey, blurred ghosts ("see" in grey before it lands; "help" pre-shown in grey at 10.0 s).
- **Layout:** the hero device or card is centred at y ≈ 40–60 %. Sans lines sit top-left of the hero (y ≈ 25–35 %); the handwritten word overlaps the hero's top-right edge or sits directly under it. On the carousel, each card gets **its own handwritten word floating above it** ("actually · help · you · go · viral", "upload · new · projects") and a second row below ("every · single · week"), so the words map onto objects spatially.
- **Assets:** earnedits / earnera website (hero "The #1 Marketplace for Viral After Effects Project Files", green buttons), iMac, iPhone mockup, AE project cards (Ae badge, thumbnail, "00:45" duration, title, green "Download This Viral Edit AE Project" button), framed logo poster, 3D green badge, printouts of carousel posts, IG comment sheet with the S-tier avatar.
- **Finishing / FX:** strong vignette (corners ~40 % darker), shallow depth of field (edges of every UI plane defocused), lens flares (warm hotspot, rainbow arc), floor reflections in the void, twinkle particles, motion blur on every camera move, **stepped 18 fps motion** in the two "room" sections against smooth 30 fps in the carousel.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** 7 scenes in 19.65 s (0.36 cuts/s). The long scenes (2.3–3.8 s) are kept alive by continuous camera moves. There are only 4 hard cuts (5.37, 8.24, 15.32, 19.55); everything else changes through dissolves, push-ins and pull-backs.
- **Easing signature:**
  - Camera moves are **long ease-in-out curves of 20–30 frames**. Example: the carousel truck, pan_x −1.1 → −4.7 → −8.8 → −4.7 → −2.0 → −0.3 over f304–f332, symmetric, no overshoot.
  - Pull-backs start fast and decay: −2.2/fr → −0.4 over ~25 fr (f258–f284); −3.75 → −0.9 over 10 fr at 15.32.
  - **Moves accelerate into hard cuts** (ease-in): the desk tilt ramps pan_y 3.0 → 5.7 over 7 fr before the cut to black, and the green-desk whip peaks at the dissolve.
  - No sign flips in pan/zoom during settles, so there is no overshoot anywhere.
- **Frame-rate texture:** the room/pinboard sections (0–2 s and 3.7–5.3 s) hold 2 of every 5 frames (≈ 18 fps effective, measured from near-zero frame differences on f8, 11, 13, 16, 18, 21…). The carousel and desk sections run at a smooth 30 fps. The stepped sections feel "filmed/handheld", while the product-carousel section feels slick.
- **Entrance / exit vocab:**
  - Blur-focus-in for sans lines and words (6–8 fr, blur ~16 px → 0, with the ghost copy fading in alongside).
  - Handwritten words blur/fade in (8–10 fr), sometimes pre-shown as a grey ghost before they are spoken.
  - Device slide-in from the frame edge with blur (9 fr, ease-out): screen at 0.13 s, phone at 5.37 s.
  - Frosted terminal window scale-up/fade (~8 fr) followed by typewriter text at ≈ 12–15 chars/s.
  - Printouts sliding in from the corners (8–10 fr).
  - Numbered pagination dots popping in at a 4-fr stagger.
  - Keyboard key pop-up typing.
  - 1-frame punch-in jolt (f200).
- **Camera:** a virtual camera in a 3D space throughout: pull-back + rotate (shot 1, net zoom −36, rot +20); top-down push (shot 3, +25.5 zoom, pan_y +56); orbit/truck along the curved carousel (shot 4, pan_x −100, rot +26); dive into a card and pull back out (shot 5, zoom −41 net); flat-lay pull-back (shot 6). Nothing is ever static for longer than ~0.4 s.
- **Secondary motion:** twinkle stars flicker in the void; the lens flare drifts; the terminal caret blinks; card thumbnails play their own animated content; floor reflections move with the cards.
- **Sync:**
  - **Hard cuts are beat-locked:** 8.24 vs beat 8.22 (+0.02); 15.32 vs beat 15.32 (0.00), both also strong onsets. 5.37 is −0.13 s before beat 5.50.
  - **On-screen words trail the VO:** a word starts revealing ~0.1–0.2 s after it is spoken and is fully legible ~0.3–0.45 s after (e.g. "barely" 0.20 → 0.50; "Editors" 0.76 → 1.17; "Projects" 7.64 → 7.94; "week" 14.20 → 14.48; "Site" 16.10 → 16.58). This suits a casual first-person voice: the type echoes the speaker rather than pre-empting them.
  - The terminal typewriter tracks the VO almost exactly ("helped" 2.58 → typing starts 2.6).
  - The only text that leads the VO is the grey "ghost" previews.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Set dissolve with a persistent foreground element** (3.7–4.0 s, ~10 fr): the whole room cross-dissolves into a new space while the frosted terminal window stays locked in the same screen position and keeps typing. Rebuild: render the terminal on a top layer outside both scenes; cross-fade the scenes underneath over 10 fr; slide the new set's printouts in from the corners during the fade.
2. **Hard cut onto a sliding device** (5.37 s): cut to a new set with the hero device already moving in from the edge (9 fr ease-out) and a lens flare, so the cut feels like a camera arrival.
3. **Accelerating tilt → black gap → fade-up** (8.0–8.44 s): the camera accelerates downward for 7 fr, hard cut to pure black on the beat, 6 frames of black, then the next scene fades up from black while pulling back. A punctuation pause between "what it is" and "the catalogue".
4. **Push-in into a card / pull-back out** (11.6–13.3 s): the camera dives toward one card until it fills ~85 % of the frame; while the card is full-frame, the background behind it swaps (void → wood → green glow); then the camera pulls back to reveal a new arrangement. This is a **zoom-through with a hidden background swap** that reads as one continuous shot.
5. **Beat-locked hard cut with pull-back** (15.32 s): the new scene starts 5 % zoomed in and pulls back −3.75/fr on the first frame, decaying over 10 fr.
6. **Whip + cross-dissolve** (17.52–17.92 s, ~12 fr): a lateral pan of the old scene peaks (−8.3/fr) exactly as the new UI fades in over it; the pan decelerates through the dissolve, which reads as the camera sliding onto the phone.
7. **Cut to black outro** (19.55 s, 3 fr).

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Never show a flat screenshot.** Put the product UI on a device or card in a lit 3D space (window light, blind shadows, a glowing void) and move a camera through it.
2. **One space per claim:** room (social proof) → desk (what it is) → void carousel (breadth of catalogue) → card dive (the bonus) → flat-lay (everything you get) → comment UI (action).
3. **Map words onto objects:** in the carousel, each handwritten word floats above its own card, so the sentence reads across the product.
4. **Persistent element across a set change** (the terminal window) makes a dissolve feel intentional and keeps the reading line continuous.
5. **Mixed frame rates:** stepped ~18 fps for the "real room" parts, smooth 30 fps for the product showcase. The texture shift signals a change of mode.
6. **Lock hard cuts to beats**, but let text trail the VO by ~0.3 s for a relaxed, conversational read.
7. **Zoom-through with a hidden background swap:** while a card fills the frame, change the world behind it, then pull back to reveal the new layout.
8. **A single brand green** across buttons, glows, dots, twinkles and badges, inside an otherwise neutral, cinematic grade with vignette and DOF.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `fps: 30`, duration ≈ VO length + 3 frames of black (≈ 590 frames for 19.65 s).
- **Layer stack per scene:** `<SetBackground variant="room|pinboard|deskStripes|void|greenDesk">` (an image plate or a three.js scene) → `<DeviceOrCard screen={url|video} transform3d>` → `<FloatingWord>` layers positioned in the same 3D space (CSS `transform-style: preserve-3d`) → `<Flare>` / `<Twinkles>` → `<DepthBlurEdges>` → `<Vignette strength={0.4}>` → `<StepTime fps={18}>` wrapper on selected sections.
- **Components & values:**
  - `VirtualCamera`: a wrapper with `perspective: 1400px` and an animated `rotateX/rotateY/translate3d/scale`. Pull-back: `scale 1.12 → 1.0` over 30 fr with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`. Carousel truck: `rotateY 0 → -18deg` over 28 fr with `Easing.bezier(0.45,0,0.55,1)`. Pre-cut ramp: `Easing.in(Easing.quad)` over 7 fr into the cut.
  - `StepTime`: `const f = Math.floor(frame / (30/18)) * (30/18)` (or hold 2 of every 5 frames) to emulate the 18 fps look on the room sections only.
  - `CylinderCarousel`: N cards (≈ 0.28 W wide, 9:16) placed on an arc, `rotateY(i*14deg) translateZ(-R)` with R ≈ 1.6 W; a floor reflection = a flipped copy at 25 % opacity with a `linear-gradient` mask; cards fade from black over 15 fr.
  - `BlurInWord` (sans): start at word `t + 3 fr`; blur 16 → 0 and opacity 0 → 1 over 7 fr, `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`; plus `GhostCopy` (same text, `translateY(0.6em)`, `blur(12px)`, opacity 0.35).
  - `HandWord` (script): start at word `t + 4–8 fr`; opacity + blur over 9 fr; optionally pre-render the next word at 30 % grey with 8 px blur, 6–10 fr before it is spoken.
  - `GlassTerminal`: `background: rgba(255,255,255,.08)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,.3)`, `border-radius: 16px`, traffic-light dots 10 px; text typed at the VO's word timings (characters revealed linearly between word starts), with a caret blink period of 16 fr.
  - `PaginationDots`: 4 circles of 28 px, the active one green `#5cbf4a`, the others white 70 %; each pops with `spring({damping: 14, stiffness: 220})` at a 4 fr stagger.
  - `PunchJolt`: a 1-frame `scale(1.06)` + 1-frame offset on an accented word.
  - `CardDiveSwap`: scale the target card until it covers ≥ 85 % of the frame (20 fr, ease-in-out), swap the `SetBackground` while covered, then reverse-scale into the new layout (20 fr ease-out).
  - `BlackGap`: 6 frames of pure black on a beat between sections; the next scene fades up over 6 fr.
  - `CommentCTA`: an IG comment-sheet mockup; reuse a `KeyboardTyper` with key pop-ups for the last word synced to VO.
- **Tricky effects:** a lens flare plus a rainbow arc (a screen-blended PNG drifting 0.5 px/fr); edge defocus (a duplicate blurred layer under a radial mask); the window-blind light pattern (a repeating-linear-gradient at 25° with `mix-blend-mode: overlay` and an orange tint `#ff8a3d` at 50 %).
