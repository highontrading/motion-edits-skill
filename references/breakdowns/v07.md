# v07 — "Stop watching After Effects tutorials": dark-mode typing promo for a project-file store

**Source creator handle:** earn.edits · **Format:** 1276×718 (≈16:9, 1.777), 24 fps, 19.7 s · **Shots:** 11 real scenes (8 detected; cuts at 0.83 and 4.54 are false: a feed fading in under the title, and a typing step. Detector missed 2.58, 4.08, 7.50, ~9.05, 17.67 and 19.08) · **Avg shot:** ~1.8 s · **Music BPM:** ~123 (minimal electronic bed) · **VO:** yes, English, calm, clipped "product-ad" delivery

**Style tags:** kinetic-type, typewriter, ui-mockup, cursor-ui, notification-ui, footage-led, glow, motion-blur, depth-of-field, vignette, zoom-through, slow-push, beat-synced-cuts, cta-endcard, minimal-swiss, leading-edge-colour-type, bracket-caption

**One-line essence:** an Apple-keynote-style product promo built almost entirely from small, perfectly kerned geometric-sans type typing on near-black. The type's colour fades from cyan to white as each letter lands, with real UI (YouTube feed, Gantt chart, web store, macOS + After Effects) breaking up the type every 2–3 s. It looks premium through restraint: one typeface, one accent hue, and lots of black.

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** direct-response SaaS/product ad (problem → agitation → brand → offer → proof-of-ease → CTA slogan), 16:9 for YouTube/X/LinkedIn.
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–2.6 **Hook / call-out:** "Stop watching After Effects tutorials." The viewer's own habit (a feed of tutorial thumbnails) is shown and then literally struck through.
  - 2.6–5.5 **Agitation:** "Deadline closing in, and you still haven't started." A Gantt "Workflow plan" with a deadline line, then a lonely typed sentence on black.
  - 5.5–7.5 **Brand reveal:** "Earned edits fixes that." Wordmark first, then the promise.
  - 7.5–12.2 **Offer:** "Ready-made project files for editors who actually have deadlines." Real storefront UI, then large type, then a bracketed caption with a "Downloaded …aep" toast.
  - 12.2–15.9 **Proof of ease:** "Download, open After Effects, tweak, export, done." Real macOS screen recording: click AE in the dock, the window opens with the very project that made this video, and "Done!!" pops.
  - 15.9–19.7 **Slogan + end card:** "Stop starting from scratch. Start creating like a pro." Two lines of white type on pure black, then the EARNEDITS wordmark.
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** 0.00–0.10 s is an almost black frame with a slow teal radial glow (luma 7 → 12). At 0.12 s "St" types in **cyan #28f8e0**, ~2 frames per character, while the camera slowly pulls back (zoom −0.2/frame). At 0.83 s a heavily blurred YouTube-style feed of AE-tutorial thumbnails (pink "Welcome to Ae", "VIRAL CINEMATIC ANIMATION") fades up beneath the text, tilts in 3D (rot +3°/frame peak at 1.29 s), and scrolls up fast (pan_y −5.6 → −1.0 px/frame over 13 frames). The direct imperative "Stop" is the first word, and both the text and the background are moving by 1.0 s.
- **Script → visual rule:** each VO sentence is either **typed on screen verbatim** (on black) or **shown as real UI** (feed, Gantt, store, desktop). The two modes alternate: type, UI, type, UI, type, UI, then type ×2. The type never paraphrases; it is the VO itself, lowercase sentence case.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** minimal dark-mode kinetic type + UI-mockup / screen-capture. No illustration, no photos except the thumbnails inside UI. The "tech launch" look of Linear, Vercel and Apple.
- **Backgrounds & textures:** near-black `#080808`–`#0b080c` throughout. The type scenes use a **soft off-centre radial glow**: dark teal `#061519`/`#0e191d` in the top-centre and right, with a heavy vignette. The final slogan scenes are **pure #000** (luma 0–1.5), a deliberate "lights-out" for the end. The single bright scene is the macOS Mojave dune wallpaper (`#353a4c`, `#576078`, luma ~60), which makes the screen-recording section feel like a different space. There is light compression noise and no added grain.
- **Colour:** monochrome white-on-black + **one cyan/mint accent family**:
  - typing leading edge `#28f8e0` (cyan), which settles to white `#f0f0f0`–`#f8f8f8`
  - code-bracket glyphs `< >` in mint `#28e8b4`
  - gradient tail on the hero word ("edi**tors**" → `#b8e8f0` pale cyan)
  - a small green separator dot after EARNEDITS (`~#22a85a`, dimmed)
  - grey `#909090` for secondary words ("Stop" in the final slogan, "Fixes that" before it brightens).
  The only saturated colour comes from the UI content (thumbnails, red/black product cards, green CTA buttons on the store).
- **Typography:**
  - **One family for everything:** a geometric/humanist product sans. Google Sans / Product Sans is most likely (double-storey `a`, round `o`, a short-tailed `t`). The alternatives are Inter Display, SF Pro Display and General Sans.
  - Weights: Medium (500) for sentences, **SemiBold/Bold caps** for the EARNEDITS wordmark, Bold for "Done!!".
  - Case: sentence case/lowercase for all VO type, ALL CAPS only for the brand.
  - Sizes (cap-to-width): "Stop watching" ~18% of frame width; "and you still haven't started" ~30%; "EARNEDITS · Fixes that" ~37% (x-height ~4.5% of frame height); **"For editors" is the hero at ~47% of width, cap height ~13% of frame height**; "< who actually have deadlines >" ~45%; slogan lines ~25%; end wordmark ~8%. The scale contrast between scenes (8% → 47%) does the job that weight contrast would do elsewhere.
  - Tracking: default to slightly tight (−1%). The EARNEDITS caps are tracked +4–6%.
  - Effects: **colour leading edge** (new characters arrive cyan and ease to white over ~6–8 frames, so a typed line always has a cyan "tail" at its right end); a **soft blur-in per word** in the slogan; no shadows, no strokes. "After effects tutorials" sits in a translucent grey chip (`rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`, 6 px radius) with a white **strike-through** line drawn L→R.
  - Emphasis: colour (cyan/mint) and size only, never weight changes inside a line.
- **Layout & composition:** every type line is **dead centre** horizontally and vertically (y ≈ 48–52%), single line, with ~25–30% empty margin on each side. The Gantt and store are centred UI panels ~65% of frame width with large dark margins. The desktop recording fills the frame. Nothing goes in the corners.
- **Assets:** a YouTube-style results list (thumbnails + grey text lines), a custom Gantt "Workflow plan – Motion Designer" (Feb 15 → Apr 30 date ruler, pill bars "Concept & Research · 4 days", "Storyboard & Animatic · 12 days", "Video Submission", "Motion Design", "Final Polish & Delivery", "Client Review", "Revisions", "Feedback", a "Project Kickoff" milestone, a vertical cyan "today/deadline" line); the earnedits.com storefront (category tabs, 4 product cards, green "Add to cart" buttons, pagination dots); a macOS toast pill "Downloaded Earnedits.aep" with a green download icon; a macOS desktop + dock; the After Effects UI showing the video's own comp; a macOS hand cursor.
- **Finishing / FX:** strong **depth-of-field blur** + **directional motion blur** on the hook feed (thumbnail text unreadable on purpose); a vignette on all dark scenes; the screen recording is dimmed ~50% (luma 60 → 31) under the "Done!!" pop; no grain, no glow on the type itself beyond the anti-aliasing.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** ~0.56 scene changes/s (11 scenes / 19.7 s). Scenes run 0.6–3.7 s; the two UI-heavy scenes (feed, desktop) are the longest (2.6 s, 3.7 s), and pure type cards run 0.8–2.3 s. The edit is calm compared with other earn.edits pieces: the energy comes from type motion inside the scene, not from cut frequency.
- **Easing signatures (measured):**
  - **Expo ease-out scrolls/settles:** feed scroll pan_y decays ×0.87/frame (−5.6 → −1.0 over 13 fr); the desktop settle push decays ×~0.86/frame (0.92 → 0.09 over 14 fr); the store slide-up settles in 5 fr (−3.7 → 0). No overshoot anywhere (no sign flips in pan/zoom). This is cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1) territory.
  - **Ease-in exits:** the whip-left before the Gantt accelerates over 13 frames to its peak velocity *at* the cut (−3.7 px/fr analysis = ~26 px/fr real), and the zoom-through accelerates over 10 frames into the dissolve. Exits build speed into the cut; entrances start fast and decay. That is the standard "speed-ramp through the cut" grammar, done gently.
  - **Type-on:** ~2 frames per character (12 cps) for headlines, ~1.5 fr/char for the longer sentence. The colour on each new glyph eases from cyan to white over 6–8 frames.
  - **Word pops:** in bracket captions each word appears in **1 frame** (luma step, no tween); the brackets jump to their new positions in the same frame.
  - **Blur-in words:** ~5 frames, blur and opacity together.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:** character type-on with a colour leading edge (5 uses); single-frame word pop with expanding brackets (2); per-word blur-in (1); UI panel stagger build (Gantt bars every 3–4 fr, store cards every ~2 fr); push-up scene replace (title up, page up from below); scale-up from origin (AE window from the dock, "Done!!" from the lower third); toast slide-in (4 fr); strike-through draw-on (~4 fr).
- **Camera:** a virtual camera is always on, but subtle: slow pull-backs on type scenes (zoom −0.05 to −0.2 per frame), slow push on "For editors" (+0.1 to +0.45), a 3D tilt on the hook feed (rot up to 3.5°/fr), and push/drift moves inside the screen recording to guide the eye to the dock, then the window, then the menu. Net zoom on shots: +4.4 (feed), +12.3 (store zoom-through), +4.5 (desktop).
- **Secondary motion:** the teal radial glow breathes/brightens across type scenes (luma +5–7 over ~1.4 s); the cursor travels with natural ease; dock icons magnify.
- **Sync:**
  - **VO → type:** typed text starts within ±0.1 s of the spoken word on the type-on cards ("and" typed 4.10 vs VO 4.08; EARNEDITS 5.50 vs "Earned" 5.60; "Fixes" 6.2 vs 6.22). Single-frame word-pop cards **lag the VO by ~0.2–0.4 s** ("who" +0.40, "creating" +0.24, "like" +0.23, "a" +0.18, "pro" +0.21). The effect reads as "the screen echoes what was just said", which works for a calm voice.
  - **Cuts → VO:** scene changes lead the sentence by 0.1–0.2 s (EARNEDITS −0.10, store −0.18, desktop −0.13 before "open").
  - **Cuts → beats (123 BPM, 0.488 s period):** 3 of 9 hard cuts land within 0.07 s of a beat (2.58/2.647, 4.08/4.11, 12.21/12.19), and the strong-onset cluster at 12.45–13.7 s matches the AE launch. Beat sync is used for the big visual-mode changes (type→UI) only.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Whip-out with motion blur → hard cut** (2.0–2.58 s): the outgoing UI accelerates left over 13 frames (ease-in, peak ~26 px/frame real) with growing horizontal blur. The cut lands at peak speed and the next scene starts static. Rebuild: `translateX` with `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)` over 12 fr from 0 → −30% plus `blur(0 → 12px)`, then `<Sequence>` switch.
2. **Push-up scene replace** (7.50 s): the old title moves up ~8% and fades over 6 fr while the new UI slides up from +15% with expo ease-out (settled in 5–8 fr), and child cards stagger by 2 fr. It reads like scrolling a web page.
3. **Zoom-through dissolve onto type** (8.4–9.3 s): the camera accelerates into the store headline (10 fr ease-in, scale ~1 → 2.2). In the last 4 fr the UI fades to black while the next text ("For") fades in at the position where the zoomed word was, and the zoom continues decelerating for 6 fr in the new scene. The match is positional ("files" → "For"), not literal.
4. **Hard cut type → type** (4.08, 5.50, 9.88, 15.88, 17.67, 19.08): same black background, so the cut is only a change of text. It is clean and abrupt, like slide changes in a keynote.
5. **Hard cut to the bright screen recording** (12.21 s, luma 11 → 63 in 1 frame): the only brightness jump in the video, placed on a beat, and it acts as an "impact".
6. **Dim-down + overlay pop** (15.0–15.5 s): the screen recording dims to ~50% while "Done!!" scales up from below, then a hard cut to pure black.

## 6. Sound design
A minimal electronic bed at ~123 BPM sits under the VO (rms modest, VO dominant). The strong-onset cluster 6.27–7.75 s is likely soft whoosh/riser accents under the brand reveal and store slide-up; 12.45–14.3 s has clicks and whooshes for the dock click, window open and menu; 15.79–16.21 s is an impact/drop at the cut to black before the slogan. The VO is unhurried, with ~0.5 s silences between sentences (2.08–2.80, 5.06–5.60, 7.14–7.68), and each silence carries a visual change. There is no music stop or silence moment; the bed carries through the black end card.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **One typeface, one accent hue, mostly black.** Product-sans type at Medium weight, white plus a single cyan/mint accent, reads as "tech brand" immediately.
2. **Type-on with a colour leading edge.** New characters arrive in the accent colour and cool to white, which makes plain typing feel alive and branded at zero cost.
3. **Alternate type cards with real UI.** The pattern is sentence, proof, sentence, proof. Real screenshots and screen recordings (your actual product) do the persuading, and type on black does the talking.
4. **Size is the emphasis tool.** Nearly every line is small (18–37% width), so the one hero line ("For editors", 47%) hits hard without bold or colour fills.
5. **Speed ramps through cuts:** exits accelerate into the cut, entrances decay out of it (expo ease-out, no overshoot). This is calm but never static.
6. **Visual metaphors from UI:** strike through the thing you tell people to stop doing, and use a Gantt chart with a deadline line for "deadline closing in".
7. **Code brackets `< >` as a caption frame** add a "for creators/devs" flavour and give word-by-word captions a container that grows.
8. **Meta proof:** the screen recording opens the very AE project that made the ad, which is the strongest possible demo of "ready-made project files".
9. **End on pure black** with the slogan then the wordmark, and drop all glow and colour for the final 3.8 s so it lands like a keynote close.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1920×1080`, `30 fps` (scale the frame counts ×1.25 from the 24 fps reference), duration = VO + 0.6 s (≈ 600 frames for a 20 s VO). A 9:16 variant works with the same components: type sizes ×1.6 relative to width.
- **Layer stack (type scene):** `<AbsoluteFill bg="#080808">` → `<RadialGlow color="#0e2a2a" x="60%" y="15%" radius="70%" opacity={interpolate(f,[0,40],[0.6,1])}>` → `<Vignette strength={0.55}>` → `<Camera scale={interpolate(f,[0,dur],[1.04,1.0])}>` (slow pull-back) → `<TypeOnLine>` / `<BracketCaption>` centred.
- **Layer stack (UI scene):** background → `<UIPanel src>` (screenshot or rebuilt HTML) inside `<Camera>` with a scripted push/drift → optional `<Toast>` → `<CursorPath>`.
- **Components & timings (30 fps):**
  - `TypeOnLine({text, startFrame, framesPerChar: 2.5, accent: '#28f8e0', settle: 9})`: render each char with `opacity: f >= charStart ? 1 : 0` and `color: interpolateColors(f - charStart, [0, settle], [accent, '#f2f2f2'])`. Left-anchor the text box so the final string ends up centred (measure with `measureText` from `@remotion/layout-utils`).
  - `BracketCaption({words: [{w, start}], bracketColor: '#28e8b4', lagFrames: 6})`: words appear in 1 frame at `wordStart + 6` (the ~0.2 s lag), brackets are flex siblings so they move automatically, with no tween.
  - `BlurInWords({words, dur: 6})`: per word `filter: blur(${interpolate(t,[0,6],[8,0])}px)`, opacity 0 → 1, `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`; per-word colour (grey / white / pale cyan `#b8e8f0`).
  - `GradientTailWord`: `background: linear-gradient(90deg,#fff 55%,#b8e8f0)`, `background-clip: text`.
  - `StaggerBuild({items, every: 4, from: {x: -12, opacity: 0}, dur: 8, easing: Easing.bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)})` for Gantt bars and store cards (every 2–3 fr for cards).
  - `StrikeThrough({drawFrames: 5})`: an absolutely positioned 2 px white bar with `scaleX` 0 → 1, `transform-origin: left`.
  - `PushUpReplace({dur: 10})`: outgoing `translateY 0 → -8%`, opacity 1 → 0, `Easing.in(cubic)`; incoming `translateY 15% → 0`, `Easing.bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)`.
  - `WhipOut({dur: 15, dist: '-30%', maxBlur: 14})`: `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)` and a horizontal SVG `feGaussianBlur stdDeviation="${b} 0"`; cut at the end.
  - `ZoomThroughDissolve({inDur: 12, outDur: 8, maxScale: 2.2, focus: {x, y}})`: `transform-origin` on the target word, scale with `Easing.in(Easing.quad)`, crossfade over the last 4 fr; the incoming scene starts at scale 1.15 and eases to 1.0 over 8 fr.
  - `Toast({text: 'Downloaded Earnedits.aep', icon: 'download', slideFrom: 20, dur: 5})`: a dark pill `#16181a`, 1 px border `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`, radius 10.
  - `ScreenRecording` (user footage): `<OffthreadVideo>` inside `<Camera>` keyframes (push 1.0 → 1.08 with expo-out over 18 fr when it enters, then drifts toward the area of action). Dim it with a black overlay at opacity 0 → 0.5 over 10 fr before the overlay text.
  - `PopRise({text: 'Done!!', from: {y: 25%, scale: 0.7, opacity: 0.3}, spring: {damping: 200, stiffness: 180}})`: no overshoot (high damping).
  - `EndCard({wordmark: 'EARNEDITS', size: '8vw', tracking: '0.05em'})` on `#000`.
- **Fonts:** `@remotion/google-fonts` → "Google Sans" isn't on Google Fonts. Use **"Product Sans"-like alternatives: `Outfit` 500 or `Inter` 500 with `font-feature-settings: 'ss01'`**, or load Google Sans locally if licensed.
- **Tricky bits:** the 3D-tilted, blurred hook feed is a tall screenshot inside `perspective: 1200px; rotateX(18deg) rotateZ(-4deg)`, translating up with expo-out, plus `filter: blur(3px)` and a directional blur layer. Fade it in under the title (opacity 0 → 1 over 12 fr) while the title moves `translateY -40%` and fades out.

## 9. Variation ideas
1. **SaaS feature launch (e.g. a calendar app):** the same type-on-black grammar with the brand accent replacing cyan; UI breaks show the real app (week view, drag-to-schedule) with cursor paths; the slogan and wordmark end on black.
2. **Course/creator offer ("Stop watching 3-hour Figma tutorials"):** the hook feed becomes a blurred YouTube search for the topic with a strike-through; the proof is a screen recording of opening the template; the bracket captions keep the dev/creator voice.
3. **Fintech/productivity explainer in 9:16:** stack the type lines vertically at 60–70% width, use a Gantt or budget chart as the "pain" UI, a phone notification toast as the "relief", and keep a single mint accent and the pure-black end card.
