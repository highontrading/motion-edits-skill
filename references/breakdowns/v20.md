## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** history micro-story with a moral ("three discoverers → the one who understood gets the credit → lesson → CTA").
- **Beats:**
  - 0.00–6.84 **Hook + discoverer 1:** a date ("1771"), a chemist, a gas from heated mercury oxide, which he called "fire air".
  - 7.18–12.12 **Discoverer 2:** a Swedish scientist found the same gas independently; "neither published first".
  - 12.64–19.60 **Discoverer 3:** in 1774 an English chemist repeated it and mentioned it to a French scientist over dinner.
  - 19.94–28.58 **The turn:** the Frenchman understood it, ran tests, linked the gas to combustion and breathing, and **named it oxygen**.
  - 28.58–32.42 **Payoff:** "History remembers him, not the two men who found it first."
  - 33.10–37.66 **Moral (antithesis pair):** "Being first doesn't guarantee credit. / Understanding what you've found does."
  - 38.12–42.53 **CTA:** project file on earnedits.com.
- **Hook mechanics:** frame 0 is a sunny meadow with a cut-out scientist (a bobble-head-style photo head on a lab-coat body) holding up a flask that **emits a glowing yellow-green gas plume**. Within 0.07 s four white cards **flip in one by one to spell "1771"** in red (spoken at 0.18 s). By 1.6 s the digit cards **fly at the camera** (zoom-through exit) and a curved "tweet" card starts **orbiting the scientist**. Three distinct motions happen in the first 2 s, and a date sits in the first spoken word pair.
- **Script → visual rule:** each discoverer gets **their own period-coded set and "text device"**: a tweet card for the first chemist, a Game Boy LCD for the Swede, a cassette label + Wikipedia-style bio for the Englishman, a gilded frame + 80s TV for the Frenchman. Pure statements ("neither published first", the moral) go on flat colour voids.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** photo-collage dioramas. Cut-out people and engravings live in staged 2.5D sets with real perspective (meadow horizon, desk top-down, bedroom shelf, library depth), and each set uses props from a different decade (1700s portrait frames, 80s TV and Walkman, 90s Game Boy and TI-83). The anachronism is the joke.
- **Backgrounds & textures:** XP-style sky + grass meadow (#3fa0bb / #5b792f) → torn blue paper + grey grid paper desk (#50596a / #bcb6b6) → **black grain void** (#161616) → walnut desk + graph-paper notebook with **window-blind shadow stripes** (#693d29 / #cdcccc) → **deep red wall with blind shadows** (#681b13) → dim 80s bedroom (#4d413b) → dark library with DOF (#201713) → **mustard paper vignette** (#d19f4f/#e7b159) → **bottle-green paper vignette** (#0e493c) → black flash frame → sky (CTA).
- **Lighting textures:** diagonal **venetian-blind shadow bands** (soft, ~15% darker, ~10% of frame height apart) across the notebook and the red wall add "room light" to flat scans.
- **Colour:** one dominant hue per scene + an accent. Recurring: **red #e32b1f / orange-red #ee3d14** (dates, "NEITHER PUBLISHED FIRST", names, caption box, scribble), **yellow #f5c21b** (FIRE AIR, INDEPENDENTLY badge, sparkle), white type on dark photos.
  - **The moral inverts its palette:** line 1 is dark green #0e493c on mustard; line 2 is mustard #e1b15c on dark green. The colour swap carries the "but" of the antithesis.
  - In the payoff the "losers" are **tinted red** (the two king cards wash to ~#c0303a at ~70% over 0.4 s) while the winner's card stays full colour.
- **Typography:**
  - **Punch type:** ultra-black wide grotesk caps (*Archivo Black / Montserrat Black / Druk Wide-like*), tight leading.
    - NEITHER / PUBLISHED / FIRST in #ee3d14 on black, the middle line smaller (~60% cap height). This is a size zigzag: big–small–biggest, with FIRST ~80% width.
    - BEING FIRST / DOESN'T / GUARANTEE / CREDIT is centred, ~75% width, all lines similar in size.
  - **Cartoon title:** HE CALLED IT in red #e32b1f with a dark maroon outline, over FIRE AIR in **yellow #f5c21b with a dark-brown 3D extrude (~6 px down-right)**, ~85% width. A 4-point yellow sparkle star is the accent.
  - **Silver headline:** HISTORY in white→light-grey vertical gradient with a soft dark shadow, ~85% width; then REMEMBERS HIM / NOT THE TWO MEN / WHO FOUND IT FIRST in the same face at ~45% of HISTORY's cap height.
  - **Condensed caption:** THAT FRENCHMAN in condensed grotesk (*Bebas Neue / Oswald SemiBold*), off-white, ~66% width, above the frame. UNDERSTOOD WHAT NOBODY ELSE DID in the same face, small (~2.3% frame height), under the frame.
  - **Prop text:**
    - Game Boy LCD: dark-olive dot-matrix pixel caps with **LCD ghosting** (a doubled, offset copy at 40%), skewed with the device, and an underscore caret.
    - Cassette label: "1774" in a black geometric sans.
    - Bio blocks: red heavy name ("Joseph Priestley", "Antoine Laurent") with a slight glow, then black bold small grotesk body that types on.
    - 80s TV caption: a **red box (#d9322b) with a double dark-red border**, white heavy caps with a faint glow, built line by line.
    - CTA Game Boy: **lowercase** pixel font on the green LCD.
  - **Badges:** INDEPENDENTLY in white heavy caps with a dark outline on a **yellow-amber rounded rectangle with a dashed inner stitch border** (#f0b81c), ~45% width.
  - **Emphasis rules:** the newest word fades grey→full colour. The key verb gets a doodle (a red marker scribble over the Frenchman's portrait). The "winner" stays in colour while the others are tinted.
- **Layout:** voids centre the type at y ≈ 45–55%. Sets put the hero object centre-frame and the type in the **upper third** (FIRE AIR y≈30%, HISTORY y≈25–35%, red TV caption y≈12–25%, THAT FRENCHMAN y≈22%). Small supporting captions go directly under the hero object.

- **Finishing / FX:** a halftone dot screen on photos, grain and dust on the voids, DOF (foreground grass and library are blurred), a volumetric glow on the gas plume, blind-shadow gobos, heavy directional motion blur on every camera move, a soft vignette on the colour voids, and **1-frame black flashes** before two scenes.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Frame rate:** on twos throughout. 142 frames show >1.0 flow followed by a <0.15 duplicate frame, so the effective rate is 15 drawings/s in a 30 fps file.
- **Pacing:** ~0.28 cuts/s; scenes 1.0–6.3 s. Story scenes run 2.6–6.3 s, the punch voids 1.6–2.6 s. The notebook section (12.6–19.9) is the most kinetic: four framings in 7.3 s joined by camera moves.
- **Easing signatures:**
  - **Shot-open settle** (shots 2, 4, 8, 9, 10, 11): peak motion in the first 0–2 frames after the cut, exponential decay to rest in **21–45 frames** (the largest magnitudes take the longest: z −30 → 41 fr; z −7.7 → 21 fr). → `Easing.bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, scale ~1.10–1.15 → 1.0. Shot 2 adds a **rotation settle** (~−10° → 0) on the same curve.
  - **Big in-shot camera moves:** the tilt-up (49 up / 45 down, ~3.1 s) and the Game Boy drop (10 up / 37 down) are ease-in-out and ease-out respectively; no overshoot (no sign flips in pan/zoom).
  - **Blur-masked cuts:** at 14.43, 17.37 and 18.93 motion **ramps up for 23–62 frames into the cut**, the cut lands on the blurriest frame, and the next framing decelerates from the same velocity in the same direction. A whip pan is thus built from two separately composed framings of one board.
  - **Digit flip:** each card rotates ~90°→0° on Y over ~6–8 frames, staggered ~2–3 frames L→R.
  - **Zoom-through exit:** the 1771 cards scale ~1→3× and blur in ~10 frames as the camera pushes in.
- **Entrance / exit vocab:**
  - Word blur-fade: opacity 0.3→1, blur ~6 px → 0, over ~6 frames.
  - Left-edge slide-in for the first word of a void card (HISTORY, BEING, UNDERSTANDING; ~4–5 frames).
  - Typewriter at speech rate with an underscore caret (Game Boy ×2).
  - Fast decorative typing for bio paragraphs.
  - Badge appears, then its text types in.
  - Marker scribble write-on (~18 frames).
  - Red caption box: lines append as the VO reaches them.
  - Tint wipe (cards → red, ~12 frames).
  - Picture-in-TV cross-dissolve and scale-up.
  - Sparkle fly-in on a curved path (~24 frames).
  - Exits are hard cuts, except the zoom-through of the 1771 cards and the void card's slow shrink before the cut.
- **Camera:** every set has a virtual camera. Each shot opens with a settle and has one decisive move (tilt-up, pan, pull-back, drop). Net zoom at cut-in: −6 to −33. The tilt-up travels ~440 px at 720 width.
- **Secondary motion:** the glowing gas plume flickers and rises, birds flap across, the tweet card bends along its path, the Game Boy CTA wobbles, the TV picture changes, and the card fan spreads.
- **Sync:**
  - **Cuts ↔ VO:** extremely tight. Cuts land within ±0.04 s of the sentence's first word (7.20/"A" 7.18; 11.00/"neither" 11.02; 12.63/"In" 12.64; 19.90/"That" 19.94; 22.77/"He" 22.74; 33.10/"Being" 33.10; 35.67/"Understanding" 35.64; 38.13/"Editors" 38.12). The single late cut is HISTORY (cut 29.03, VO 28.58, +0.45 s): the "named it oxygen" picture is allowed to land first.
  - **Text ↔ VO:** words appear 0 to +0.2 s after being spoken (PUBLISHED +0.2, FIRST +0.13, REMEMBERS HIM ~+0.3, NOT THE TWO MEN ~0). The INDEPENDENTLY badge lands ~0.35 s after the word, as a confirmation beat.
  - **Beats:** cuts sit at a mean |offset| of 0.11 s from the 117.5 BPM grid (chance ≈ 0.13 s), so editing is VO-driven. Four cuts land within ±0.07 s of a beat (14.43, 18.93, 19.90, 38.13).

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Hard cut + expo scale settle** (most cuts): the new scene appears at ~110–115% and eases to 100% in 21–45 frames. Optionally add rotation (−10° → 0, shot 2).
2. **Continuous tilt-up between "scenes"** (≈4.6–5.3): instead of cutting from the meadow to the sky title, the camera follows the rising gas plume up into an empty sky that becomes the next "card". Rebuild: one tall composite (meadow at the bottom, sky above), `translateY` by ~60% of frame height over 90 frames with `bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)` and a vertical motion blur ∝ velocity.
3. **Blur-masked cut / composite whip** (14.43, 17.37, 18.93): accelerate the camera for ~0.8–2 s into the cut, swap to a separately framed layout at the blur peak, and decelerate with the same vector. Rebuild: scene A `CameraMove` ends at velocity v, scene B starts at v and ease-outs; add a directional blur of 20–30 px over 2 frames either side of the cut.
4. **1-frame black flash** (22.77, 38.13): one fully black frame between scenes resets the eye before a big new set (bedroom, CTA). Rebuild: a 1-frame (on twos, 2-frame) `<AbsoluteFill style={{background:'#000'}}>`.
5. **Zoom-through exit** (1.5–1.9): foreground title cards scale past the lens with blur while the background pushes in.
6. **Colour-inverted twin cards** (33.10 → 35.67): the same layout and type style with background and text colours swapped. The cut itself is the antithesis.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Give each character a set and a device.** When a story has several actors, assign each a distinct era-coded prop that carries their text (tweet card, Game Boy, cassette + bio, gilded frame). The viewer tracks who is who by look alone.
2. **Hide cuts in camera blur.** Ramp a camera move into the cut and continue the same vector after it, so several framings of one board read as one fluid move.
3. **Follow a motivated element across a "transition"** (the gas plume rising into the sky where the name of the gas appears).
4. **Antithesis = inverted palette.** Set the two halves of a moral on swapped background and text colours, with the same layout.
5. **Tint the losers.** A 12-frame colour wash on the non-chosen elements makes "not the two men" visual without new assets.
6. **Cuts on the first syllable** (±0.04 s), with exactly one deliberate late cut to let a payoff image land.
7. **A 1-frame black** before a big set change reads as a "blink" and adds punch without a whoosh graphic.
8. **On-twos + halftone + grain + blind-shadow gobos** turn mixed stock into one tactile, lit world.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, duration = VO + 0.3 s; step all animation with `const f2 = Math.floor(frame/2)*2`.
- **Layer stack per set:** `<SetBackdrop>` (photo/texture + `<BlindShadows angle={-12} spacing={0.1} opacity={0.15}>`) → `<Cutouts>` (people, props, each with a contact shadow) → `<PropText>` (LCD / label / bio / caption box, transformed with its prop via a shared CSS transform or `perspective` + `rotate`) → `<HeroType>` → `<Doodle>` / `<Sparkle>` → `<Halftone>` + `<Grain>` → wrapped in `<SettleIn>` and `<CameraMove>`.
- **Components & timings:**
  - `SettleIn` — `scale 1.12→1` (+ optional `rotate -10°→0`), 21–45 frames, `Easing.bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)`; frames scale with move size.
  - `FlipDigits` — per digit card `rotateY(90deg)→0` with `perspective: 800px`, 8 frames each, stagger 2–3 frames, `spring({damping: 14, stiffness: 200})`.
  - `ZoomThroughExit` — `scale 1→3`, `blur 0→12px`, `opacity 1→0` over 10 frames, `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)`.
  - `OrbitCard` — the card follows an elliptical path around the subject (`x = cx + rx·cos θ`, `y = cy + ry·sin θ`, θ over ~56 frames, ease-out), its z-index flips when it passes behind the subject (sin θ < 0), and the card is bent with an SVG `feDisplacementMap` or by splitting it into 8 vertical strips with per-strip `rotateY`/`translateZ` to fake curvature.
  - `TiltReveal` — one tall composition translated `0 → -60%` over 90 frames, `Easing.bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)`, plus a vertical motion blur (`feGaussianBlur stdDeviation="0 ${v*0.5}"`).
  - `SparklePath` — a 4-point star on an SVG path (`getPointAtLength`), 24 frames, ease-out, with a scale pulse 1→1.3→1 on landing.
  - `LCDTypewriter` — pixel font (*Press Start 2P / VT323*), colour `#2d3a12` on `#8fbf3a`, a ghost copy offset (2px, 1px) at 35% opacity, a caret `_` blinking every 8 frames, typing mapped to the VO word span.
  - `BioTypeOn` — red name (`#e32b1f`, `text-shadow: 0 0 6px rgba(227,43,31,.4)`) then body chars at ~40 chars/s; new chars start at 40% grey and darken over 4 frames.
  - `CaptionBox` — `#d9322b` fill, a 3px `#7a1410` border + an inner 2px border inset 6px; lines append on VO timestamps; each word blur-fades in 6 frames.
  - `MarkerScribble` — SVG path `strokeDashoffset` over 18 frames, `#c41a1a` at 85% opacity, `mix-blend-mode: multiply`, stroke width ~5% of the frame width.
  - `TintWash` — overlay `#c0303a` with `mix-blend-mode: multiply` on the selected cards, opacity 0→0.7 over 12 frames.
  - `EdgeSlideWord` — `translateX(-60%) → 0` over 5 frames, `bezier(0.2,0.9,0.3,1)`, with a horizontal blur 10px→0.
  - `BlurMaskedCut` — pass `exitVelocity` from scene A to scene B; B's `CameraMove` starts with that velocity and decays over 40–60 frames.
  - `BlackFlash` — 2 frames of black.
  - `DropIn` (CTA) — `translateY(-80%) → 0` over 47 frames, `Easing.bezier(0.2,0.8,0.2,1)`, `rotate` damped sine ±2.5°.
- **Type CSS:**
  - Cartoon extrude: `color:#f5c21b; -webkit-text-stroke: 2px #5a1a0a; text-shadow: 1px 1px 0 #5a1a0a, 2px 2px 0 #5a1a0a, … 6px 6px 0 #5a1a0a`.
  - Silver headline: `background: linear-gradient(#fff, #c9c9c9); -webkit-background-clip: text; filter: drop-shadow(0 4px 6px rgba(0,0,0,.5))`.
