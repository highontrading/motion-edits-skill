## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** community/brand promo (the creator's free Discord). The structure is **pain → place → benefits ×3 → empathy → future → scarcity → CTA**.
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–2.5 **Hook (pain):** "Editing alone is a lonely road." Typing hands, then a lone figure walking a road.
  - 3.2–6.7 **Answer:** "So we built a place for you, the Earned Edits Discord." A laptop wakes up with an eye on screen, then the EARNEDITS / DISCORD PC.
  - 7.5–10.3 **Value prop:** free, for editors who want to get better (FREE sign on a spotlit silhouette; editors isolated in glass bell jars).
  - 10.9–17.9 **Benefits:** stuck on an animation → ask and we will help (CRT error dialogs → SOLVED). Want feedback → people tell you the truth (video card + magnifier + comment cards).
  - 18.5–20.5 **Empathy beat:** "You don't have to struggle alone anymore" (huddled figure in a spotlight, a hand reaches in).
  - 21.2–27.7 **Future:** "And soon… editing tournaments. Compete, try new things" (progress bar → two gamers under a trophy spotlight).
  - 28.3–31.4 **Scarcity / early-mover:** "The server is new. The first editors are still to come" (calendar "new", line-up with the 1ST outline).
  - 31.6–39.5 **CTA:** Join the Discord → "make something worth watching" (trophy raised, clapping hands) → "Comment DISCORD to get the link."
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** two hanging yellow pixel lamps in darkness. At 0.27 a cloud of red pixel noise sits where the title will be, and **"EDITING" resolves from pixels by 0.53**. "ALONE" (white, textured) resolves from pixels by 0.8. Meanwhile pale cut-out hands in red sweater sleeves rise from the bottom onto a pixel keyboard (pull-back −16 zoom units in 15 f, peak at f2). The title is fully readable at 0.8 s, the moment the VO says "alone" (0.40) and "is" (0.74).
- **Script → visual rule:** one diorama per sentence. The sentence's **pain or benefit noun goes into the red label badge** (ALONE → DISCORD → FREE → EDITORES → ANIMATION? → ASK, → HELP. → ON YOUR EDITS? → STRUGGLE → new → WORTH WATCHING → "DISCORD"). Connector words sit above it in a small lowercase grotesk or white pixel caps.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** mixed-media "pixel collage". Pixel-art characters and props (8-bit people, CRTs, calendar, trophy, clapperboard mascot) are mixed with **halftone-printed cut-outs** (hands, laptop, phone, bell jars, magnifying glass, boot footage, torn paper note). Everything sits on dark, grainy, vignetted backgrounds with a halftone screen over it, like a riso-printed zine about a video game.
- **Backgrounds** change per scene and are almost all dark: near-black olive (`#0a0b05`/`#111202`) for intro and outro; beige plaster wall `#dac6a5`/`#cec3a7` for the "lonely road" and CRT desk scenes (a warm wood desk `#6b4a2a`); flat green halftone `#19310d` for the Discord PC; charcoal concrete with a diagonal light beam and faint handwriting for "and soon", "the server is new" and the Discord badge; a circular warm **spotlight pool** (`#e8d6b0` → black) for FREE and STRUGGLE; and a yellow-olive `#4d420c`/`#795d0d` trophy spotlight for the tournament.
- **Colour:** dark neutral base + **signal red `#e21e08`/`#e60104`** (badges, sleeves, hoodies, scarf) + **warm cream `#e5d6bf`** (type, paper) + **mustard/amber `#fab208`/`#e6cf07`** for "good news" moments (SOLVED screen, lamps, trophy, lightning bolts, 1ST outline). Discord blurple `#5865f2` appears exactly once, for the DISCORD CTA badge. Contrast comes from value (cream on near-black), not saturation.
- **Typography:**
  - **Pixel display face** (bitmap-style caps with stepped diagonals and 1-pixel notches; closest: **Pixelify Sans Bold / Silkscreen / "Jersey 10"**), ALL CAPS, cream `#e5d6bf` with a grainy texture fill and a dark 1-step pixel shadow. Used for EDITING ALONE, WANT FEEDBACK, THE SERVER IS, EDITING TOURNAMENTS, COMPETE, AND MAKE SOMETHING, COMMENT. Size 55–95% of frame width for headlines ("WANT FEEDBACK" spans ~95%) and ~30% for side labels (COMPETE, TRY NEW THINGS).
  - **Red pixel label badge:** a rectangle with **stair-stepped pixel corners** (2-step notch), red `#e21e08`, cream pixel caps inside, and a hard dark drop shadow offset ~6 px down-right. Badge height ≈ 6% of frame height, width hugs the word + ~10% padding.
  - **Connector line:** a heavy geometric grotesk in lowercase (closest: **Inter Tight ExtraBold / Satoshi Black**), tight tracking (−4%), mid-grey `#6b5f4f` on beige scenes and cream on dark ones, at ~45% of badge-word size ("stuck on an", "and we will", "made for", "who wants to").
  - **Emphasis:** STRUGGLE is set huge in **maroon `#7a0f14` pixel caps** inside the spotlight, deliberately low-contrast so it feels heavy. "WORTH WATCHING." and "“DISCORD”" are red pixel caps with no badge. The calendar's "new" is red lowercase pixel script.
  - **Source typos:** "LONELEY", "EDITORES". This is a signal of speed, not intent. Don't copy it.
- **Layout:** the headline sits in the upper third (y ≈ 10–30%), the hero diorama in the centre third, and the lower 25% stays dark (vignette) except for comment cards and clapping hands. Type often sits **behind** a character or prop (the trophy covers the middle of TOURNAMENTS, the hero's body covers letters of AND MAKE SOMETHING / COMMENT).
- **Assets:** pixel characters (a trench-coat walker with a red scarf, faceless suit silhouette, four "editor" avatars, two gamers in red racing chairs, a hero with a flowing red scarf holding a trophy), pixel CRTs/PCs, Win-style error dialogs, a tear-off calendar, a progress bar, a red clapperboard mascot with eyes, halftone cut-out hands/laptop/phone/bell jars/magnifier, a YouTube-style video player card, comment cards with pixel avatars and like/dislike icons.
- **Finishing:** a heavy **halftone dot screen** on every cut-out and gradient, film grain (~8% opacity), strong vignettes (corners to ~black), hard pixel drop shadows, soft spotlight cones, light motion blur on whip moves, and **on-twos stepping** on all motion (15 fps).

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** 0.36 cuts/s. Scenes are 0.8–1.8 s in the hook and value-prop section (0–8 s) and 2.3–4.4 s in the benefits section, so each sentence gets one scene. The last scene is a 5.9-s loop for comment-reading time.
- **Frame rate as style:** ~44% of frames are duplicates. All character, prop and camera motion is **on twos (15 fps)**, which gives the stepped, hand-made, retro-game cadence. The per-frame flow alternates value/0 (e.g. zoom −3.7, 0, −4.0, 0, −1.7, 0…).
- **Easing signature (measured on unique frames):** camera moves are short ease-outs with **peak speed at 2–4 f** (1–2 unique frames) and a **6–15 f decay**. Intro pull-back: zoom −3.7, −4.0, −1.7, −2.2, −1.8, −1.4, −1.0, −0.7, −0.3 (exponential decay). Whip pan 24.77: peak at f4, settled at f13. Wide pull-back 26.1: −1.0, −0.4, −3.2, −4.9, −4.3, −3.6, −3.0, −2.4, −2.1 (ease-in-out, peak at mid-move). There is no overshoot on camera moves. The **pendulum jars** have the only overshoot: rotation +2.3 → +1.7 → −1.1 → +0.8 → −0.8 per step, a damped swing.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:**
  - *Pixel-resolve type* (the signature): the word appears as random red/cream pixel blocks for 2–4 unique frames (4–8 f), then snaps to the clean glyphs. Example: EDITING 0.27→0.53, DISCORD 6.13→6.4, STRUGGLE 19.2→19.47.
  - *Empty badge → content*: the red badge lands first (1–2 f) and the word pixel-builds inside it ~4–8 f later.
  - *Word push*: a new connector phrase slides in from the left and shoves the old one out of frame (12.5–13.07, ~8 f).
  - *Rack-focus entrance*: the scene opens 15–20 px blurred and sharpens over 14–16 f (1.57, 10.73).
  - *Slide-in with blur* (FREE badge, arm with phone, hand reaching in): ~6–8 f, ease-out.
  - *Pendulum swing-in* (jars): enter oversized from off-frame, settle with 2–3 damped oscillations over ~12 f.
  - *Tilt-and-settle* (retro PC, CRT): object enters rotated ~20–25° and rights itself in ~5 f.
  - *Pixel-dissolve morph* between props (note → video card, calendar in).
  - *Spotlight switch-on* in pixel steps (22.67–22.93).
- **Camera:** mostly locked with slow drifts (jars: net zoom −27 over 2.7 s). Punctuation moves are tilt-up (feedback comments), tilt-down (line-up), one whip pan and one pull-back reveal in the tournament scene.
- **Secondary motion:** looping pixel cycles (walker, typing fingers, blinking eye, mascot expressions every ~0.27 s, scarf flutter, clapping hands every ~0.33 s), shock tick-marks and sparkles, floating red pixel squares, and a stepped progress bar.
- **Sync:**
  - Cuts land **0–0.2 s before or at** the sentence start: 5.63 vs "The Earned" 5.54 (+0.09), 8.00 vs "made" 7.98, 10.73 vs "Stuck" 10.92 (−0.19), 14.20 vs "Want" 14.14, 18.47 vs "You" 18.52 (−0.05), 21.30 vs "And soon" 21.24, 28.20 vs "The server" 28.26 (−0.06), 23.77 vs "Compete" 23.98 (−0.21). ## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Hard cut into a defocused frame + rack focus** at 1.57 and 10.73: frame 1 of the new scene is ~20 px Gaussian-blurred and slightly over-scaled (≈1.05×), then it sharpens to 0 px and scale 1.0 over 14–16 f with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`. It reads as the "camera finding focus", which suits a contemplative VO.
2. **Defocus-out → cut** at 5.6: the outgoing scene blurs over the last ~4 f before the hard cut.
3. **Plain hard cuts on sentence starts** (3.37, 7.17, 8.00, 14.20, 18.47, 21.30, 22.47, 28.20, 33.57): always with a big change of background value (beige ↔ near-black ↔ green) so the cut reads even on a small screen. Luma jumps of 15–95 units in 1 f.
4. **Pixel-dissolve scene/prop change** (15.73 note → video card; ~31.9 line-up → empty wall + DISCORD): the outgoing element breaks into ~12–20 px squares that scatter and vanish over 4–6 f, and the incoming element assembles the same way.
5. **In-shot whip pan + pull-back reveal** (24.77, 26.1): the close-up of each gamer is joined by a lateral whip (4 f to peak), and a final pull-back recomposes into the wide establishing frame. This is the reverse of the usual wide → close order.
6. **Camera tilt to reveal the next beat** (16.6 tilt-up to the comments, 29.6 tilt-down to the line-up): the headline leaves the top or stays while new content enters from below. It avoids a cut inside one sentence-pair.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Pick one retro medium and commit fully**, including frame rate. Pixel art + halftone + animated on twos is a coherent world, and the 15-fps stepping is part of the look.
2. **One badge, one keyword per sentence.** The red pixel-cornered label is the spine of the edit; viewers can read the story from the badges alone (ALONE → DISCORD → FREE → EDITORS → HELP → FEEDBACK → STRUGGLE → NEW → DISCORD).
3. **Build type from the medium's own particles.** Every word assembles from pixels in 4–8 f, so the type animation *is* the art style, not a generic fade.
4. **Value-contrast cuts.** Alternate beige, near-black and coloured backgrounds between sentences, so each cut is a clear chapter break even at phone size.
5. **Emotional pacing:** short, fast scenes for the pitch (0.8–1.8 s) and longer, darker, spotlit holds for the empathy beat (STRUGGLE, 2.8 s). Low-contrast maroon type there makes the word feel heavy.
6. **Small loops make dioramas live**: blinking eyes, typing fingers, scarf flutter and clapping hands, all at ~0.27–0.33 s cycles.
7. **Let type sit behind the subject** (trophy, hero) so the pixel world feels layered instead of captioned.
8. **Scarcity before the CTA** ("the server is new", 1ST outline) gives the comment ask a reason.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, but quantise all animation to twos: `const f = Math.floor(frame / 2) * 2;` and feed `f` to every `interpolate`/`spring`. Duration ≈ VO + 0.3 s (≈1185 f).
- **Scene stack, bottom → top:** `<DarkBG color tint="olive|charcoal|beige|green">` → `<SpotlightPool x y r={0.35} warm="#e8d6b0" />` or `<LightBeam angle={-30}/>` → `<PixelSprite src frames fps={7.5} loop/>` characters/props → `<HalftoneCutout src dot={4} shadow="8px 8px 0 #000a"/>` → `<PixelHeadline text color="#e5d6bf"/>` + `<PixelBadge text color="#e21e08"/>` + `<ConnectorLine text/>` → `<HalftoneScreen dot={3} opacity={0.18}/>` → `<GrainOverlay opacity={0.08}/>` → `<Vignette strength={0.6}/>`.
- **Components & timings:**
  - `PixelResolveText`: render the text to an offscreen canvas, split it into a 12×12 px cell grid, and for f ∈ [0, 8) draw each filled cell with probability `p = f/8` at a random jitter ±1 cell, with a random 30% of empty cells as noise in the badge colour. At f = 8 draw clean glyphs. Start at `wordStart + 2 f`.
  - `PixelBadge`: an SVG path with 2-step stair corners (step = 6 px at 1080 w), fill `#e21e08`, hard shadow `translate(6px, 6px)` in `#1a0a05`. Enter: scaleX 0.85→1 over 2 f (on twos = 1 step), then `PixelResolveText` inside at +4 f.
  - `RackFocusIn`: `filter: blur(interpolate(f,[0,16],[20,0]))`, `scale` 1.05→1, `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`.
  - `PendulumSwingIn`: rotation `spring({frame: f, fps: 30, config: {damping: 6, stiffness: 120, mass: 0.6}})` mapped from 35°→0°, origin at the cable top. translateY −40%→0 over 6 f.
  - `TiltSettle`: rotate 25°→0°, scale 1.2→1 over 6 f, `Easing.out(Easing.back(1.2))`, then quantised.
  - `WordPush`: the outgoing line moves translateX 0→+110% while the incoming line moves −110%→0 over 8 f, `Easing.inOut(Easing.cubic)`.
  - `WhipPan`: camera translateX of one frame-width over 8 f with `Easing.bezier(0.7,0,0.2,1)` and a 12 px horizontal blur at peak.
  - `PixelDissolveSwap`: reuse the PixelResolve cell mask on images (`clip-path` from a generated SVG of random squares). Out over 4 f, in over 4 f, overlapped by 2 f.
  - `CommentCard`: rounded cream card + pixel avatar + like/dislike icons. Stagger 0.27–0.5 s; the second card is highlighted amber `#fab208`.
  - `ProgressBarStepped`: width steps 33 → 57 → 80 → 99% every 6 f, red striped fill.
- **Tricky bits:** keep halftone consistent by applying one global halftone shader (an SVG `feTurbulence`-free approach: a CSS `radial-gradient` dot pattern with `mix-blend-mode: multiply`) over cut-outs, not per asset.
