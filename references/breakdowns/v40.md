**Source creator handle:** earn.edits · **Format:** 720×960, 3:4 (IG feed portrait), 30 fps container (the embedded clips update on 2s ≈ 15 fps), 14.4 s · **Shots:** 1 (a locked poster; all motion is inside three embedded video windows, each with ~8–10 internal cuts) · **Avg shot:** n/a (inner clips ~0.8–1.3 s per cut) · **Music BPM:** ~144 · **VO:** Whisper returned no words. The on-screen copy in all three tapes follows the same motivational script ("pressure… reveals you… ideas… plans… someday project… until the timer starts… excuses go quiet… make it count"), so a shared, low-mixed VO/music track is likely. **One-line essence:** a frozen, print-perfect 1970s video-club poster (cream paper, huge condensed headline, retro stripes, terminal-style VOTE bar) holds three slightly tilted VHS-case "cartridges", each playing a different editor's cut of the same script in sync. The static frame makes the three moving windows the only thing to compare, so viewers rewatch and vote.

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** community contest / finalist showcase ("THE FINAL THREE / ORIGINAL EDITS"). One carousel slide of a vote post. The brand (EarnEdits) frames user-made edits as collectible tapes.
- **Beats (as seen inside the tapes; all three hit the same script moments within ~0.1–0.3 s):**
  - 0.0–1.6 **Hook:** "PRESSURE… RUIN YOU" → "IT REVEALS YOU" (footballer, man on a rock, Sisyphus-style man and boulder).
  - 1.6–3.4 **Setup:** men in suits; "What they could…" (KUNAL serif caption).
  - 3.5–6.3 **List:** IDEAS (3.5–4.6) → PLANS (4.8–5.4) → SOMEDAY PROJECT (5.6–6.7), shown in each tape as a big word or a framed picture.
  - 6.4–9.5 **Turn:** white flash (HAMMAD) → "Until the timer starts" (clock) / PENDING WORKS drawer / a wristwatch close-up → "EXCUSES GO QUIET" (8.1–9.6).
  - 9.6–11.3 **Payoff:** a big clock face, a man raising a paper, "MAKE IT COUNT" (left tape cuts to a black card at 10.77; middle holds the serif "Make it Count.").
  - 11.3–14.4 **Hold:** all three tapes sit frozen on their last frame; only the poster remains, as a "now vote" beat.
- **Hook mechanics:** frame 0 is the complete poster. The headline FRAME WAR (87% width, near-black on cream) reads in under 0.5 s, and three windows are already cutting (internal cuts at 0.03, 0.20, 0.27 s), so the eye bounces left→centre→right straight away. The red TOP 03 roundel is the only saturated warm disc on the page and pulls the eye to the "finalist" framing.
- **Script → visual rule (poster level):** the poster never animates. Every message is printed (title, subtitle, names, handles, CTA "SWIPE TO PLAY", VOTE bar) and the motion budget is spent on the content inside the windows.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** the outer frame has zero cuts. Inside the tapes, cuts come every ~0.8–1.5 s, and the list section (3.5–6.3 s) is the fastest at ~0.8 s per word. The last 3.1 s (21% of runtime) is fully still, which suits a carousel slide: the viewer either swipes or reads the vote options.
- **Easing signatures:** the poster has none. The tapes use hard cuts on beats, one defocus transition (~4 frames), one white flash (9 frames total: ~3 up, ~6 down, luma +8 on the *whole* frame, so the flash is bright enough to light the poster's average) and line-by-line serif reveals in KUNAL (~8 frames per line).
- **Frame-rate texture:** diff alternates high/low on every other frame for long stretches (e.g. 7.4–9.5 s). The embedded edits update at ~15 fps inside a 30 fps poster, which gives them a slightly "tape" cadence against the perfectly still print.
- **Camera:** none on the poster (net_zoom 1.5 over 432 frames = noise). The tapes contain their own slow pushes (KUNAL clock push 9.6–10.4).
- **Secondary motion:** none on the frame, on purpose. No idle wobble of the cases and no shine sweep.
- **Sync:**
  - Inner cuts land on strong onsets: 3.70 vs 3.67 (+0.03), 4.57 vs 4.51 (+0.06), 8.07 vs 7.96 (+0.10). The flash resolves on beat 6.66.
  - The cut cluster at 1.47–1.77 straddles beat 1.56.
  - The three editors hit the same script words within ~0.1–0.3 s of each other, because all three cut to one shared audio track. That makes the comparison fair and the poster readable as a set.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
The poster has no transitions. The cuts inside the windows are:
1. **Hard cut on onset** (3.70, 4.57, 8.07, 9.53): 1-frame cuts within ±0.1 s of a strong onset.
2. **Defocus dissolve** (KUNAL, 1.47–1.60): the subject blurs out over ~4 frames as the next shot appears.
3. **White flash** (HAMMAD, 6.33–6.63): the window blows out to white (3 frames up), then reveals the new scene (6 frames down). Rebuild: a white solid with opacity `[0,1,0]` over `[0,3,9]` frames, `Easing.out(Easing.quad)` on the decay.
4. **Cut to black end card** (AFTER VISION, 10.77): the content cuts to black with small glowing caps, so the left tape "ejects" first.
5. **Freeze hold** (11.3–14.4): every window stops on its last frame. In Remotion, clamp each `<OffthreadVideo>` to its own end, or use a `<Freeze>` after its duration.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Freeze the frame, move the content.** A 100% static, print-perfect layout makes the small video windows read as precious "exhibits". Motion is only where the viewer should look.
2. **Physical frames for digital clips.** Turning each 9:16 clip into a collectible object (VHS case with label colour, spine text, barcode, "FINALIST SERIES") adds narrative ("tapes") and credibility at no motion cost.
3. **One colour per contender.** Orange / teal / oxblood label bars carry through to the captions and VOTE order, so attribution is instant.
4. **Editorial hierarchy:** one giant ultra-condensed headline (87% width), one subtitle, one micro-label, one red roundel. Four sizes of type, all on a 38 px margin grid.
5. **Slight rotations (−4° / +1° / +3°) and soft shadows** stop a three-column grid from feeling like a spreadsheet. The centre card sits highest and on top.
6. **Same script, synced windows:** comparison content only works when the three clips run on one clock, so the eye can jump between windows at the same beat.
7. **Built-in CTA furniture:** "SWIPE TO PLAY →" plus a terminal-style VOTE bar gives a printed interface the viewer can act on (comment 1/2/3).
8. **End on a hold:** ~3 s of stillness after the content ends gives reading and voting time and creates a seamless loop.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1440` (3:4) at `30 fps`, duration = longest embedded clip + 90 frames hold (here 432 frames at 720×960 → scale all px values ×1.5).
- **Layer stack:** `<PaperBackground color="#dcd3b8" grain={0.04}>` → `<MarginTicks x={22} every={93}>` → `<Masthead left="EarnEdits.com" right="FRAME WAR / VIDEO CLUB" rule>` → `<Kicker>` → `<Headline text="FRAME WAR" font="Bebas Neue" fitWidth={0.875}>` (use `fitText` from `@remotion/layout-utils` to compute the font size for 945 px) → `<Rule weight={4}>` → `<Subtitle>` + `<Roundel color="#cf4420" top="TOP" big="03" d={130}>` → `<TapeRow>` → `<CreditsRow>` → `<CTA text="SWIPE TO PLAY" arrow>` → `<RetroStripes colors={["#d9632b","#b8322a","#e0a43a"]} h={6} gap={3}>` → `<VoteBar mono options={[...]}>`.
- **`<VHSCase>` props:** `{src, label, handle, index, color, rotate, z}`. Outer 308×495 px (at 1080 w), black bezel radius 6 px, top label bar 46 px in `color` with name (condensed bold 20 px), handle (12 px) and index right. Spine text uses `writing-mode: vertical-rl`, 10 px tracked. The inner `<OffthreadVideo>` is 225×400, `objectFit: cover`. The bottom cream label has a "VHS" box, "FINALIST SERIES" and a barcode SVG. Shadow: `filter: drop-shadow(9px 15px 20px rgba(33,35,26,.35))`. Transform `rotate(${rotate}deg)` with rotate = −4, 1, 3.
- **Sync:** put all three clips at `from={0}` with the shared audio as a separate `<Audio>`. Mute the videos. When `frame > clipDuration`, wrap each in `<Freeze frame={clipDuration-1}>` so it holds its last frame.
- **Optional (not in the reference, keep subtle):** a 20-frame entrance on carousel slide 1 only. Each case drops in with `spring({damping: 18, stiffness: 140})` from `translateY(40px) rotate(rotate*2)`, staggered 4 frames. Leave the headline static.
- **Typeface fallbacks:** headline Bebas Neue / Oswald 700; body Inter 600 / 400; VOTE bar Courier Prime or IBM Plex Mono 500.
