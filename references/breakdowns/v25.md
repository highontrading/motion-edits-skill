**One-line essence:** top-down "lab notebook" flat-lays of real product cut-outs with halftone and fringe finishing. Every idea gets one literal metaphor (iceberg = product vs problem, brain + syringes = injected fear, bottles as chart dots), labelled with yellow sticky notes and red ellipses, while a small serif subtitle pill reads out the VO underneath.

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** marketing-history micro-documentary ("surprising origin → struggle → the one change → mechanism → proof in numbers → CTA").
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–4.2 **Hook:** the mouthwash you own was once sold as a surgical antiseptic. An everyday object plus a surprising past.
  - 4.1–7.9 **Struggle:** a nested-shape zoom carries us into a Victorian apothecary set; for decades the product could not find a market.
  - 7.9–12.4 **Turn:** "they changed one thing". The iceberg metaphor: not the product (tip) but the problem (underwater).
  - 12.4–19.8 **Mechanism:** a medical term (halitosis newspaper) → a social fear (brain + syringes) → live-action payoff (a man in a crowded lift, a woman covering her nose).
  - 19.8–26.9 **Proof:** a line chart made of bottles; $100,000 → $4 million; "In just six years" on a blank card.
  - 26.9–31.9 **CTA:** "Editors, grab the After Effects project file" → yellow EARNEDITS.COM end card.
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** frame 0 is already a composed flat-lay (bottle, sticky note "Mouth Fre…" typing on, subtitle pill). In the first 1.07 s the **hero bottle swaps 5 times** (blue Cool Mint → pink → orange Original → purple Total Care → white Sensitive, one swap every 4 frames = 0.27 s) while the toothbrush, Marvis tube and petri dish ease in with a scale settle (zoom +3.1 → 0 over 14 frames). The rapid product flicker is the scroll-stopper.
- **Script → visual rule:** one clause = one set. The clause's key noun becomes a physical prop on the desk (syringe, petri dish, newspaper, brain, calculator), and its keyword becomes either a yellow sticky note in handwriting or a big serif word with a red pen ellipse. The full spoken text is always available in the small subtitle pill.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** top-down **flat-lay on grid paper** (5 of 13 shots), plus three full-frame illustrated "dioramas" (apothecary, iceberg, brain) and one live-action clip. Everything is a cut-out photo with a soft contact shadow, finished with halftone dots and colour fringing, so stock photos read as printed collage.
- **Backgrounds & textures:** pale grey grid paper (cell ≈ 60 px at 720 w) for the lab desk and CTA desk; dotted notebook paper for the chart desk; damask wallpaper + bookshelves + wood table (apothecary); black paper sky with halftone clouds and moon over a teal sea (iceberg); a blank white card with heavy vignette for the "six years" type; a flat mustard field for the end card. Each new idea gets a new surface.
- **Colour:** desaturated cool greys dominate (`#dbdbdb`, `#c8c7c8`, `#b0afb1`; saturation 10–17/255 on the desk shots). The one recurring accent is **sticky-note yellow** (`#f2d43a` by eye; desk palettes pick it up as `#ae9a37`/`#b6ab34`), echoed by the highlighter, the brain and the end card (`#d7a52a` centre → `#735c18` vignette). A secondary accent is **pen red** (`#c8323a`-ish) for ellipses and "Social Fear" (maroon `#9b2c3a`). Dark scenes are teal-black (`#101e20`, `#2b4d56`) or brown (`#4c392f`, `#6e5542`). The shape-zoom transition is the only fully saturated moment: orange `#e9a050`, red `#cf4552`, teal `#58776d`, peach `#e5bf9f`, violet `#6b3df0`, white (sat jumps 14 → 161).
- **Typography (four voices):**
  - **Subtitle pill** (always on): a semibold text serif (Source Serif 4 / Newsreader class), sentence case, white on a dark rounded pill (`#2a2a2a` at ~80%, radius ~4 px, padding ~6×10 px). Cap height ≈ 20 px at 720 w (font ≈ 26 px = 3.6% of width). Centred at **y ≈ 76%**. It shows 2–4-word chunks that change with the VO.
  - **Handwritten sticky notes:** a casual marker hand (Gochi Hand / Patrick Hand class), dark green-black on yellow paper rectangles rotated −3…+5°, with a soft drop shadow. Text writes on left to right.
  - **Editorial serif headline:** a bold transitional serif with tight negative tracking (Newsreader Bold / Libre Caslon class), near-black `#1c1c1c`. "$4 million" ≈ 40% of width; "In just six years" ≈ 63% of width, on the vertical centre.
  - **Brand pixel type:** chunky pixel/stencil caps with ragged edges ("EDITORS", "EARNEDITS"), black on the desk or white in black boxes on the end card. "EARNEDITS" ≈ 45% of width.
  - **Accents:** a maroon bouncy hand-lettered script ("Social Fear", three sizes); a white marker scrawl over the live-action clip ("convincing people", "that bad breath", ≈ 32% width at y ≈ 56%).
  - **Emphasis rules:** the keyword is circled with a hand-drawn red (or graphite) ellipse, highlighted with a yellow marker swipe (HALITOSIS), or placed on a sticky note. Weight is never used for emphasis; the device is.
- **Layout:** flat-lay props hug the frame edges (pens, ruler, keyboard, calculator cropped at corners); the hero is centred at y ≈ 35–55%; the subtitle pill stays fixed at y ≈ 76%. The upper 15% and lower 15% are mostly props, not text.
- **Assets:** real product packshots (Listerine variants, Marvis), a syringe, petri dish with droplets, an antique apothecary bottle, an iceberg photo, apothecary bottles floating underwater, a vintage halitosis newspaper page with a woman-covering-mouth ad, a highlighter, an anatomical brain with a blue vein overlay, DNA helices, cut-out hands, a stock/AI elevator clip, a ruler, BIC pens, a calculator, a keyboard, a monitor, a tablet.
- **Finishing / FX:** a fine **halftone dot screen** over all imagery (visible on the bottle and clouds in key frames); **chromatic aberration** fringes (red/blue) on high-contrast edges; strong **vignette** (edges ~35% darker); film grain; soft contact shadows; deliberate **depth of field** (foreground sticky notes and DNA strands blurred).

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** ~0.41 cuts/s; shots 1.2–5.3 s, median ~2.0 s. The fastest beat is the iceberg's three framings in 4.5 s (1.9 / 1.2 / 1.5 s). The longest holds are the chart (5.3 s, kept alive by a camera pan) and the hook (4.1 s, kept alive by a 5-swap flicker and a tilt). Every shot has internal motion; no shot is a dead still except the "six years" card.
- **Easing signatures (measured):**
  - **Settles** after a cut or a pop: decaying zoom/pan over 6–14 frames (0.4–0.9 s) with no sign flip, i.e. a critically damped ease-out (no overshoot).
  - **Accelerating exits into a cut:** five shots end with a zoom-out that ramps over 7–9 frames (e.g. −0.3 → −6.3) and then continues at full speed on the first frames of the next shot before decaying. This is a **velocity-matched zoom cut**: ease-in on the outgoing shot, ease-out on the incoming shot, peak velocity at the cut.
  - **Camera moves inside a shot:** asymmetric ease-in-out, 7 frames to peak, 7–14 frames to settle (tilt at 1.9 s, desk pan at 22.9 s).
  - **Write-ons:** ellipses take 4–8 frames (0.27–0.53 s); highlighter swipe ~4 frames; typewriter 1–1.75 chars/frame at 15 fps.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:** rapid product swap (4 frames per variant), scale-settle of props, sticky note write-on (handwriting L→R), duplicate-note swarm at several depths, nested-shape zoom, iris open, paper slide-up, hands slide-in from the edge, line chart draw-on with bottle pops, serif typewriter, ellipse/highlighter write-on, card drop-on, logo pop + drift.
- **Camera:** a virtual camera over flat artwork. There are slow push-ins (+0.4–0.8/frame at 180 px analysis width), a tilt (1.9 s), a lateral desk pan (22.9 s), punch-in/punch-out reframes on one set (9.73 / 10.93) and the accelerating zoom-outs into cuts. Net zooms per shot are small (−38 … +16) because moves are short and sharp.
- **Secondary motion:** the levitating bottle bobs; bottles drift underwater; the syringe fills; DNA strands sway; the live-action clip has its own handheld jitter; the end-card logo drifts.
- **Sync:**
  - **Cuts vs VO:** cuts sit within ±0.25 s of the phrase they illustrate: 5.33 vs "Listerine" 5.44 (−0.11), 9.73 vs "Not" 9.76 (−0.03), 10.93 vs "but" 10.88 (+0.05), 12.40 vs "They" 12.46 (−0.06), 15.07 vs "social" 15.00 (+0.07), 19.80 vs "Sales" 20.04 (−0.24), 25.07 vs "just" 25.28 (−0.21), 29.60 vs "earnedits" 29.82 (−0.22). Two late cuts (17.07, 0.37 s into "people"; 26.93, 0.51 s into "Editors") hold the previous image until its word finishes.
  - **Text vs VO:** the subtitle pill changes on the chunk boundary (±0.1 s). Sticky-note and serif typing track the words (+0.05…+0.3 s). The exception is "$4 million", which types 0.3–0.5 s **before** it is spoken, as a visual "setup".
  - **Music:** cuts are not beat-locked (mean |offset| to the 112 BPM grid ≈ 0.16 s, about what chance gives). Cuts at 7.87, 10.93, 12.40, 15.07, 19.80 and 26.93 fall within 0.2 s of strong onsets, which are probably whoosh SFX placed on the transitions.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Nested-shape zoom** (4.07–5.33, 19 frames). Flat polygons in saturated colours, each born as a dot in the centre of the previous one and scaled to overfill the frame in ~4 frames, then replaced. The sequence ends with a circle that contains the next scene and opens like an iris. Rebuild: an array of `{shape, colour}`; each child starts at scale 0.02, scales with `Math.pow(60, t)` over 4 frames (exponential = constant perceived speed), rotation ±10°. Mount the next scene inside an SVG `clipPath` circle whose radius grows from 40 px to the frame diagonal over 3–4 frames.
2. **Velocity-matched zoom-out cut** (7.87, 15.07, 17.07, and 4.07 into the shapes; the 25.07 cut ramps out but lands on a static card). The outgoing shot zooms out with ease-in (7–9 frames), the cut lands at peak velocity and the incoming shot continues zooming out with ease-out (6–12 frames). Rebuild: scale `1 → 0.82` with `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)` on A, then `1.18 → 1` with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)` on B; add a 4–8 px radial blur on the 2 frames around the cut.
3. **Punch-in / punch-out reframe** (9.73, 10.93): a hard jump between a wide and a close framing of the same artwork, followed by a short drift settle (8–10 frames). Use it for "not X, but Y" contrasts.
4. **Zoom-blur entry into footage** (17.07): the footage starts at ~2.5× scale on a detail and pulls back to 1× in 4 frames with radial blur. The handwritten caption arrives smeared by the same blur.
5. **Paper slide-up** (12.40): a newspaper page slides up from below and decelerates (peak 3 frames in, 12-frame settle) while its bottom edge casts a dark shadow band.
6. **Camera pan to a new area of the same desk** (22.13–22.93): this replaces a cut. The chart leaves the frame left and the calculator enters top-right.
7. **Hard cut to a blank type card** (25.07) and **hard cut to a flat colour end card** (29.60): this is the only "clean" grammar, saved for the big number and the brand.

## 6. Sound design
A soft, steady music bed (~112 BPM; RMS 0.13) under a close, calm VO with almost no pauses (the longest gap is 0.6 s, at 12.0). Strong onsets at 7.87, 11.03, 12.31/12.45, 15.19, 17.5, 19.6 and 26.75 coincide with zoom transitions, which suggests whoosh/swish SFX on every velocity-matched cut. The typing sections (22.9–23.5, 25.1–26.1, 27.7–29.1) probably carry pen/typing clicks. The end card has a brand sting near 29.6–30.6 (onsets at 29.33, 30.02, 30.60).

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **One metaphor per idea, built as a physical set.** An iceberg for "product vs problem", syringes into a brain for "injected fear", product bottles as chart dots. The viewer understands the abstract idea from a single picture.
2. **Two-tier text system.** A small constant subtitle pill carries the full script, so the art-directed type can show only 1–3 keywords per scene (sticky note, ellipse, big serif number).
3. **Restricted accent palette:** desaturated grey desk + sticky-note yellow + pen red. Save one burst of full saturation for the transition (the shape zoom).
4. **Velocity-matched zoom cuts.** Ramp the outgoing move into the cut and let the incoming shot carry the same speed out. Four of 12 transitions work this way (4.07, 7.87, 15.07, 17.07), plus the pan-matched punch-out at 10.93, so the edit feels like one continuous camera.
5. **Print finishing on everything** (halftone, colour fringe, vignette, grain, soft shadows) makes mixed stock sources (packshots, AI footage, illustrations) look like one printed collage.
6. **Duplicate-at-depth swarm:** the same sticky note at 3 depths with DOF blur turns one line of text into a feeling of pressure ("everyone was saying it") at almost no cost.
7. **Data as props:** the chart's points are the product, scaled to value, and the number arrives as a typed serif with a red ellipse. It feels like editorial infographics, not dashboard UI.
8. **Write-ons at 4–8 frames:** ellipses, highlights and handwriting draw in at human-pen speed, never instantly and never slowly.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps` (double the reference's frame counts), duration = VO + 0.5 s. Drive everything from a VO word-timestamp JSON.
- **Typical scene stack (bottom → top):** `<SurfaceBG texture="grid|dots|damask|nightPaper" />` → `<Cutout src shadow={{blur:18, y:10, opacity:.35}} />` props → `<StickyNote text rotate={-3} />` / `<SerifHeadline />` → `<PenEllipse />` / `<HighlighterSwipe />` → `<CameraRig>` wrapping all of the above (scale/translate/rotate) → `<HalftoneOverlay dot={3} opacity={.18} />` → `<ChromaticFringe px={2} />` → `<Vignette strength={.35} />` → `<Grain opacity={.07} />` → `<SubtitlePill />` (outside the camera rig, so it never moves).
- **Components & timings (30 fps):**
  - `SubtitlePill({chunks})`: chunks of 2–4 words from the VO timestamps, swap on chunk start (hard swap, no animation); Source Serif 4 SemiBold 38 px on `rgba(30,30,30,.8)`, radius 6, padding `8px 14px`, centred at `top: 76%`.
  - `ProductSwap({srcs, framesEach: 8})`: hard swaps between packshots (0.27 s each), landing on the hero.
  - `StickyNote({text, startFrame})`: yellow `#f2d43a` rectangle, rotate −5…+5°, `box-shadow 0 6px 12px rgba(0,0,0,.25)`; text reveals by a `clip-path: inset(0 X% 0 0)` wipe at ~2 chars/frame; Gochi Hand 48 px `#22301f`.
  - `NoteSwarm({text, layers:[{scale:2.2, blur:10},{scale:1, blur:0},{scale:.45, blur:2}]})`: each layer pops in with `spring({damping:14, stiffness:220, mass:.6})` staggered 1–2 frames.
  - `PenEllipse({w,h,color:'#c8323a', frames:12})`: an SVG ellipse path drawn slightly more than once (overshooting end, 370°) via `strokeDashoffset` with `Easing.inOut(Easing.quad)`; stroke 3–4 px, slight wobble (perturb rx/ry ±3%).
  - `HighlighterSwipe({frames:8})`: `#f5e04a` at 70% with `mix-blend-mode: multiply`, `scaleX 0→1` from the left.
  - `NestedShapeZoom({shapes, framesEach:8})`: see §5.1 (8 frames each at 30 fps).
  - `IrisOpen({frames:6})`: `clipPath` circle r 0 → 1.2×diagonal, `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)`; add a 2 px red/blue offset stroke on the rim.
  - `VelocityZoomCut({outFrames:16, inFrames:20, scaleOut:0.82, scaleIn:1.18})`: see §5.2.
  - `LineChartProps({points, drawFrames:30})`: SVG polyline with `strokeDashoffset`; at the frame the line reaches node *i*, mount a cut-out bottle with `spring({damping:12, stiffness:200})` scale 0 → `size_i` (size proportional to value).
  - `SerifTypewriter({text, cps:30})`: Newsreader Bold, `letter-spacing:-0.03em`, `#1c1c1c`; at 30 fps 1 char/frame; follow word timestamps when spoken.
  - `HandheldFootage({src, shake:{amp:6, freq:4}})` plus `ZoomBlurIn({fromScale:2.5, frames:8})` for live-action inserts; caption in a marker font, centred at y ≈ 56%.
  - `EndCard({word:'EARNEDITS', suffix:'.COM'})`: mustard radial gradient `#e2b030 → #735c18`, pixel font in black boxes, pop with `spring({damping:10, stiffness:180})`, then drift −15 px over 16 frames; suffix pops on its word.
- **Tricky effects:** the halftone overlay is easiest as a pre-rendered dot-screen PNG in `mix-blend-mode: multiply` at 15–20%, or an SVG `feTurbulence`-free pattern of circles. Chromatic aberration: render the scene three times with `mix-blend-mode: screen`, R/B channels offset ±2 px (or one SVG `feColorMatrix` + `feOffset` filter). The underwater tint on the iceberg is a teal multiply layer masked below the waterline.

## 9. Variation ideas
1. **Consumer-psychology series** ("How De Beers made diamonds 'forever'"): same flat-lay grid desk, product swap hook, iceberg or scale metaphors, sticky notes for the old vs new framing, a bottle-dot chart becomes a ring-dot chart.
2. **Health/science explainer** ("Why sugar was blamed on fat"): lab desk with petri dishes and syringes, newspaper-clipping evidence with highlighter swipes, a brain/organ hero with handwritten labels; swap pen red for clinical blue.
3. **SaaS growth story** ("How Slack grew without sales"): replace packshots with app icons on the desk, shape zoom in the brand palette, a chart whose points are logo cut-outs, and the type card for the headline metric; end card in the brand colour with the URL suffix pop.
