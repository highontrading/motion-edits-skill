# v10 — Costco's free samples: the reciprocity principle (prop-collage + statement-card explainer)

**One-line essence:** every sentence becomes either a single-prop "poster" (a hero object on a seamless or textured set, with a stacked keyword the object physically sits in front of) or a black card with white/orange focus-pull type. The props are chosen as literal puns (eggs as the O's, a cloche lift, a bagged stack of cash, a shopping cart you're pushing), so the visual explains the line before you read it.

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** consumer-psychology explainer ("surprising business fact → it's not X, it's science → named principle → mechanism → scale/proof → reframe → CTA").
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–6.5 **Hook:** Costco gives away millions in free food every day, "and it's not generosity".
  - 6.5–12.6 **Authority/number:** "It's science"; free samples can lift sales "up to 2,000%".
  - 12.6–17.1 **Turn + named principle:** the psychology isn't about the food → "RECIPROCITY".
  - 17.1–25.7 **Mechanism:** a free gift → your brain registers a debt → unconscious obligation to return the favour.
  - 25.7–31.4 **Concrete example:** one cube of cheese → you walk out with something you never planned to buy.
  - 31.4–39.8 **Scale proof:** the programme is tied to ~$46B a year = 17% of revenue.
  - 39.8–44.9 **Reframe/moral:** the most powerful word in marketing was never SALE, it was always FREE.
  - 44.9–50.35 **CTA:** "Editors get the project file… earnedits.com" over a phone showing the site.
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** frame 0 is a grey-scale Costco storefront at dusk (an empty parking lot, store far away). On the first steps (0.1–0.47 s) red letter **shards fly together** above the roof and lock into COSTCO / GIVES / AWAY, a three-line red stack whose bottom line is clipped by the roof. A constant push-in runs underneath (~+1 zoom per step). At 1.1 s a paper tape label "MILLIONS OF DOLLARS" flutters down onto the top line in 3 steps while the push gets a velocity bump (1.0–1.8 s, peak at 1.27 s). The first second gives you colour pop on grey, assembling type and camera motion.
- **Script → visual rule:** one sentence = one scene. The **noun** becomes a hero prop or set (storefront, cloche platter, word-search grid, glass display case, laptop dialog, cheese, cart, cash). The **key phrase** becomes stacked heavy type layered *behind* that prop. Abstract or pivot sentences ("It's science", "Your brain registers a debt", "17%", "It was always free") drop to a black card with one orange keyword.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** 0.42 cuts/s. Shots are longer than in v09 (median ~2.2 s): prop scenes 2.1–3.7 s, black cards 1.0–2.3 s, CTA 5.5 s. Pace is kept up by *internal* animation (hands, cloche lift, ribbons, typing, the cart shove) rather than by cutting.
- **Frame cadence:** graphics and camera are stepped at ~12 fps. The **typewriter UI (shot 12) and the Costco-photo pan update every frame**, so UI elements look "live" against the stop-motion collage.
- **Easing signatures (measured):**
  - *Prop entrances* (cart, bag, cheese, phone): peak velocity in the first 1–4 steps, then exponential decay. Cart: 0.6 s total (`Easing.out(Easing.exp)`, ~18 frames @30). Cheese: ~0.35 s ramp + ~0.8 s decay (`Easing.bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1)`, 36 frames). Phone: 1.5 s+ tail (`Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`, 45 frames). **No overshoot**: zero sign flips in pan/zoom on any entrance.
  - *Camera pans onto photos:* start at full speed on the cut frame (≈51 px/frame) and decay with a ~0.25 s half-life, i.e. the cut lands mid-whip and the shot settles. Equivalent to `spring({damping: 200, stiffness: 120})` on translateX starting ~350 px offset (at 1080 w).
  - *Ambient push:* constant ~0.5–1%/step scale on most holds, plus a mid-shot ease-in-out "bump" when a secondary element lands (shot 1, 1.0–1.8 s).
  - *Exits from black cards:* an accelerating push or smear over 3–5 steps (`Easing.in(Easing.cubic)`, 10–14 frames) straight into a hard cut.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:** shard-assemble letters, paper-tape label drop, overexposed focus-pull reveal, hand-in-frame prop reveal (cloche lift), ink-blot luma matte, highlighter bar wipe, focus-pull word-by-word, 3D letters dropping onto an object, chart draw-on with arrowhead, swinging ribbon bands in/out, caret → box-grow text layer with handles, sticker build, typewriter dialog, slide-in-behind-object type, throw-up prop with rotation and rim glow, POV shove, label-box type-on, progress-ring draw, pixel-dissolve assemble, snap-up blur exit.
- **Camera:** gentle push-ins on most sets (shot 1 +24.8, shot 9 +12.6), trucks on the laptop (−150 px) and the hall (−64 px), a pull-back on the reveal shots (shot 2 −17.5, CTA −22.5), and a punch-in cut (11 → 12) instead of a zoom.
- **Secondary motion:** butler hands, slow notebook roll, cheese rim-light shimmer, demo clips playing on the CTA phone, dust and grain flicker.
- **Sync:** cuts follow **VO word onsets**: median |cut − word| = 0.046 s, 17 of 21 within 0.10 s. The median distance to the nearest beat is 0.10 s with a 0.44 s beat period, which is chance level, so the cuts are not beat-synced. The first word of every card appears at its spoken onset (IT'S +0.11, YOUR +0.05). Later words are front-loaded by 0.3–1.0 s (DEBT on screen at 20.55 vs spoken 21.20; the notebook sticker is complete 1.1 s before "free"), so the full phrase holds for reading. Typed UI text (dialog, label) is the exception and tracks the VO word for word.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Hard cut on the word** (most cuts): cut frame = word onset ± 2 frames.
2. **Glare cut** (2.10 s): cut to an overexposed, defocused frame that resolves in ~0.5 s. Rebuild: `brightness(1.8) blur(14px) scale(1.12)` → `brightness(1) blur(0) scale(1)` over 15 frames, ease-out.
3. **Ink-blot luma matte** (4.77–5.3 s): 2 steps of flat red, then irregular black blobs grow and the next scene shows through the red "holes". The blobs then shrink to a dark centre spot and disappear (~16 frames total). Rebuild: animate an ink-spread video or a turbulence-displaced SVG mask (`feTurbulence` + `feDisplacementMap` on growing circles) as an `alpha` mask on the incoming scene.
4. **Ribbon swing-through** (12.61–15.35 s): curved bands enter with heavy motion blur and later exit the same way, leaving an empty set for 0.3 s before the cut. This is an object wipe built from the type itself.
5. **Punch-in cut** (23.02 s): a jump from a medium to a close framing of the same layout, with the background type now cropped and defocused. Simulates a rack zoom without animating one.
6. **Accelerate-and-smear exit** (38.10, 39.77, 41.94 s): the card pushes in or slides with increasing speed for 3–5 steps; the last 1–2 frames are horizontally smeared, then a hard cut.
7. **Pixel-dissolve swap** (42.0, 43.2 s): the line breaks into or assembles from white blocky fragments in 2–3 steps. Rebuild: a mask of random 6–10 px squares with per-cell random thresholds, animated 0→1 over 8 frames.

## 6. Sound design
- A ~136 BPM bed (upbeat, light) under a steady narrator, with clear 0.2–0.6 s pauses between sentences; cuts fall in those pauses.
- Strong onsets coincide with cuts within ±0.1 s at 4.77, 6.51, 19.59, 38.10, 39.77, 41.94 and 44.88 s, which suggests whoosh/impact SFX on most black-card cuts. Clusters at 3.1–3.8 s (cloche lift), 10.1–11.3 s (chart arrow, TWO THOUSAND PERCENT), 14.2–14.6 s (ribbons) and 37.5–38.6 s (ring draw and whip) indicate SFX on the hero actions. Likely typing clicks under the dialog (23–25.5 s) and the Costco label (31.5–33.5 s).

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Pun-level prop choice:** pick the object that *is* the sentence (eggs as the O's in FOOD, a cloche lift for "free food", a bagged cash brick for "$46 billion", a POV cart for "you're walking out"). The viewer gets it before they read it.
2. **Sandwich type behind the prop** in almost every poster (store roof, cloche, cash bag, cheese, cart rim). It adds depth and makes the type feel physical.
3. **Black-card pivots with one orange word** turn abstract sentences into rhythm breaks. They cost nothing to build and reset the eye between busy collages.
4. **Front-load the phrase:** sync the first word to the VO, then build the rest quickly so the full line holds 0.5–1 s before the speaker finishes it.
5. **Decelerate-only entrances:** props and cameras hit peak speed in the first 1–4 frames and settle exponentially, with no bounce. This reads as weighty and expensive.
6. **Mix cadences on purpose:** collage at 12 fps, UI typing at the full frame rate. The UI feels live and the collage feels handmade.
7. **One accent colour across the whole video** (orange) plus a different set colour per scene keeps variety high and the brand consistent.
8. **Design-tool and office metaphors** (Figma selection handles, an Apple alert dialog, a notebook sticker, a word search) make psychology concepts feel like everyday interfaces.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, duration = VO + 0.4 s. A `<Stepped fps={12}>` wrapper for collage/graphic layers; UI typing and photo pans run unstepped.
- **Typical poster stack (bottom → top):** `<Seamless color vignette={0.45}>` or `<TexturedPaper tint>` → `<KeywordStack lines font="heavy" color behind>` → `<PropCutout src entrance="throw|shove|drop|slide" shadow rimGlow?>` → optional `<ForegroundHands>` → `<HalftoneOverlay opacity={0.1}>` → `<Grain opacity={0.08}>` → `<DustSpecks density={0.3}>`.
- **Components & timings (frames @30):**
  - `ShardAssemble({text, frames: 11})`: split glyphs into 4–6 polygon shards via `clip-path`; each shard starts at a random offset (±200 px, ±40°) and lands with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`, staggered by 1 frame.
  - `GlareReveal({frames: 15})`: see transition 2.
  - `FocusPullWord({startFrame})`: `blur(9px)→0`, `opacity .35→1`, `scale 1.08→1`, 12 frames, ease-out. In `StatementCard({words, accentIndex, accent:'#e8730c'})`, word i appears at `min(onset_i, onset_0 + i*7)`, so the line finishes early.
  - `PropThrow({from:'bottom', rotate:[-25,0], frames: 36, ease: Easing.bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1), rimGlow:'#ffb347'})`.
  - `PovShove({frames: 18, ease: Easing.out(Easing.exp), fromY: 45%})`.
  - `PhotoWhipSettle`: `translateX = 350 * (1 - spring({frame, fps, config:{damping:200, stiffness:120}}))`, no blur after frame 3.
  - `RibbonBand({text, color, pathD, swingIn: 10, swingOut: 8})`: SVG thick stroke path + `<textPath>`. Rotate the group −40°→0 around an off-screen pivot with motion blur (`feGaussianBlur stdDeviation="12 0"` while the angular velocity is high).
  - `TextLayerBox({text, handleColor:'#f0a020'})`: a caret (2 frames) → box width 0→100% (6 frames) → text fades in (4 frames) → handle dots appear.
  - `TypewriterDialog({text, cps: 10, style:'appleAlert'})`: unstepped, synced to the word timestamps.
  - `ProgressRing({value: 0.17, frames: 15, color:'#e8a00c', track:'#4a2a05'})`: SVG `strokeDashoffset`, starting at −90°.
  - `InkMatteTransition({frames: 16})`, `PixelDissolve({cell: 8, frames: 8})`.
  - `CutMatCTA({phoneSrc, title:'EDITORS', sub:'GET THE PROJECT FILE'})`: the phone enters from `x+60%, rotate 12°` → centre over 45 frames ease-out cubic.
- **Tricky bits:** for behind-object type, render the prop twice: the full cut-out above the type, and (if needed) a hand-painted occlusion mask so only part of the prop overlaps the letters (the cart rim covers WALKING but the basket doesn't cover the inner text). Object-as-letter: set the word with a transparent "O" (or render the letters individually) and absolutely position the egg PNGs at the O glyph centres, measured with `measureText` / `@remotion/layout-utils`.
