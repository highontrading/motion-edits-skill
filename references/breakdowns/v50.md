## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** 17 scenes in 42 s (0.4 cuts/s, median ~2.4 s). The pace is slow and luxurious compared with typical Shorts, but every shot contains **internal** motion (swing, slide, whip, type), so nothing is static for more than ~1 s. Fastest stretch: 20–28 s (whips every ~1.3 s).
- **Easing signatures (per_frame data):**
  - **Exits accelerate into the cut, entrances decelerate out of it**, in the same direction. Roll into the 12.23 cut: rot −0.8 → −3.7°/frame over 10 frames. Tilt into 14.23: py −0.5 → −2.6, then the incoming continues −3.2 and decays over 12 frames. Rotate into 28.67: r −0.3 → −0.75, then the incoming −1.5 decaying.
  - **Zoom landings are long ease-outs:** 20.53–22.0 ramps in 6 frames to −3.6/frame, then decays over ~40 frames. The Munch drop-in peaks at frame 4 and decays over 15.
  - **Whips are symmetric ease-in-out** of ~18–20 frames (23.4–24.1, 25.27–25.9) with a peak of ~56 px/frame real and directional blur.
  - **Physical objects use under-damped springs.** The pendulum swing has ~1.3 s period, amplitude 35° → 8° after one half-swing, settled in ~2.2 s. The magnifier pill pops with a small overshoot.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:**

  - Per-character type-on (1892, THE SCREAM, Paint, EDITORS, URL: 2–4 frames per char).

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Smoke reveal** (0–0.75 s): a full-frame smoke plate translates up and out (and fades) over the scene. Rebuild: a smoke video/PNG sequence with `translateY(0 → -110%)` over 22 frames, `Easing.inOut(Easing.cubic)`, and a scene luma ramp from +60% exposure to normal.
2. **Defocus out + hard cut on beat** (2.50): the outgoing shot blurs 0 → 8 px over 3 frames.
3. **Drop-in layer** (4.13): the new scene arrives at 115% scale and blurred, settling to 100% over ~15 frames on top of the previous set.
4. **Zoom-through blur** (5.87–6.10): the outgoing scales up ~+3%/frame (ease-in, 7 frames) with growing blur, then the next set is revealed.
5. **Lamp-bloom white-out** (9.2–9.45): exposure ramps up from the light source (luma +100, saturation → 0 over 8 frames), then resolves into the greyscale WWII set. Rebuild: radial white gradient from the lamp position, opacity 0 → 1 over 8 frames + `saturate(0)`; the incoming fades from white over 6 frames.
6. **Motion-matched roll/tilt/rotate cut** (12.23, 14.23, 28.67): the outgoing accelerates (ease-in, 8–10 frames) and the cut lands at peak velocity. The incoming starts at that velocity and decays (ease-out, 12–20 frames).
7. **Hard cut dark → white** (20.17): a 1-frame luma jump of 190 diff for a "new chapter" reset, followed by a 0.35 s empty hold before content arrives.
8. **Whip pan between grid columns** (22.6, 23.4–24.1, 25.3–25.9): ~18–20 frames, ease-in-out, horizontal motion blur.
9. **Match cut on the hero object** (31.80): the painting keeps the same size and position while the whole environment changes (green mat → blue room).
10. **Shrink-to-light** (33.83–34.0): the entire frame scales down to a small square with a white glowing border (~6 frames), then collapses to a star point and the void remains.
11. **Blur-collapse** (36.4–36.5): all words blur and shrink to dots in 4 frames, then cut.
12. **Pill morph** (39.73–40.0): a white capsule covers the headline and stretches into the search bar.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **One hero object, many sets.** Keep the subject constant and change the room, light and surface each sentence. It feels like a museum tour, not a slideshow.
2. **Stage facts as physical events.** A frame that swings on a nail, versions that slide onto a wall and a magnifier that beams out a detail turn information into action.
3. **Give each set its own typeface.** Use a red italic didone for emotion, heavy rounded caps for titles, a serif italic for "storyteller" asides and a clean grotesk for labels. Each set gets one face and one colour.
4. **Pin abstract words onto the image** where they mean something (Fear on the sky, Anxiety on the figure).
5. **Cut on the beat, at peak velocity, and continue the motion** in the next shot (roll → roll, tilt → tilt).
6. **Under-damped springs for hung or placed objects** (~1.3 s period, 2–3 visible swings) give a tactile realism that keyframed eases lack.
7. **Unify mixed sources with a global finish:** halftone dots + grain + vignette + per-set grade, so AI renders, archival photos and UI icons sit in one world.
8. **Use silence and darkness as punctuation:** a near-black single-word card before the "strange fact", and a void with scattered words for the thesis.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** 1080×1920, 30 fps, duration = VO + ~0.3 s. Put a global `<HalftoneGrainVignette>` on top: an SVG dot-pattern overlay at 6 px pitch, `mix-blend-mode: soft-light`, opacity 0.25; animated grain at 0.06; a radial vignette from 0 → 55% black at the edges.
- **Typical scene stack:** `<SetPlate src grade>` (AI still with a slow `scale 1.00 → 1.06` over the shot) → `<LightRig>` (radial-gradient lamp pool, optional cone with `filter: blur(30px)`, drifting flare orbs) → `<FramedArtwork src frame="gold|thin" shadow>` → `<AnchoredLabel>`s → `<HeroType>` → global overlay.
- **Components & timings:**
  - `PendulumSwingIn`: `rotate = spring({frame, fps, config: {mass: 1, stiffness: 23, damping: 4}, from: -35, to: 0})`, with `transform-origin: 50% -8%` (the nail above the frame); translateY from −40 px with the same spring; settles in ~66 frames.
  - `DropInLayer`: `scale 1.15 → 1`, blur 12 → 0 px, ease-out cubic over 15 frames.
  - `TypeOnChars`: `{text, framesPerChar: 2-4, order: 'ltr'|'random'}`, each char pops with opacity 0 → 1 and scale 1.2 → 1 in 2 frames. Use `random` for THE SCREAM.
  - `UnderlineWipe`: a red bar with `scaleX 0 → 1`, `transform-origin: left`, 6 frames, `Easing.out(Easing.quad)`.
  - `TornSticker`: an SVG polygon with jagged edges (yellow `#ffe23a`), text in red; reveal via `clipPath: inset(0 100% 0 0) → inset(0 0 0 0)` over 6 frames.
  - `AnchoredLabel`: `{text, x, y}` relative to the artwork, so the label follows the artwork's transform; pop at VO word start − 3 frames: scale 0.85 → 1 with `spring({damping: 14, stiffness: 200})`.
  - `SingleWordCard`: words from the VO timestamps, each replacing the last, ±2-frame crossfade, Playfair Display Bold Italic.
  - `MagnifierCallout`: `{source: {x,y,w,h}, target: {x,y,w,h}, zoom: 4}`. Draw the small outlined capsule on the art, a trapezoid SVG polygon from the source edges to the target edges (`fill: rgba(255,30,30,.45)`), and a big capsule (`border-radius: 999px`, white 3 px border, `box-shadow: 0 0 24px #ff2a2a`) holding a cropped, scaled, `grayscale(1) contrast(1.4)` copy of the source region. Grow it over 16 frames with `spring({damping: 12, stiffness: 140})`.
  - `BlockCursorTypewriter`: per word, a white rect at the word's bbox appears for 2 frames, then shrinks to the right (`scaleX 1 → 0`, origin right, 3 frames) revealing the word; baseline jitter `y = (seed % 3 - 1) * 6px`.
  - `MotionMatchedCut`: the outgoing value `v(t) = v0 * Easing.in(Easing.cubic)(t)` over 10 frames; the incoming starts at the same velocity and decays with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)` over 15 frames. Apply to rotate, translateY or scale.
  - `WhipPan`: one wide canvas holding the 2×2 grid, `translateX` with `Easing.inOut(Easing.sin)` over 18 frames + horizontal blur proportional to velocity.
  - `ShrinkToLight`: wrap the scene, `scale 1 → 0.12` over 6 frames (ease-in), `outline: 6px solid white; box-shadow: 0 0 60px 20px rgba(255,255,255,.8)`, then `scale → 0` while a star sprite flashes for 3 frames.
  - `WordCloud`: words placed by seeded polar coordinates (r 120–380 px), each fading in at VO + 9 frames; the container rotates −0.06°/frame and scales 1 → 0.85; exit: blur 0 → 10 px and `scale → 0.2` in 4 frames.
  - `SearchBarCTA`: a pill morph `width 160 → 620 px` in 8 frames (spring, no overshoot), URL typed at 3 frames/char, a magnifier button popping in at char 4.
- **Tricky bits:**
  - For the AI sets, generate each room as a still with empty space where the painting goes, then composite the real artwork with a separate shadow layer so it can swing.
  - The halftone look: `background-image: radial-gradient(circle, rgba(0,0,0,.35) 1px, transparent 1.4px); background-size: 6px 6px` with `soft-light` blend.
  - Keep the cut list on a 152 BPM grid (`beatFrames = 30*60/152 ≈ 11.84`) and snap scene starts to the nearest beat frame.
