## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** sponsored AI-tool demo / tutorial promo (the meta hook: "the sound of this video was made by the tool").
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–3.8 **Hook:** "You see this video? The entire thing was sound designed by InVideo." Self-referential claim; it makes the viewer listen to the video they are watching.
  - 4.2–8.4 **Setup / process 1:** uploaded the edit, asked the agent. Shown as a prompt card ("INTRODUCING AGENT TWO") with the video's own first frame attached as a thumbnail.
  - 8.8–15.4 **Proof:** the dark agent workspace generating SFX (ElevenLabs SFX, Lyria lines visible in the log). VO lists "Music, sound effects, ambience, everything."
  - 16.1–19.3 **Control:** "if something feels off, I just tell the agent." The chat composer is enlarged and typed: "Fix the volume of the sounds effects…".
  - 19.7–23.0 **Payoff:** "Just like that, the entire edit has a complete sound design." White void with a dark timeline card.
  - 23.6–26.4 **CTA:** InVideo Editor logo over a blurred, whitened homepage; "Comment "invideo" for the link".
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** a pure white frame (0–0.13 s) opens through a soft cloud-shaped mask to a blue sky. A MacBook rises and tilts into place over 4 frames of acceleration and ~11 frames of settle. "You see" fades in left and "This video" right, each on the spoken word. At 1.0 s the whole shot starts accelerating left (pan_x −0.5 → −2.7 px/frame at analysis scale) and hard-cuts at peak speed to white. Three moves happen in 1.3 s, and the shot ends with momentum.
- **Script → visual rule:** each VO sentence begins on a new surface, cut within ±0.05 s of its first word. Nouns are shown as real UI (the prompt card, the agent log, the timeline). Short emphasis phrases ("the entire thing was", "Just like that", "The entire edit", "Complete sound design") become plain bold captions on an empty void.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** ~0.5 cuts/s overall. The opening is fast (4 surfaces in 2.8 s), the middle is one long 8.7 s hero shot carried by virtual-camera moves instead of cuts, and the ending returns to ~1–3 s shots. This rhythm comes from the VO: one surface per sentence.
- **Cut ↔ VO sync (measured):** cuts land on the first word of a sentence with a mean offset of **−0.02 s** (4.233 vs "I" 4.24; 8.767 vs "It" 8.82; 19.733 vs "And" 19.74; 20.767 vs "the" 20.74; 1.333 vs "The" 1.30). Mid-sentence reframes land on the stressed verb or noun ("asked" +0.25 s, "sound" −0.05 s, "fix" −0.05 s). Caption words appear **on** the spoken word (±1 frame), never before.
- **Beat sync:** weak. Cuts sit 0–0.18 s from the 161 BPM grid, which is within the tolerance of a VO-driven edit. Exceptions: the zoom pulses at 12.8–13.0 s track the onsets at 12.89 s, and the 1.833 invert flash lands exactly on a beat (0.001 s).
- **Easing signatures (per_frame.csv):**
  - Virtual-camera moves in the UI shot are **symmetric ease-in-out** over 24–32 frames (10.1 s move: 14 frames accel / 12 frames decel; 11.3 s move: 15 / 14 frames, flow peak 6.2 analysis px ≈ 44 real px/frame). Bezier ≈ `(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)`.
  - The opening reveal is an **expo ease-out**: 4 frames to peak, ~11 frames of decay, no overshoot (zoom stays negative after the peak, with a gentle −0.3 drift).
  - **Ramp-into-cut:** 1.0–1.33 s, pan_x grows −0.50 → −0.69 → −0.93 → −1.34 → −2.01 → −2.74 (≈ ×1.35 per frame, i.e. exponential ease-in) and cuts at maximum velocity.
  - **Residual-slide punch-in (17.43):** the new framing enters already moving and decays by ≈ ×0.7 per frame (−5.56, −3.47, −2.47, −2.09, −1.71…), like an inertial camera catching up.
  - Caption entrances: 3–4 frames opacity + blur (8 → 0 px), no scale overshoot anywhere. **This video never bounces.**
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:** cloud-mask iris reveal (8 frames); laptop rise + tilt; label fade+deblur (4 frames); grey→black word fade (3–4 frames per word); blur-in white type on black (3 frames); typing with a gradient leading edge (2–4 chars/frame); thumbnail fly-in to a slot (~20 frames); halftone glow-ring bloom (~18 frames); radial halftone portal (8 frames); composer pop-over (~5 frames); card scale-down from blur (~30 frames ease-out); logo wipe-on (4 frames).
- **Camera:** a "virtual camera" on flat UI: push/pan pairs, punch-in reframes at 2.2× and ~1.6×, one snap zoom-out (1 frame), and slow pull-backs on the CTA. Net zoom per shot is small (−12.6 … +9.5) because moves go out and come back.
- **Secondary motion:** the pastel glow breathing around the active card, the blue square caret hopping between words, the "Thinking"/"generating" spinners in the IDE, and cloud drift in the sky.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Ramp-into-cut** (1.33 s): the shot accelerates (exponential ease-in, ~10 frames) and hard-cuts at peak speed to a still white frame. Rebuild: `translateX = -k * (e^(t/τ) - 1)` with τ ≈ 3 frames over the last 10 frames, then cut. No blur needed at this speed.
2. **Glitch-streak invert flash** (1.80–1.83 s): 1 frame with a magenta horizontal streak and smeared letters, then the palette inverts (white bg/black type → black bg/white type) while the same words stay in place and are briefly blurred (3 frames). Rebuild: keep the text layer, swap bg/fg colours at frame N, apply `blur(10px) → 0` over 3 frames, plus a 1-frame RGB-offset smear at N−1.
3. **Blue wash + white bloom** (2.57–2.83 s, 8 frames): electric blue `#0a5cff` rises from the bottom over the black (3 frames), fills the frame, blows out into a white cloud texture (2 frames), and the white dissolves to reveal the next scene (3 frames). Rebuild: a linear-gradient layer translating up 100% over 3 frames, then a cross to a white cloud image with `opacity 1 → 0` over 4 frames.
4. **Thumbnail-into-slot continuity** (4.23–5.1 s): the previous scene appears as a small rounded thumbnail flying into the next UI. It carries the story ("I uploaded my edit") as an object.
5. **Punch-in reframe** (6.13, 7.67, 17.43 s): a hard cut to a 1.6–2.2× crop of the same UI, on a stressed word. 17.43 adds an inertial slide-in that decays over 8 frames.
6. **Radial halftone portal** (8.77–9.1 s): a dark circle with halftone-dot edges (dots get larger toward the rim, purple `#7b5cff` / blue) expands from the centre until it becomes the dark app window over the sky. Rebuild: an SVG radial mask with a halftone pattern (`feTurbulence` is not needed; use a dot `pattern` whose radius is mapped to distance from the centre), scale 0 → 1 over 8 frames with an ease-out.
7. **Blur-morph text swap** (20.67–20.80 s, ~4 frames): the old caption's letters tint blue and blur and jitter vertically; the new caption resolves in the same spot while a blurred dark card fades in under it. Rebuild: cross-fade two text layers with `filter: blur()` peaking at ~12 px at the midpoint, a blue tint on the outgoing layer, and a per-letter y-jitter of ±6 px.
8. **Hard cut to blurred-UI end card** (23.70 s): background content is reused as a texture (a heavily blurred, whitened homepage) under the logo.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **One surface per sentence.** Cut to a new environment (sky / white / black / dot-grid) exactly on the sentence's first word (±1 frame). The cut is the VO's punctuation.
2. **Put UI in a world, not on a desktop.** Floating real product UI over a dreamy sky/landscape plate turns a screen recording into a keynote.
3. **Always be typing.** Every UI moment has text being written (prompt, chat, caption) with a coloured leading edge, which gives the eye a moving focal point without any bounce animation.
4. **Calm type, small type.** Captions at 12–23% of frame width in a neutral grotesk, blur-in rather than pop, read as premium and confident.
5. **A virtual camera instead of cuts in a long UI shot:** symmetric 24–32-frame ease-in-out pans/pushes, plus 1-frame zoom pulses on music onsets.
6. **Reframe with punch-ins on stressed words** (1.6–2.2× hard crops) to direct attention to the exact UI element the VO names.
7. **Carry the object across cuts** (the edit's own frame becomes the attachment thumbnail), so process steps feel continuous.
8. **Colour only as light:** neutral layouts, with saturated colour reserved for glows, halftone blooms and the typing edge.
9. **End on a blurred version of the product** with the logo and a comment-keyword CTA, so the brand stays present while the frame stays quiet.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1920×1080`, `30 fps`, duration = VO + 0.3 s (≈ 792 frames). For vertical delivery, keep the same centred layout at `1080×1920`, scale the windows to 90% of the width and stack the captions above/below.
- **Layer stack (UI-in-sky scene):** `<SkyPlate src="sky_clouds.jpg" drift={0.2px/f}>` → `<GuideLines thirds opacity={0.35}>` (optional) → `<VirtualCamera keyframes>` wrapping `<UIWindow src screenshot|video radius={14} shadow="0 30px 60px rgba(0,0,0,.25)">` → `<Dock icons y="94%">` → `<ComposerOverlay>` → captions.
- **Components & timings:**
  - `SentenceCut`: schedules scene boundaries from the whisper sentence starts (`startFrame = round(word.s*30) - 1`).
  - `TypeOnGradient({text, cps: 60–120, edgeLen: 8})`: render the substring `0..n`; the last `edgeLen` chars get `background: linear-gradient(90deg,#4ade80,#facc15,#e040fb,#fb923c); background-clip:text; color:transparent`, and earlier chars are `#111`. The edge colour cycles hue as n grows.
  - `WordFadeCaption({words, from:'#c9d6ea', to:'#111', frames:4, blur:8})`: each word fades in at `word.s` (ease-out cubic); a 14 px blue `#1a6ff8` square caret sits 6 px after the last visible word.
  - `InvertFlash({at})`: swap bg/fg; 1 frame before it, add a `translateX(±8px)` RGB-split copy in magenta.
  - `BlueWashBloom(8f)`: gradient `#0a5cff` → transparent translating up over 3 f, white cloud plate opacity 0→1 (2 f) → 0 (3 f).
  - `ThumbnailFlyIn({src, from:{y:+300, scale:1.4}, to:slotRect})`: `spring({damping: 200, stiffness: 120, mass: 1})` (no overshoot), ~20 frames.
  - `HalftoneBloom({x,y})`: SVG `<pattern>` of dots; mask radius 0 → 220 px over 18 f, opacity 1 → 0 in the last 6 f, a gradient fill pink `#ff5fd2` → blue `#5b8cff`.
  - `HalftonePortal(8f)`: a circular mask with a dotted rim, scale 0 → 1 with `Easing.out(Easing.exp)`, revealing the window layer.
  - `VirtualCamera`: interpolate `scale/translate` between keyframes with `Easing.bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)`, 24–32 frames per move; `ZoomPulse({at, amount:1.03, frames:2})` on music onsets.
  - `PunchIn({scale:2.2, focus:{x,y}, residualSlide:{px:-40, decay:0.7}})`: a hard cut, then `x = px * decay^f`.
  - `BlurMorphText({from, to, frames:4, tint:'#4c92f5'})`.
  - `CardSettle`: `scale 1.15 → 1`, `blur 12 → 0` px; the blur takes 3 frames and the scale takes 30 frames with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`.
  - `EndCard`: background `filter: blur(20px)` + a `rgba(255,255,255,.65)` wash + a bottom white gradient; the logo pops in (spring `{damping: 14, stiffness: 200}`, 6 f), the wordmark wipes L→R in 4 f, and the subline uses WordFadeCaption.
- **Tricky bits:** the inertial punch-in and the ramp-into-cut must use per-frame exponential curves rather than springs; springs start fast and would look wrong at the cut boundary. For the halftone dot rim, generate the dots in a canvas once per frame, with dot radius = `clamp((r - d)/rim, 0, 1) * maxDot`.

## 9. Variation ideas
1. **SaaS feature launch** (e.g. a design-tool AI feature): the same sky world, but swap the agent IDE for your app. Use one surface per VO sentence and the gradient-edge prompt typing as the hero device. Keep it 16:9 for X/LinkedIn.
2. **Creator tutorial ("how I edit with X")**: replace the sky with a blurred frame from the user's own footage, use the user's clip as the flying thumbnail, and punch into their NLE timeline on stressed words.
3. **Fintech/productivity app ad, vertical**: the same calm grotesk captions on white/black voids alternating with phone mockups floating over a landscape plate; zoom pulses on music onsets; end card over a blurred app home screen with a comment-keyword CTA.
