**One-line essence:** a "surprising origin" story built from photoreal AI-generated period dioramas (lab, jail, courtyard) and tabletop flat-lays (bone on wood, toothbrushes on lined paper, Polaroid on grid paper). Every sentence switches to a **different typographic register**: glowing serif italic, 3D-extruded red caps, single serif words, a scrolling ribbon, a neon multicolour stack, a scattered word cloud, a staircase cascade. Cuts land ~2 frames after the beat, and whole sections are animated on 2s for a tactile, stop-motion feel.

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** "invention origin" micro-documentary with a twist hook, then a creator-product CTA.
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–4.4 **Hook (contrast):** "Most great inventions come from labs. This one came from a jail cell." A warm Edison-style lab, then a cold grey cell wall with the red title THIS ONE and a toothbrush stabbed into it.
  - 4.4–11.5 **Setup:** "In 1780, William Addis was imprisoned in England… he noticed how poorly people cleaned their teeth." Date card, Polaroid ID card that flips to a behind-bars photo, inmates rubbing their teeth with fingers.
  - 11.8–16.2 **Invention:** "he used a small bone, drilled holes into it, and inserted animal bristles." Top-down hands-on-table demo.
  - 16.6–21.0 **Scale-up:** "After his release, he improved the design and began producing toothbrushes commercially." Gate opens; a conveyor of evolving toothbrush designs.
  - 21.4–25.6 **Impact:** "His simple idea eventually became something billions use every day." Neon stacked type in a stitched phone-shaped frame.
  - 25.8–31.7 **Twist recap:** "And the strangest part? A tool we barely think about started as a prisoner's solution to a simple problem." Scattered word cloud → modern man brushing (fisheye) → back to the jailed Polaroid.
  - 32.1–39.7 **Bridge + CTA:** "There has to be a better way. The techniques are easier to understand when you can see the actual timeline. earnedits.com" Staircase serif text → tablet showing an After Effects timeline → search bar.
- **Hook mechanics:** frame 0 is a soft-focus empty corner of a warm lab. In 0.5 s the camera tilts down and pulls back (pan_y −6 px/f analysis, zoom −3.6/f peak at 0.63 s) while foreground figures sweep in out of focus. At 0.8 s a glowing yellow serif "Inventions" blooms in. The twist line ("This one came from a jail cell") gets a **whip-tilt into a grey cell** at 2.67 s and red 3D caps with a toothbrush flying up and **stabbing into the title** like a shiv (3.2–3.47 s). That is a visual pun for "prison" and "toothbrush" in one image.
- **Script → visual rule:** each sentence gets its own environment and its own type treatment. Concrete nouns are staged literally (bone, drill/knife, bristles, gate opening on "release", Polaroid flipping to bars on "imprisoned"). Abstract sentences (impact, twist, bridge) drop the scene and become pure type compositions on dark or paper grounds.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** mixed. (a) Photoreal AI period dioramas (lab, cell, courtyard, inmates) with 2.5D camera moves and rack focus; (b) top-down tabletop flat-lays with real hand cut-outs and props; (c) pure kinetic-type plates (neon stack, word cloud, staircase); (d) a device mockup for the CTA. The modern bathroom scene is deliberately different (ultra-wide fisheye, comedic face) to mark "today".
- **Backgrounds & textures:** amber lab with window light → grey brick + barred window with god-rays → **burnt-orange grid paper with window-mullion shadow** (date + Polaroid) → dark stone cell → **dark grey wood planks** (top-down) → sepia courtyard → **blue-grey lined notebook paper** with vignette → **charcoal concrete** (neon) → **deep navy noise** (word cloud) → pale-blue bathroom → **white grid paper with torn red (top-left) and navy (bottom-right) paper corners** → near-black studio gradient (CTA).
- **Colour:** each scene is one dominant field + one accent, with strong warm/cool alternation that tracks the story: warm amber lab `#c1874d` `#5d3520` (freedom/science) vs cold grey jail `#2e2b2d` `#8c8886` (prison), with the hook's red accent `#b21e1e`; orange paper `#c37f36` `#905322`; wood greys `#514e4e` `#181717` with skin `#c38c72`; lined paper `#bdc0c4` + ribbon red `#d14648`; neon plate `#1e1e1e` with line colours white `#f2f2f2`, cyan `#3fd0e8`, red `#ef3a3f`, yellow `#f2c331`, green `#45d64a`; navy word cloud `#030a11` `#0d171e`; Polaroid plate grey/white + red `#8b1a1a` + navy `#13305a`; CTA `#181818` + timeline keyframe colours (orange, green, magenta, yellow). The grade is filmic with soft vignettes on every scene.
- **Typography:** seven type registers, one per beat.
  1. **Glowing serif italic headline:** high-contrast Didone italic (Playfair Display Italic class). Key words "Inventions"/"Labs." in yellow `#f3e03b` at cap height ≈ 9% of frame width with a strong yellow outer glow (~20 px). Connectors "Most Great"/"come from" in white/grey at ~4%. Mixed scale on a staggered baseline, upper-third placement (y ≈ 15–25%).
  2. **Red 3D-extruded caps:** heavy grotesk (Anton / Archivo Black class), red `#e31b1b` with a dark extrude/shadow down-right, THIS ONE spanning ~83% of frame width, sitting in the scene with the same depth of field as the wall.
  3. **Condensed heavy italic numerals:** "1780" (Bebas Neue / Oswald Bold Italic class), white with a soft emboss/gradient, ~22% of width, floating with a slight 3D tilt over the orange paper.
  4. **Single-word serif captions:** Playfair Italic, white on wood or black on paper, cap height ≈ 2.5% of width, **one word at a time** at y ≈ 67–70%, replaced on every spoken word.
  5. **Ribbon marquee:** black serif italic words on a red `#f04444` fabric ribbon spanning the full width at y ≈ 20%. The ribbon scrolls right → left and the words at the ribbon ends are **defocused** (a fake curved-surface depth of field).
  6. **Neon stacked block:** ultra-heavy rounded display caps with grunge texture (Bowlby One / Rubik Black class), 6 lines, each a different colour and size so the block reads as a justified ~50%-wide column ("HIS SIMPLE IDEA" small white → "EVENTUALLY" cyan → "BECAME" red, largest → "SOMETHING" yellow → "BILLIONS" green → "USE EVERY DAY." small white), each with its own coloured bloom.
  7. **Serif italic scattered / staircase:** white glowing serif italic words scattered at random angles ("And · the · strangest · part"), then black serif italic words stepping diagonally down-right in a **staircase** ("There / has / to / be / a / better / way"), ~5% of width each.
  - CTA: thin geometric sans (Inter Light / Poppins Light class), white `#e6e6e6`, one word at a time centred above the tablet, ~5% cap height.
  - Polaroid caption "William Addis": condensed bold sans (Bebas / Oswald Bold class), black.
  - **Emphasis rule:** colour and scale encode importance (yellow glow on "Inventions/Labs", red on THIS ONE, the biggest line of the neon stack is the verb "BECAME"). Elsewhere the type is deliberately small and quiet.
- **Layout & composition:** heroes centred; titles in the upper third; single-word captions in the lower third; the CTA word sits above the device. Tabletop scenes are shot exactly top-down. Props enter from frame edges (hands from top-left and right, Polaroid from bottom or right, toothbrushes from right).
- **Assets:** AI period scenes, a hand cut-out holding a bone, a knife, a bristle tuft, 5 toothbrush designs of increasing refinement, a Polaroid with two photo states (portrait / behind bars), torn coloured paper corners, a stitched rounded-rect "phone" outline, a glassy tablet mockup with a real AE timeline screen recording, and a search-bar pill.
- **Finishing / FX:** fine grain + halftone-like screen, heavy shallow depth of field and rack focus, god-rays through bars, glow/bloom on all "light" type, motion blur on every whip and prop entrance, soft vignettes, exposure flash, and a 1-frame black blink.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** ~0.4 scene changes/s; scenes are 0.8–5.9 s, median ~2.5 s. The fastest stretch is the hook/setup (0–9 s, a change every ~1.5 s, including in-scene events such as the shiv, the flip and the whips). The neon impact plate is the longest hold (4.7 s), and the CTA runs 5.9 s.
- **Mixed frame rate:** frames 0–4.3 s, 18–21.3 s and 29.3–34 s are **animated on 2s** (every other frame is a duplicate: diff ≈ 0, e.g. 0.33/0.37, 2.90/2.93). The other sections run at a full 30 fps. Stepped sections feel handmade and "stop-motion paper"; smooth sections feel cinematic. Alternating the two is itself a rhythm device.
- **Easing signatures (measured):**
  - Opening tilt/pull: ramp **~19 f** to peak, decay **~30 f** (an asymmetric ease-in-out, roughly `bezier(0.5,0,0.15,1)`).
  - Whips (2.33–2.63, 8.8–9.1, 32.33–32.4): **6 f pure ease-in** to 9–12.5 px/f analysis (36–50 px/f real), cut on the motion, and the incoming shot continues and decays over 15–20 f.
  - Pull-back reveals on new scenes (shots 3, 9, 13, 16): peak zoom on the first 2–3 frames after the cut (−5 to −7/f), then an exponential ease-out over 10–15 f (`Easing.out(Easing.cubic)` over ~14 f).
  - Prop entrances (Polaroid, hands, toothbrushes): 6–12 f ease-out with motion blur and **no overshoot**. The card flip ramps its rotation over 12 f (ease-in), swaps at edge-on, and eases out over ~15 f.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:** glow-bloom word reveal (~6 f); rise-from-below caps with blur (~16 f); prop "shiv" fly-in and stick (~8 f); orange panel shape-wipe from a vertical strip (~16 f); fade+focus numerals; Polaroid slide-up; Polaroid spin-flip with image swap (~25 f total); whip exits; hand entrances from the edges; one-word cut-on captions; ribbon marquee; conveyor slide-ins; stitched-frame swing-in with rotation (~24 f); per-letter neon flicker-on (2–3 f/letter); word fly-in cloud and recede; staircase word cascade; exposure flash land; 1-frame black blink; slide-up plate; pill → search-bar stretch.
- **Camera:** big net moves on photoreal plates (shot 1: pan_y −115, zoom −66; shot 4/5: rot +45; shot 6: pan_y −140; shot 7: rot +30), and near-static holds on type plates (shot 11 mean flow 0.41, shot 12 0.52). Rack focus is used on every diorama entrance (blurred → sharp).
- **Secondary motion:** god-rays, the website/timeline auto-scroll, the brushing loop, the ribbon scroll, the drifting window-shadow on orange paper, and neon bloom flicker.
- **Sync:**
  - **Beat lock with a consistent ~2-frame lag:** scene cuts fall 0.04–0.09 s **after** the nearest beat at 144 BPM (2.67 +0.05, 4.00 +0.08, 4.83 ±0.00, 12.00 +0.04, 16.50 +0.06, 17.83 +0.07, 21.33 −0.01, 26.00 +0.09, 29.87 flash +0.01). Treat cut = beat + 2 f as the rule.
  - **VO:** cuts land on or just after the first word of the sentence ("jail" 3.84 → cut 4.00; "In" 4.78 → wipe 4.80; "After" 16.62 → cut 16.50, a 0.12 s lead; "he improved" 17.78 → cut 17.83; "His" 21.40 → cut 21.33; "A tool" 27.16 → cut 27.33).
  - **Type lag depends on register:** single-word captions (bone table, CTA) are within ±0.1 s of the spoken word (tight karaoke); serif headline +0.17 to +0.3 s; THIS ONE +0.35 s; neon letters +0.3 to +0.6 s (they light up as the line is being said, so they trail); ribbon marquee words reach centre 0.4–1.0 s after being spoken (they are mood, not captions); search-bar typing trails "earnedits" by ~0.3 s.
  - **Literal action-on-word:** the hand with the bone enters on "small bone"; the knife on "into"; the gate opens on "release"; the Polaroid flips to the bars photo on "England" right after "imprisoned"; the toothbrush stabs THIS ONE as "jail cell" lands.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Change the typographic register with the rhetorical register.** Narrative claims get a glowing serif, the twist gets 3D red caps, process gets tiny single words, scale gets a neon multicolour stack, a question gets a scattered cloud, and a call to action gets a staircase. The viewer feels a new chapter without any title card.
2. **Visual puns sell the hook.** A toothbrush stabbed into the title like a prison shiv combines the two halves of the premise in one image.
3. **Stage the verbs:** hands on a table perform each step on the spoken word (bone → drill → bristles). This is clearer than any diagram.
4. **Physical reveals for state changes:** the Polaroid flips from portrait to behind-bars on "imprisoned"; the gate swings open on "release"; the toothbrush conveyor shows "improved the design" as evolution.
5. **Alternate 15 fps and 30 fps sections** to separate "paper/tabletop" moments from "cinematic" moments.
6. **Warm vs cold scene grading** carries the story's freedom/prison polarity.
7. **Cuts on the beat + 2 frames** feel tight but human. Every scene-change whip ends on a beat.
8. **Recall the hero prop** (the Polaroid returns at 29.3 s with a flash) to close the loop before the CTA.
9. **Every diorama entrance starts defocused** and racks into focus while pulling back. It is a cheap way to make AI stills feel shot with a lens.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, duration = VO + 0.3 s (~1195 f).
- **Typical scene stack (bottom → top):** `<StillScene src camera focusPull={{from:20,to:0,frames:12}}>` → props (`<PropSlide>`, `<HandCutout>`, `<CardFlip>`) → type register component → `<Vignette>` → `<GrainOverlay opacity={0.06}>`; wrap stepped sections in `<OnTwos>`.
- **Components:**
  - `OnTwos({children})`: `const f = Math.floor(useCurrentFrame()/2)*2;` and pass `f` via context so all children animate on 2s.
  - `GlowSerifHeadline({lines:[{text, size, color, glow}]})`: Playfair Display Italic; reveal each line with blur 12→0 px, opacity 0→1 over 6 f, plus `text-shadow: 0 0 18px ${color}`.
  - `Extrude3DTitle`: Anton, red `#e31b1b`, 8 stacked `text-shadow` layers (1 px steps, `#5a0808`), entering `translateY(30%) → 0` + blur 10→0 over 16 f with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`; `ShivProp`: a PNG flying from below (y +60% → 0, rotate −20° → −8°) in 8 f, `Easing.out(Easing.back(1.2))` for the "stick" thunk, with a 2-frame 6 px camera shake on impact.
  - `ShapeWipePanel({color, from:0.06})`: an expanding centre strip as in §5.3.
  - `CardFlip({front, back, frames:28})`: `rotateZ` 0→360° (or partial) combined with `rotateY` 0→180°, `perspective: 1200px`, `backfaceVisibility: hidden` on both faces; ease-in-out cubic.
  - `OneWordCaption({words})`: shows the word whose `start ≤ t < next.start`, appearing at word.start − 1 f with a 3 f fade; Playfair Italic 2.5% cap, positioned at y = 68%.
  - `RibbonMarquee({text, speed: 180 px/s})`: a red band with an inner text track translating left; edge defocus via a CSS `mask-image` gradient on a blurred duplicate layer (blur 4 px at the edges, 0 at the centre).
  - `Conveyor({items, interval: 20 f})`: each item enters `translateX(60%) → slot` over 6 f with directional blur, shifting the others left by one slot.
  - `NeonStack({lines:[{text,color,size}]})`: per-letter reveal; letter i turns on at `lineStart + i*2` f with an opacity flicker sequence `[0,1,0.3,1]` over 4 f; each line has `text-shadow: 0 0 6px color, 0 0 24px color`, and a background radial glow of the line colour at 25% opacity.
  - `StitchedFrame`: SVG rounded rect with `strokeDasharray="6 4"` in white at 60% opacity, double-stroke, entering via `spring({damping: 20, stiffness: 120})` from rotate 15°, x −40%.
  - `WordCloudFlyIn({words, positions})`: each word from an off-frame vector with blur, 4 f ease-out; group `scale` 1 → 0.6 over 12 f to exit.
  - `StaircaseText({words, step:{x:65,y:65}})`: word i at (x0 + i·dx, y0 + i·dy), 3 f fade on word.start.
  - `ExposureFlash`: a white overlay at opacity 0.6 decaying exponentially over 12 f, plus a 1-frame black `<Sequence>` before it.
  - `TabletMockup` (glass rounded rect, 1 px light border, inner `<OffthreadVideo>` screen recording) + `SearchBarCTA` (pill width 18% → 52% over 8 f, then typing at 16 cps with key clicks).
- **Tricky bits:** for the bulging panel edges, use an SVG path whose left/right edges are quadratic curves with a control-point offset of ~4% of width during motion, settling to straight. For rack focus on stills, use a CSS `filter: blur()` animated from 18 → 0 px, combined with scale 1.08 → 1.0 so the blur edges never show.
