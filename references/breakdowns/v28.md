- **Hook mechanics:** frame 0 is a saturated red halftone still (strong thumbnail colour) and frame 1 is black. On frame 2 a **red/blue duotone cut-out scene slides up into place** with an RGB-offset outline on Peter. Within 1 s the whole image squeezes into a speech-bubble shape and the title letters tumble in. That gives three distinct visual events before 1.5 s.
- **Script → visual rule:** **one lyric phrase = one poster layout**. The stressed word is set as a giant word filling 50–90% of the width. **Sung elongations become literal repeated letters** ("LESSOOOON", "thissssss"). **Repetition in the lyric becomes visual repetition** ("IT" tiled ~60 times).

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** poster/zine collage built from **movie stills and cut-outs** (Tom Holland's Peter, many Spider-Man suit variants as toy-like cut-outs) and graphic shapes (flat skyline with paint drips, parallelogram bands with pinstripes, torn-paper film strips, web line-art). Each scene is styled like a different print artefact: duotone screen print, newspaper headline, key-art poster, social post, polaroid scrapbook, ransom note.
- **Backgrounds & textures:** light grey paper `#bfbdbc`/`#e6e2e4` with a strong dark vignette (LEARNED, MAN, tweet); solid deep red `#6a0307` (TRIED); black `#040104` with red web line-art `#7a0a10` (THE MESSAGE, THAT'S MY LIFE, poster); navy-blue sky `#0c0c65` with royal blue `#1b17b0` (opener). **Halftone dots on every surface** (clearly visible on the type and faces at ~3 px pitch), plus film grain.
- **Colour:** Spider-Man palette, used scene by scene as **one dominant field + one counter colour**: red `#e1292e`/`#a21c1e` + navy `#0c0c65` (opener), blue bubble `#1f56b0` on black, red type `#a01218` on grey, white type on red, blue `#1d4fa8` band under red, red stripes `#c8202a` on white. White `#f2f0f0` and black are the neutrals. CTA: AE navy `#0a0a5c` with lavender `#9999ff` outline.
- **Typography:**
  - **Hero words:** heavy grotesks in all caps. LEARNED / LESSOOOON use an ultra-bold grotesk (Franklin Gothic Heavy / Impact class) in red `#a01218` with a darker 2 px outline and a soft drop shadow, letters ~25% of frame height. LESSOOOON runs ~3 frame-widths long.
  - "**MY**": red bold slab-serif (Rockwell / Clarendon Bold class), ~10% H. It is the only serif and contrasts with the grotesk.
  - "**TRIED TO**": white heavy sans (Montserrat Black / Anton class) with a **halftone fade-out to the bottom** (dot gradient), ~75% W.
  - "**CORRECT IT**": white condensed bold grotesk (Bebas Neue / Oswald Bold class) with halftone texture, on a black strip, ~50% W, with tiny poster billing text below and a "Spider-Man: Brand New Day" logo.
  - "**I WRECKED**": royal blue Impact-class heavy condensed with a white 3 px stroke, on a blue band overlaid with cracked-glass lines, ~65% W.
  - "**MAN,**": white heavy condensed sans on a red pinstripe band, wrapped in a white web net, ~25% W. "**A CRISIS**": red distressed/grunge condensed type with a white outline.
  - "**THE MESSAGE**": white heavy geometric sans (Montserrat Black / Futura Bold class) in a blue rounded speech bubble.
  - "**THAT'S**": red grunge condensed. "**MY LIFE**": black outline letters (open counters) behind white web line-art. "**US**": ransom-note mix (red U on newspaper, black S on paper scrap).
  - **Social UI:** SF Pro / Helvetica bold black for "Peter Parker" and the post text, light grey handle "@peter parker", round avatar.
  - **CTA:** white Helvetica Neue / Arial Bold, title case, two centred lines, ~32% W.
- **Layout & composition:** 1:1 frame, centre-weighted. The hero word sits on the horizontal centre line (y 40–60%), and supporting cut-outs cluster at the corners (Spider-Man figures top-left, bottom-left, right). Layouts are **dense, edge-to-edge** with no empty margins except the CTA. Scale contrast is extreme: tiny 2–4% figures against 75%-wide words.
- **Assets:** movie stills (Peter on rooftop, Peter in a dark corridor, Spider-Man unmasked close-up), rotoscoped Peter cut-out, ~10 Spider-Man figure cut-outs (classic, Iron Spider, black suit, Miles-like), a real macro eye clip, a Spider-Man mask lens graphic, web line-art PNGs, torn-paper strips containing mirrored film frames, a city skyline photo strip, a sunset sky strip, newspaper/ransom letters, a social-post card UI, AE "AEP" icons.
- **Finishing / FX:** halftone screen over everything; **RGB-offset stroke** on cut-outs (a red outline offset +4 px and a blue outline offset −4 px on Peter, a misregistered-print look); chromatic aberration on footage edges; heavy oval vignette darkening the corners of the paper scenes by ~30% luma; soft glow / lens flare on the mask eye; a **thin white inner frame line** (rounded rect at 2% inset) on the red TRIED page; **white 1-frame flash frames** at 9.00 and 19.73.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** 0.55 detected cuts/s, but the perceived rate is much higher because every shot holds 3–6 internal events (type build, push, reveal, swap). There is a new visual event every ~0.35–0.5 s, i.e. on every beat. Shots last 0.9–3.4 s. The CTA is the only calm section.
- **Easing signatures (per_frame.csv):**
  - **Landing settles are short exponential ease-outs with no overshoot:** bubble shrink −5.13 → −3.88 → −2.67 → −1.59 → −1.10 → −0.92 → −0.83 (f20–26); flare zoom-out −8.72 → −5.26 → −3.78 → −2.81 (f139–142); eye −6.73 → −4.09 → −2.78 → −2.11 → −1.11. They decay ×0.65–0.75 per frame and settle in 6–10 frames at 15 fps (12–20 at 30 fps).
  - **Pushes and scrolls are ease-in-out bells:** LEARNED push 1.0 → 1.52 → 2.65 → 4.46 → 4.64 → 3.71 → 2.59 → 1.73 → 0.99 (f45–54), about 4 frames up and 5 down. The LESSOOOON scroll accelerates over 6 frames (−0.55 → −10.0).
  - **Card drop:** 0.69 → 1.53 → 3.10 → 6.30 → 8.58 → 5.85 → 3.57 → 2.49 (f207–214): ease-in 4 frames, ease-out 3 frames. This is an ease-in-out cubic with a slightly harder landing.
  - **Shake:** alternating-sign rotation and pan on landings (poster rot −5.4/+0.9/+2.6/+3.5/−1.3/−2.9/−3.3°; IT impact pan_y +7.3/−5.1/−5.4). It is a 6-frame damped wiggle, the only place where sign flips occur.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:** scattered per-letter tumble-and-snap (THE MESSAGE, MAN), out-of-order letter drop (LEARNED), per-letter type-on (CORRECT IT, A CRISIS, THAT'S, post text), scale-pop with blur ghosting (TRIED TO), stretched-word scroll, mask morph full-frame → speech bubble, perspective band unfold, tile stacking + tumble, word-by-word opacity fade (CTA), staggered photo pops, card drop, overflow-and-shatter exit.
- **Camera:** extremely active virtual camera. Net moves per shot: bubble zoom −40, LEARNED/LESSON pan_x −41 with zoom +17, poster pan −24 / rot −20, IT wall zoom −47, MAN zoom +18 with pan_y +70, MY LIFE pan_y −87. There are push-ins, pull-back reveals, lateral scrolls, a long tilt, shakes and whips. There is no static hold until the CTA.
- **Secondary motion:** tiny Spider-Man figures crawling or swinging at the edges, web line-art drawing on, film-strip papers sliding in parallax, the lens flare twinkling, the IT tiles jittering on 2s/3s (stop-motion feel).
- **Sync (music-only, vocal-driven):**
  - Beat grid 162 BPM (0.371 s). Major cuts land within ±0.12 s of a beat: 2.27 (−0.03), 6.93 (−0.11), 11.47 (+0.09), 17.40 (−0.11), 19.73 (+0.09). The 9.00 flash sits between beats, right before onset 9.17 (−0.17): the white frame **pre-empts the hit** and the flare blooms on it.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Flash-frame open** (0.00–0.13): 1 saturated halftone frame + 1 black frame before the first scene. Rebuild: two 1-frame (2 at 30 fps) `<Sequence>`s.
2. **Full-frame → speech-bubble mask morph** (0.93–1.47): the scene is clipped by a rounded-rect + tail path that starts at frame size and shrinks to ≈ 45% W while its fill crossfades to flat blue `#1f56b0` (~6 frames), on black. Rebuild: SVG `clipPath` whose rect `width/height/rx` interpolate from (W, H, 0) to (0.45W, 0.3H, 40), plus a tail path; overlay a blue rect with opacity 0 → 1.
3. **Whip pan** (2.07–2.20): 3 frames of accelerating lateral move (≈ 24 px/frame real) with motion blur, then a hard cut to a bright page. Rebuild: `translateX` with `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)` + SVG horizontal blur `stdDeviation="20 0"`.
4. **Colour-block expand wipe** (5.07–5.53): the type band becomes a solid red rectangle, then grows vertically with **wavy, liquid edges** to fill the frame in 3 frames. Rebuild: SVG path whose top and bottom edges are sine waves (amplitude 2% H) and height interpolates 18% → 110% H, `Easing.in(Easing.quad)`.
5. **Torn film-strip wipe** (6.87–7.00): 4–5 torn-paper strips (each 12–15% H, containing film frames) sweep horizontally in alternating directions across the outgoing frame, and the new scene is in place behind them. The same strips stay on as decoration. Rebuild: PNG strips with torn alpha edges, `translateX` from ±120% to the final positions over 4 frames, alternating direction, 1-frame stagger.
6. **Dissolve-push into macro footage** (8.07–8.27): the outgoing poster scales in toward Peter's eye while crossfading over 3 frames to a real macro eye clip, which then settles. A match on "eye".
7. **White flash + match-cut** (9.00): 1 white frame (luma 214) between the real eye and the mask lens graphic positioned in the same screen area; the lens flare blooms immediately. Rebuild: `<AbsoluteFill style={{background:'#f4f2f2'}}>` for 1 frame (2 at 30 fps), then the incoming at scale 1.35 → 1 over 8 frames with `Easing.out(Easing.exp)`.
8. **Perspective band unfold** (11.47–11.73): a pinstripe band appears as a thin vertical sliver in perspective and unfolds to full width (CSS 3D `rotateY(85° → 12°)` with `perspective: 800px`, 4 frames).
9. **Card drop-over** (13.80–14.13): a UI card falls from above the frame and lands over the current scene, followed by a push-in until it fills the frame. The card becomes the next scene without a cut.
10. **Overflow shatter** (16.80–17.33): the scrolling text piles against the card edge and the edge breaks into falling glyph debris (particles with gravity and rotation), then the frame whites out. Rebuild: per-glyph spans at the edge get `translateY += v; v += g` with random `rotate` (±180°) and opacity fade over 8 frames; background crossfades to white.
11. **Tilt-down follow** (18.27–19.33): the camera follows a web line down from the title block to the hanging hero (≈ 1 frame height of travel in 16 frames), with an ease-in-out bell.
12. **White flash to CTA** (19.73): 1 white frame, then black.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Each lyric line is its own poster.** Change the print format every line (duotone print → headline → key-art → social post → scrapbook → ransom note), but keep one IP palette (red/blue/white/black) so it stays coherent.
2. **Visualise the vocal performance, not just the words.** Held notes become repeated letters plus a camera scroll; repeated words become tiled walls; "wrecked" makes the layout physically fall apart.
3. **Halftone + RGB-offset outline on every cut-out** turns mixed movie stills, toys and graphics into one printed world.
4. **Never hold a static frame.** Each layout gets a landing settle (6–10 frames, no overshoot), one internal push or pull reveal, and an exit move, so the camera is always travelling.
5. **1-frame white flashes and match-cuts on shapes** (eye → lens) give hits without needing extra footage.
6. **Use UI as a scene.** A social-post card dropping in and filling the frame is a cheap, relatable "scene change" that also carries dialogue-like text.
7. **Extreme scale contrast:** a 75%-wide word next to 3% figures makes a small square frame feel epic.
8. **Snap scene changes to beats and type hits to onsets.** At 15 fps the beat is only 5–6 frames, so every hit is within ±2 frames.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1080`, `30 fps` (double the frame counts quoted from the 15 fps reference), ~22 s = 660 frames. Drive everything from a `beats[]` array plus a hand-entered `lyrics[]` array (`{text, start, end, style}`), because ASR will likely fail on sung vocals.
- **Scene stack (bottom → top):** `<PaperBg tone vignette={0.35}/>` or `<SolidField color/>` → `<WebLineArt drawOn/>` → `<FilmStill src border="#a01218" halftone/>` / `<CutoutFigure src rgbOffset={4}/>` → `<HeroWord style="grotesk|slab|condensed" anim="tumble|drop|pop|type|scroll"/>` → `<Camera>` wrapper (scale/translate/rotate keyed per scene) → `<HalftoneOverlay pitch={4} opacity={0.18}/>` + `<Grain/>` + `<FlashFrame/>`.
- **Components & timings (30 fps):**
  - `TumbleLetters`: each glyph starts at a random offset (±0.3 W, ±0.3 H, rotate ±60°, scale 1.3) and springs home with `spring({damping: 13, stiffness: 220, mass: 0.6})` and a 1-frame stagger. The total settle is ~16 frames.
  - `DropLetters`: glyphs reveal in a shuffled order, 1 glyph per frame, each `translateY(-30%) → 0`, 3 frames, `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`.
  - `StretchedWordScroll`: the text appends `repeatChar` every 2 frames while a note is held; the camera `translateX` follows the text end with a `spring({damping: 30, stiffness: 120})` follower (gives the measured 6-frame acceleration). It ends when `note.end` is reached.
  - `MaskMorphBubble`: SVG clipPath interpolating rect W→0.45W, H→0.3H, rx 0→40 over 12 frames, `Easing.out(Easing.exp)`; overlay tint opacity 0→1 over 8 frames.
  - `RGBOffsetCutout`: three stacked copies of the cut-out PNG (red `#ff2a2a` and blue `#2a3aff` silhouettes via CSS `filter: brightness(0) ... drop-shadow`), offset ±4 px under the original, plus a 3 px white outer stroke.
  - `HalftoneOverlay`: an SVG `<pattern>` of circles in `mix-blend-mode: multiply` at 15–20%. For halftone *fades* on type (TRIED), mask the type with a dot pattern whose radius scales with a vertical gradient.
  - `LandingSettle`: incoming scene `scale: interpolate(f, [0, 14], [1.25, 1], {easing: Easing.out(Easing.exp)})`; optional `Shake` with `rotate = 5·e^(−f/4)·sin(f·1.6)` deg for 12 frames.
  - `PushBell`: `scale: 1 + 0.12·sin(π·t)` style bump over 10 frames (ease-in-out), used mid-scene on onsets.
  - `CardDrop`: social card `translateY(-110% → 0)` over 14 frames with `Easing.bezier(0.55, 0, 0.35, 1)`; then `scale 1 → 1.6` over 30 frames to fill.
  - `OverflowShatter`: glyph particles with gravity `g = 1.2 px/frame²`, random `vx ±3`, `rotate ±12°/frame`, opacity fade over 16 frames.
  - `FlashFrame`: a 2-frame white `#f4f2f2` AbsoluteFill on chosen beats.
  - `CTAWordFade`: words `opacity 0.15 → 1` over 6 frames with a 4-frame stagger, Inter/Helvetica Bold 0.045·H white; AE icons `translate` from the corners with `spring({damping: 26, stiffness: 80})`.
- **Tricky effects:** **IT-wall tumble**: generate a grid of ~60 `<div>` tiles. Phase 1 drops them in row by row (1 row per 2 frames). Phase 2 re-randomises each tile's rotation every 4–6 frames (stop-motion), drawn from an increasing range (±5° → ±45°), on 2s, so it reads as collapsing. **Wavy colour-block fill**: SVG path with sine-wave top/bottom edges animated by phase plus a height interpolation. **Eye match-cut**: align the macro eye's pupil centre and the lens-flare centre to the same (x, y) in both shots, then cut through a 1-frame white flash.

## 9. Variation ideas
1. **Athlete / sports hype edit** (a player's quote or a rap verse): team colours instead of red/blue, jersey and stadium stills as film-strip papers, the stretched-word scroll on a crowd chant, an IT-wall made of repeated jersey numbers that tumble on a "lost" lyric.

3. **Brand campaign with a customer-quote voiceover:** replace movie stills with UGC photos, keep the social-post card drop and overflow-shatter for an "I'm never like this" style testimonial, and end on a word-fade CTA card with the product icon in place of the AE icons.
