# v41 — "Frame War" finalist card: a VHS-sleeve showcase frame around an AI-cinematic motivational edit ("Pressure doesn't ruin you")

**Source creator handle:** earn.edits (hosts a guest edit by @After.vision_ inside its "Frame War / Video Club" tournament template) · **Format:** 720×960, 3:4, 30 fps, 14.37 s · **Shots:** 1 static outer frame holding 11 inner shots (the detector found 2 because only the inner window changes; the inner cuts are at 1.70, 3.27, 4.07, 5.20, 6.07, 6.90, 8.20, 9.57, 11.20, 12.27) · **Avg shot:** ~1.3 s (inner) · **Music BPM:** ~123 (cinematic bed) · **VO:** yes, English, deep male motivational narrator, slow and deliberate

**Style tags:** frame-in-frame, device-mockup, minimal-swiss, giant-keyword, glow, word-by-word-captions, slow-push, vignette, depth-of-field, cta-endcard, text-behind-subject *(proposed in v01)*, ai-still-animation *(proposed in v24)*, showcase-frame *(new)*, persistent-vote-bar *(new)*, neon-flicker-on *(new)*

**One-line essence:** a fully static, print-designed "VHS sleeve" poster (cream paper, charcoal case, orange accents, editorial type) frames a 9:16 cinematic edit in a window that is only 38% of the frame width. The stillness of the frame makes the moving picture inside feel precious, and the persistent "COMMENT 1 TO VOTE" bar turns the whole thing into a participation post.

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** community-contest showcase ("Frame War tournament, Finalist 01"). The outer layer is the brand's reusable template; the inner layer is a ~14 s motivational micro-edit (AI-generated mid-century cinematic scenes + one-word keyword type + VO).
- **Beats (inner edit):**
  - 0.0–3.27 **Hook / thesis:** "Pressure doesn't ruin you, it reveals you" over a lone footballer (blue #10 shirt, a 1994-penalty homage) on a spotlit pitch, then a close-up of him head-down.
  - 3.27–8.20 **Setup (the talkers):** people who talk about what they *could* make: beach-promenade men with a comic speech bubble "I COULD MAKE THAT", then a triptych of office men for "IDEAS / PLANS / SOMEDAY PROJECT".
  - 8.20–11.20 **Turn:** "until the timer starts and the excuses go quiet" (a glowing wrist timer, a man from behind scratching his head).
  - 11.20–14.37 **Payoff:** "this is the timer" (a man presents an hourglass) → cut to black → "MAKE IT COUNT" flickers on.
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** the frame is fully legible from frame 0 (contest title, finalist number, vote bar), so the viewer instantly knows the format. Inside the window, a spotlight cone switches on at ~0.13 s (strong audio onset at 0.07/0.14 s) and "PRESSURE" glows in by 0.27 s. The motion is small, but it is the only thing moving on a still page, so the eye goes straight to it.
- **Script → visual rule:** every noun in the list ("ideas", "plans", "someday project", "excuses") gets its own new AI scene with a new character, and the noun itself becomes a glowing giant keyword set *behind the character's head*. Verbs and connectors are never typed.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** two layers with opposite logic.
  - **Outer (template):** flat print/editorial layout in the spirit of minimal Swiss design: a cream page, hairline rules, a big condensed headline, small tracked metadata, vertical side labels and a VHS cassette sleeve as the hero object. Nothing in it moves during the whole video (frame-to-frame diff outside the window ≈ 0).
  - **Inner (guest edit):** AI-generated cinematic stills/clips in a 1950s–60s Americana look (warm tungsten offices, wood desks, rotary phones, suits, a pastel seaside promenade), each animated with slow 2.5D drifts and push-ins.
- **Backgrounds & textures:** the outer page is a flat cream `#e0d6bb` with a column of tiny tick marks down the left edge (x ≈ 15 px, every ~62 px), like a ruler or film edge. The inner scenes carry their own film-like softness, warm haze and vignette. The final inner shot is pure black.
- **Colour:**
  - Template: cream `#e0d6bb` (51% of pixels), charcoal `#231d16` (case, headline, vote bar), signal orange-red `#cf4113`/`#d04d27` (accent: "NOW PLAYING", the 01 badge, the sleeve header band, the play icon, the bar's number box), mustard `#dc9a3a` (second stripe), and a sleeve tri-stripe of orange / mustard `#fad295` / sage `#acbba8`. Inner sleeve card is a lighter cream `#f0e7c8`.
  - Inner edit: warm amber/olive interiors (lamp highlights ~`#e8b060`, walls olive-brown `#635644`), with deliberate cool exceptions: Italy-blue shirt, seaside sky-blue, and a **cyan glow** for the digital timer and the final "MAKE IT" flicker. Cyan is the colour of "the timer", so it marks the payoff.
- **Typography:**
  - Template headline "AFTER VISION": tall condensed grotesk, all caps (Bebas Neue / Oswald Bold class), charcoal, cap height ~60 px = 6.3% of frame height, width ~330 px = 46% of frame width, left-aligned at x = 38.

  - Vote bar: **monospace typewriter caps** (Courier Prime Bold / IBM Plex Mono Bold class) in cream on charcoal: `COMMENT "1" TO VOTE`, with a small mono "YOUR PICK" and a minus glyph at the right.
  - Inner keywords: **ultra-heavy condensed grotesk caps** (Anton / League Gothic / Impact class) in warm white `#f0ece4` at ~85–90% opacity, with an outer glow (~8–12 px, 40%) and a faintly eroded/grungy fill. They are set **huge, 80–90% of the inner window width**, in 1 or 2 stacked lines (SOMEDAY / PROJECT, EXCUSES / GO QUIET). - **Layout & composition (720×960):** header block y 20–192 (≈20% of height); sleeve x 162–560, y 215–838; video window x 232–505, y 288–773 (273×485 px, ~9:16, **38% of frame width**); the sleeve is shifted slightly left of centre, with "PLAY ▷" at the right (x ≈ 625, y ≈ 790–815) balancing it. Two stripes run full-bleed at y ≈ 872–890 (orange 6 px, mustard 6 px, black hairline), and the vote bar sits at x 38–680, y 898–945.
- **Assets:** a VHS sleeve built from rectangles (charcoal case with a hard drop shadow offset ~8 px right/down in a darker taupe, a cream card insert, an orange header band, an outlined "VHS" logo box, a barcode, the tri-stripe), a play-triangle icon, and the AI scenes (footballer, promenade, office men, wrist timer, hourglass). There is also a comic speech bubble with hand-lettered text.
- **Finishing:** the template is clean vector with only the case shadow. The inner edit has glow on its type, warm vignettes, soft depth of field (blurred backgrounds behind the footballer and the promenade), and a light/flare cone on the hook shot.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** ~0.77 inner cuts/s; inner shots 0.80–1.70 s (median 1.3 s), with the black end card held for 2.1 s. The list section (5.20–8.20) is the fastest: three shots of 0.83–1.30 s. The outer frame never cuts.
- **Easing signature:** almost no positional animation on type. Keywords **fade/glow on in ~4–8 frames with no scale or slide**, as if a light were switched on behind them. All camera motion is in the AI clips themselves: slow linear-feeling push-ins of 2–10% per shot. The single assertive move (shot 10) rises to its peak in ~10 frames and decays over ~8 (an ease-out arc). Measured global flow is tiny (mean 0.07–0.28 px/frame at 180 px) because 85% of the frame is static, so inner motion is diluted ~6× in the metrics.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:**
  - glow-on keyword (opacity 0→0.9 + glow radius 0→10 px, 4–8 frames);
  - word-by-word glow-on (shot 1–2, ~8 frames per word);
  - two-line stack appearing together (shots 7, 9);
  - comic speech-bubble pop (~3–4 frames) that survives a punch-in cut;
  - neon flicker-on (on 2 frames, off 1–4, on, then a ~8-frame colour cool-down from cyan to white);
  - exits are always hard cuts with no outro.
- **Camera:** the outer frame is locked. Inner: slow push-ins (shots 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8), one slight pull-back (shot 3) and one arc/dolly (shot 10). No shakes, whips or zoom transitions.
- **Secondary motion:** the spotlight switch-on in shot 1, the glowing timer display in shot 8, characters' small actions (car leaving, men exiting, the hourglass being placed). These are AI-video micro-actions rather than designed animation.
- **Sync (cuts vs. VO keyword onset):**
  - IDEAS +0.06 s, PLANS +0.05 s, EXCUSES +0.05 s, "this" +0.04 s, "make" +0.01 s, "it" +0.12 s, "someday" +0.18 s, "timer" −0.22 s (the timer close-up leads the word).
  - **Median: the cut lands 1–2 frames after the keyword starts**, so the word and the new image hit together.
  - Keywords are fully visible by the first or second frame after the cut, i.e. on the spoken word.
  - Beats (123 BPM, 0.488 s) are not the driver: cut-to-nearest-beat offsets scatter from −0.23 to +0.20 s.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **A static designed frame around moving content** makes any clip look curated and "exhibited". Spend the design effort on a print-quality layout (hairline rules, metadata, a vertical label, a numbered badge), then leave it completely still.
2. **Treat the window like a physical object** (a VHS sleeve, film can or polaroid). The metaphor ("Video Club", "Side A", "Hi-Fi Stereo") gives the brand a world at zero motion cost.
3. **Accent discipline:** one orange-red (`#cf4113`) is used for every interactive or status element (badge, "now playing", play icon, the vote number). Everything else is cream and charcoal.
4. **Participation CTA baked into the layout:** a persistent monospaced "COMMENT 1 TO VOTE" bar that stays visible for 100% of the runtime instead of an end card.
5. **One noun = one scene = one glowing word behind the subject.** Depth-compositing the keyword behind the head makes flat AI stills feel 3D and cinematic.
6. **Cut on the keyword's first syllable (+1–2 frames).** Picture, word and type all land together, and the narrator's pauses do the breathing.
7. **Save a new colour for the payoff:** cyan appears only for "the timer" and "MAKE IT", so the finale reads as a different register.
8. **End on black with small type.** After 12 s of giant type, a small, centred end line feels intimate and final.

- **Layer stack (bottom → top):**

     - a case `#231d16` with `box-shadow: 12px 12px 0 #9c9283`;
     - an insert card `#f0e7c8`;
     - an orange header band with title, handle and number;
     - vertical sleeve texts;
     - a bottom strip with `<TriStripe colors={["#cf4113","#dc9a3a","#acbba8"]}/>`, an outlined VHS box, "FINALIST SERIES", and a barcode drawn as a repeat of 1/2/3 px bars.
  5. `<VideoWindow x={348} y={432} w={410} h={728}>`: an `overflow:hidden` div containing the guest `<OffthreadVideo>` or the inner composition
  6. `<PlayGlyph x={938} y={1210}>`
  7. `<StripeBand y={1308} colors={["#cf4113","#dc9a3a","#1e1c18"]} heights={[9,9,2]}>`
  8. `<VoteBar text='COMMENT "1" TO VOTE' right="YOUR PICK" font="Courier Prime 700" bg="#1e1c18" numberBg="#cf4113">` with a 4 px offset shadow
- **Inner edit components:**
  - `<KeywordBehindSubject word plate matte>`: stack plate → type → subject matte (a roto'd PNG/alpha video of the head and shoulders) so the type sits behind the head. Type: Anton, `fontSize = 0.85 × windowWidth / (chars × 0.5)`, colour `#f0ece4`, `opacity: 0.88`, `textShadow: 0 0 10px rgba(255,245,225,.45)`, and a subtle grunge mask (`mask-image` noise PNG).
    - Entrance: `interpolate(frame, [0, 6], [0, 1], {easing: Easing.out(Easing.quad)})` on opacity, with glow blur 0→10 px over the same 6 frames; no scale.
    - Start it at the keyword's whisper start − 1 frame.
  - `<WordGlowOn words>` for multi-word lines: per-word timestamps; each word uses the same 6-frame glow-on.
  - `<SlowPush from={1} to={1.08} frames={durationInFrames}>`, linear or `Easing.inOut(Easing.sin)`, applied to the plate only. The type stays fixed, so the subject moves relative to the word.
  - `<SpeechBubble text font="Comic Neue / hand-lettered" pop={spring({damping: 14, stiffness: 260, mass: 0.5})}>`, placed on a layer above the shot switch to survive the punch-in cut.
  - `<FlickerOnText text="MAKE IT COUNT" pattern={[0,0,1,1,0,1,1,1]} coolFrom="#5fe6ff" coolTo="#ffffff" coolFrames={10}>`, revealing word-by-word on VO timestamps.
- **Cut timing:** `cutFrame = round(wordStart × fps) + 1` for each keyword shot.
- **Tricky bit (text behind subject):** generate the matte with a background-removal model per shot (rembg/SAM on the AI still, or a matte video for moving shots), then layer it in Remotion as `<Img>` or `<OffthreadVideo transparent>` above the type. Because the plate pushes in, apply the same `transform` to the plate and the matte.

## 9. Variation ideas
1. **Creator / UGC contest round-ups:** keep the sleeve template and swap the finalist number, handle and stripe colours per round. Rotate "Side A/B" for head-to-heads and make the vote bar read `COMMENT "1" OR "2"`.
2. **Film-club or book-club recommendation series:** replace the VHS sleeve with a cassette, vinyl sleeve or paperback cover. The window plays the trailer, the header names the pick, and the bar says "SAVE FOR MOVIE NIGHT".
3. **SaaS customer-story showcase:** same static-frame logic with a cleaner palette (white page, one brand accent). The window plays the customer's screen recording and the vertical labels carry metrics ("+38% RETENTION / CASE 04").
