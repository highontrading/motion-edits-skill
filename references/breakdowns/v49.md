# v49 — "I asked an AI agent to build this flicker transition" (sponsored NLE-agent demo, floating-UI showcase)

**Source creator handle:** earn.edits · **Format:** 720×1280, 9:16, 23.976 fps (about 19% of frames are exact duplicates, so the real motion cadence is ~19–20 unique fps, a slightly stuttery screen-capture feel), 26.7 s · **Shots:** ~17 real scenes (78 detected; ~60 of them are the deliberate background luma flicker and the in-image "square strobe" flashes, not cuts) · **Avg shot:** ~1.6 s per scene, but the picture changes every 2–6 frames because of the flicker · **Music BPM:** ~152 (light electronic bed) · **VO:** yes, English, calm first-person creator voice. Whisper hears the sponsor "invideo" as "NVIDIA" and "reel" as "real".

**Style tags:** ui-mockup, device-mockup, minimal-swiss, kinetic-type, word-by-word-captions, before-after, frame-in-frame, slide-push, whip-pan, zoom-through, motion-blur, depth-of-field, drop-shadow-heavy, chat-ui, cta-endcard, documentary-vo, *flicker-transition (new)*, *background-strobe (new)*, *curved-screen-ui (new)*, *blur-in-type (new)*

**One-line essence:** a near-monochrome off-white world where screen recordings float as bent, shadowed panels and push, whip and zoom through each other, while the subject (a strobing-squares transition) also drives the edit: the background, the article photos and the reel thumbnails all flicker in 2–4-frame bursts, so the whole video demonstrates the effect it is selling.

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** creator tool demo and sponsored integration ("I saw an effect → asked the AI tool → it built it → I tweaked it → result → comment for discount").
- **Beats:**
  - 0.00–1.84 **Hook / reference:** "I saved this flicker transition…". An Instagram-post mockup ("Reel 09", heart/comment/bookmark icons) plays the reference reel with flickering squares, then a zoom/blur punch into its content.
  - 1.84–3.17 **Instant payoff preview:** a "Final Output" label and a news-article card (Vox "Stripe is buying OpenRouter for $7.5 billion") flicker between portraits. Result first, process second: the before/after lands inside 3 s.
  - 3.17–9.93 **Setup:** "so I asked invideo to build it… sent the reference clip… let it study exactly how those squares strobed." Shows the NLE, then the agent chat, then a grid of saved reels with the question typed across it.
  - 9.97–13.72 **Process:** "Broke down the timing / Dropped in shape layers / Zero manual work." Each claim gets its own panel and caption.
  - 13.74–18.30 **Human touch:** "I nudged blink rate, tightened rectangle size, pushed contrast… inside chat." A three-line list builds word by word under a shrunken NLE.
  - 18.32–22.0 **Result:** "My footage now snaps and lands just like the reel I'd bookmarked months ago." The article flickers full-frame, then sits side by side with the original reference reel.
  - 22.08–26.73 **CTA:** Comment "INVIDEO" + "I'll send a link for up to 50% off on annual plans", over a washed-out flickering article, with the brand mascot. Fades to black.
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** frame 0 is a heavily blurred, over-scaled crop of the reel card. Within 6 frames it pulls back (zoom −1.8…−2.6 per frame) and racks into focus, revealing a phone-post mockup whose content is already strobing (black/white rectangles swapping every 2–3 frames). At 0.96 s a 1-frame smear zooms into the reel, so the word "like" fills the frame. Three changes per second plus the visible "bookmark" icon tie straight to the VO "I saved this".
- **Script → visual rule:** every VO sentence gets one floating "screen" (reel card, NLE, chat panel, article). The key verb phrase of each sentence ("It broke down the timing", "Dropped in Shape Layers", the three tweaks, the CTA) becomes a small, quiet caption. The type never shouts; the motion and flicker carry the energy.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** minimal Swiss / Apple-keynote UI showcase. Real screen recordings (an NLE with timeline markers, the agent chat sidebar, a Vox article) are treated as physical cards floating over a paper-white void. The NLE panel is **warped like a curved monitor** (its top and bottom edges bow outward ~10–15 px at 720 px width) and casts a soft grey drop shadow.
- **Backgrounds & textures:** always warm off-white `#f4f4f2` to `#f5f5f3`, with a different faint pattern per act:
  - 0–3.2 s: giant grey letterforms (earn.edits branding, "EDITS", "EARN") at ~15–20% contrast, set diagonally ~30°, cropped by the frame and drifting/rotating behind the cards.
  - 3.2–7.5 s: huge cropped "INVIDEO" (top edge) and "EDITOR" (bottom edge) in pale grey `#dcdcdc`, half off-frame.
  - 7.6–10 s: the background drops to mid-grey `#797979` (dimmed, like a modal overlay) behind the blurred reel grid.
  - 13.7–22.5 s: a pale grey squiggle-doodle pattern (thin random wavy strokes ~`#c9c9c9`, 2–3 px), like a soft topographic or noodle texture.
  - CTA: the flickering article is washed under a ~80% white overlay.
- **Background strobe:** across most of the video the whole background flashes from off-white `#f4f4f2` to light grey `#d1d1d1`–`#d6d6d6` (−15 to −25 luma) for **2–4 frames, every ~6–14 frames**, irregularly and not beat-locked. This is the "flicker" idea applied to the canvas itself, and it accounts for most of the detector's false cuts.
- **Colour:** essentially greyscale. Off-white `#f4f4f2`, flicker grey `#d4d4d4`, UI charcoal `#1f1f20`/`#222223`, near-black text `#111111`. Colour comes only from content: the NLE's blue clip bars (~`#2f7fd8`), the IG heart red (~`#e5484d`), the article photos (orange hair, red/blue backdrops), and the **colour-inverted cyan rectangles** (~`#5fb0d8` faces) inside the flicker effect. Contrast strategy: dark UI panels on light ground, black type on white.
- **Typography:**
  - One family throughout: a neo-grotesk display sans with tight negative tracking (Inter Display / SF Pro Display / Helvetica Now Display), sentence case, Regular weight for captions and Medium for the CTA headline.
  - "Final / Output" label: 2 stacked lines at top-left, ~22% of frame width, black at ~85% opacity, Regular.
  - Process captions ("It broke down the timing", "Dropped in Shape Layers"): one line, ~60–64% of frame width, cap height ~2.5% of frame height, `#333` on white, centred above the panel at y ≈ 8–25%.
  - Tweak list: 3 centred lines ("blink rate / tightened rectangle size / pushed contrast"), ~58% width, line height ~1.25, at y ≈ 64–75%.
  - "How those squares strobed?": the only big type. White, ~10% of frame width per glyph height, wider than the frame, set over the dimmed grey with a soft white bloom/ghost trail.
  - CTA: «Comment "INVIDEO"» in Medium, ~68% of width, black. The subtitle «I'll send a link for up to 50% off on annual plans» is ~45% of the headline size and fills in word by word from grey (`#9a9a9a`) to black.
  - **Emphasis rule:** no colour highlights. Emphasis comes from order and timing, with the newest word arriving blurred and grey and settling to black.
- **Layout & composition:** the hero panel is centred or slightly above centre. Captions sit in the top quarter (y 8–25%) or the lower-middle (y 64–75%) and never overlap the panel. In the result section, two portrait cards sit side by side (article left ~45% width, reference reel right ~32%) as a before/after diptych. The CTA headline sits at y ≈ 40%, so the mascot can fill the lower right.
- **Assets:** an IG-post frame mockup, NLE screen recording (timeline with blue markers, monitor, inspector), AI-agent chat panel screenshots, a Vox news article with Getty portraits, a 2×2 grid of saved reels ("$525Mil" money reel, sneakers, a walking figure), and the invideo blob mascot (black blob, two white eyes, one satellite dot).
- **Finishing / FX:** heavy Gaussian blur used as depth of field and as transition smear (panels arrive and leave 15–30 px blurred). Soft long drop shadows under cards (offset ~20 px down-right, ~25% black, blur ~40 px). A light chromatic fringe (orange/blue edge) on the flicker rectangles in the reel mockup. Slight sensor grain on the grey states. No vignette, no glow apart from the white bloom on the big question.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** ~17 scenes in 26.7 s (0.64 scene changes/s, median scene ~1.3 s). The *perceived* cut rate is ~3/s because of the flicker (the detector logged 2.9 cuts/s). The process section (8–14 s) changes a panel every ~1.2 s. The tweak list holds for 3.4 s, the calm "I'm in control" moment. The CTA holds 3.9 s.
- **Easing signatures (from per_frame data):**
  - **Arrivals are ease-out from blur:** the whip into the wide NLE peaks on frame 3–4 (pan −8.6) and decays over ~16 frames with no overshoot or sign flip. The zoom-out at 13.72 peaks in 1 frame (z −5.3) and decays over ~10 frames.
  - **Departures are ease-in:** the chat panel exits up with velocity 1.5 → 2.1 → 3.4 → 3.9 over 4 frames. The card spin-away at 2.6–3.17 accelerates from rot −0.7 to −4.8/frame over 12 frames. Each exit ends in a hard cut at peak velocity.
  - **Push-ins are linear:** NLE push 4.3–5.8 s at a steady +1.1…+1.8 zoom/frame.
  - The cut always lands at **peak speed**, and the incoming shot continues the same vector (motion bridge). This makes the cuts read as one continuous camera move: upward at 11.34, zoom-in at 17.1–17.4, zoom-out at 18.35–18.5.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:**
  - *Blur-in panel:* starts 20–30 px blurred and 105–110% scale, settles in 6–8 frames.
  - *Word blur-in:* each word fades from 0 to grey `#bdbdbd` with ~4 px blur, then to sharp black over 4–6 frames, appended to the line, which re-centres.
  - *Line fade-from-blur:* "Dropped in Shape Layers" arrives as a whole line in 6 frames.
  - *Italic-drop exit:* the list skews ~12° and slides down ~20 px with vertical smear over 4 frames.
  - *Crawling question:* type-on plus a constant leftward crawl.
  - *Mascot slide-ins* from the left and right edges.
  - *Fade to white* (3 frames) and *fade to black* (10 frames).
- **Camera:** a virtual camera over a 2.5D stack of cards. It uses one linear push (+~40% over 1.3 s), two whips (right at 5.5 s, left at 12.4 s; ~32–34 px/frame real), one vertical rise (~45 px/frame), two zoom-throughs (17.1 s in, 18.35 s out) and one zoom-out reveal (13.7 s). There's no shake and no rotation apart from the 3D card spin at 2.6 s.
- **Secondary motion:** the background strobe (2–4 frames grey every 6–14 frames), in-image square flicker on every piece of "output" footage (swap every 4–6 frames), slow letterform drift behind the cards, and the large mascot's idle bob. The ~19 fps duplicate-frame cadence adds a subtle stutter that suits the strobe theme.
- **Sync (measured against Whisper word times):**
  - Scene cuts land on or just after the key noun. The NLE cut is at 3.17, 0.03 s after "invideo" (3.14). The whip starts at 5.46 with "editor" at 5.50. The shape-layers cut is at 11.47, with "shape" at 11.46. The list zoom-out is at 13.72, with "I" at 13.74. The article cut is at 18.44, with "footage" at 18.42. The cut to "Final Output" is at 1.84, **0.14 s before** "try" (1.98).
  - Captions lag the voice. The process captions trail by ~0.05–0.15 s ("timing" spoken 10.34, sharp 10.51). The tweak-list words trail by 0.1–0.25 s ("tightened" 14.64 → faint 14.72 → sharp 14.85; "size" 15.20 → 15.35/15.52; "contrast" 16.02 → 16.22/16.35). The CTA subtitle trails by 0.3–0.5 s ("50%" 24.52 → 25.0).
  - Beats (152 BPM, 0.395 s) are **not** used for cuts. The flicker cadence (2 swaps per beat) gives a rhythmic texture that floats over the music grid instead.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Square-strobe flicker (the subject, also used as a transition between photos):** 18.6–22.4 s, 1.9–3.1 s, CTA background. Before the next image appears, 1–2 rectangles flash over the current one for 1–2 frames each. A rectangle can be a crop of the next image, a **colour-inverted** (cyan-negative) crop, a white block, or a black bar. Rectangles cover ~20–45% of the image area, are axis-aligned and placed at random. Then a hard cut to the next image. Image hold: 4–6 frames. Rebuild: per marker, render 2 frames of `<Rect>`s (random x/y/w/h within the card, `mix-blend-mode: difference` on white for the inverted ones, or `filter: invert(1)` on a cropped copy of the next image), then switch the source.
2. **Background strobe:** the whole canvas dips to `#d4d4d4` for 2–4 frames at irregular intervals of 6–14 frames. Rebuild: a seeded random schedule, `<AbsoluteFill style={{background: flicker ? '#d4d4d4' : '#f4f4f2'}}>`.
3. **Whip pan (card-to-card):** 5.46–5.84 and 12.39–12.72. 1 smear frame, then the incoming shot decelerates over 10–16 frames. Rebuild: translateX of the whole stage, `Easing.bezier(0.6,0,0.2,1)`, plus a directional blur (SVG `feGaussianBlur stdDeviation="24 0"`) scaled by velocity.
4. **Vertical slide-out/rise:** 7.34–7.88 and 11.2–11.8. The outgoing panel accelerates upward (ease-in, 4–5 frames) and the next layer continues upward, decelerating. Motion bridge.
5. **Zoom-through:** 17.10–17.40 (in) and 18.31–18.52 (out). Scale accelerates on the outgoing shot (+4%/frame), cut, then the incoming continues at the same rate and decays over 5 frames. Both sides are blurred 10–20 px at the cut.
6. **3D card spin-away:** 2.6–3.17. The article card rotates around Z (and slightly around X/Y) up to ~5°/frame while shrinking ~2%/frame, then a hard cut to a blurred landing.
7. **Grade dip:** 7.9–8.1, background to `#797979` with blur on everything except the type. Works like a modal "focus" state for the question.
8. **White flash cut:** 9.97 (luma 120 → 219 in 1 frame), resetting from the grey question state to the bright process state.
9. **Fade to white wash → CTA:** 22.31–22.44 (3 frames). The content stays at ~20% behind a white overlay.
10. **Fade to black:** 26.3–26.7, linear over 10 frames.

## 6. Sound design
Light, airy electronic bed at ~152 BPM (soft plucks/pads, 0.395 s beat), mixed under the VO and never ducked hard. Strong onsets at 5.32, 6.69–7.52 (cluster), 9.29, 10.12–10.40, 12.40, 13.77, 14.63, 15.93, 17.58, 18.39, 22.25 and 25.24 sit close to the whip (5.5), the panel rise (7.3–7.7), the white flash (9.97), the whip (12.4), the zoom-out (13.72) and the zoom-in (18.35–18.44). ## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Let the subject drive the edit.** The flicker the video is about is also its background, its photo transitions and its CTA texture, so the piece *is* the demo.
2. **Show the result in the first 3 s, then explain.** Reference (0–1.8 s) → "Final Output" (1.8–3.2 s) → process.
3. **Treat screen recordings as physical objects.** Float them on an off-white void, bend the wide ones like a curved monitor, give them long soft shadows, and move them with a camera rather than with cuts.
4. **Cut at peak velocity and continue the vector.** Exits ease in, entrances ease out from blur, and the direction carries across the cut (up → up, zoom-in → zoom-in).
5. **Quiet type, loud motion.** One neo-grotesk, sentence case, Regular weight, ~60% of frame width, black on white, revealed word by word with blur. The single exception (the white question across a dimmed screen) becomes the turning point.
6. **Monochrome stage, colour only from content,** so the inverted-cyan flashes and the orange-haired portraits pop.
7. **Irregular micro-flicker (2–4 frames)** keeps a static UI screen alive without any camera motion.
8. **Before/after diptych** (output card next to the original reference reel) proves the claim visually without saying "look how similar".

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** 1080×1920, 30 fps (or 24 for authenticity), duration = VO + ~0.5 s fade. Background `#f4f4f2`.
- **Layer stack per scene (bottom → top):** `<StrobeBackground base="#f4f4f2" flash="#d4d4d4" seed minGap={6} maxGap={14} flashLen={[2,4]}>` → `<BackdropPattern variant="giantLetters|squiggle">` (opacity 0.15–0.25, slow drift 0.3 px/frame) → `<FloatingPanel src curved shadow>` → `<FlickerMedia>` inside panels/cards → `<BlurInCaption>` → `<Mascot>` (CTA only).
- **Components:**
  - `FloatingPanel`: props `{src, curve: 0.04, shadow: '0 30px 60px rgba(0,0,0,.25)', enter: 'blur'|'slideUp'|'whip', exit}`. For the curved monitor, render the screenshot through an SVG `<feDisplacementMap>` with a vertical-gradient map, or slice it into 12 vertical strips with `perspective(1200px) rotateY()` per strip. A cheaper option is a 3D `rotateY` on two halves.
  - Blur-in entrance: `scale 1.08 → 1`, `blur 24px → 0`, `opacity 0 → 1` over 8 frames, `spring({damping: 26, stiffness: 170, mass: 0.9})` (no overshoot).
  - `FlickerMedia`: `{images[], holdFrames: [4,6], flashFrames: [1,2], rects: 1-2, invertChance: 0.5}`. For each swap, pick seeded random rects (20–45% area). Draw one as `<div style={{clipPath: inset(...)}}><Img src={next} style={{filter:'invert(1) hue-rotate(180deg)'}}/></div>`, and the other as a solid white or black block.
  - `BlurInCaption`: `{words: [{text, start}], font: 'Inter Display', weight: 400, tracking: '-0.03em', color: '#111'}`. Each word starts at VO start + 3–6 frames, goes opacity 0 → 1 and blur 6 → 0 px over 5 frames, colour `#bdbdbd → #111` over 6 frames, `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`.
  - `ItalicDropExit`: `skewX(0 → -12deg)`, `translateY(0 → 24px)`, `blur(0 → 8px)` vertical, `opacity → 0` over 4 frames, ease-in.
  - `WhipTransition`: both scenes in one stage; `translateX` of −110% over 10 frames with `Easing.bezier(0.6,0,0.2,1)`, horizontal blur `stdDeviation = |velocity| * 0.6`.
  - `ZoomThrough`: outgoing `scale 1 → 1.25` (ease-in, 4 frames) + blur, cut, incoming `scale 0.85 → 1` (ease-out, 6 frames) + blur 16 → 0.
  - `CardSpinAway`: `rotateZ 0 → -25deg`, `rotateX 0 → 15deg`, `scale 1 → 0.55` over 12 frames, `Easing.in(Easing.quad)`, then hard cut.
  - `CrawlQuestion`: white 120 px text, typed word by word; container `translateX = -max(0, textWidth - 0.9*W) * progress`. Ghost trail: 2 copies at 30% and 15% opacity offset +12 px and +24 px.
  - `CTAEndcard`: headline Medium 64 px at y = 40%, subtitle 28 px with word-by-word grey → black, small mascot `translateX(-60px → 0)` in 4 frames, large mascot `translateX(200px → 0)` + `blur(8px)` constant (depth of field) + idle `translateY = 6*sin(f/12)`; content behind under a `rgba(255,255,255,.8)` overlay.
- **Tricky bits:** keep all random flicker **seeded** (`random(seed + frame)`) so renders are deterministic. To get the duplicate-frame stutter, quantise time with `Math.floor(frame / 1.25) * 1.25` on motion layers only.

## 9. Variation ideas
