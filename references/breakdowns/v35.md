# v35 — "Free agentic video editor": invideo Agent Two UI-walkthrough promo

**Source creator handle:** earn.edits · **Format:** 720×1280, 9:16, 23.976 fps, 37.4 s (897 frames) · **Shots:** 13 detected; ~8 real scene/environment changes, carried by ~24 virtual-camera states (punch-ins, pull-outs, scrolls). Detected cuts 1–2 are strobe frames and cuts 9–10 are one glitch flash · **Avg shot:** ~4.7 s per environment, ~1.55 s per camera state · **Music BPM:** ~108 (soft electronic bed) · **VO:** yes, English, calm creator-recommendation tone ("I found a free…")

**Style tags:** ui-mockup, product-showcase, kinetic-type, glassmorphism, search-bar-cta, cursor-ui, chat-ui, typewriter, word-by-word-captions, serif-editorial, zoom-through, masking-reveal, shape-wipe, slow-push, motion-blur, glow, light-leak, logo-reveal, cta-endcard

**One-line essence:** a software promo built almost entirely from re-animated UI. The product's prompt box, chat and timeline are rebuilt as clean vector layers, framed by a virtual camera that punch-zooms to whatever the VO names. The UI is bookended by dreamy AI-landscape "glass" scenes that open and close through shaped iris windows.

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** creator-led tool recommendation / sponsored product walkthrough (hook → what it does → how-to steps → proof in the timeline → "it's free" → comment-keyword CTA).
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–3.6 **Hook:** the creator's own badge + word captions: "I found a free agentic video editor that can save you hours of work." The benefit is typed into a search bar over an idyllic landscape.
  - 3.7–8.9 **What it does:** the invideo logo and "Introducing Agent Two" over a sunlit desk. Footage thumbnails are dragged into the prompt box and a long prompt types itself; the camera punches in and the cursor clicks Send.
  - 9.0–15.3 **How-to steps:** dark UI scene. "Open invideo, head to the editor (punch-in on the Agent/Editor toggle), upload your footage (a thumbnail drops in), create an agent, tell it how you want it assembled (prompt types)."
  - 15.3–21.0 **Agent working:** the chat view with the user bubble and an "Analyzing your attachment…" checklist that ticks green line by line.
  - 21.0–32.2 **Proof:** the NLE timeline. Clips assemble, the agent posts a summary pop-up, clips get re-arranged on the tracks, and coloured semantic bars and green audio tracks glow in ("fully editable…").
  - 32.2–35.9 **Free:** a blob-shaped iris opens onto a sunset meadow; the search bar types "InVideo Editor is completely FREE", the camera punches in and the cursor clicks the arrow; a phone mockup rises.
  - 36.2–37.4 **CTA end card:** the black invideo blob logo pops in on white, with "Comment 'invideo' to try it out".
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** frames 0–3 strobe white/black/white/black (4 frames, 1 frame each) while a black disc flips in edge-on. On frame 4 it lands as the creator's round **EARNEDITS** badge with three app icons under it. From 0.21 s a speech-bubble caption above the badge swaps word-chunk by word-chunk ("I found" → "a free" → "agentic" → "video editor"), each bubble tilted to a different angle. Personal voice + flicker + constant text change.
- **Script → visual rule:** every UI noun the VO says gets a literal UI action at that moment: "head to the editor" → punch onto the Editor toggle; "upload your footage" → a thumbnail is dragged in; "builds your first edit on the timeline" → clips slide onto tracks; "move clips" → clips jump between tracks; "free" → the word FREE is typed. Benefit phrases ("save you hours of work", "completely FREE") are typed into a search bar, never set as big titles.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** a UI mockup / product demo in two moods. (a) **Dreamy glass scenes**: AI-generated photoreal landscapes (river meadow 1.7–3.7 s, sunlit desk room 3.7–8.9 s, sunset alpine meadow 32.5–35.9 s) with frosted-white UI floating on them. (b) **Dark product scenes** (9.0–32.2 s): near-black canvas with a faint dot grid, where the actual dark-theme UI is rebuilt and re-animated. White open and close bookend both.
- **Backgrounds & textures:** opening and end card are a white/light-grey radial vignette (`#f5f5f5` centre → `#cdcdcd` corners). The opener also has a faint pale-grey pattern of UI glyphs. Dark section: `#0b0b0b`–`#1a1a1a` with a ~12 px dot-matrix grid at ~6% opacity and a soft spotlight behind the active UI. The room scene adds columns of faint white chevrons (`>>>`, `vvv`) drifting over the walls, and prism/rainbow light-leak streaks.
- **Recurring "pixel-grid" motif:** UI elements materialise or flash through a grid of tiny squares or plus-signs. Cyan "+" grid when the search bar appears (2.0 s); yellow dot-matrix over the room as the iris finishes (3.84 s) and on the thumbnail drop (5.0 s); an orange mosaic flash over the card when Send is clicked (8.26 s); a yellow halftone dot field inside the blob iris (32.5 s). This is the video's signature finishing texture.
- **Colour:** neutrals carry everything. White `#efefef`/`#fdfdfd` UI cards, black `#0b0b0b`, dark card grey `#2b2b2b`. Each scene has one accent colour:
  - **Amber** `#d0a04c`/`#ae7f25` marks the newly typed words in the room-scene prompt.
  - **Blue** `#4aa3e8` marks the newest letters in the search bar.
  - **Green ticks** `#3cc35a` in the agent checklist.
  - The timeline's semantic bars are muted red `#963d3f`/`#b77074`, ochre `#bd974c`, indigo `#4c5f9a` and pink `#e6b1c1`; clips are teal `#245b55` with orange thumbnails; audio tracks are lime green.
  - Landscapes are saturated sky blue `#4181d2` and green, then a warm golden sunset.
- **Typography:**
  - **Hook captions:** neutral grotesk (SF Pro Text / Inter Medium), sentence case, black on white rounded pills (radius ~10 px, soft 0 4 12 rgba(0,0,0,.15) shadow). Cap height ~3.5% of frame width (~32 px font at 1080 w). Pills are tilted −8° to +5°, varying per chunk.
  - **Badge:** "EARNEDITS" in white bold grotesk caps, tracked +5%, inside a black circle ~24% of frame width.
  - **Search-bar text:** Inter/SF Regular, grey `#666`, ~4.5% W; the last 2–4 typed letters are tinted blue before settling to grey.
  - **Hero title "Introducing Agent Two":** **condensed high-contrast editorial serif** (Instrument Serif / PP Editorial New Condensed-like), regular weight, title case, off-white `#e8e8e8`. Set at ~68% frame width at wide framing; under punch-ins it fills more than 100%. Words fade in one by one with blur.
  - **UI text:** Inter-like 13–16 px equivalents in white or grey, "invideo" wordmark in a rounded bold sans.
  - **End-card CTA:** "Comment 'invideo' to try it out" in a **heavy condensed grotesk** (Anton / League Gothic-like), black, sentence case, ~74% of frame width, at y ≈ 70%.
- **Layout & composition:** everything centred on the vertical axis. Hero UI cards span 83–92% of frame width and sit at y ≈ 37–60%; logos sit 15–20% above them. Lower thirds are left empty, partly for the IG UI and partly as air. Punch-ins re-centre on the element the VO names (a toggle, Send button, chip, track).
- **Assets:** creator badge with app icons (music note, green Reels icon, purple bookmark), 3D emoji stickers (alarm clock, laptop, flame), the invideo ghost-blob logo, rebuilt UI (prompt box with "+ Agent | Editor" toggle, "Agent Two Ultra ⌄" model picker, round Send arrow, reference chips with thumbnails), chat bubble with avatar, NLE timeline (Edit/Audio/Color/Preview/Structure tabs, transport controls, "Semantic" track header), hand-grab cursor, macOS arrow cursor, a phone mockup showing a social feed.
- **Finishing / FX:**
  - Heavy **motion blur** on every camera move and element entrance (e.g. 5.00 s, 15.52 s, 17.52 s, 20.27 s, 26.03 s).
  - **Bloom/glow** on the timeline bars as they wipe in.
  - Frosted-glass cards (backdrop blur ~20 px, white at ~85%).
  - Prism light-leaks in the room; a soft vignette everywhere.
  - **Mirror-edge tiling** when the camera pans past the edge of the room plate (7.4–8.9 s: plants and the chair appear mirrored, like AE Motion Tile with "mirror edges").

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** only ~8 real scene changes in 37 s (0.21/s), but a camera-state change roughly every 1.5 s (≈24 states). Rhythm comes from punch-ins and pull-outs inside one UI canvas, not from cuts. The fastest section is the hook (0–3.7 s: 4 visual states). The longest holds are the explanation of agent work (prompt 3.9 s, chat 4.75 s) and the final "keep editing" timeline hold (2.5 s, almost static).
- **Easing signatures (from per_frame.csv):**
  - **Punch-ins / whips land at peak velocity.** The first frame of the new framing already carries the maximum flow, then decays exponentially. Whip at 7.38: −9.4 → −5.2 → −7.1 → −2.7 → −2.3 → −2.0 → … −1.1 px/frame by frame 10. That is an ease-out of ~10 frames with no ease-in (`Easing.out(Easing.exp)` or `bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)`).
  - **Zoom pulses:** 20.27 pull-out ramps over 3 frames (z −0.6 → −1.8 → −2.4), then decays in ~8 frames, a smooth ease-in-out of ~11 frames (`bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)`). The 35.16–35.49 pull is the same shape over 9 frames.
  - **Jolts:** single-frame spikes (2.71 s rot −3.5°, 4.96–5.00 s pan_y +4.2/−5.2) followed by ~4 frames of residual. These act as hit punctuation on stressed words. There is no measurable overshoot (no sign flip in zoom) on camera moves. Overshoot only appears on small elements (logo pop, stickers).
  - **Hard punch cuts** (10.30, 11.43, 24.77, 27.24) show a 1-frame diff spike and then < 0.5 flow: an instantaneous reframe, often with 1–2 frames of residual scale settle.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:**
  - **Edge-on disc flip** + strobe (frames 0–4).
  - **Chunk caption swap** in tilted pills (hard swap, no tween, about 1 chunk per 0.4 s).
  - **Pixel-grid materialise** for glass UI (~6 frames).
  - **Typewriter with colour-lead**: the last 3–5 words/letters are tinted (amber, blue or grey) and settle to the base colour ~0.3 s later. Rate is ~20–25 chars/s.
  - **Word-by-word blur fade** for the serif title (~6 frames per word, 0.3 s stagger).
  - **Drag-and-drop thumbnail** with the hand cursor (≈0.6 s path, drops with a blur→sharp settle of ~4 frames).
  - **Checklist spinner → green tick**, ~1 s apart.
  - **Glow-wipe bars** L→R (~8–10 frames with a bloom trail).
  - **Pop-up card scale** from 0.9 + fade (~5 frames).
  - **Logo pop with wobble** (~10 frames).
  - **Phone slide-up** (~6 frames).
- **Camera:** a virtual 2D camera over flat UI. Net zoom across the timeline section (shot 11 in summary) is −25.8 with rot −29.5 accumulated: many small pushes and pulls rather than one move. Typical framings are ~1× (wide card), ~2.3× (toggle or Send button) and ~1.4× (timeline close). Slow pushes of +4–6% over 2–3 s fill the "reading" holds. There are no 3D orbits.
- **Secondary motion:** the cursor is always doing something (hand grab, arrow click), checklist spinners rotate, chevron columns drift on the room walls, floating cards bob ±4 px in the meadow scene, and light-leaks shimmer.
- **Sync:**
  - **VO-locked.** Camera events land 0.0–0.15 s **before** or on the word that names them: punch to the Editor toggle at 10.30 vs "head" 10.22 (+0.08) and "editor" 10.74; pull-out at 11.43 on "upload" 11.30; jolt on "hours" (+0.01); zoom-out on "parts" (+0.01); punch on "the best part" (−0.05); blob iris −0.08 before "An"; final white wash on "it" (+0.01).
  - **Beat alignment is secondary but present** at section boundaries. River cut 1.67 vs beat 1.65 (+0.02). Punch 10.30 vs beat 10.33 (−0.03). Search-bar cut 34.70 vs beat 34.71 (−0.01). Glitch 21.06 vs beat 21.13 (−0.07).
  - Typed text runs slightly *ahead* of speech ("FREE" completes as "free" is spoken; prompt text streams far faster than VO, as background texture).

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Strobe-in** (0.00–0.17): white/black alternating single frames ×4 while the logo disc flips in. Rebuild: a `<Sequence>` of 4 frames with `background: frame%2 ? '#000' : '#f2f2f2'`; the disc uses `rotateY(90°→0°)`, a 3-frame ease-out and a 6 px blur→0.
2. **White-haze → scene dissolve** (1.67–1.88): the frame starts as a pure blue sky gradient with white haze from the bottom, and the landscape fades up within ~5 frames. Rebuild: crossfade from a white-to-sky gradient plate to the scene with an opacity ramp of 5 frames and scale 1.04→1.
3. **Circular lens iris** (3.67–3.84): a circle with a 2 px white rim and an inner glass refraction edge grows from ~60% to past the frame diagonal in 4 frames, revealing the next scene inside it. A yellow dot-matrix flash follows for ~4 frames. Rebuild: `clipPath: circle(r at 50% 50%)` with r from 30% to 120% over 4 frames, `Easing.in(Easing.quad)`, plus a `box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 2px #fff8` ring div.
4. **Punch-in whip** (7.38): the cut lands on a 2.3× framing already moving left at ~38 px/frame and decelerates over 10 frames; mirror-edge tiling fills the exposed background. Rebuild: `scale: 2.3`, `translateX: interpolate(f, [0,10], [+120, 0], {easing: Easing.out(Easing.exp)})`, with a directional blur that decays in the same 10 frames; tile the background plate mirrored ×3.
5. **Dip to black with zoom kick** (8.88–9.01, 3 frames): opacity 1→0 with a ~3% scale-down.
6. **Hard punch / pull reframes** (10.30, 11.43, 22.11, 24.77, 27.24, 28.57, 29.74): instant scale change between preset framings, each starting on the VO keyword. Rebuild: a `CameraRig` with keyframed `{scale, x, y}` and `step` or 2-frame interpolation, plus a 1–2 frame settle.
7. **Collapse-streak** (15.31–15.52, ~5 frames): the title letters blur and spread, and the card squashes vertically into a thin motion-blurred horizontal line that then vanishes. The next UI fades up. Rebuild: `scaleY 1→0.05`, `scaleX 1→1.1`, `filter: blur(0→12px)` horizontal, `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)`.
8. **Scroll-whip** (17.43–17.77): content translates up ~40% of the frame with vertical motion blur. Use an 8-frame `bezier(0.7,0,0.2,1)`.
9. **Glitch flash** (21.02–21.15, 3 frames): two near-white frames with offset black rectangles and torn UI fragments. Rebuild: 2 frames of `#d8d8d8` with 3–4 randomly offset black `div` slices and a copy of the outgoing UI shifted ±30 px horizontally.
10. **Blob-shape iris** (32.24–32.57, ~8 frames): the brand's own logo silhouette (cream fill, thin white outline) scales up from the centre, carrying the next scene and a yellow halftone texture inside, until it overfills the frame. Rebuild: an SVG `<clipPath>` with the logo path, `scale 0.1→3.5`, `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)`, with a halftone `radial-gradient` pattern overlay that fades out over the last 3 frames.
11. **Slide + wash to white** (35.87–36.16, 7 frames): scene elements slide left while the luma ramps to white, and the end card's text is already present underneath. Rebuild: a white solid with opacity 0→1 over 7 frames, the outgoing layer translating −15% X.

## 6. Sound design
- A soft, airy electronic bed at ~108 BPM under a calm, close-mic VO. The music never competes, and there is no drop.
- Strong onsets at 0.09/0.23 (the strobe/badge hit), 2.21, 3.74 (iris), 7.36 (whip), 9.10 (title), 11.29–11.33 (pull-out), 14.28–15.44 and 21.71–23.52 (timeline build). These suggest light whooshes on camera moves, soft UI clicks on Send and the toggle, and keyboard ticks under the typing.
- VO pauses between clauses are short (0.2–0.5 s). The largest gap is 13.28→13.82.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Rebuild the UI as vector layers, don't screen-record it.** Clean, oversized, motion-blurred UI pieces you can punch into at 2–3× without pixelation look far more premium than captured footage.
2. **One virtual camera, many framings.** Keep a single UI canvas on screen and change framing on the keyword (≈1.5 s per framing). Cuts are reserved for environment changes.
3. **Land moves at peak velocity.** Punches and whips start at full speed on frame 1 and ease out over ~10 frames. There is no wind-up.
4. **Colour-lead typing.** Newly typed words glow in one accent colour and then settle, so the eye follows the type cursor even when the text is unreadably small.
5. **A pixel-grid materialise/flash motif repeated 4–5 times** gives a stock UI demo a recognisable house texture.
6. **Shape irises from the brand.** The outro transition uses the product logo's silhouette as the mask, and the intro uses a lens circle.
7. **Bookend dark UI with dreamy photoreal "glass" scenes.** Light, airy plates for emotional claims (save hours, it's free) and a dark canvas for functional proof.
8. **VO-locked micro-events:** every UI verb said aloud (head to, upload, move, change) is visualised within ±0.15 s.
9. **Comment-keyword CTA end card**: white, logo plus one line of heavy condensed type, held for at least 1.2 s.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, ~1125 frames (37.5 s). Drive all event times from the VO word-timestamp JSON.
- **Layer stack (dark UI scene):**
  1. `<DotGridBg color="#0b0b0b" dot="#ffffff10" spacing={18}>`
  2. `<Spotlight radial>`
  3. `<CameraRig keyframes>` wrapping the UI canvas
  4. `<PromptCard theme="dark">`, `<ChatThread>`, `<TimelineMock>`
  5. `<Cursor type="hand|arrow" path>`
  6. `<Vignette>`
- **Layer stack (glass scene):**
  1. `<BgPlate src=landscape mirrorEdges>`
  2. `<LightLeak prism>`
  3. `<ChevronColumns opacity={0.25}>`
  4. `<GlassCard blur={20} bg="rgba(255,255,255,.85)" radius={28}>`
  5. `<EmojiSticker>` ×3
  6. `<PixelGridFlash color>`
- **Components:**
  - `CameraRig`: props `[{at, scale, x, y, mode:'cut'|'ease'}]`. For `cut` mode, start a 10-frame decay from an offset: `x = target + offset*Math.exp(-0.35*t)`. `ease` mode uses `Easing.bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)` over 9–11 frames. Add a horizontal motion blur proportional to velocity (SVG `feGaussianBlur stdDeviation={v*0.6} 0`).
  - `TypeOnColorLead`: `charsPerSec=22`, `leadWords=4`, `leadColor="#d0a04c"` (or `#4aa3e8`), lead → base colour over 8 frames, plus a 1 px caret that blinks every 16 frames.
  - `SerifWordReveal`: Instrument Serif; each word opacity 0→1 and `blur 8→0px` over 6 frames, 9-frame stagger.
  - `ChunkCaptionPill`: chunks from the transcript (1–2 words), a hard swap at word start −2 frames, rotation chosen from `[-8,-3,2,5]`, a white pill with shadow.
  - `PixelGridMaterialize`: a canvas of 12 px cells; each cell switches on at `random*6` frames (a "+" glyph or a square); the target element's opacity follows the share of lit cells; cells fade out by frame 8.
  - `IrisReveal shape="circle"|svgPath`: `clipPath` scale 0.1→3.5 over 4–8 frames, `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)`, 2 px white rim.
  - `GlitchFlash`: 2 frames of light grey with random black slices.
  - `GlowWipeBar`: width 0→100% over 9 frames with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`, and `filter: drop-shadow(0 0 12px color)` that fades from 1→0.2 over 15 frames.
  - `ChecklistItem`: spinner (rotate 360°/20 frames) → green check scale 0.6→1 with `spring({damping: 14, stiffness: 220})`.
  - `LogoPop`: `spring({damping: 9, stiffness: 160, mass: 0.7})`, scale 0.15→1, rotate −20°→0.
- **Tricky bits:**
  - **Mirror-edge background:** render the plate 3× wide as `[flipX | normal | flipX]` so whips never reveal edges.
  - **Collapse-streak:** animate `scaleY` on the card wrapper while stretching a duplicate with heavy horizontal blur.
  - **UI fidelity:** build the UI in JSX/CSS (Inter font, real radii) so punch-ins stay crisp; render the UI canvas at 2× and scale down.
