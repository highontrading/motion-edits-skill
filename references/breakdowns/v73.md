Process footage plays as fast as a montage.
- **Script → visual rule:** one sentence per shot. The sentence builds word by word on an eighth-note grid, and the single most important number or noun gets an extra treatment: "billion" is 2× size with a hand-drawn yellow ellipse, "of the end." gets a yellow underline, and "25 MILLION" is circled with a white marker loop.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** photoreal **AI-generated stills** (the CEO in a suit in several settings, a wood desk with AI newspapers that carry garbled body text, a library office, a gallery wall), rotoscoped and rebuilt in 2.5D with real show stills for the montage. Each still gets a fake camera move plus a lighting pass (lamp cone, window-light stripes on the gallery wall).
- **Backgrounds & textures:** a **full-frame screen texture** over the finished edit: fine vertical scanlines (~2 px pitch) plus a halftone dot grid, visible on every key frame. Light film grain, a strong dark vignette, and soft grey haze in the upper corners.
- **Colour / grading:** backgrounds are **desaturated to near-B/W** (street, office, gallery, HQ: sat 10–40 on the 0–255 scale). The hero keeps colour: a navy suit `#1c2a4a` and blue tie `#2c4fa0` against grey, wood browns `#513228`/`#624034` on the newspaper desk, and the montage's native colours (Squid Game red `#7f2145` on teal `#3a5b68`; Wednesday and Stranger Things in cold blue `#16283c`). **One accent hue, acid yellow**, is used for every annotation: ellipse and underline `#e6dc3a`, money stacks recoloured yellow-green (`#c9d41e` highlights, `#8c980e` mids).
- **Typography:**
  - Captions: **high-contrast italic transitional serif** (Instrument Serif Italic / PP Editorial New Italic / Playfair Display Italic-like), sentence case, warm white `#efebe4` with a soft dark shadow (blur ~6 px, 40%) and a faint glow. Size tiers: body words ≈ 22–30% of frame width per line (cap height ≈ 3% of frame height), and the hero word ≈ 43% of width ("billion", cap height ≈ 6%). Lines are stacked ragged and centred or left-aligned in the negative space beside the subject. Tracking is tight.
  - Small date line "In 2022" under "Netflix" in the same serif at ~60% size: a museum-placard layout under the gallery frame.
  - Finale: switches to a **bold geometric/rounded sans** (Gilroy Bold / Product Sans Bold-like), lowercase "the streaming wars" in pure white with a strong white outer glow (radius ~12 px), pinned onto the HQ facade **behind the CEO's head**. "Netflix" earlier appears the same way as glowing signage on the building. The switch from serif story captions to a glowing sans "signage" style marks the payoff.
  - Annotations: yellow hand-drawn ellipse (stroke ≈ 5 px) around "billion", yellow brush underline under "of the end.", and a thin white marker loop around the 25 MILLION headline.
- **Layout:** the subject is centred or slightly right, and type sits in the left or upper-left negative space (x 6–50%, y 25–50%). Text is **layered behind the subject** whenever the subject overlaps it ("beginni[ng]" is cut by the CEO's shoulder; "Netflix didn't just survive" passes behind him). Montage titles sit dead centre over the face or chest at y ≈ 50–55%.
- **Assets:** AI stills (CEO ×5 poses, newspapers, money stacks, library, office), real show stills (Squid Game guard, Wednesday, Eleven), real posters (Delete, The Kitchen), a gold ornate picture frame PNG, and a lamp-light cone shape.
- **Finishing / FX:** volumetric lamp cone (cream trapezoid, Add/Screen at ~40%), window-light gradient stripes on the gallery wall, defocus blur in and out on transitions, motion blur on whips, the scanline+halftone overlay, and vignette.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** the AE section cuts every 0.2–2.0 s (comp jumps). The final edit runs 13 shots in 17.2 s (avg 1.32 s). Story shots are 1.8–2.2 s, the show montage is 0.8 s per show, and the finale is a 3 s hold. Within a shot the pace comes from the word builds (one every 0.25 s), not from cuts.
- **The eighth-note word grid:** every caption word or phrase appears exactly **6 frames (0.25 s) after the previous one**: "Netflix lost" 8.75 / "one million" 9.0 / "subscribers" 9.25 / "in a single" 9.5 / "quarter" 9.75; "People" 10.0 → "of the end." 11.0 → underline 11.25; "Forty" 14.0 / "five" 14.25 / "billion" 14.5 / "dollars" 14.75; "and" 18.0 → "moments." 18.75; "the" 23.5 / "streaming" 23.75 / "wars" 24.0. At 123 BPM an eighth note is 0.244 s, so the text ticks in time with the music.
- **Easing signatures:**
  - **Push into a zoom-through:** exponential ease-in over 16 frames (z +0.4 → +5.2/frame), then a 6-frame ease-out on the far side.
  - **Push-ins and pull-backs** after a cut: ease-out decays over 16–22 frames (z +3.7 → 0.25; −5.6 → −0.2, ratio ≈ 0.82/frame). There is no overshoot anywhere (no sign flips).
  - **Whip pans:** a 4–7-frame ease-in up to ~40 px/frame, 3–6 frames of blur with a roll (±2°/frame), then a ~13-frame ease-out on the new scene.
  - **Caption entrances:** blur-in (≈10 px → 0, opacity 0 → 1) over ~4–8 frames with no positional motion. Phrase replacement (shot 2) is an instant swap with a 2-frame blur.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:** word blur-in on a 6-frame grid, in-place phrase replacement, stacked one-word-per-line build, slide-in behind the subject (22.5), annotation write-on (ellipse ~6 frames, underline ~4 frames), colour flip on the keyword (money grey → yellow), keyword-colour flicker, defocus-in for montage stills (2–3 frames), defocus-out before a cut (5 frames).
- **Camera:** every shot is a still with a virtual camera move. Moves are short and decisive: push-in after the cut, a pan to reveal the second half of a sentence (shot 3), two whips to link three locations (shots 6–8), and a pull-out plus defocus to end. Parts of the final render step at ~15 fps (a duplicate frame every 2–3 frames in shots 3, 6–8 and 12), which gives the moves a slightly staccato, filmic cadence.
- **Secondary motion:** money colour flicker, light-cone shimmer, small handheld-like rotation drift (rot ±0.5°) in the finale.
- **Sync:** every final-edit cut falls on the **eighth-note grid**: either on the beat (11.875 −60 ms, 13.667 +14 ms to strong onset, 15.833 −26 ms to strong onset, 21.208 −61 ms to onset, 7.917 −94 ms) or on the half-beat (+0.19–0.24 s: 9.79, 16.08, 17.58, 19.54, 22.0). The keyword hits (yellow money at 14.5 = beat 14.396 +0.1; "wars" at 24.0 ≈ beat 24.242 −0.24) follow the same grid. There is no VO to sync to.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Process-to-final hard cut** (7.92): the AE UI disappears and the finished render starts full-frame on the beat. It works as a reveal: "here's what it becomes".
2. **Zoom-through picture frame** (8.0–8.9): an accelerating push into a framed photo (B/W, placard caption) until the frame's inner edge leaves the screen, then the photo, now in colour, continues as the next shot. ~16 frames ease-in plus 6 frames ease-out. Rebuild: scale the wall comp from 1 → ~5.5 with `Easing.in(Easing.exp)` while cross-fading the photo's saturation from 0 → 1 during the last 6 frames.
3. **Grade-drop cut** (9.79): a hard cut from a colour shot to a B/W shot, so mood changes with the grade.
4. **Whip pan with roll** (16.0–16.5, 17.3–17.8): linked lateral whips across one virtual office. Peak ~40–44 px/frame at 720 px, rotation ±2°/frame and directional blur during 3–6 frames, and the scene swap hidden at peak blur.
5. **Defocus out → cut** (19.33–19.54) and **defocus-in** on the incoming montage stills (19.54, 20.42, 21.21; 2–8 frames).
6. **Keyword-colour flip** (14.5) and **flicker** (15.0–15.8): the selective-colour element toggles between grey and acid yellow every 2–6 frames, a strobe within the shot.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Show the build, then the result.** 8 s of sped-up AE timeline (roto, solids, light cones, gizmos) establishes craft and makes the final 17 s feel earned. This hook works well for tutorial and "how I made this" accounts.
2. **Stills become cinema** through roto, a single lighting addition per scene (lamp cone, window stripes) and a decisive 16–22-frame camera move. No footage is needed.
3. **Desaturate the world, colour the point.** B/W sets with the subject's suit, the money or the show still in colour, and exactly one annotation accent (acid yellow).
4. **Italic editorial serif + word builds on an eighth-note grid** (6 frames at 24 fps, 123 BPM) give a calm, premium documentary voice even without VO.
5. **Type lives in the scene:** captions tuck behind the subject, sit on building facades as glowing signage, and pan out of frame with the camera.
6. **Hand-drawn emphasis on the number** (ellipse, underline, marker loop), drawn in 4–6 frames exactly when the number lands.
7. **Physical transitions only:** zoom through a picture frame, whips through one continuous office, defocus pulls. There are no generic dissolves.
8. **A screen texture** (scanlines + halftone + vignette) unifies AI stills, show stills and posters into one look.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `24 fps` (or 30 fps with a `posterize` step of 2 on camera moves), ~410 frames for the edit alone, or ~600 including a process intro (screen recording supplied as video).
- **Layer stack per scene (bottom → top):** `<StillPlate src grade={{saturate: 0.1}}>` → `<HeroCutout src saturate={1}>` (the roto'd subject or object as a PNG, or a segmentation matte on user footage) placed **above** the caption layer for text-behind-subject → `<LightCone>` (SVG trapezoid with a linear gradient, `mix-blend-mode: screen`, opacity 0.4) → `<SerifCaption>` → `<Annotation>` → `<VirtualCamera>` wrapping everything (scale/translate/rotate) → `<ScreenTexture>` (scanlines + halftone + vignette) → `<Grain>`.
- **Components:**
  - `<WordGrid words startFrame stepFrames={6}>`: each word `opacity: interpolate(f,[0,5],[0,1])`, `filter: blur(interpolate(f,[0,5],[10,0])px)`, `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`; supports `mode="build"|"replace"|"stack"` (one word per line). Derive `stepFrames` from BPM: `fps * 60 / bpm / 2`.
  - `<SerifCaption>`: `font-family: 'Instrument Serif'; font-style: italic; color: #efebe4; text-shadow: 0 2px 6px rgba(0,0,0,.4), 0 0 18px rgba(255,255,255,.15)`; base size `0.045 * width`, hero ×2.
  - `<GlowSign text>` for the finale: a bold sans (Gilroy/Poppins 700), `text-shadow: 0 0 12px #fff, 0 0 28px rgba(255,255,255,.6)`, perspective-matched to the facade with `transform: perspective(800px) rotateY(-8deg)`.
  - `<HandEllipse>` / `<HandUnderline>`: SVG path, stroke `#e6dc3a`, width 5, `strokeDashoffset` from length → 0 over 6 frames (ellipse) or 4 frames (underline), `Easing.out(Easing.quad)`.
  - `<ColourFlip mask>`: a duplicate of the plate masked to the money, `filter: grayscale(1)` → `sepia(1) saturate(6) hue-rotate(20deg)`; flicker by toggling on frames `[0,2,5,6,9,…]`.
  - `<ZoomThroughFrame>`: scale `interpolate(f,[0,16],[1,5.5],{easing: Easing.in(Easing.exp)})`, centred on the frame's inner rectangle; the incoming plate starts at scale 1.2 and eases to 1.0 over 6 frames.
  - `<WhipPan dir>`: translateX ease-in 6 frames → swap at peak → ease-out 13 frames (`Easing.bezier(0.6,0,0.2,1)` across both), plus `rotate` ±4° at the peak and `@remotion/motion-blur` `<CameraMotionBlur samples={10}>`.
  - `<PushIn>` after each cut: `spring({damping: 30, stiffness: 60, mass: 1})` scale 1 → 1.08 (≈20 frames, no overshoot).
  - `<DefocusOut frames={5}>`: `filter: blur(0→12px)` before the cut; `<DefocusIn frames={3}>` on the incoming shot.
  - `<ScreenTexture>`: `repeating-linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(0,0,0,.12) 0 1px, transparent 1px 3px)` + a radial-dot halftone PNG at 10% `multiply` + a radial vignette (`rgba(0,0,0,.55)` at the edges).
  - `<PosterizeTime fps={15}>`: compute camera transforms from `Math.floor(frame / 1.6) * 1.6` for the stepped feel.
- **Process intro (optional):** a `<Video>` of the AE screen recording at 4–8× speed (`playbackRate`), hard-cut every 0.4–1.6 s on the beat grid, then a hard cut into the final edit on a downbeat.

## 9. Variation ideas
1. **Founder or company story for any brand** (e.g. "Nike almost went bankrupt…"): the same AI-still + roto grammar, B/W sets with the brand colour kept, and serif word builds on the music grid. Swap the acid yellow for the brand's accent.
2. **Personal finance Short** ("How $1,000 became $45,000"): a desk of cash with a selective-colour flip on each number reveal, hand-drawn ellipses on figures, and a zoom-through a framed bank statement as the opener.
3. **Editor or agency portfolio reel:** 6–8 s of the AE or Remotion timeline (or the user's own editing UI) sped up, a hard cut to the finished piece, and a glowing-sign finale with the creator's handle placed on a building behind them (text-behind-subject on user footage via a segmentation matte).
