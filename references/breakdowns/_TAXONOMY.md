## Added during analysis (canonical tag ← aliases used in individual breakdowns)
Breakdowns were written in parallel, so some use alias names. To find every precedent for a device, grep for the canonical tag **and** its aliases.

### Frame rate & camera
- **on-twos**: animation stepped at 12–18 fps inside a 24/30 fps file ← animate-on-twos, on-twos-12fps, on-twos-15fps, on-twos-animation, on-twos-hold, stepped-fps, posterized-frame-rate, posterized-framerate, mixed-frame-rate
- **momentum-cut**: the outgoing move accelerates into a hard cut at peak speed and the incoming shot continues the same direction, then decays ← velocity-matched-cut, speed-matched-cut, momentum-matched-cut, ramp-into-cut, zoom-punch-cut, blur-masked-cut, spin-zoom-transition
- **settle-zoom**: every scene opens ~108–118% scaled and decays to 100% ← zoom-settle
- **canvas-reframe**: one large layout explored by a virtual camera instead of cuts ← virtual-camera-ui, one-world-continuous-camera
- **letterbox-reframe**: animated black bars change the canvas shape ← letterbox-bars, letterbox-format-shift
- **sentence-surface-cut**: a hard cut to a new background surface on each sentence's first word
- **motion-bridge**: object motion continues across a cut, or the next hero pre-laps over the outgoing shot ← object-pre-lap

### Flashes, focus & colour transitions
- **flash-frame**: 1–3 white/colour frames, or an exposure ramp, as cut punctuation ← flash-cut, flash-frame-cut, strobe-flash
- **invert-flash**: 1–3 colour-negative frames on a cut or impact word ← invert-strobe, strobe-flicker
- **color-wash-flash**: a full-frame flat brand colour floods in for 2–5 frames ← colour-sweep-wipe, logo-color-flash, click-ripple-flash
- **background-strobe**: irregular 2–4 frame brightness dips of the canvas as texture
- **bloom-defocus-cut**: a cut hidden inside a full-frame blur + exposure bloom
- **focus-pull-cut**: the incoming shot racks from blurred to sharp ← defocus-transition
- **desat-handoff**: the outgoing shot drains to grey, the incoming colour returns
- **selective-color**: a B/W or monochrome world with one meaningful accent in colour ← selective-colour-accent, era-color-coding, monochrome-accent-world

### Masks, wipes & reveals
- **iris-reveal**: a circular or organic reveal growing from a meaningful point ← lens-iris-wipe, iris-portal-transition, burn-iris, spotlight-iris, portal-transition
- **nested-shape-zoom**: a chain of flat shapes each born in the centre of the previous one
- **pixel-dissolve**: tiles, dots or pixels materialise or dissolve ← pixel-grid-dissolve, pixel-tile-dissolve, dot-matrix-dissolve, dot-grid-transition, halftone-dot-fill, pixel-dissolve-type
- **block-censor-wipe**: solid redaction blocks cover words or resolve into them ← redaction-reveal
- **cloud-wipe** · **paper-tear-transition** · **glass-shatter-transition** · **twirl-transition** · **foreground-occluder-wipe** (a foreground body covers the frame while the scene changes)
- **film-burn**: a warm light-leak/burn flash, sometimes shaped like a logo ← film-burn-logo-transition
- **nested-reveal-zoom-out**: the scene shrinks to become content inside a container in the next scene ← thumbnail-carry, editor-ui-pullback
- **hidden-swap-zoom-through**: push into an object until it fills frame, swap the world, pull back ← card-flip-portal
- **card-flip-reveal**: a card or photo flips to show the "but actually" side
- **object-swap**: a prop is replaced in place on its spoken word ← colorway-swap, mirrored-set-reversal

### Type
- **text-behind-subject**: a keyword layered between the background and a cut-out or roto subject ← text-behind-object, prop-occludes-type
- **focus-pull-type**: words resolve from blur, low opacity or heavy weight to sharp ← blur-in-type, blur-in-words, blur-word-reveal, blur-focus-reveal, weight-morph-type
- **ghost-word-settle**: words enter small and grey, then settle solid
- **leading-edge-colour-type**: typed characters arrive in an accent colour and cool down
- **bracket-caption**: a caption framed by brackets or design-tool selection handles ← selection-handles-caption, selection-box-ui, selection-frame-ui
- **subtitle-pill**: a persistent phrase-chunk subtitle layer separate from the hero type ← persistent-subtitle-bar
- **in-scene-captions**: text lives on or inside scene objects and UI and moves with them ← ghost-type-over-footage, tracked-label, prop-as-text-surface, native-ui-typing, app-icon-label-captions
- **dof-typography**: the same phrase on blurred foreground/background planes around a sharp line ← foreground-ghost-type
- **word-swap**: words cycle or scroll inside a fixed container or slot ← poster-template-swap, slot-reel-type, scroll-line-type, list-highlight-scroll
- **object-as-letter**: a prop or icon replaces a glyph ← glyph-icon-substitution
- **colour-swap-antithesis**: contrasting statements on cards with swapped colours ← colour-world-per-line, antithesis-world-swap
- difference-blend-type · crt-glow-type · neon-flicker-on · colour-fill-sweep · stretched-word-scroll · scattered-word-cloud · single-word-spotlight · marquee-ribbon · eighth-note-word-grid · text-selection-highlight · text-around-subject · pixel-type
