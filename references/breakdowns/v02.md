## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** ~0.5 cuts/s on average, but uneven. The opening is slow (3.0 s single shot), the middle is fast (0.2–1.5 s shots, 9–19 s), and the store, insights and CTA sections are long holds (2.8–4.9 s) carried by caption builds and in-screen video. The motion chart shows violent spikes only at transitions (3.8, 6.9, 9.3, 10.5, 13.0–13.5, 19.6, 20.9, 22.8, 24.3 s) and near-zero flow during the second half (24–37 s mean flow < 0.2). **Energy front-loaded; calm, legible CTA.**
- **Easing signatures (measured from per_frame):**
  - **Ease-in into transitions:** zoom velocity multiplies by ~1.3–1.6× per frame for 7–8 frames before the cut (3.60–3.80: 0.96→1.28→1.60→2.12→2.76→3.69→5.86; 6.57–6.84: 0.07→0.18→0.36→0.67→1.05→1.31→3.24→4.25→5.61). Use `Easing.in(Easing.exp)` or `bezier(0.7,0,0.84,0)`.
  - **Ease-out after the cut:** the incoming scene starts moving and decays over 12–20 frames (13.41 zoom-out: peak on frame 2, then 12 frames of decay; carousel slide: 2-frame rise to 5.7, then ~0.8× per frame for 13 frames). Use `Easing.out(Easing.exp)` or `spring({damping: 30, stiffness: 120, mass: 1})` with no overshoot.
  - The only overshoot is the store whip-zoom (19.95 s, one rebound frame z +1.7 after peaks of −13.6): a micro-settle.
- **Entrance / exit vocab:**
  1. **Ghost-word settle** (signature caption): each new word appears at ~40 % opacity, ~75 % scale, offset ~10–20 px up-right (or down-right for "guarantee"), then settles to baseline at 100 % white over ~6–8 frames. Earlier words stay put. The caption group is replaced as a whole on the next phrase (4–6 frame crossfade).
  2. Glow fade-up of serif type (4 frames) with letter-by-letter assembly for FACT.
  3. Ghost-grey → white brighten for the bold italic statement (3 states over 0.7 s).
  4. Typewriter URL (1 char / ~4 frames).
  5. Row-stagger fade of the insights panel (~7-frame stagger).
  6. Fixed-slot word grid (words light up in place).
  7. Dialog scale-forward snap (AE settings, ~4 frames).
  8. Exits: crossfade to the next phrase, fade to black, or the flash.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Ease-in zoom into black** (3.60–3.87): the camera accelerates into the dark centre over 7 frames while luma drops 34→12. Cut in the dark, and the next shot fades up from black in 4 frames. Rebuild: `scale` 1→1.35 with `Easing.in(Easing.exp)` + a brightness ramp to 0.3, then cut, then opacity 0→1 over 4 frames.
2. **Zoom-through streak** (6.57–7.05): the text/screen scales toward the camera with vertical motion-smear slices (horizontal bands of displaced pixels). The incoming scene starts zoomed in and settles out (z −3.9/frame → 0). ~8 frames in, ~20 out.
3. **White exposure flash** (9.08–9.28, 16.92–17.08, 32.13–32.30): desaturate + brighten to luma ~110 over 4 frames, hold 1–2 frames, cut. The incoming shot either starts at full exposure and decays in ~4 frames (32.23: 89→37) or has 2–3 frames of glitch slices (9.31–9.41). Rebuild: a white solid with `mix-blend-mode: screen`, opacity 0→0.75 over 4 frames, `saturate(0)`, cut at the peak, then 0.75→0 over 4 frames on the new shot.
4. **Whip-zoom into the screen** (10.44–10.75): fast zoom (5-frame rise, 16-frame decay) into a UI element, then crossfade/double exposure to the next UI.
5. **Push + exposure ramp to white → dissolve** (12.85–13.31): the frontal push brightens the whole frame to near-white (8 frames), and the next image (sketch) dissolves out of the white.
6. **Zoom-out reveal to grid** (13.41): the cut starts tight on one cell, then pulls out fast to reveal a 3×3 wall (peak on frame 2, 12-frame decay).
7. **Hard cut + whip-zoom-out** (19.49): the new scene starts already moving at peak speed (17.6 analysis px/frame) and decays in 10 frames with a single-frame rebound.
8. **Carousel slide** (20.85, 22.3): in-scene horizontal translation by one card width (2-frame rise, 13-frame exponential decay), like a real swipe.
9. **Cut to black + text-only scene** (24.32, 27.10): resets the energy before the promise and the CTA.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **A total monochrome grade across CG, UI and footage**, with one brand accent (green), makes cheap screen recordings look like a luxury campaign.
2. **Open with an object reveal, not the product:** a draped, spot-lit object plus an editorial serif lockup creates mystery in 1 s.
3. **Mix 3–4 typefaces only in the hook** (serif italic, condensed didone, heavy grotesk, tiny lowercase). After that, stay in one clean sans for legibility.
4. **Captions that "settle":** each word arrives as a small grey ghost and slides into its slot. It is calmer and more premium than pop-in karaoke.
5. **Proof through real screens** (AE, store, analytics) with the content still playing inside them. Never a static screenshot.
6. **Ease-in into the cut, ease-out after it.** Transitions accelerate for 7–8 frames into a flash or darkness, and the next shot decays over 12–20 frames.
7. **White flashes are 4-frame exposure ramps**, not 1-frame strobe cuts.
8. **Energy curve:** violent transitions early, then long calm holds with text only for the promise and CTA.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, 30 fps, ~1115 frames (37.1 s). Global `filter: grayscale(1) contrast(1.1)` on all non-brand layers; keep the brand-green layers outside the filter.
- **Layer stack (UI scene):** `<UIPlate>` (screen recording video, optionally inside a `<TiltedScreen rotateX={15}>` with a green `box-shadow: 0 40px 80px rgba(60,200,90,.35)` underglow) → `<VirtualCamera>` (scale/translate wrapper) → `<GhostSettleCaption>` → `<FlashOverlay>` → `<Grain opacity={0.05}>` + `<Vignette>`.
- **Components:**
  - `<GhostSettleCaption words={[{text,start}]}>`: each word `opacity: interpolate(f-start,[0,7],[0.35,1])`, `scale: interpolate(...,[0.75,1])`, `translate: interpolate(...,[{x:12,y:-16},{0,0}])` with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`. Inter Regular 500, white, word-spacing 0.3em. Group exits with a 5-frame crossfade.
  - `<SerifHookLockup>`: absolutely-positioned spans in "Editorial New" Italic/Light, a condensed didone for FACT (or "Playfair Display SC" at a narrow `font-stretch`), and Inter Black for NO, with `text-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(255,255,255,.5)`. Letter-by-letter opacity steps 2 frames apart.
  - `<DrapeReveal>`: a cloth PNG/video (or a Three.js cloth sim) falling `translateY 0→60%` over 8 frames with `Easing.in(Easing.quad)` to reveal the icon.
  - `<ZoomInToBlack>`: scale 1→1.35 + `brightness(1→0.3)` over 7 frames, `Easing.in(Easing.exp)`.
  - `<ZoomThroughStreak>`: scale 1→2.2 over 8 frames with `Easing.in(Easing.exp)` plus 10–14 horizontal slice copies offset in y by a random ±40 px, and an SVG feGaussianBlur `stdDeviation="0 18"`. The incoming scene starts at scale 1.15 and settles to 1 over 20 frames with `Easing.out(Easing.exp)`.
  - `<ExposureFlash>`: overlay `#fff`, `mix-blend-mode: screen`, opacity [0,0.75,0.75,0] over frames [0,4,5,9] with the cut at 5. Optional 3-frame RGB-split glitch on the incoming shot.
  - `<WhipZoomOut>`: the incoming scene `scale` 1.6→1 over 10 frames with `Easing.out(Easing.exp)`, a small `rotate(-4deg→0)` and a motion blur (`blur(6px→0)`).
  - `<CarouselSwipe>`: `translateX` by one card width with `Easing.out(Easing.exp)` over 15 frames.
  - `<TypeOnURL>`: 1 char every 4 frames with a blinking caret (15-frame period).
  - `<RowStagger>`: rows fade + 8 px rise, 7-frame stagger.
  - `<WordGrid>`: CSS grid with fixed slots; each word's opacity is keyed to its VO timestamp.
  - `<FootageCTA>`: the B/W clip with `scale` 1→1.04 over the whole shot, captions positioned in negative space, and the LINK keyword at 2.2× caption size.
- **Fonts:** `Inter`/`SF Pro Display` 400–500 for captions; `Gilroy ExtraBold Italic` (fallback `Poppins 800 italic`) for the statement line; `Editorial New`/`Ogg` for the hook serif.
