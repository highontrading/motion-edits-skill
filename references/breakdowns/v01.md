# v01 — "Stop wasting hours": black-marble statue in a 3D studio (one-world CG promo with UI inserts)

**Source creator handle:** akarsh_suhane · **Format:** 720×1280, 9:16, 30 fps, 18.7 s · **Shots:** ~11 visual segments in one continuous 3D world (the detector found only 1 cut, at 5.80 s; every other change is hidden in a camera move, an occluder pass, a light flash or a whip) · **Avg segment:** ~1.7 s · **Music BPM:** ~161 (detector; it feels like an 80–81 BPM half-time bed) · **VO:** yes, English, calm first-person promo read ("I found…")

**Style tags:** 3d-render, device-mockup, ui-mockup, kinetic-type, giant-keyword, serif-editorial, glow, depth-of-field, vignette, grain, slow-push, whip-pan, zoom-through, object-wipe, masking-reveal, cursor-ui, cta-endcard, foreground-occluder-wipe (new), text-behind-subject (new), one-world-continuous-camera (new)

**One-line essence:** a single moody CG set (a glossy black marble philosopher statue working on a laptop under a hard top-light) is filmed by a virtual camera that never really cuts. Every idea change is hidden behind the statue's body, a light flash or a whip into the laptop screen, and short, pristine white grotesk captions sit in the space or behind the statue's head.

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** product promo / lead-magnet ad for an editor marketplace (earnedits.com, AE project files), ending in a "comment LINK" CTA.
- **Beats:**
  - 0.00–2.80 **Hook / pain:** "Editors, stop wasting hours every day". The word EDITORS is the first frame, then STOP WASTING HOURS is carved into the back wall.
  - 2.80–5.04 **Agitate:** searching for ideas while clients wait. The statue rubs its head and then bites its knuckle (thinking/anxious poses).
  - 5.80–11.26 **Solution:** "I found earnedits.com", real open AEP files you can re-edit and learn from. The camera dives into the laptop: the website, AEP icons, an AE New-Composition window, then an AE comp viewer with a timeline.
  - 11.90–15.88 **Benefits:** "Less searching, less stress, just re-edit proven ideas" over a tilted, scrolling marketplace grid, then back to the statue.
  - 16.40–18.70 **CTA:** "Comment LINK and I'll send it" on a frontal hero shot of the statue at the laptop. A 1.7 s hold.
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** frame 0 already shows a full composition: the statue's shoulder in the foreground left, a laptop glowing with an AE timeline and a green Ae badge, and a ghosted giant serif EDITORS with a ✦ sparkle on the wall. The camera is already moving (orbit plus zoom-out, rot −1.4/frame). At 0.80 s the statue's shoulder fills the frame (luma drops 45→24), and at 1.00 s it whips past into a brighter rear view with STOP WASTING HOURS (luma 24→85 in 10 frames). Within one second the viewer gets a dark → bright reveal plus a type slam.
- **Script → visual rule:** the camera stays in one world. Each VO clause changes the camera angle and statue pose (pose = emotion: head-scratch = searching, knuckle-bite = waiting, chin-rest = proven ideas). Each product claim dives into the screen. Only 1–3 words of each clause become type.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** photoreal 3D render. A glossy black marble statue (classical bust, curly hair, toga), a dark stone desk, and a space-grey laptop in a dark gallery room. The real product UI is composited onto the laptop and used full-frame as "screen" inserts. Premium tech-commercial lighting: a single hard top/rim light and specular highlights on the black marble.
- **Backgrounds & textures:** the dark room wall carries a light installation of horizontal glowing white bars (an abstracted AE timeline/layer stack, 2–3 % frame height each, with dotted LED lines) that blurs into bokeh. From 2.8 s a green-tinted wall of blurred posters/thumbnails (DOF background). From 12.3 s a tilted product grid. From 14.7 s a plain mid-grey wall `#4f5b54`→`#6b6f6c` with a soft light pool.
- **Colour:** near-monochrome charcoal and green. Shot-1 palette `#1d231f` (37 %), `#3e5043`, `#6b8170`, `#9fb0a1`, `#d5e0d3`. Screen sections `#303532`, `#4f5b54`, `#ecf4f1` and the brand mint/green `#90dabe` / UI green `#3ddc6a`. The only saturated colour is the brand green (Ae badge, website buttons, green circle UI) plus the orange/red poster thumbnails on the site. Grade: a lifted-black teal-green wash in the middle section (saturation 25 → 47–60 during the screen dive, then 18–20 at the end: the final CTA is almost B/W).
- **Typography:**
  - **Opening title:** EDITORS in a thin, high-contrast display serif (Editorial New Thin / Cormorant Light style), all caps, ~85 % frame width, ghost grey `#9fa7a2` at ~40 % opacity with a 4-point ✦ sparkle between letters. It is set on the back wall in 3D space, so it is defocused with the scene.
  - **Wall slogan:** "Stop / wasting / hours" in an ultra-heavy grotesk (Inter Black / SF Pro Display Heavy), sentence case, three left-aligned stacked lines with negative leading. "Stop" ≈ 70 % frame width; the smaller lines are ~45 %. Rendered as **debossed/embossed dark type on a dark wall** (`#2b302d` on `#1d231f`, with a top-edge highlight and a bottom shadow), so it reads as relief.
  - **Captions:** a clean neo-grotesk (SF Pro Display / Inter Bold), white `#f2f5f3`, tight tracking (≈ −3 %), lowercase, centred, 2 lines. Size ~55 % frame width for "searching" and ~40 % for the smaller "while clients are waiting." The emphasis word is set in **bold italic** of the same family ("for *ideas*").
  - **Benefit lockup:** "Less" in Inter Black white on a black rectangular highlight box (box ≈ 35 % width, slight transparency), plus "searching"/"stress" in a **light italic** of the same family underneath and offset right. A heavy/light weight contrast in one lockup.
  - **Payoff:** "Just / re-edit / proven / ideas" in a narrower medium-bold grotesk (Helvetica Neue Medium Condensed / Inter Tight). "re-edit" is huge (~65 % width), set behind the statue's head (depth-composited). Lines are staggered right with negative leading.
  - **CTA:** "Comment *"LINK"*" in SF Pro Display Bold with "LINK" in bold italic inside curly quotes, ~80 % width. White with an **outer glow** (white, ~8 px blur, 40 %). "and I'll send it" sits below in medium, ~45 % width, revealed word by word.
  - **Inline brand text:** "I found" (small, ~15 % width) above "EarnEdits.com" in heavy grotesk, dark green `#2f4a37` at ~50 % on a dark wall: tone-on-tone, typed on letter by letter.
- **Layout & composition:** text always sits in the **upper 15–35 % band** (y ≈ 180–450 px of 1280). The statue/laptop occupies the lower 60 %. Heavy foreground occluders (shoulder, arm, fist) take 30–50 % of the frame in the over-shoulder angles. The CTA is centred at y ≈ 23 %, above the statue's head.
- **Assets:** 3D statue (it changes pose between angles, so there are several animated or posed renders), laptop model, real screen recordings of the earnedits.com website and store, phone-sized product preview cards, AE "New Composition" dialog, AE comp viewer plus timeline, and floating AEP file icons (rounded squares, white outline, "Ae / AEP" label, tilted ±20°).
- **Finishing / FX:** strong depth of field (background text and light bars are blurred), heavy vignette, fine grain, specular bloom on the marble and screen, light flash transitions (luma +40 in 6 frames), and RGB-split/blur glitch on the 12.0 s transition. Motion blur on all camera whips.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** ~0.6 visual changes/s, but only **one real cut** (5.80 s) and one hard cut inside a whip (9.30 s). Segments last 0.45–2.5 s and are longest at the end (CTA 2.15 s). The motion-energy curve shows 8 pronounced peaks (1.0, 2.8, 4.6, 5.7, 7.2, 9.3, 12.1–12.4, 14.1) separated by calm valleys (flow < 0.5). It moves in **pulses**: about 0.5 s of violent camera move, then ~1.2–1.8 s of near-still hold for reading.
- **Easing signatures (measured):**
  - Camera hero moves are long S-curves: 0.23–1.93 s rises over 23 frames to the peak and decays over 28 frames. The pull-back at 14.1 s rises over 23 frames and decays over 42, a long ease-out tail.
  - UI slides: 7 frames up to a peak of 7.3 px/frame (analysis) ≈ 29 px/frame real, then a clean exponential decay (7.3 → 5.8 → 4.7 → 3.9 → 3.0 → 2.6 → 2.1 → 1.8 → 1.5 → 1.2 → 0.9), a ratio of ~0.8 per frame. No overshoot (pan_y never changes sign). Use `Easing.out(Easing.exp)` or `bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)`.
  - **Ease-in whip into a cut** (9.03–9.27): velocity multiplies by ~1.37 per frame (0.88, 1.57, 2.50, 3.64, 5.07, 6.95, 9.48, 13.04), then the cut at full speed. Use `Easing.in(Easing.exp)` over 8 frames.
  - Light-flash pushes: luma ramps +40–60 over 6–12 frames while the camera keeps moving.
- **Entrance / exit vocab:** soft fade-in of whole caption lines (3–6 frames), fade from 30 % ghost to 100 % for the emphasis word (≈8 frames), per-character typing (EarnEdits.com ~1 char/frame; "stress" with a caret), word-by-word build at a 0.27 s cadence (Just/re-edit/proven/ideas), a 3-frame glow fade (CTA), staggered tumble-in of AEP icons (±20°, ~3-frame stagger), and blur-dissolve exits (~15 frames).
- **Camera:** always moving. Orbit plus dolly around the statue (net rot −44° in shot 1 and −88° across the remainder), push-ins into the screen, whip-ins, and a tilted top-down scroll on the UI grid. Never a locked-off shot until the CTA.
- **Secondary motion:** the statue changes pose (hand to head, knuckle bite, chin rest), the laptop screen content animates, AE playhead scrubbing, and the LED bars in the background flicker and slide.
- **Sync:**
  - The real cut (5.80) lands exactly on the VO "I" (5.80) and the strong onset at 5.83.
  - The glitch transition peaks at 12.13–12.20, right on the onset at 12.10 and 0.2 s after "Less" (11.90).
  - Caption timing is loose. Captions usually trail the spoken word by +0.1…+0.5 s ("searching" VO 2.80 → text 2.93, +0.13; "while clients" 4.00 → 4.53, +0.5, because it waits for the occluder; "just" 14.18 → 14.67, +0.5; "Comment" 16.40 → 17.00, +0.6, landing on "link" 17.14). Inference: the editor fires the caption once the camera move settles, not on the word.
  - The 0.27 s word cadence of the payoff is ≈ one beat at 161 BPM (0.37 s) shortened. The CTA "and I'll / send it" uses the same ~8-frame spacing.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Foreground-occluder wipe** (0.80, 4.27, 5.63–5.80): the camera moves behind a dark foreground element (the statue's shoulder or torso), which covers 80–100 % of the frame for 2–4 frames. The scene/angle changes while the frame is covered. Luma dips to ~24 (near black) at the covered moment. Rebuild: put a large dark PNG or 3D element in front, animate it across in 8–10 frames, and switch the background scene at the frame of maximum coverage.
2. **Ease-in whip to hard cut** (9.03–9.30): the content accelerates downward for 8 frames (exponential), the cut happens at peak velocity, and the next scene starts already settled (no matching ease-out). It feels like being yanked into the next screen.
3. **Screen dive / zoom-through** (6.97–7.30, 9.70–10.33): the camera pushes into the laptop or comp, then the UI slides into place with an exponential ease-out (7 frames in, 11–12 out).
4. **Light-flash push** (2.60–3.00): exposure ramps (luma +50) and a green tint washes in while the camera pans. The brightness peak covers the change of set.
5. **Glitch-flash** (11.93–12.27, ~10 frames): horizontal slice displacement, RGB split and blur, with luma ramping 52→117. It masks the switch from the AE viewer to the store grid.
6. **Rotational pull-back** (14.05–14.70): the tilted UI grid rotates and recedes while the 3D desk enters from below; a ~20-frame move with a ~40-frame decay.
7. **Text blur-dissolve** (16.0–16.5): the payoff words blur out one after another as the camera arcs, which leaves a clean frame for the CTA.

A dark, minimal electronic bed (161 BPM grid with half-time feel, rms 0.13), sitting under a clear, close-mic VO. ## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **One world, no visible cuts.** Hide scene changes behind foreground occluders, light flashes and whips. The video feels like one expensive camera move.
2. **A single hero subject with changing poses** carries the emotion (statue = "the editor"), so the viewer reads the mood without extra graphics.
3. **Monochrome set + one brand colour.** Charcoal/black marble everywhere; only the product green is saturated.
4. **Type lives in the space:** debossed slogans on the wall, words depth-sorted behind the subject's head, and ghost serif titles defocused with the background.
5. **Weight contrast in a single lockup** (Black + Light Italic, Bold + Bold Italic) gives editorial polish with one font family.
6. **Pulse pacing:** about 0.5 s of violent camera motion, then 1.2–1.8 s of stillness for reading. Never constant motion.
7. **Real product UI composited on a device**, then full-frame dives into it, proves the product without a "screen recording" look.
8. **Caption only the 1–3 key words** of each clause. Small, white, and placed in the top 35 %.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, 30 fps, 561 frames (18.7 s).
- **Layer stack (statue scenes):** `<BackgroundPlate>` (pre-rendered 3D clip or still of statue/desk; the camera move comes from Blender/Spline, or fake it with 2.5D layers) → `<WallType variant="emboss">` (inside the plate's z-space, `filter: blur(2px)`) → `<Subject>` (statue cut-out with alpha so text can sit behind it) → `<Caption>` → `<Foreground occluder>` → `<Vignette>` → `<Grain opacity={0.06}>`.
- **Components:**
  - `<OccluderWipe from to switchAt>`: a dark blurred PNG (shoulder/torso) translating across 10 frames with `Easing.inOut(Easing.cubic)`; swap the children at the frame of maximum coverage.
  - `<WhipCut>`: the outgoing scene `translateY` with `interpolate(f,[0,8],[0,-600],{easing:Easing.in(Easing.exp)})` and `filter: blur()` via an SVG feGaussianBlur `stdDeviation="0 24"` ramp. The incoming scene starts static.
  - `<ScreenDive>`: a `scale` 1→1.25 over 5 frames, then the UI `translateY` from −180 px→0 with `spring({damping: 200, stiffness: 120})`, or `Easing.out(Easing.exp)` over 12 frames.
  - `<FlashTransition>`: an overlay `background:#dfeee5; mix-blend-mode:screen` with opacity 0→0.6→0 over 12 frames, plus a green `hue/sat` lift.
  - `<GlitchFlash>`: 6–8 horizontal slices with random `translateX` (±40 px) and an RGB split (`text-shadow`/three offset copies with `mix-blend-mode:screen`) for 10 frames, with brightness 1→2.2.
  - `<WordBuild words cadence={8}>`: each word fades in with opacity 0→1 over 4 frames and blur 6→0 px; the exit uses the reverse stagger at 6 frames.
  - `<TypeOn text perChar={1} caret>` for the URL and "stress".
  - `<WeightLockup bold="Less" light="searching" box>`: Inter 900 white on a `rgba(0,0,0,.75)` box with 6 px padding; the second line Inter 300 italic, offset +12 % x.
  - `<GlowTitle>`: `text-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(255,255,255,.45)`, fade 3 frames.
  - `<TiltedScroll>`: wrap the UI screenshot in `perspective: 1200px; rotateX(18deg) rotateZ(-8deg)`, `translateY` −4 px/frame and scale 1→1.08.
- **Text behind subject:** render the caption between the background plate and a roto'd subject layer (an alpha-matte PNG sequence of the statue). For user footage, use a person-segmentation matte (e.g. pre-computed with RVM/MediaPipe) as the top layer.
- **Font stack:** `Inter Display` 900/700/300-italic, a light display serif for the hook word, and `SF Pro Display` as the alternate.

## 9. Variation ideas
1. **SaaS/AI tool promo:** replace the statue with a 3D mascot or a scanned bust of a "founder". Keep the dive-into-laptop sequence for product UI, the brand colour as the only saturated hue, and the "comment KEYWORD" CTA.
2. **Personal-brand talking head:** shoot the user in a dark room with a hard top-light. Use their body as the foreground occluder for transitions (walk past camera / turn shoulder) and place keywords behind their head with a segmentation matte.
3. **Luxury product launch (watch/headphones):** one CG pedestal set, orbiting camera, flash transitions between feature close-ups, a debossed feature word on the wall, and a single accent colour from the product.
