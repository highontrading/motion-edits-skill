## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** history micro-documentary with a myth-busting turn ("you think X, but actually Y") → CTA.
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–5.6 **Hook:** "Before superheroes, before special forces, there were the Vikings." Labelled logs SUPERHEROES and SPECIAL FORCES are placed on a stump, then a Viking chops into them.
  - 6.7–8.0 **Time stamp:** "It's the 8th century" (counter 0TH → 8TH).
  - 8.6–13.5 **Setting:** Scandinavia map assembles → banner planted → fog → longships on the horizon.
  - 14.1–17.1 **Characterisation:** "Fast, silent and terrifying" (dive into the lead ship).
  - 18.0–24.0 **Action:** raids, treasure, disappearing back to sea (one big collage canvas explored by camera).
  - 24.7–26.0 **Turn:** "But here's the crazy part" (spotlight iris closes to a circle).
  - 26.7–33.9 **Reframe:** warriors → explorers → traders → sailors (one figure morphing four times on a plinth).
  - 34.2–39.2 **Proof:** route map to North America, and a timeline of ~1000 AD vs Columbus 1492 ("~500 years").
  - 40.2–44.0 **Thesis:** "didn't just raid history, they sailed straight into it" (engraving-style plates with label strips).
  - 44.5–49.7 **CTA:** "The editors making work like this started with a project file from earnedits.com."
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** frame 0 is a close-up of a huge textured tree stump under a spotlight. By 0.2 s a muscular arm swings in from top-left with motion blur carrying a log stamped SUPERHEROES, and the camera simultaneously pulls back (zoom −3/step decaying). At 1.6 s a second arm drops SPECIAL FORCES. Every word of VO lands as a physical object.
- **Script → visual rule:** nouns become **props on a set** (logs, map, flag, ships, hut, chest, figure). Keywords become **ornate headline type placed inside the set**. "X, Y and Z" lists become **morphs of one figure** or **one canvas the camera travels across**, never separate cuts.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** mixed-media "museum diorama". Photo-real cut-outs (stump, logs, lantern, hay bale, chest, AI-rendered Viking figures) sit on parchment. Painterly or stylised layers (flat teal sea with drawn wave lines, illustrated longships, a hand-coloured antique Scandinavia map, an old world map with a dotted route) and two B/W **engraving-style** plates near the end. Everything shares the same paper texture and spotlight, so the mix feels like one world.
- **Backgrounds & textures:**
  - Warm parchment `#e4bb90`/`#dcae7c`/`#ceb187` with paper fibre, foxing stains and faint map or handwriting watermarks.
  - An **oval spotlight vignette**: bright ellipse centre, edges falling to `#251a10`/near-black. The ellipse is ~85% of frame width and ~75% of height and is visible on almost every shot.
  - Halftone-dot patches near the corners, fine grain throughout, and low-lying smoke/fog banks at the plinth's base (27–34 s).
- **Colour:** sepia world (parchment + bark brown `#6d4f37`/`#322213`) + **blood red accent** `#910f0d`/`#973421` (keywords ATTACKED, TREASURE, LONG SHIPS, CRAZY, WARRIORS, 8TH, EARNEDITS.COM). There is one secondary hue, **teal sea** `#1e6952`/`#0a4c3e`, used only for water. Gold parchment label `#fbc889`/`#dfac75` appears on the engraving plates. Grade: warm, low-contrast mids, crushed edges. The engraving section (40–44 s) desaturates (sat 30 vs 100–145 elsewhere) to mark the closing "legend" register.
- **Typography:**
  - **Primary headline:** inscriptional flared-serif caps (Cinzel / Trajan class; "LONG SHiPS" even uses a lowercase-style i, which suggests **Cinzel Decorative** or a similar Uncial-flavoured face). It is set in **stacked three-tier headlines**: small dark caps line ("SUDDENLY", `#3a2a1a`, ~35% width), huge red line ("LONG SHIPS", `#9a3a2a`, ~80% width) and small dark caps line ("APPEAR ON THE HORIZON", ~85% width). There is a slight inner texture and soft shadow. Swash variants with decorative curled terminals on A/K/R (ATTACKED, TREASURE, CRAZY) are underlined by a thin rule with a central flourish ornament.
  - **Carved / stamped labels:** condensed grotesk caps (Bebas Neue / Oswald Bold) in dark red, **embossed or burned into wooden logs** (SUPERHEROES, SPECIAL FORCES). They follow the log's perspective and rotation, with a distressed edge.
  - **Ink stamp numerals:** "8TH" in a distressed condensed grotesk, red `#910f0d`, ~35% width, over a pink-red **ink blot circle** (`#b2765e`-ish, ~25% width). "IT,S THE" (sic) is small black condensed caps above, and "CENTURY" is black condensed caps below.
  - **Signboards:** EXPLORERS / TRADERS / SAILORS in a condensed high-contrast wood-type serif (Bodoni Poster Compressed / Playfair condensed class), near-black `#1c0905`, ~65% width. They sit on a die-cut parchment plaque with scalloped corners, a thin double rule, and a top ornament (compass rose or ship's wheel) plus a bottom ornament (longship icon).
  - **Label strips (engraving plates):** a torn gold parchment strip (`#fbc889`) with small red-brown serif caps (≈ 3% height), word-by-word: "THE" → "DIDN'T JUST" → "RAID HISTORY" → "THEY" → "THEY SAILED" → "STRAIGHT" → "INTO IT." These are classic caption chunks dressed as museum labels.
  - **Data type:** "~1000 AD" (red numerals + dark "AD") and "~500" years / COLUMBUS 1492 in the same inscriptional serif, separated by a horizontal **arrow-tipped rule** and a vertical divider (a two-column comparison).
  - **Colour-sweep fill:** "FARTHER THAN" fills with a moving gold → red gradient wipe L→R (~0.6 s).
- **Layout & composition:** the hero object is centred and low (stump base at y ≈ 75–100%), so the headline sits in the top third (y ≈ 8–25%). Labels on the engraving plates sit at y ≈ 62–68% across the figure. A centred iris circle frames the "turn" line. The comparison timeline is centred in the middle third.
- **Assets:** cut-out tree stump (recurring plinth), carved logs, swinging oil lantern, hay bale, AI-rendered Viking figures (warrior, explorer with map, trader with scales, sailor with shield, shieldmaiden), animated arms and hands, an antique Scandinavia map split into tiles, a banner flag, an illustrated longship fleet, a hut with flying axes, a treasure chest with coins, a palm-tree beach, an old Atlantic map with a dotted route and a ship icon, an arrow timeline, B/W engraving-style ship and warriors, and an earnedits website UI card with a torn black frame.
- **Finishing / FX:** oval spotlight vignette, grain, paper texture, smoke wisps, **RGB chromatic aberration plus motion blur on fast entrances** (axe swing at 4.27–4.53, arm swings), depth-of-field defocus on transitions, and drop shadows under all props.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** ~0.25 real cuts/s (one per ~4 s), but motion energy is constant. Every shot holds a continuous camera move or prop animation. Montage rhythm comes from **within-shot beats** (prop drops, word builds, morphs) every ~0.5–1.0 s rather than from cuts. The longest takes (7–8.6 s) are the ship fleet, the village canvas and the character-morph plinth.
- **Animated on 3s:** props, figures and camera update every 2–3 frames (≈ 10–12 fps), which gives a tactile, stop-motion "diorama" feel. The fog and smoke layers appear smoother.
- **Easing signature:**
  - Entrances are **exponential ease-out settles**. The opening pull-back runs −5.3 → −3.5 → −3.3 → −2.3 → −1.6 → −0.9 per step (~1.2 s). The warrior's smoke materialisation runs −6.1 → −4.9 → −4.1 → −3.1 (~0.8 s).

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Build a set, not slides.** One recurring plinth (the tree stump), one spotlight and one parchment world tie dozens of assets into a single diorama.
2. **Evolve the subject, don't replace it.** For "they were X, Y, Z", morph the same figure on the same plinth via an ink dissolve on each keyword. This is more memorable than three cuts.
3. **Travel a single tall canvas** for a list of actions (raid → treasure → escape) with S-curve whip-tilts, then pull out to reveal the whole canvas as a summary.
4. **Typography as artefact:** words are burned into logs, stamped in ink, carved on plaques, printed on museum label strips and engraved headlines. The type always belongs to the world.
5. **Three-tier stacked headlines** (small / huge red / small) for VO sentences, built word by word ~0.1–0.3 s after each word.
6. **Iris to a circle for the plot twist** ("here's the crazy part"). Darkening everything else is a cheap, strong attention reset.
7. **One accent red + one secondary hue (teal, water only)** on a sepia base keeps a busy collage coherent.
8. **Physical delivery of props** (arms swinging in, an axe smashing, a hand planting a flag or grabbing coins) adds a human, tactile energy on 10–12 fps stepped animation.
9. **Data as period artefact:** a map route with a travelling ship icon and an arrow timeline with rolling year counters make the factual "proof" beat feel like part of the story.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, duration = VO + ~0.5 s. Wrap set animation in a stepped-time helper: `const f = Math.floor(frame / 3) * 3` (≈ 10 fps). Use full fps for fog, smoke and particles.
- **Layer stack (bottom → top):**
  1. `<Parchment tint="#e4bb90" stains watermark="map|script">`.
  2. `<CanvasCamera>` (a large `div` holding props; animate `transform: translate/scale/rotate`).
  3. Props (`<Cutout src shadow>`): stump plinth, logs, figures.
  4. `<SmokeBank>` (looping smoke video at 40% `screen`).
  5. `<HeadlineStack>`.
  6. `<SpotlightVignette rx="45%" ry="38%" feather={180} edge="#140c06">`.
  7. `<Grain opacity={0.1}>` + `<HalftoneCorners>`.
- **Key components:**
  - `SettleIn`: scale `interpolate(f, [0, 30], [1.12, 1], {easing: Easing.out(Easing.exp)})`, or `spring({damping: 14, stiffness: 60, mass: 1})` for a softer tail.
  - `ArmDeliver`: an arm + prop PNG pair from off-frame at `rotate(-25deg)` to its resting pose over 20 frames with `spring({damping: 10, stiffness: 140, mass: 0.7})` (2–3° overshoot). The arm exits on frames 20–30 with `Easing.in(Easing.quad)`. Add `blur(6px)` while the velocity is high.
  - `HeadlineStack`: tiers `[{text, size: 0.35, color: '#3a2a1a'}, {text, size: 0.8, color: '#9a3a2a'}, {text, size: 0.85}]`. Font `Cinzel Decorative`/`Cinzel` 700. Each word enters at (word start + 4 frames) with opacity 0 → 1, `blur(8px → 0)` and scale 1.06 → 1 over 6 frames with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`. Optional `<FlourishRule>` (SVG path) draws on with `strokeDashoffset` over 10 frames.
  - `InkStampCounter`: a blot SVG scales 1.3 → 1 over 3 frames; the number steps through an array `[0,5,7,8]` or interpolates with `Easing.out(Easing.quad)` and is rendered with a distressed texture via `mask-image`.
  - `TileAssemble`: map split into a 6×6 grid; each tile flies from a random offset (±200 px, ±20°) to place over 12–18 frames with stagger 1 frame, starting `blur(8px)` → 0.
  - `InkMorph`: `<svg><filter><feTurbulence baseFrequency="0.02" numOctaves="3"/><feDisplacementMap scale={lerp(0,60)}/><feComponentTransfer>` alpha threshold `t`, sweeping `t` 0 → 1 over 8 frames for A out and 1 → 0 for B in, offset 3 frames. Add a brightness flash `brightness(1 → 1.3 → 1)` at the midpoint and spawn 150–300 black specks drifting outward with `Easing.out(Easing.quad)`.
  - `SpotlightIris`: `mask-image: radial-gradient(circle at 50% 55%, black r, transparent r+40px)`, r `interpolate(f, [0, 28], [1300, 240], {easing: Easing.inOut(Easing.cubic)})`, outside overlay `rgba(10,6,3,0.75)`.
  - `RoutePath`: SVG dotted path (`stroke-dasharray: 2 10`, red `#8d1b17`). The ship icon is positioned with `getPointAtLength(progress·L)` and rotated to the tangent, progress `Easing.inOut(Easing.sin)` over 60 frames.
  - `RollingYear`: `Math.round(interpolate(f, [0, 42], [948, 1000], {easing: Easing.out(Easing.cubic)}))` + "AD". The container rotates from 18° to 0° with the same easing.
  - `LabelStrip`: torn-edge parchment (`clip-path` polygon with jitter) `#fbc889`, text `#7a2a14` serif caps at 30 px, content swapped per caption chunk with no animation.
  - `DefocusCut`, `FogWipe` and `AxeSmash` as described in §5.
  - `TextMorph` (CTA): letters of string A explode into particles, which re-gather into string B over 12 frames.
- **Tricky bits:**
  - AI-rendered figure variants must share the same pose footprint and position on the plinth so the morph reads as a transformation. Generate them with the same seed or pose control and align the feet at a fixed y.
  - The fleet: a parallax stack of 3 ship rows on separate sea bands with sine bobbing (`translateY = 6·sin(f/12 + i)`, `rotate = 1.5·sin(f/15 + i)`).

## 9. Variation ideas
1. **Brand or company history** ("Before Apple there was…"): stump plinth → a product pedestal. Morph one product through its generations on each keyword (iPod → iPhone → Watch), with a route map for global expansion and a rolling-year timeline vs a competitor.
2. **Science explainer** ("Before microscopes…"): a lab-bench diorama under a spotlight, an iris for the reveal moment, and an ink-dissolve morph from cell → organism → organ. Swap the parchment for an old textbook page with a teal accent.
3. **Sports or athlete legend recap (user footage):** freeze-frame cut-outs of the athlete on a plinth, morphing between career eras on each keyword. A stats timeline uses rolling counters. Real match clips sit inside a torn-paper frame with museum label-strip captions, and engraving-style grading is used for the "legacy" closing line.
