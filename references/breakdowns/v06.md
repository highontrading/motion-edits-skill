**One-line essence:** each VO clause becomes a single flat-colour "poster" (one paper colour, one hero prop, one heavy-type phrase with a grey 3D extrude), printed in halftone and animated on 2s/3s at ≈12 fps. That gives a cheap-looking-on-purpose, print-and-stop-motion feel that reads as editorial rather than template.

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** marketing case-study micro-documentary ("cultural phenomenon → why it works → proof → moral → CTA"), same series as v05.
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–5.94 **Hook:** "Every December, millions of people voluntarily become unpaid advertisers for Spotify." A calendar, a crowd, a money bag, a logo.
  - 6.56–15.02 **Setup:** no reward, no incentive, just a card telling you that you listened to Taylor Swift for 32,000 minutes.
  - 15.26–21.04 **Name + claim:** "This is Spotify Wrapped, the most brilliant $0 marketing campaign in history."
  - 21.44–30.58 **Mechanism:** Wrapped sells your identity, not Spotify → you don't just share it, you defend it.
  - 30.76–42.48 **Proof:** 400M posts in 48 hours → Apple / Amazon / YouTube Music all copied it → it cost almost nothing.
  - 42.48–51.44 **Moral:** they didn't show data, they made you feel seen; that brand never has to chase you again.
  - 51.70–56.84 **CTA:** "Editors get the project file… earnedits.com."
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.3 s):**
  - Frame 0 is an empty top-down wood desk with props cropped at the edges (blue phone, espresso cup, pen).
  - At 0.10 s a **folded paper sheet unfolds** in 3 stepped drawings (0.10, 0.17, 0.27) to reveal a vintage **1957 "December" calendar**.
  - "EVERY" drops in at 0.23 and "DECEMBER" at 0.47, word-locked to the VO (0.00 / 0.28). Two big changes within half a second.
  - The cut at 1.27 swaps to a saturated orange crowd grid. The first two backgrounds already contrast (brown wood → orange halftone).
- **Script → visual rule:** one clause = one poster.
  - The **concrete noun becomes a prop**: calendar, crowd icons, money bag, UNO reverse card, iPhone, cassettes, Win95 dialog, bar chart, mirror, social icons, LED clock, brand logos, CRT, dollar bundle, WhatsApp notification.
  - The **clause itself becomes stacked heavy type**, often *partly hidden behind the prop*.
  - Brand names are shown as real logos on their brand colour.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** flat-colour editorial collage. Photoreal cut-out props (often AI-generated or stock: money bag, mirror portrait, CRT room, hanging bills) on solid, textured paper-coloured backgrounds, plus big heavy type. It is closer to a printed magazine spread than to v05's busy collage: only 1–2 props per scene, lots of negative space.
- **Backgrounds & textures:**
  - Every scene is a single paper colour with **visible halftone dot screen** (≈3–4 px dots at 720 px), film grain, a soft centre-bright vignette (edges ≈ −25% luma) and a slight overall softness/bloom.
  - Secondary textures are used once each: wood grain (hook), grid paper (money bag, CTA), ruled lines (bar chart), clouds/sky (phone), XP-style hills (dialog), street photo (WhatsApp).
- **Colour:** one hue per scene, changing on nearly every cut. The palette sequence (from summary.json + eye):
  - wood `#5c4937` → orange `#c2694b` → light grid `#dcdcdc` + blue type `#2a5bd7` → olive `#5a5b24` → red `#de3333`
  - → black `#040404` + UNO yellow → sky `#588db6` → navy `#1a447c` → XP blue/green `#557d9f`/`#5c7046` → mustard `#b57b1d`
  - → studio grey `#a5a5a3` (B/W photo) → warm beige `#d6d0c4` + rust label `#b0521c` → white `#e1e1e1` → black
  - → Apple pink `#eb4a61` → Amazon teal `#1ea3ab` → YouTube red `#da3637` → red `#ea2526`
  - → retro room → olive grid `#48511d` → black → street photo → blue `#4678b3` → white + blue type → dark green grid `#2f441c` + AE purple `#9b4dff` glow.
  
  Rules: type is white on mid/dark grounds and black on light grounds. Red and black are reserved for negation ("NO REWARD", "DIDN'T JUST…"). Logos sit on their own brand colour.
- **Typography:**
  - *Primary:* **wide ultra-heavy grotesk** (Arial Black / Archivo Black / Helvetica Neue Black-like), mostly ALL CAPS. Some soft words are set lowercase in the same weight ("millions of people", "become", "you listened", "costSpotify").
    - Tight leading (≈0.85–0.95), lines centred.
    - Width: hero stacks fill **55–65% of frame width** ("YOU DON'T JUST" 65%, "JUST CARD THAT TELLS YOU" 50%, "FEATURE THAT COST" 65%). Giant keywords bleed to 90–100% ("FORTY / HOURS" 92%, "ALMOST NOTHING." ≈100%). Quiet lines are small, single line ≈50–55% width ("SELLS YOUR IDENTITY", "DIDN'T JUST SHOW YOU DATA").
  - *Type effects:*
    - **3D extrude / drop shadow** in a darker tone of the type colour (white type gets a grey `#8a8a8a` extrude ≈4–8 px down-right; blue type on white gets a dark-blue extrude).
    - **Halftone inside the letterforms** (dots visible at the edges of JUST, FORTY, COST).
    - **Soft outer glow/bloom** on white type over black ("JUST CARD…", "FORTY EIGHT HOURS").
  - *Label badges:* solid black rounded-rect bars with white heavy caps (NO REWARD / NO INCENTIVE, SPOTIFY WRAPPED, ADVERTISERS) and a thin grey bevel/glow edge. One rust-orange bar holds white caps that **morph from SHARE IT to DEFEND IT**.
  - *UI type:* Win95-style dialog ("Be aware" title bar in `#0000b0`, grey body, dark-grey Inter/Helvetica Regular text with a caret); iOS lock-screen SF Pro; a WhatsApp notification in heavy caps "THEY MADE YOU FEEL SEEN"; a 7-segment LED clock "48:00" with a tiny "HOURS" label.
  - *Special:* **sky-writing / cloud type** on the CRT, "costSpotify" built from puffy, noisy white cloud letters.
  - *Emphasis:* the payoff word goes on the largest line ("JUST", "COST", "HOURS", "UNPAID"), the label carries the verb that changes, and negations are in black bars.
- **Layout & composition:**
  - The type block sits in the **upper-middle (y ≈ 15–45%)** when a prop is below (money bag, mirror, CRT, phone) and in the **dead centre** on type-only posters.
  - The hero prop is centred and ≈40–60% of frame height.
  - Recurring **prop-occludes-type** device: the UNO card hides "A" and "HA" in CARD/THAT/TELLS, the LED clock hides "EIGHT", the mirror hides "ID" of IDENTITY, the dollar bundle hides "MO"/"THI" of ALMOST NOTHING, and the phone sits in front of "you listened". The viewer completes the word, and the type reads as a physical layer behind the object.
- **Assets:**
  - Props/photos: vintage 1957 calendar, wood desk props, flat person icons (6×4 grid in a dashed box), green canvas money bag, Spotify logo, UNO reverse card (3D), black iPhone 5 (3D, flips), Taylor Swift "Lover" now-playing lock screen, cassette tapes, XP-like desktop with retro icons, white bar chart, B/W man-and-mirror photo, social icons (WhatsApp, Instagram, X, Facebook), LED clock, Apple/Amazon/YouTube Music logos, a 90s CRT TV with stickers (MTV, Jurassic Park, Thrasher) in a collector's room, hanging dollar bundle on twine, hand holding an iPhone X in a European street, After Effects icon stack with neon purple glow, the earnedits.com website screen.
- **Finishing / FX:** global halftone + grain + vignette + mild bloom (a "printed then rephotographed" look); motion blur on fast word entrances; DOF blur on words arriving from depth; heavy directional blur on the final website swap; clouds/smoke drifting *in front of* the phone (foreground parallax layer).

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Frame rate feel:** 66% of frames are exact holds. Change frames are spaced **2, 3, 2, 3…** (189 gaps of 2 frames, 185 gaps of 3 frames), so all animation is quantised to ≈12 drawings/s inside a 29.97 fps file: stop-motion "on twos/threes". Cuts and the VO stay frame-accurate; only the motion is stepped. This single choice is the main reason the piece feels handmade/print-like rather than template-smooth.
- **Pacing:** 26 shots / 57.2 s ≈ 0.45 cuts/s, median 2.0 s.
  - The hook is 1.0–2.3 s per poster.
  - The "copy" montage is the fastest section (Apple → Amazon → YouTube → "all copied it": 0.93, 1.17, 1.40, 0.94 s).
  - Longest holds are on the data/claim moments (phone 4.7 s, dialog 4.4 s) and the CTA (5.4 s).
- **Easing signatures (measured per drawing):**
  - Camera zoom-outs and push-ins are **exponential ease-outs** (×0.85–0.88 per drawing, settling over 1.2–1.5 s).
  - Data growth (bars, block scale-ups) is **ease-in-out with a long tail**: peak at the 3rd–4th drawing (≈0.3 s), then ≈0.7 s decay.
  - Word entrances are **3-drawing depth landings** (~0.23–0.25 s): from ≈1.4–1.6× scale, 6–10 px blur, 30–50% opacity and an off-axis offset of 10–25% of frame width, down to 1×, sharp and opaque. There is no overshoot; the snap comes from the stepping.
- **Entrance / exit vocab:**
  - *Depth drop-in* (words/props from large and blurred): 3 drawings.
  - *Scatter-converge* (several words fly from random depths into a block): 5–6 drawings.
  - *Label bar wipe + typewriter:* bar in 2 drawings, then ≈1 char per drawing.
  - *Dot-assemble* (specks merge into a logotype): 5–6 drawings.
  - *3D card/phone flip-in:* 180° Y-rotation in 4–6 drawings.
  - *Drop-in with settle* (money bag, dollar bundle on string): 3–4 drawings + a pendulum idle.
  - *Slide-in with motion blur* (ALMOST NOTHING, Amazon logo): 3 drawings.
  - *Rise from bottom* (YouTube button): 4 drawings.
  - *Row-by-row icon pop:* 1 icon per drawing.
  - *Label text morph* (SHARE → DEFEND): 2 drawings + a block scale-up.
  - *Cloud-writing:* 5 drawings.
  - Exits are almost always hard cuts. Nothing animates out.
- **Camera:** gentle and continuous. Slow push-ins (+11 to +22 net zoom) on the device shots, slow pull-outs (−6 to −15) on the posters, a slight lateral drift on photos. There are no whip pans; energy comes from the stepped entrances and the colour changes.
- **Secondary motion:** pendulum swings (bundle, UNO card rock), cassettes drifting outward, clouds passing in front of the phone, an orbiting icon cluster, a blinking caret, a counter, the screen-in-screen video replay in the CTA.
- **Sync:**
  - Cuts follow the VO, not the beat. Mean |cut − nearest word onset| = 0.09 s; mean |cut − nearest beat| = 0.09 s against a half-period of 0.2 s (chance level).
  - Cuts typically land **0.05–0.15 s after the clause's first word** (Apple 35.17 vs 35.10; "Wrapped" 21.52 vs 21.44; "And" 25.19 vs 25.04; "They" 45.18 vs 45.06). This is the opposite of v05's slight lead; in this creator's work both happen, and the tolerance is ±0.15 s.
  - Within a poster, words appear on or up to 0.1 s after the spoken word. Typed labels finish ≈0.1–0.3 s after the word ends.
  - Strong music onsets sit on many cuts (15.30, 21.43, 30.70–30.81, 35.99, 38.64, 39.57, 41.52, 49.60, 51.78), which implies whoosh/impact SFX placed on cuts.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Hard cut between posters (default)**, about 20 of 25 cuts. The colour field changes completely (hue jump ≥60° or a luma flip), so a plain cut reads as a page turn. Rebuild: `<Series>` of scenes with no transition component; just change background colour and texture.
2. **Desaturate handoff** at 36.00–36.17 and 37.10–37.30. The outgoing brand colour drains to neutral grey over 3 frames (sat 168 → 146 → 29), the cut lands on the grey frame, and the incoming scene starts at ~70% saturation and reaches 100% over ≈6 frames. Rebuild: `filter: saturate()` from 1 → 0.15 over the last 3 frames, then 0.7 → 1 over the first 6 frames of the next scene, with `Easing.out(Easing.quad)`. It links a list of competitors as "the same thing in a different colour".
3. **Jump punch-in with defocus** at 20.59. Same scene, scale 1 → 1.6 in one frame, `blur(8px)` for 3 frames, then sharp. It works as an emphasis beat on "in history".
4. **3D object entrance as a scene opener** at 8.78, 10.68 and 51.75. The scene cut lands on an edge-on card/phone already mid-rotation, so the motion carries the cut. Rebuild: `rotateY(110deg → 0deg)` with `perspective: 900px`, quantised to 12 fps, over 5 drawings, `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`.
5. **Dot-assemble reveal** at 5.61 and 38.67. Rebuild: render the logotype as a mask; scatter 40–80 white dots inside the glyph bounds and scale them from 0 to a radius that covers the glyph over 6 drawings; then crossfade to the solid glyph and add a glow.
6. **Screen-content motion-blur swap** at 54.6 (inside the CTA phone): a 2-frame vertical smear (scaleY 1.4, `blur(0 12px)`) between screen contents.
7. **Reprise inversion** at 49.62: the same phrase on the complementary ground (blue/white → white/blue). It's a visual echo rather than a transition, but it is placed exactly on a cut.

## 6. Sound design
A 152 BPM lo-fi/hip-hop-ish bed runs under a continuous VO with no pauses longer than ~0.6 s. Onsets cluster on cuts and reveals, which implies whoosh/impact hits on scene changes, typewriter clicks under the label and dialog typing (15.4–16.4, 17.5–19.7), a counter tick under 48:00, and a pop under the dot-assemble logos. No silence moments; the CTA rides the same bed.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Animate on twos/threes (≈12 fps) inside a 30 fps edit.** Stepped motion plus halftone and grain makes digital collage feel printed and handmade, and hides cheap 2.5D moves.
2. **One poster per clause, one flat paper colour per poster.** Change hue on every cut; let colour do the transition work.
3. **Hide part of the keyword behind the hero prop.** The occlusion creates depth with zero 3D and makes viewers read harder.
4. **Heavy wide grotesk with a same-hue extrude** for every line; vary only scale (from a small single line to bleeding full-width) to set the emphasis.
5. **Negation lives in black label bars, and labels can morph** (SHARE IT → DEFEND IT) to show a change of meaning in place.
6. **Use real brand assets as proof** (logos on brand colour, UI, a familiar lock screen). A desaturate handoff turns a list of competitors into one gesture.
7. **Data as tactile objects:** stepped bar growth, an icon grid filling row by row, a ticking LED clock, an orbiting icon cluster. No line charts.
8. **Echo the moral twice** (colour-inverted reprise) and **end on a phone replaying the video itself**, so the CTA doubles as a showreel.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps` (keep 30 for VO and cut accuracy), duration = VO + ~0.4 s (≈1716 frames).
- **12 fps stepping:** wrap all animated layers (not the cut timing) in a `useSteppedFrame()` hook: `const f = useCurrentFrame(); const stepped = Math.floor(f / 2.5) * 2.5;`. Every `interpolate`/`spring` should read `stepped` instead of `f`. This reproduces the measured 2-3-2-3 cadence.
- **Typical scene stack (bottom → top):**
  - `<PaperBackground color texture="plain|grid|ruled|wood">` (solid + noise PNG at 8% + radial vignette `radial-gradient(circle at 50% 45%, transparent 40%, rgba(0,0,0,.28))`)
  - photo/prop layer(s) with `drop-shadow(0 12px 18px rgba(0,0,0,.35))`
  - `<HeavyStack lines align="center">`
  - `<OccluderProp>` placed *above* the type
  - `<LabelBar>`
  - `<HalftoneOverlay dot={3} opacity={0.18}>` (SVG pattern of circles, `mix-blend-mode: multiply` on light grounds and `screen` on dark)
  - `<GrainOverlay opacity={0.07}>`
  - `<Bloom>` (duplicate type layer with `blur(6px)`, `opacity .5`)
- **Components:**
  - `HeavyStack({lines, font: 'Archivo Black', extrude: {color: '#8a8a8a', depth: 6}})`. Extrude = 6 `text-shadow` layers offset 1 px down-right each, plus one soft shadow `0 8px 16px rgba(0,0,0,.25)`.
  - `DepthWord({word, at})`: on `stepped` frames 0→7.5 interpolate scale 1.5→1, blur 8→0, opacity 0.4→1, translate from an off-axis (±15% W, ±8% H) to 0; `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`.
  - `ScatterConverge({words, at, spread: 0.25})`: same as `DepthWord` with random seeded origins via `random(seed)`; total 15 frames.
  - `LabelBar({text, bg: '#0b0b0b' | '#b0521c', at})`: `clip-path: inset(0 100% 0 0)` → `inset(0 0 0 0)` over 5 frames, then a typewriter at 1 char per 2.5 frames. `morphTo({text: 'DEFEND IT', at})` swaps text with a 2-drawing scale pulse 1→1.08→1.
  - `DotAssemble({src: logoSvg, at, dots: 60})`.
  - `Flip3D({src, from: 110, at})`: Y rotation over 5 drawings, `perspective: 900px`.
  - `Pendulum({amp: 15, period: 30, damping: 0.85})`: rotate around the top string anchor; idle on `stepped`.
  - `BarChartGrow({values, at, stagger: 2.5})`: each bar scaleY 0→1 with `Easing.bezier(0.45, 0, 0.2, 1)` over 25 frames.
  - `IconGridFill({cols: 6, rows: 4, at, perIcon: 2.5})`.
  - `OrbitCluster({icons, radiusFrom: 0.35, radiusTo: 0.2, degrees: 180, frames: 60})`.
  - `LEDCounter({from: '10:00', to: '48:00', frames: 12})`, DSEG7 font, white with a glow.
  - `DesatHandoff({outFrames: 3, inFrames: 6})`.
  - `PunchDefocus({scale: 1.6, blurFrames: 3})`.
  - `CloudText({text})`: render the text and apply an SVG `feTurbulence` + `feDisplacementMap` (scale 12) + `blur(2px)` in white at 85%, revealed by a growing mask.
  - `WinDialog({title: 'Be aware', text, cps: 27})`.
  - `PhoneReplay`: the CTA phone screen is an `<OffthreadVideo>` or a nested `<Sequence>` of the composition's first 4 s scaled into the screen rect.
- **Colour plan:** keep an array of scene colours and enforce a hue shift ≥60° or a luma flip ≥80 between neighbours.
