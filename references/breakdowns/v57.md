**One-line essence:** a product ad for a project-file marketplace that alternates **one-line Helvetica captions on a near-black dot-grid "void"** (lit by a single drifting green glow) with dense **3D stacks of glossy UI cards, device mockups and real After Effects screen recordings**. ## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** product promo / ad for the creator's own marketplace (EarnEdits), told as "problem → reveal → how it works → pain → promise → comment CTA".
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–4.95 **Hook (relatable question):** "Ever find an insane edit online and think, how the hell did they make this?" The viewer is shown the "insane edits" as a fanned deck of glossy cards, and a cursor clicks one.
  - 4.9–10.7 **Desire list:** "smooth animations / crazy transitions / clean text movement". An After-Effects-style **Effects & Presets folder tree** grows one entry per noun, beside a shuffling stack of template thumbnails.
  - 10.9–12.37 **Turn:** "What if you could make the same?" (typed alone on black, "same?" in green).
  - 12.37–17.40 **Reveal:** a hyperspace starfield, a laptop flies in, "Introducing EarnEdits" in a green label → "A marketplace built specifically" → "VIDEO EDITOR / MOTION DESIGNERS." over an editing-UI collage.
  - 17.40–28.33 **How it works (3 steps):** browse (a 3D carousel of product boxes) → find & download (a tilted card with 3D Download / Watch Tutorial buttons, cursor click) → open in After Effects (a MacBook with a real AE screen recording, push-in).
  - 28.33–31.23 **Pain:** "Stop spending hours building everything from scratch". A clock spins, then the camera zooms out to show it is itself an AE comp.
  - 31.23–35.3 **Promise:** "Start with a professional foundation and create videos you can charge hundreds for". A giant green "professional" zooms down into place, then an explosion of cards.
  - 35.3–39.33 **CTA:** "Want access?" → an Instagram comment box types "PROJECT FILE" → a reply from earn.edits: "send you the link".
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** a pure-black frame with only a tiny caret. "Ever" types in **wrapped in changing code brackets** (`/ Ever /` → `< Ever find >` → `{ Ever find an }`, the brackets swap every word, ~8 frames apart). At 1.07 "insane" replaces the line, and at 1.40 a **saturated green card flashes in** (luma jumps 36 → 75 in one frame). The ad goes from black to colour in 1.4 s.
- **Script → visual rule:** abstract clauses (questions, pain, promise) are **type-only on the dark void**. Concrete clauses (edits, presets, marketplace, editors, browse, download, open in AE) get a **"proof" visual made of real UI**. Keywords the brand owns ("same?", "EarnEdits", "VIDEO", "MOTION", "professional", "charge", "for") are set in brand green.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** dark-mode SaaS keynote crossed with a creator flex reel. It mixes kinetic type, glassmorphic 3D UI cards and device mockups with **real screen recordings** of After Effects. The cards are actual template thumbnails (purple-gradient landing page, green liquid render, landscape with a "+" glass button, AI dashboard "Supercharge Your Business"), so the product is shown, not illustrated.
- **Backgrounds & textures:** one constant set. Near-black `#030703` with a **soft green radial glow** (`#0d1d12`→`#0b200e`, ~45–60% of frame width, off-centre). The glow **drifts between scenes** (centre-left in the typing scenes, lower-right behind the card decks, under the subject in the product shots). Over it sits a **faint dot grid**: 1–2 px dots at ~74 px pitch (≈5.8% of frame width), ~10–15% opacity, visible mainly at the frame edges. Nothing else changes, and the background never cuts. Only the foreground content cuts or morphs, so the whole ad reads as one space.
- **Colour:** the background is 85–95% near-black/dark green. The single brand accent is **green `#3baf49`** (the EarnEdits label, keywords, the 3D cursor; deeper `#2f883a` in shadows). White `#f8f6fb` is used for type. Secondary UI colours appear only inside cards: violet `#6c5ce7`-ish gradients, sky landscapes, the orange "Watch Tutorial" button `#db700e`, mixer blue `#1f5bd8`, and the green-screen comp `#008904`. Mean luma stays 11–30 for type scenes and 48–54 for card scenes, so the white cards provide the brightness peaks.
- **Typography:**
  - **Primary: Helvetica Neue** (the AE Character panel in the screen recording literally shows "Helvetica Neue"). Mostly Roman/Medium, sentence case, white, tight default tracking. Caption size is small and keynote-like: "Ever find an" ≈ 9% of frame width line-length with cap height ≈ 2.2% of frame height. "A marketplace / built specifically" is 2 lines at ≈ 33% of frame width (x-height ~3.5% of frame height). "Stop" ≈ 18% of frame width.
  - **Weight-morph entrance (signature):** each new word appears **ultra-heavy and blurred, like a choked/ink-bled Black weight**, and relaxes to Regular over 4–8 frames (seen on "insane", "How they / Make this" 4.27, "specifically" 14.9, "Stop / spending" 28.4–28.8, "Want access?" 35.4–35.8). The previous words stay Regular. The heavy blob is wider than the final word, so the line visibly "exhales".
  - **Typewriter variants:** (a) letter-by-letter with a **green vertical caret** `|` (≈2 px wide, cap-height tall) on "A marketplace|"; (b) a **wide-tracked typewriter** "W h a t  i f  y o u  c" (≈ +400 tracking) that **tightens to normal tracking** once the line completes ("What if you could make the same?", 10.9→11.8 s); (c) the code-bracket typewriter in the hook.
  - **Label badge:** "EarnEdits" in Helvetica Neue Bold, white, on a flat green `#3baf49` rectangle (no radius, ~10 px padding), set under a white Bold "Introducing". The label assembles through a **pixel/glitch scramble** (black blocky glyph fragments over the green for ~6 frames, 12.8–13.1).
  - **Display type:** "VIDEO / EDITOR" and "MOTION / DESIGNERS." use a heavy extended grotesk (Helvetica Neue Heavy Extended / Monument-Extended-like), ALL CAPS, ~22–28% of frame width. The first word is green and the second white, with a 2–3 copy **stacked echo/glitch offset** on the swap. "Spotify" in the UI also triple-echoes.
  - **Giant keyword:** "professional" in green Helvetica Neue Roman at **~180% of frame width** (only "ssiona" visible, cap height ≈ 46% of frame height). It shrinks to a ~16%-width inline word in "professional foundation" within ~10 frames.
  - **Mixed-weight emphasis:** "create videos you can **charge** hundreds **for**", where the green words are the same size and weight and only colour signals emphasis.
- **Layout & composition:** captions are **dead centre** (a single line at y≈50%, two lines at y≈46–56%). Card decks sit **right of centre** (x 45–90%) with a left-aligned text column (the Effects & Presets tree at x≈10–37%, y≈42%) to give a classic 1/3 : 2/3 split. Device mockups are centred and bottom-anchored (the laptop bleeds off the bottom edge). In the CTA the phone sits right (x 50–75%) and the caption left (x 5–25%).
- **Assets:** template thumbnails as rounded cards (r ≈ 3% of card width), a 3D product-box carousel ("AFTER EFFECTS PROJECT FILES" packs: RETRO EDITS, UI, cyber-AI, etc.), a MacBook mockup with the EarnEdits site and a real AE recording, a **3D glossy green cursor arrow** (extruded, with highlight) plus a white macOS hand cursor, 3D extruded buttons (Download green → grey when pressed, Watch Tutorial orange), editing-UI props (level meters, histogram/RGB scopes, Spotify mini-player, blue fader cards, green timeline bars), the iPhone Instagram comment sheet, and a clock comp on green screen.
- **Finishing / FX:** soft bloom on white cards and green glows, card drop shadows (large, ~40 px blur, 50% black), **depth-of-field** on far cards in the carousel, heavy **motion blur** on every fly-in, a hyperspace **starfield streak** layer (white 1–2 px streaks radiating from centre, 12.27–12.5), an **RGB-split / white smear glitch** exit at 10.6–10.7, and a vignette deepening the corners.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** ~20 scenes in 39.3 s = **0.51 scene changes/s**, but only 8 hard cuts (0.2/s). Most changes are **in-place morphs on the persistent dark set**. Shot lengths: type-only beats 0.6–1.7 s, proof collages 2.0–5.7 s (the longest is the Effects & Presets tree, 5.7 s, which runs under three nouns). The fastest section is 28.3–36.1 (scenes of 0.6–2.4 s).
- **Easing signatures (measured):**
  - **Fly-in / zoom-through:** a 1–2 frame impulse then an exponential tail. Clock reveal zoom: −7.6 → −1.6 → −1.1 → −0.9 → −0.65 → −0.49 → −0.43 → −0.36, i.e. **~50% of the motion in frame 1**, then a 10–12-frame tail at ×0.75–0.8 per frame (≈ `Easing.out(Easing.exp)` or a spring `{damping: 26, stiffness: 300, mass: 0.6}`). The card explosion at 33.2 shows the same shape (−7.3 → −1.2 → −1.3 → −1.1 …).
  - **Warp fly-in (laptop at 12.37):** zoom +2.5, +2.8, +2.0, +1.4, +1.05, +0.66: an 8-frame expo ease-out with no overshoot.
  - **Exits are ease-in:** the laptop accelerates for 20 frames (flow 0.2 → 3.1) and the cut lands on peak velocity (`Easing.in(Easing.cubic)`, 0.67 s). Exit velocity hands off into the next scene's entrance.
  - **Overshoot is rare.** It appears only as a 1-frame sign flip on the clock zoom-out (+2.9 between −7.6 and −1.6). Text never bounces.
  - **Text-weight morph:** 4–8 frames from blob to Regular (e.g. "Stop": stacked echo 28.37 → Bold 28.40–28.43 → Regular ~28.47–28.5; "spending": blob at 28.80 → Regular at 29.07, ~8 frames).

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Zoom-through on a selected card** (2.87): the clicked card scales toward the lens over 2 frames (zoom +7.4/frame, 52 px/frame at full res), and the next scene is already composed around a small copy of the same card. Rebuild: scale the card 1 → 6 over 2 frames with `Easing.in(Easing.quad)` and a radial blur, cut, then land the next scene's card at scale 1.15 → 1 over 6 frames.
2. **RGB-smear glitch to black** (10.6–10.9): 3 frames of horizontal smear (R/G/B channel offsets ±20–40 px plus a 60 px horizontal blur, luma peak 62), then a 6-frame fade to near-black. Rebuild: three tinted copies with `mix-blend-mode: screen`, translateX ±30 px, `filter: blur(…)` via SVG `feGaussianBlur stdDeviation="30 0"`, opacity 1 → 0.
3. **Hyperspace warp + type shatter** (12.27–12.40): white streaks radiate from centre for 4 frames before the cut; the caption breaks into stretched blocky slabs (2 frames, near-white flash, luma 106); the next scene flies in on the same radial motion. Rebuild: a `<Starfield>` canvas (300 particles, velocity ∝ distance from centre, trail length 20–60 px) plus a `<ScrambleSlabs>` of the text using `scaleX(3)` on random glyph slices.
4. **Weight-morph caption replace** (13.80, 4.27, 35.3): the old line fades while the new word blooms in as a heavy blob and relaxes, so there is no cut. 6–8 frames.
5. **Velocity hand-off cut** (28.33): the laptop exits on an ease-in and the next caption drops in from above with an echo trail, so motion continues downward across the cut.
6. **Type-to-picture zoom-out** (29.13): "Stop spending" turns ghost-bold and fades as a full-frame clock zooms out from beyond the lens, then keeps zooming out until the AE UI frames it ("it was AE all along").
7. **Giant-word zoom-down** (31.80–32.15): a 180%-width word scales to inline size, with a smear frame in the middle.
8. **Card explosion in** (32.93–33.63): 3 waves of cards fly from z > camera to their slots, each wave a 1-frame zoom spike (−7.3, −4.0) with a soft tail; the caption sits on top.
9. **Phone slide + re-layout** (36.1 / 37.8): the phone enters from the top, then the whole phone shifts right to make room for the caption (a layout change instead of a cut).
10. **Echo-glitch out** (17.33): the display word duplicates into 3 offset copies for 2 frames, then hard cut to the next scene's black opening.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **One persistent set.** A near-black field with a drifting single-hue glow and a fixed dot grid never cuts away, so 20 scenes feel like one place. Only the foreground changes.
2. **Show the real product.** Actual template thumbnails, a real AE screen recording and a real IG comment UI work as proof. Generic icons would read as an ad.
3. **Alternate density.** Type-only beats (one small line on black) are followed by packed 3D card collages. The contrast makes both feel bigger.
4. **Make plain type move with weight, not position.** The heavy-blob → Regular morph gives every word an arrival without bouncing it around, which keeps the Swiss calm.
5. **Put one brand colour on the words the brand owns** ("same?", "EarnEdits", "professional", "charge") and nowhere else except the cursor.
6. **Use the cursor as the narrator's hand.** It moves on eased arcs, dwells, then clicks on the VO verb ("download" → press).
7. **Impulse-then-tail fly-ins:** half the distance in frame 1 and a 10–12-frame exponential settle. Exits are ease-ins that hand their velocity to the next shot.
8. **Reveal-the-tool twist:** zoom out of a finished visual into the software that made it. That is the product's whole promise in one move.
9. **Comment-keyword CTA shown as UI** (the comment typed, then the auto-reply), so the action is demonstrated rather than only spoken.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1920×1080` (or 1276×718 to match), `30 fps`, duration = VO length + 0.6 s (≈ 1180 frames).
- **Persistent layers (bottom → top):** `<VoidBackground color="#030703">` → `<DriftGlow color="#0d2a12" radius="55%" keyframes=[{f:0,x:20%,y:45%},{f:150,x:70%,y:60%}…]>` (interpolate with `Easing.inOut(Easing.sin)` over 60–90 frames between scenes) → `<DotGrid pitch={74*1.5} dot={2} opacity={0.12} mask="radial(transparent 30%, black 80%)">` → scene content → `<Vignette strength={0.5}>`.
- **Components:**
  - `WeightMorphWord({text, at})`: render the word twice. The "blob" copy is Helvetica Neue Bold with `filter: blur(6px)`, scaleX 1.15 and opacity 1 → 0 over 6 frames. The final copy is Roman with `blur(4px → 0)` and opacity 0 → 1 over 6 frames. Alternatively, with a variable font (Inter Display / Neue Haas Unica VF), animate `font-variation-settings: 'wght' 900 → 400` over 6 frames with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)` plus blur 5 → 0 px.
  - `BracketTypewriter({words, brackets:['/ /','< >','{ }'], framesPerWord:8})`.
  - `TrackingTypewriter({text, from:'0.4em', to:'0em', charsPerFrame:0.7})`: letter-spacing interpolates over the full typing duration.
  - `CaretTypewriter({text, caretColor:'#3baf49', framesPerChar:2, blink:15})`.
  - `GlitchLabel({text:'EarnEdits', bg:'#3baf49'})`: 6 frames of random black rectangles (3–8 per frame) clipping the text, then clean.
  - `CardDeck3D({cards, layout:'fan'|'stack'|'row'})`: a CSS `perspective: 1400px` container. Each card is `rotateY(-18°) rotateX(6°)`, `border-radius: 18px`, `box-shadow: 0 30px 60px rgba(0,0,0,.5)`. Entrances use `spring({fps, frame: frame - i*3, config:{damping: 22, stiffness: 220, mass: 0.7}})` for translateY 300 → 0. Shuffle: every 12 frames the front card goes translateZ −200 and opacity 0 while the next rises.
  - `ZoomThroughCard`: scale 1 → 6 over 2 frames (`Easing.in(Easing.quad)`), cut; the next scene's card goes 1.15 → 1 over 6 frames.
  - `FlyInImpulse({fromScale: 3, frames: 12})`: `interpolate(frame, [0,12], [fromScale,1], {easing: Easing.out(Easing.exp)})` plus a motion blur of 2–3 sub-frames (`@remotion/motion-blur` `<CameraMotionBlur samples={6}>`).
  - `Cursor3D({path, clicks})`: a green extruded SVG arrow on a bezier path (`Easing.bezier(0.45,0,0.2,1)`); on click, scale 0.9 for 3 frames and press the target button (translateY 6 px, fill → `#8e8e8e`).
  - `DeviceMockup({device:'macbook'|'iphone', video})` with `<OffthreadVideo>` inside the screen.
  - `RGBSmearExit` (3 frames) and `StarfieldWarp` (canvas, 4 frames before the cut, 8 frames after).
  - `GiantWordZoomDown({word, fromScale: 11, frames: 10})` landing into a flex-row caption.
  - `CommentCTA`: an iPhone comment bar sliding from the top (`spring {damping: 20, stiffness: 160}`), typing "PROJECT FILE" at 2 frames/char, then a translateX +25% re-layout with a Comments sheet and the reply.
- **Timing source:** drive every caption and tree item from the Whisper word JSON: `appearFrame = round(word.s * fps) + 1`. Clamp to the scene start when a word would fall before its scene's cut.
- **Tricky bits:** the AE screen recordings can be any user screen capture placed in `DeviceMockup`. The clock "zoom-out reveal" is a full-frame render scaled from 3.2 → 1 inside a screenshot of the AE viewer, with the UI chrome fading in from opacity 0 over the first 6 frames.

## 9. Variation ideas
