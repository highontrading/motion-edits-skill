## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** motivational lyric edit (a "quiet grind" message) and a motion-design showcase. No narrator: the song *is* the script.
- **Beats (lyric lines → scenes):**

  - 1.57–3.50 "…to everybody around". Noir spotlight on a lone man in a chair. TO EVERYBODY tag, then the camera rolls upside-down while AROUND repeats along a spiral.
  - 3.50–5.20 "Some people smile". Chrome SOME PEOPLE inside an eclipse ring, then 3D comedy/tragedy masks with "Smile" script and SMILE pill tags.

- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** frame 0 is a flat grey #323232 placeholder with orange letter fragments (1 frame). That is four distinct motions inside 1.5 s, on a surreal image (a taped-over face) that makes the viewer ask a question.
- **Script → visual rule:** **one lyric line = one colour world**, and the line's noun or verb becomes a literal prop: dreams → a blindfolded dreamer; everybody → a lone man under a spotlight; smile → theatre masks; see you → binoculars; down → a falling body; quiet → a speech bubble on a monolith; sound → speakers plus an ink explosion; success → a diamond; ground → a crown and crowd.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** 14 scenes in 14.7 s (~0.95 changes/s), plus 2–4 sub-moves inside each scene, so something big changes every ~0.3 s. Scenes are 0.5–1.9 s. The longest holds are the "breath" moments: the spotlight (1.9 s), the red poster (1.7 s) and the finale (1.4 s).
- **On twos:** almost all animation steps every 2 frames (15 fps feel at 30 fps). Combined with smear frames this gives a punchy, stop-frame "edit" texture. Rebuild at 30 fps and hold every other frame (`Math.floor(frame/2)*2`) on the animated layers, or render at 15 fps.
- **Easing signatures:**
  - **Entrances are ease-out-heavy.** Card spin: peak velocity at frames 5–7 out of 18, then a long tail. Spotlight fade: luma +7 → +2 per step, decaying.
  - **Big camera rolls are ease-in-out:** the 180° roll ramps over ~6 steps, peaks and decays over ~6 steps, ~0.9 s total.
  - **Settles are long:** the finale decays over ~30 frames, the KEEP IT monolith over ~24 frames.
  - **Overshoot shows up as sign flips** in zoom on several impacts: 9.17 z +4.1 then −1.95, a pop-and-recoil; 12.33 z +1.9 then −2.0 then +1.2.
- **Entrance / exit vocab:**
  - tumbling per-letter drop (DON'T, Make): each letter ~3–4 frames, staggered 1–2 frames;
  - **strip split stagger** (DREAMS letters jump into two rows in 2 frames);
  - random-order letter reveal (TO EVERYBODY, ~20 frames total);
  - text on a spiral path (AROUND);
  - 3D fly-in assemble (SUCCESS, ~7 frames);
  - whip-spin with motion blur (masks, 3 frames);
  - radial pop of stickers (SMILE tags);
  - slam-in with no tween (THE GROUND, WHAT, BE: 1–2 frames);
  - rise from bottom (binocular hands, fedora man, M);
  - burst (ink splat, 2–3 frames).
- **Camera:** dominated by **rolls and zooms**, not pans. Net rotation per shot: −42 (card), −25 (roll scene, visually ~180°), −18 (DOWN), +12 (ink wipe). Net zoom: −56 on the spotlight scene, −13 to −18 on the green, KEEP, ink and finale scenes, which are mostly pull-backs. Lateral pans appear only in the finale build (pan_x −12).
- **Secondary motion:** HUD crosshair rotation, a flickering spotlight, blinking sparkles on the diamond, flame flicker, an RGB-split jitter on the hands, and a constant micro-drift so no frame is ever static (min flow ≈0.4 even on holds).

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Vertical camera jump** (0.70): the scene translates down ~1/3 frame in 2 frames to reveal a new band above; no blur, just a hard step on twos. Rebuild: `translateY` of the whole collage from 0 to +33% over 2 frames.
2. **Zoom-out punch to black** (1.57–1.63): the DREAMS layout scales down/away violently (flow 54 px/frame at 180 px ≈ 215 px/frame real) and black takes over; the next scene fades up from black. 2–3 frames, then a 21-frame light-up.
3. **Camera roll through** (2.4–3.1): a ~180° roll with a spiral of text; the scene ends upside-down and dims. ~0.9 s ease-in-out.
4. **Dim / eclipse** (3.43–3.67, 4.13–4.30): luma drops to ~13 over 4 frames, or a black disc rotates over a glowing ring.
5. **Echo-smear + rising shape wipe** (5.07–5.20): the outgoing object duplicates into stretched trails (time-displacement), while a flat green triangle rises from the bottom edge and fills the frame in 4 frames. Rebuild: 4–6 copies of the layer offset 6–10 px with decreasing opacity, plus an SVG polygon whose apex `y` animates from 100% to −20%.
6. **Diagonal roll into new layout** (6.0–6.3): the frame rotates ~25° and white blotches eat through it, then a cut to the next layout already rotated, which then straightens out (match on angle).
7. **Horizontal smear + desaturation** (7.57–7.70): a 2-step lateral streak (pan_x −10.9) and zoom −9.9 while saturation goes 65→0. 4 frames.
8. **Bar-to-monolith morph** (8.10–8.53): the black text bar rotates to vertical and becomes the pillar the next prop stands on (object morph-transition).
9. **Saturation fade-in with zoom kick** (9.07–9.37): grey scene → red poster by colour fade over 9 frames with a +4 zoom pop.
10. **Ink / liquid wipe** (10.83–11.27): organic black tendrils sweep across a paper card until the frame is black. ~13 frames. Rebuild: an animated SVG blob path (or a luma-matte video of ink) as a mask.
11. **Tilt-down reveal** (12.0): the camera travels down from the black sky (diamond) to the red ground with hands.
12. **Shard dim** (13.2–13.33): red glass shards pass the lens and darken the frame before the final slam.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **One line, one world.** Change the dominant hue, texture and font for every lyric or VO line. Seven colour worlds in 14.7 s, each limited to one hue + black/white.
2. **The keyword is a set piece, not a subtitle.** Set it huge (70–90% of frame width) and build the scene around it: letters split around a falling body, text behind a crown, a word on a spiral.
3. **Depth sandwich.** Put type between a foreground cut-out and the background so it feels physically placed.
4. **Beat beats syllable.** Land cuts and slams on the music grid (±0.1 s) even if that's 0.2–0.4 s after the word is sung.
5. **Animate on twos plus smear frames** for a tactile, hand-made energy at no extra cost.
6. **Literal, slightly absurd props** (masks for "smile", binoculars for "see you", a speech bubble for "quiet") make abstract lyrics instantly readable.
7. **Use saturation and luma as transitions** (crush to grey, fade from black, colour bloom) so cuts feel motivated without generic dissolves.
8. **HUD ornaments + grain + vignette** (crosshairs, circuit lines, barcodes, tiny body text) make simple layouts look designed.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1080` (or 1080×1350/1920 with the same centre-stage layout), `30 fps`, ~450 frames. Animated layers are stepped on twos with `const f = Math.floor(frame/2)*2`.
- **Layer stack per scene:**
  1. `<TextureBg kind="chalk|stage|green-noise|grey-seamless|red-poster|mustard-paper">`
  2. `<Vignette strength={0.6}>`
  3. back props (`<Cutout>`)
  4. `<KeywordType>`
  5. front props (cut-out hands or heads overlapping the type)
  6. `<HudOrnaments>` (crosshair circles, circuit polylines)
  7. `<GrainOverlay opacity={0.1}>`
  8. `<ChromaticSplit px={2}>` (on intense scenes)
- **Components (props and timing):**
  - `LyricScene({hue, texture, font, keyword, startFrame})`, with a scene length snapped to the beat grid (`beatFrames = 27.15` at 66.3 BPM; cut = `Math.round(n*beatFrames)`).
  - `StripStagger`: letters on a strip; at `t0` each odd letter `translateY(-100%)` and each even letter `+0%` over 2 frames (no easing, stepped).
  - `TumbleLetters`: per letter `spring({damping: 10, stiffness: 220, mass: 0.5})` on translateY from −120% and rotate from ±40°, stagger 2 frames.
  - `RandomOrderReveal`: shuffle the letter indices and reveal one per 2 frames.
  - `SpiralText`: SVG `<textPath>` on an Archimedean spiral, repeated word, plus a whole-scene `rotate` from 0→180° over 28 frames with `Easing.inOut(Easing.cubic)`.
  - `CardSpinIn`: rotate 45°→10° and scale 0.9→1 over 18 frames, `Easing.out(Easing.exp)`.
  - `EchoSmear(layer, copies=5, offset=8px)`;
  - `RisingShapeWipe(color, frames=4)`;
  - `InkWipe` (an SVG blob path morphing via `interpolate` between 3 path keyframes, or a luma-matte MP4 used as a CSS `mask-image`);
  - `SatCrush` (`filter: saturate()` 1→0 in 4 frames);
  - `ZoomPunchOut` (scale 1→0.3 over 3 frames, then black).
  - `SlamType`: scale 1.15→1 in 2 frames, plus a 1-frame white flash at 20% opacity and a camera-shake of ±6 px for 4 frames.
  - `FrameInFrame`: two white 2 px rectangles inset 0 and 8 px, appearing in 1 frame.
- **Tricky effects:**
  - Chrome 3D type: CSS gradient fill `linear-gradient(#fff, #8a8a8a 45%, #fff 55%, #555)` with `background-clip:text`, a stacked `text-shadow` extrude (8 layers, 1 px, #333) and `filter: drop-shadow(0 0 12px #fff8)` for bloom. Or use Three.js `TextGeometry` with `MeshStandardMaterial({metalness:1, roughness:0.2})` and an env map.
  - The night-vision green: a solid #3d8848 background + `radial-gradient` vignette + SVG `feTurbulence` noise at 15% opacity + text in #1e3d22 with a 10-layer extrude.
