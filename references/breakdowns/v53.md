**Source creator handle:** earn.edits · **Format:** 720×1280, 9:16, 16 fps (delivered file; the cadence of the locker push, alternating ~0.9/~1.4 zoom units per frame, points to a 24/25 fps master that was re-encoded), 20.1 s · **Shots:** 12 real (14 detected: cut 1 at 1.44 is a number swap inside shot 1, and cuts 12+13 at 18.31/18.44 are one 3-frame zoom-through) · **Avg shot:** ~1.7 s (median ~1.1 s) · **Music BPM:** ~112 (beat every 0.535 s) · **VO:** yes, English, upbeat male announcer, fast and clipped

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** promo / CTA for a monthly editing contest ("Frame War") run by the channel. It is a direct-response ad: claim → doubt → proof → offer → mechanics → CTA.
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–2.3 **Hook (money claim):** "You just earned $250…" over a gold-and-marble "wealth" collage. The number counts 200 → 250.
  - 2.3–4.2 **Qualifier:** "…editing 13 seconds". A cloche is lifted to reveal the Ae/Pr icons, then a hand pushes a stopwatch into frame.
  - 4.2–5.2 **Doubt beat:** "Sounds unreal?" A shocked voxel character sits at a keyboard.
  - 5.2–7.6 **Proof:** "Last month somebody did exactly that." A September calendar, then the voxel character lifting an emerald trophy.
  - 7.6–9.9 **Offer:** "Now October's spot opens up." A wall of locked achievement lockers; one padlock drops and its locker opens with green light.
  - 9.9–14.6 **Mechanics:** "Any style, free entry, your timeline versus every other editor." This runs through a stationery flat-lay, a magnifier, a pointing fist, and an arena of voxel editors at desks.
  - 14.6–17.3 **How to enter:** "Comment 'earn edits', we'll DM the link." A green calculator rises; its keys spell EARN/DITS and its LCD types EARNEDITS.
  - 17.4–20.1 **CTA/title:** "Frame War is live, go claim it." FRAME WAR pixel title, the trophy rises, the camera zooms through it, and the shot holds on the jumping hero with LIVE behind.
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** frame 0 already shows the full collage (Corinthian columns at a tilt, floating gold "$" glyphs, black checkerboard), with "YOU" on a cream paper strip. By 0.25 s a small navy "JUST" badge sticks under it. At 0.44–0.50 s an orange sticker with a **page-curl peel** slaps over the strip reading "EARNED". At 0.75–1.0 s chunky pixel digits "2", "0", "0" pop in one by one while copper rods swing in carrying gold coins. That is four layered events in 1.4 s, then the 200 flips to 250 at 1.44 s with a hand-drawn gold ellipse drawn round it.
- **Script → visual rule:** each clause gets a literal object metaphor: money → columns and coins; editing → a cloche "serving" the Ae/Pr apps; 13 seconds → a stopwatch; last month → a calendar; opens up → a locker unlocking; any style → a scrapbook of footage; your timeline → a fist pointing at the viewer; versus every editor → an arena of desks; comment code → keys on a calculator. Only the keyword of each clause is set as type.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** a deliberate **hybrid**. (a) Halftone cut-out photo collage (columns, coins, hands, cloche, stopwatch, fist on a stick) on flat grey or black grounds. (b) **AI-generated voxel/"blocky-game" renders** (characters, trophy, lockers, arena), shot with shallow depth of field and cinematic lighting. (c) A stationery flat-lay (grid paper, post-it, torn paper, paperclip, pen, polaroid frames) with **live footage playing inside the photo frames**. Three looks are unified by one finishing pass: a fine halftone dot screen plus grain over every frame.
- **Backgrounds:** black checkerboard (shot 1), seamless light-grey studio sweep with vignette (2, 4 calendar on grid paper, 9), dark teal-black game interiors (3, 5, 6, 10), blue-to-gold sunset sky over a voxel city (11, 12). Light and dark grounds alternate almost every cut: luma per shot 71 → 159 → 60 → 143 → 72 → 33 → 166 → 136 → 168 → 38 → 132 → 107. That alternation is a big part of why each cut reads as an impact.
- **Colour:**
  - Shot 1 money collage: black `#0a0a09`, marble grey `#cfcac8`/`#968d7d`, signal orange `#e65e1f`, gold `#d9c58a`-ish $ glyphs, copper rods.
  - Studio scenes: light grey `#b1aaa9`–`#d6d7d1` with one hot accent: red `#c12427` (EDITING), blue `#2259c7` (TIMELINE), yellow `#e2d53d` (post-it/tape).
  - Game scenes: near-black teal `#0a0f0a`/`#122b1a`/`#282e2a` with emerald `#088476`/`#1fae5a` (trophy gem, opened locker glow) and gold `#c9a24a`.
  - Finale: sky `#4c92b9`/`#a4cdd6` to sunset gold `#d7b978`, hero jacket red.
  - **Accent rule:** emerald green is the "prize" colour (gem, opened locker, calculator, arena lights). It appears from the trophy onward and owns the second half.
- **Typography:**
  - **Heavy wide grotesk** (Archivo Black / Rubik Black / Montserrat Black class), ALL CAPS. "YOU" black on a cream strip (~45% frame width); "EARNED" black on the orange sticker (~55% FW); "SOUNDS UNREAL" in **muted brick red `#b5383d` with a soft pale outer glow**, two lines filling ~90% FW and set in the top 25% of frame.
  - **Tall condensed grotesk** (Anton / Bebas Neue Bold class): "EDITING" in red `#c12427` at ~95% FW, partly **hidden behind the cloche** (text-behind-object depth); "YOUR" dark grey `#2a2a2a` + "TIMELINE" blue `#2259c7`, each line ~85% FW, sandwiched **behind the pointing fist**.
  - **Pixel/bitmap font** (Minecraftia / Pixeloid Sans Bold / "Press Start"-wide class) for the game half: "SOMEBODY DID / EXACTLY THAT" white on **per-word black label boxes** (each word its own box, boxes slightly misaligned vertically); "WE'LL DM THE LINK" white with a dark shadow and no box; the "FRAME WAR" title as huge white pixel letters at ~70% opacity, ~85% FW, stacked 2 lines; counter digits 200/250 in a chunky pixel face, white with a heavy black outline over orange.
  - **Handwriting** (Caveat Bold / Gochi Hand class): "Any" in black on a yellow post-it, "Free Entry" on a torn cream paper scrap, "EarnEdits" in rounded bold on yellow tape. It is paired with "STYLE" in heavy blue sans with a print-distress texture. This is the casual-vs-bold contrast pair.
  - **Logo lock-up:** "FRAME WAR" in a gold bevelled game-logo face with "Earnedits" in a small sans between two rule lines, used on the arena backdrop.
  - **Emphasis devices:** hand-drawn ellipse round the key number (gold on 250, red on SEP), and a sticker peel for the verb.
- **Layout:** the hero object is centred. Type sits either in the top quarter (SOUNDS UNREAL, FRAME WAR) or across the centre band (EDITING, YOUR TIMELINE) with the hero object in front of it. The lower 20% is mostly left to props, and captions never sit below y ≈ 75%.
- **Assets:** marble columns, coins, copper rods, a silver cloche, Ae/Pr app icons, a stopwatch, hands (halftoned stock cut-outs), a fist-on-a-stick meme prop, a desk calendar, calculators, a pen, post-its, polaroids, tape, a paperclip, a magnifier ring, and a set of voxel/blocky characters with matching trophy, lockers and arena (AI-generated stills, animated with 2.5D moves only).
- **Finishing / FX:** a **fine halftone dot screen over the entire frame** (visible on skin, metal and sky at full res), film grain, soft vignette, strong shallow DOF on the renders (backgrounds ~8–15 px blur), **RGB split + radial blur during zoom-throughs**, glow on the opened locker, and a soft-edged circular vignette for the magnifier view.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** 12 shots in 20.1 s (0.6 cuts/s). The fastest stretch is 4.25–5.94 (three shots in 1.7 s around "Sounds unreal? Last month"). The longest hold is the arena/calculator shot (4.25 s), which carries the comment-code instruction, because the viewer must read it. The finale holds 1.7 s.
- **Easing signatures (from per_frame.csv, 16 fps):**
  - **Landings after a zoom-through** are exponential ease-outs over 8–10 frames (0.5–0.6 s). Shot 3: −5.8 → 0 over 9 frames, then a **+1.4 rebound for 1 frame** (overshoot, sign flip). Shot 7: +8.2 → +0.5 over 10 frames. Shot 4: +5.6 → +0.6 over 8 frames, half-life ~2 frames.
  - **Prop rises** (stopwatch hand, calculator, trophy): ease-in for ~4–6 frames, then a long ease-out tail of 15–34 frames. The calculator reaches peak velocity at frame 6 and is still moving 2 s later. This is the "heavy object floats up and settles" feel, so use bezier `(0.35, 0, 0.1, 1)` over ~40 frames at 24 fps.
  - **Pushes:** shot 6 is a *linear* constant-speed push (no easing) to build tension, then a 2-frame acceleration into the zoom-through. Shots 8 and 10 also end with a 3–5 frame acceleration (zoom rises 0.3 → 4.6) into the next cut, so the outgoing shot leans into the transition.
  - Type pops are 1–2 frames (hard pop or a 2-frame scale). Ellipse write-ons take 3–4 frames.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:** sticker slap with page-curl peel (2 frames); per-digit pixel pop; counter swap 200→250 (1 frame); hand-drawn ellipse write-on (3–4 frames); prop rise from the bottom edge (stopwatch, calculator, trophy); objects dropping in from the top (voxel desks, 3–6 frames each, cascade ~0.4 s); per-word boxed pixel caption pops; handwriting write-on (~4 frames per word); out-of-order letter fade (title, ~5 frames total); LCD scramble-resolve (4 frames); text revealed by an object lifting (cloche → EDITING).
- **Camera:** every scene has a 2.5D camera move: zoom-out reveals (shot 5 −30, shot 3 −24), constant push-ins (shot 6 +57.5 net), and punch-in settles. Rotation is used only inside transitions (shot 3 net +12°, shot 5 −7°). There is no lateral whip in this piece; all energy is on the Z axis.
- **Secondary motion:** footage clips cycling inside the polaroid every 4–5 frames; the trophy-holding character bobbing; coins swinging on rods; the glow on SOUNDS UNREAL flickering.
- **Sync:**
  - **Beats (~112 BPM):** 9 of 12 real cuts land within ±0.1 s of a beat. Examples: 1.44 vs beat 1.39 (+0.05), 2.31 vs 2.37 (−0.06), 4.25 vs 4.20 (+0.05), 5.25 vs 5.34 (−0.09), 5.94 vs 5.88 (+0.06), 7.56 vs 7.57 (−0.01), 11.81 vs 11.75 (+0.06), 17.38 vs 17.48 (−0.10), 18.44 vs 18.55 (−0.11).

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Zoom-through with RGB split** at 4.0–4.31 (stopwatch → voxel character), 5.88–6.0 (calendar → trophy) and 18.31–18.44 (trophy → LIVE). Outgoing: 2–3 frames of scale-up toward a target point (the stopwatch face, the trophy gem) with rotation +2…+4.5°/frame, radial blur and a red/cyan channel offset of ~6–10 px. Incoming: starts over-scaled (~1.6×) and blurred, and lands in 8–10 frames with ease-out and a 1-frame overshoot. Total ≈ 5 frames of chaos plus 8 frames of settle. Rebuild: `scale` interpolate 1→2.5 (outgoing, `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)`, 3 f) + SVG radial blur (several scaled copies at low opacity) + R/B channel `translate` ±8 px; incoming `scale` 1.6→1 with `spring({damping: 14, stiffness: 160})`.
2. **Zoom-into-light (portal)** at 9.81–10.06: a linear push accelerates into the glowing open locker; 1 frame of motion blur, 1 frame of full-frame green `#1fae5a` glow, then the next scene bursts out with radial blur. Rebuild: as above but insert a 1–2 frame solid-colour frame sampled from the target light.
3. **Magnifier/loupe wipe** at 10.94–11.06: a thick black ring (the magnifier rim, ~20% frame width thick) slides in from the right over 2 frames. The cut happens under the ring, and the next frame is the zoomed view with a soft circular vignette (feather ~15% radius). Rebuild: a ring SVG translateX 120%→0 in 2 frames with blur; swap to a `<MagnifiedView>` (scale 2.2, radial-gradient mask transparent 55% → black 75%).
4. **Iris-expand from a dot** at 12.94–13.19: a soft black dot (~12% FW) appears centre on the light background, then on the cut the next scene is shown *inside* an expanding soft circle (radius ~35% → 100% over 2–3 frames) while the outside fades to the scene too. Rebuild: `clipPath: circle(r at 50% 50%)` animated 0 → 75% over 3 frames with a 40 px feather (radial-gradient mask).
5. **Object exit/drop as transition cue** at 12.81–13.0: the fist drops down and out while a new-scene prop falls in from the top *before* the iris opens, so the cut is seeded by motion.
6. **Hard cuts on beat with luma flip** (2.31, 5.25, 7.56, 11.81, 17.38): no effect at all, but each one flips light ↔ dark ground (e.g. 55 → 184 luma at 2.31, 51 → 144 at 5.25, 35 → 132 at 17.38), which gives a "flash" impact without a white flash.
7. **In-shot value swap** at 1.44: the counter changes 200 → 250 in one frame with a 1-frame camera jolt (zoom −10.6), so it reads as a cut.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Mixed media, one finish.** Collage cut-outs, AI 3D renders and stationery flat-lays can share a video if every frame gets the same halftone dot screen + grain + vignette pass.
2. **A literal object for every clause** (stopwatch, calendar, locker, calculator, trophy) turns a dry CTA script into a sequence of tiny visual gags.
3. **Travel through objects on the Z axis:** zoom into the stopwatch, into the locker's light, through the trophy. Every big transition is motivated by an object in the frame.
4. **Alternate light and dark grounds** on consecutive shots so hard cuts hit like flashes (luma swings of 80–130 points).
5. **Type behind the hero object** (EDITING behind the cloche, YOUR TIMELINE behind the fist) gives depth and makes big type feel physical.
6. **Match the font to the world:** heavy grotesk in the collage world, pixel font in the voxel world, handwriting on the stationery. Typeface switches are scene-semantic, not random.
7. **Landings settle in 8–10 frames with a 1-frame overshoot.** Heavy props rise with a long 1–2 s tail.
8. **Put the call to action on a physical prop** (calculator keys spelling the comment code) and hold that shot longest (4.25 s).
9. **Cuts within ±0.1 s of the beat and 0–0.25 s ahead of the VO word**, with captions appearing on the spoken syllable.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, ~600 frames (20 s). Drive everything from a `words.json` (whisper timestamps) and a `beats.json`.
- **Layer stack per scene (bottom → top):** `<Backdrop variant="checker|studioSweep|gridPaper|renderStill">` → `<BackgroundType>` (giant word, placed *behind* the hero) → `<HeroCutout>` or `<RenderStill dofBlur={10}>` → `<Props>` (rise or drop) → `<Caption variant="boxedPixel|handwritten|grotesk">` → `<EllipseScribble>` → `<HalftoneOverlay dot={3} opacity={0.18}>` → `<Grain opacity={0.08}>` → `<Vignette strength={0.35}>`.
- **Components:**
  - `ZoomThrough({target:{x,y}, outFrames:3, inFrames:9})`: outgoing `scale 1→2.6` with `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)` + `rotate 0→8deg` + `<RadialBlur samples={8}>` + `<RGBSplit px={8}>`. Incoming `scale 1.6→1` via `spring({fps, frame, config:{damping: 13, stiffness: 170, mass: 0.8}})`, which gives the ~1-frame overshoot measured here.
  - `PunchSettle`: scale 1.12→1 with an exponential ease-out over 8 frames (`Easing.out(Easing.exp)`).
  - `PropRise({from:'bottom', frames:40})`: translateY 110%→0 with `Easing.bezier(0.35,0,0.1,1)`; optional rotate −4°→0.
  - `DropIn({stagger:4})`: desks/characters fall from −40% with `spring({damping: 10, stiffness: 220})`.
  - `BoxedPixelCaption`: each word is a `<span>` with a `#000` background, padding `0.1em 0.25em`, and a random y-offset of ±4 px; it pops at `wordStart − 1 frame` with scale 0.9→1 over 2 frames. Font: Pixeloid Sans / Minecraftia, white, ~7% FW cap height.
  - `StickerPeel`: an orange `#e65e1f` blob that scales 1.15→1 in 2 frames, with a curled-corner PNG (or a CSS gradient wedge) that flattens over 4 frames.
  - `CounterSwap`: discrete value change on a beat, plus a 1-frame `scale 0.96` jolt.
  - `EllipseScribble`: an SVG path with `strokeDashoffset` 100%→0 in 4 frames, stroke 6 px, gold `#d4a72c` or red `#c8262c`.
  - `MagnifierWipe`: ring PNG translateX 120%→0 in 2 frames, then `<MagnifiedView scale={2.2} mask="radial-gradient(circle, #000 55%, transparent 75%)">` over black.
  - `IrisExpand`: `clipPath: circle()` from 6% to 80% over 3 frames, with a feathered edge via a radial-gradient `mask-image`.
  - `FootageInFrame`: `<OffthreadVideo>` clips cut every 4–5 frames, masked into the polaroid rect with a slight paper-tint `mix-blend-mode: multiply`.
  - `LcdScramble`: random A–Z glyphs for 3 frames, then resolve left→right one glyph per frame.
  - `OutOfOrderLetters`: each letter fades 0→0.75 opacity over 3 frames with a random 0–6 frame delay.
- **Halftone overlay:** an SVG `<pattern>` of 3 px dots (or a pre-rendered dot PNG) at `mix-blend-mode: soft-light`, opacity 0.15–0.2, applied over the *entire* composition, renders included.
- **Depth-of-field sandwich (finale):** foreground trophy layer `filter: blur(14px)`, scale 1.4; hero sharp; background LIVE text `blur(6px)` at 85% white; whole stack `scale` 1→1.04 over the 1.7 s hold.
