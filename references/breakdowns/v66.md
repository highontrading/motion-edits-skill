## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** micro-biography / art-history story that pivots into a creator-education ad (selling project files). The story itself is the demo of "structure".
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–6.5 **Hook (thesis):** "He was drowning in pain and somehow turned it into some of the most beautiful art ever created." A literal visual metaphor (a man sinking under a Starry-Night sky), then the art.
  - 6.6–13.1 **Context:** the name card ("VAN GOGH"), mental and physical illness (a brain lifted out of an opened skull, a brain on old-master scales), "exact diagnoses remain debated".
  - 13.2–19.7 **Buildup / inciting incident:** "In 1888", the argument with Paul Gauguin, the ear.
  - 19.8–27.9 **Payoff:** he enters the Saint-Rémy asylum, is surrounded by his struggles, and paints The Starry Night (the museum-wall reveal).
  - 28.0–34.6 **Pivot + CTA:** "Every great edit has a structure." Puzzle pieces HOOK / CONTEXT / BUILDUP / PAYOFF (which name the four beats the video just used), then "Discover it with project files on earnedits.com" on a desk flat-lay with an Ae folder.
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** frame 0 is already a full, rich collage (navy star-sky texture with engraved clouds, cobalt water, a vintage cut-out man sinking, fish swimming at three depths). At 0.62 s, on "drowning", a 2-frame **punch-in** (~1.4× scale) brings the face closer. At 1.25 s, on "pain" (1.18), four red distressed "PAIN" words stamp in around the body. There is no title card: the metaphor itself is the hook.
- **Script → visual rule:** each clause gets one tableau. The **metaphor is made literal** (drowning = underwater; mental health = an opened skull with the brain; "debated" = a brain on scales; "surrounded by struggles" = his paintings orbiting him). Proper nouns get a label tab ("PAUL GAUGUIN", "Saint-Rémy asylum") or a title treatment ("VAN GOGH", "1888", "THE STARRY NIGHT"). The VO's key phrase is typed on screen in 3D-extruded caps.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** a painterly surreal collage. Vintage photographic cut-outs of Van Gogh (and AI-generated painterly-realistic stills/clips), placed inside sets made from his paintings (Starry Night sky, sunflowers, almond blossom, Café Terrace, Saint-Paul asylum) and baroque props (gilded frames, a brass keyhole, gilded eye ornaments, floating clock faces, roman numerals). Every scene is a "stage" with a clear foreground / midground / background, which allows parallax and DOF.
- **Backgrounds & textures:** each scene has a different surface: engraved star-sky + painted water; dark wood wall panelling; white paper with torn edges; olive-black studio wall with dust; an old-master oil painting crop; crimson velvet with damask; a dark plaster wall; a painterly studio interior; a Starry-swirl sky; a grimy hallway; a green self-healing cutting mat with a grid; a cylindrical painting-gallery room; a gothic college lawn; a stone/brick museum wall; grid paper; a wooden desk. Film grain over all of them, and a soft vignette.
- **Colour:** each scene carries **one Van Gogh hue** against a dark warm neutral. Cobalt `#163f98` / navy `#141723` (drowning); gilded gold `#b45c28` on brown `#433130` (portrait wall); orange leaves + cobalt coat on white `#cecaca`; olive-black `#211b14`/`#342a1e` (clocks); ochre `#604a2c` (scales); crimson `#2b0909`/`#401212` (velvet); sunflower yellow `#ae7f19` + grey-blue sky `#74787e`; teal-grey `#192021` (hallway); cutting-mat green `#4d6b47`/`#3d4430`; grid paper `#969794`/`#aeaea9`; desk walnut `#563221`/`#7d5440`. The accents are always chrome yellow/orange (labels, "1888", "TURNED IT INTO SOME", the folder) and a red marker (`#d02a2a`) for the final circle.
- **Typography:**
  - **Hero caption face:** a black-weight wide grotesk with slightly rounded corners (Rubik Black / Gilroy Black / Obviously Wide Black-like), ALL CAPS, tight tracking. Fill is a white → light-grey vertical gradient (`#ffffff` → `#c8c8c8`) with a **3–5 px grey 3D extrude** down-right plus a soft dark drop shadow (~8 px blur, 40%). Sizes: "THOUGH THE" ~90% of frame width; "EXACT" ~62% stacked over a smaller "DIAGNOSES" (~62% at 0.5× cap height); "LATER" ~55%; "HIS OWN STRUGGLES" 2 lines at ~85%; "EVERY GREAT EDIT" ~63%; secondary words ("VOLUNTARILY", "ENTERED", "THE") at ~25–30% width.
  - **Colour variant:** the same face in chrome orange `#f2a71b` with a darker orange extrude for "TURNED IT INTO SOME" (the turn of the thesis), and in distressed red `#d0353a` for the repeated "PAIN" stamps (~20% width each, rotated ±10°).
  - **Name title:** classical roman inscriptional capitals (Trajan Pro / Cinzel-like), white, wide letter-spacing (~0.12 em), ~83% of frame width, with thin **geometric construction lines** (circles, baselines, cap-height guides at ~30% opacity) drawn around each letter, like a type specimen or blueprint. It is used only for "VAN GOGH".
  - **Label tabs:** (a) "PAUL GAUGUIN" in white heavy caps on a chrome-yellow tab `#e2c21c` (~42% width × 30 px) placed top-left of the subject; (b) "Saint-Rémy asylum" in a small serif (Georgia/Libre Caslon-like) on a yellow highlighter parallelogram tab, slightly skewed, tucked against the frame's top-left corner.
  - **Inverse badge:** "REMAIN DEBATED" in gold `#e6b01a` heavy caps inside a white `#f4f2ee` rectangle with a soft shadow, laid over the brain.
  - **Puzzle labels:** white serif caps (Playfair / Caslon-like) with a small underline swash, inside collage-textured pieces (navy, green, rust, red).
  - **URL:** a squared techno display face (Orbitron / Audiowide-like) in white with a dark shadow, wrapped in **curly braces**: "{" in green `#22b14c` and "}" in white.
  - **Emphasis rule:** the hero phrase of each clause is typed in the extruded grotesk; nouns that are names get either the roman title or a yellow tab; an emotional turn ("turned it into some") switches to orange.
- **Layout & composition:** the subject is centred, and type sits either **above the subject's head** (y ≈ 15–30%: "THOUGH THE", "HIS OWN STRUGGLES", "EVERY GREAT EDIT") or **across the subject's torso** (y ≈ 45–55%: "VAN GOGH", "LATER", "TURNED IT INTO SOME"). The bottom 15% is left for UI except when "VAN GOGH" deliberately bleeds off the bottom edge as a cropped giant.
- **Assets:** vintage cut-out Van Gogh photo, painted-figure Van Gogh (AI-generated), AI-generated painterly scenes (studio argument, hallway walk, college lawn), real paintings (Starry Night, self-portraits, Sunflowers, Café Terrace, Saint-Paul asylum, landscapes), engraved clouds, fish cut-outs, almond-blossom branches, maple leaves, gilded frames (oval, heart, rectangle), clocks, roman numerals, a brass keyhole escutcheon, an anatomical brain (gilded), cartoon hands with a knife and ear, puzzle pieces, a yellow folder with Ae file cards, desk props (calculator, ruler, pen, tarot card, keyboard).
- **Finishing / FX:** consistent film grain (~4% luma noise), soft vignette, real depth of field (foreground leaves, the keyhole and the flying canvas heavily blurred), drop shadows under every frame and prop, motion blur on pans, chromatic smear on the zoom-out at 9.2 s, and gilded highlights.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** ~0.5 cuts/s plus ~2 in-shot events per shot (type-on, drop-in, punch, pan), so something changes every ~0.5–0.7 s. Shots are 1.3–2.2 s through the story. The two "explaining" shots are the longest (Gauguin argument 3.4 s, CTA desk 3.9 s).
- **Easing signatures (per_frame.csv):**
  - *Narrative camera pan (shot 10):* a symmetric **ease-in-out expo**. Velocity 0.28, 0.35, 0.70, 1.11, 1.66, 2.93 → **8.90, 9.87** → 3.39, 1.91, 1.22, 0.81, 0.45, 0.02. That is 14 frames at 16 fps (0.875 s), with almost all the distance covered in the middle 4 frames (peak ≈ 40 px/frame at 720 w). ≈ `Easing.bezier(0.85, 0, 0.15, 1)` over 26 frames at 30 fps. The return pan is asymmetric: 6 frames up to −10.8, then a ~10-frame tail (≈ `bezier(0.7, 0, 0.2, 1)`).
  - *Occluder whip (shots 15→16):* ease-in for 7 frames (−1.9 → −7.7), cut at peak velocity behind the dark pillar, ease-out for 8 frames (−12.2 → −2.4). Treat it as one continuous `bezier(0.6, 0, 0.2, 1)` move over ~30 frames at 30 fps, with the cut at its velocity peak.
  - *Push through the keyhole (shot 13):* a fast start then a long exponential ease-out: zoom/frame 9.16, 7.59, 6.53, 5.81, 4.94, 3.89, 2.87, 1.90, 1.69, 1.38 (≈ ×0.85/frame) ≈ `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)` over ~24 frames at 30 fps; total scale ≈ ×2.5.
  - *Prop pop (folder):* ~7-frame ease-out, no overshoot ≈ `spring({damping: 26, stiffness: 160, mass: 1})`.
  - *Lock snap (puzzle):* a 3-frame decay at ×0.4/frame, i.e. a very hard ease-out ≈ `Easing.out(Easing.exp)` over 6 frames at 30 fps, +12% scale.
  - *Punch-in cut (0.62, 8.06, 18.06):* a scale jump of 1.3–1.5× between consecutive frames, followed by a 2–4-frame settle (8.5 → 4.3 → 2.9).
  - *Brain fall / settle:* gravity ease-in out of shot 7, then a damped wobble in shot 8 (rotation peaks 2.4°, settles in ~8 frames) ≈ `spring({damping: 9, stiffness: 140, mass: 0.8})` on rotation.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:**

  - *Drop-in prop:* the gilded frame over the face, the brain from the top.
  - *Fly-in-and-settle:* the gallery frames in shot 2.
  - *Badge pop + type inside:* a 1-frame white box, then letters typed.
  - *Tab type-on, world-locked:* the Gauguin label.
  - *Draw-on:* vines up the suit, the red marker ellipse (~4–5 frames).
  - *Scale pop:* the folder.
  - *Puzzle assemble:* pieces slide inward ~30 px each and lock.
  - *Particle overlay:* falling maple leaves.

Plus 3 motivated big moves: the Gauguin ↔ Van Gogh pan pair, the keyhole push-through, and the occluder whip into the museum. ## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Punch-in match cut (0.62, 8.06, 18.06):** the same subject reframed 1.3–1.5× tighter between two frames, with a 2–4-frame settle. Used on an emotional word ("drowning", "severe"). Rebuild: `scale` jumps 1 → 1.4 at frame N, then `spring({damping: 20, stiffness: 300})` from 1.45 → 1.4.
2. **Paper-tear reveal (3.30–3.75):** ragged white-edged holes tear open from 3–4 points and merge over ~7 frames, revealing the next scene. Rebuild: animated SVG mask paths with noisy edges (`feTurbulence` + `feDisplacementMap` on a growing circle mask), a white 4 px stroke on the mask edge, and a slight paper shadow.
3. **Zoom-out chromatic blur → radial-blur resolve (9.12–9.5):** the outgoing frame pulls back (zoom −4.3) with RGB-split streaks; the incoming frame starts with a radial blur and resolves over 3 frames. Rebuild: scale 1 → 0.85 + RGB offset ±6 px over 3 frames; the incoming scene gets a zoom blur (stack of 8 scaled copies at 6% opacity) fading to 0.
4. **Motion bridge (11.31):** the brain falls out of the bottom of shot 7 and falls into the top of shot 8. The same object with the same velocity carries the eye across the cut.
5. **World-locked label pan (16.0–17.9):** not a cut, but a scene beat: the camera whips to a character, a label types on attached to the scene, and the camera whips back so the label leaves frame. Rebuild: put the label inside the panned layer, not in screen space.
6. **Vertical slide push (19.44–19.94):** the outgoing scene translates up ~25% over 4 frames with an accelerating velocity, and the incoming shot keeps drifting up for 3 frames.
7. **Defocus → keyhole push-through (21.69–22.5):** the outgoing shot blurs to ~20 px in 1–2 frames; the incoming shot starts on a blurred keyhole that racks into focus (1 frame), and the camera pushes through the keyhole opening (scale ×2.5, ~12 frames, ease-out). Rebuild: keyhole PNG with alpha as a foreground layer; scale it about the opening centre while the scene behind scales more slowly (parallax ratio ~3:1).
8. **Object-to-lens wipe (24.94):** an orbiting canvas flies at the camera until it fills the frame, then hard cut. Rebuild: the canvas scale 0.2 → 1.6 with rotateY 40° → 0° over 20 frames, ease-in; cut on the frame where it covers 100%.
9. **Occluder whip (26.6–27.5):** a lateral pan accelerates behind a dark foreground pillar, the cut hides inside the dark frame, and the next scene decelerates out. Rebuild: a black-gradient vertical bar entering from the right synced with both scenes' translateX; cut at 100% coverage.
10. **Hard cut on an onset (28.56):** the pivot from story to lesson is a clean cut onto grid paper, timed to the strongest music hit.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Build the sets from the subject's own artefacts.** Van Gogh's paintings are the skies, walls and props, so the whole piece looks like his world, not stock.
2. **Make every metaphor literal.** Drowning = underwater; diagnosis debated = a brain on scales; surrounded by struggles = paintings orbiting him. One clause, one surreal image.
3. **Motivate the cuts physically.** A keyhole push-through, a pillar occluder, a canvas hitting the lens, a falling brain bridging two shots. Each cut is hidden inside a movement.
4. **Consistent caption system.** One chunky white grotesk with a 3D extrude, always typed on letter by letter, with a colour switch only on the emotional turn and yellow tabs only for names.
5. **Ease-in-out expo for narrative pans.** All the travel happens in the middle 4 frames, so the camera looks like a deliberate operator, not a slider.
6. **The structure is the CTA.** The video uses Hook → Context → Buildup → Payoff, then names those exact four pieces as the product promise.
7. **Depth everywhere:** foreground leaves, a blurred keyhole, floating clocks at three scales, DOF and grain on every layer.
8. **Lock hits to the VO word (±0.05 s) and nudge them onto the beat** where possible; land the section pivot exactly on the strongest music onset.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, ~1040 frames (VO + ~1 s hold). Global `<GrainOverlay opacity={0.06}>` + `<Vignette strength={0.35}>`.
- **Layer stack (typical scene):** `<PaintingBackdrop src parallax={0.3}>` → `<MidProps>` (frames, clocks, numerals with `parallax={0.6}` and idle drift) → `<Cutout src shadow>` (the subject) → `<ForegroundParticles type="leaves" blur={8} parallax={1.4}>` → `<ExtrudeTypeOn>` / `<LabelTab>` → grain/vignette. The whole stack sits inside `<CameraRig>` that applies the push/pan transforms with per-layer parallax multipliers.
- **Components:**
  - `ExtrudeTypeOn({text, charsPerSec: 16, fill: 'linear-gradient(#fff,#c8c8c8)', extrude: 4, extrudeColor: '#6b6b6b'})`: reveal per char; each char `opacity` 0 → 1 over 2 frames; the extrude is 4 stacked `text-shadow`s (`1px 1px #6b6b6b … 4px 4px`) plus `0 8px 16px rgba(0,0,0,.4)`. Start at the whisper word start − 2 frames.
  - `RomanTitle({text:'VAN GOGH', font:'Cinzel', guides:true})`: type 1 char/2 frames; draw SVG guide circles/lines per glyph with `strokeDashoffset` over 6 frames at 30% opacity.
  - `LabelTab({text, bg:'#e2c21c', skew:-8, worldLocked:true})`: width animates 0 → full over 4 frames, then the text types; render it inside `<CameraRig>` so it pans with the scene.
  - `BadgePopType({text:['REMAIN','DEBATED'], box:'#f4f2ee', color:'#e6b01a'})`: the box appears at 100% on 1 frame (scale 0.9 → 1 over 2 frames), then the text types.
  - `CameraRig({keys})`: narrative pan `Easing.bezier(0.85,0,0.15,1)` over 26 frames; push-through `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)` over 24 frames; idle push 0.15%/frame.
  - `PaperTearTransition({durationInFrames: 12, seeds: 4})`: mask = union of growing noisy circles (SVG `feTurbulence baseFrequency=0.04` displacement scale 30), plus a white edge stroke via a slightly dilated mask.
  - `KeyholePushThrough({keyholeSrc, from:1, to:2.6, frames:24})`: the foreground PNG scales about the opening centre; the background scales 1 → 1.3; an initial `blur(20px) → 0` over 3 frames.
  - `OccluderWhip({frames: 30, cutAt: 15})`: both scenes `translateX` driven by one bezier; a black vertical gradient bar covers 100% around `cutAt`.
  - `MotionBridge({src, vyStart})`: the same sprite rendered in both sequences with continuous `y = y0 + v*t + ½g t²` across the cut.
  - `FallingLeaves({count: 25, layers: 3})`: per-leaf `y += v`, sine sway ±40 px, rotation, with blur by layer (0 / 3 / 10 px).
  - `PuzzleAssemble({pieces, gap: 30})`: pieces slide inward on `spring({damping: 18, stiffness: 120})`, then a 6-frame scale punch 1 → 1.12 with `Easing.out(Easing.exp)` when they lock.
  - `MarkerEllipse({drawOn: 5, color:'#d02a2a', strokeWidth: 6})`: a hand-drawn SVG path with a slight overshoot tail.
  - `BracketURL({text:'EARNEDITS.COM', left:'{', right:'}', leftColor:'#22b14c'})`: the braces start adjacent and translate apart as the chars type (width measured per char).
- **Tricky bits:** the AI-generated clips (argument, hallway, college) need subtle life. Use short image-to-video clips, or 2.5D (split the subject from the background with a matte and push at different rates). The Gauguin pan needs an image ~1.8× frame width. The cylindrical gallery orbit is easiest as a `@remotion/three` cylinder with painting textures on the inside face and a camera rotating around a cut-out plane.

## 9. Variation ideas
1. **Founder / brand origin story** (e.g. "How Dyson failed 5,126 times"): sets made from the brand's own prototypes and patent drawings, a blueprint-construction title treatment for the name, label tabs for co-founders, a keyhole push-through into the "secret lab", and a CTA that names the story structure as a framework.
2. **Science explainer** (e.g. "Why Einstein almost quit"): chalkboard and photo-plate backdrops, equations orbiting like the 1888 numerals, a world-locked label pan between rival scientists, the brain-on-scales metaphor for a debate.
3. **Music / album retrospective:** album artwork as the sets, lyrics as typed extruded captions in the album's accent colour, vinyl or cassette props as motion bridges across cuts, and a CTA flat-lay with a folder of stems instead of Ae files.
