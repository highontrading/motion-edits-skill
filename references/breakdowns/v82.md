# v82 — "Selling vs. Storytelling": antithesis explainer with alternating 3D-prop worlds

**Source creator handle:** earnera.co · **Format:** 720×1280, 9:16, 25 fps, 38.0 s · **Shots:** ~19 real scenes (13 detected; detector shot 9 at 10.52–21.52 s hides 4 scenes joined by morphs/fades/wipes, and shot 6 at 6.96 s is a 3-frame dip to black, not a scene) · **Avg shot:** ~2.0 s · **Music BPM:** ~123 (bed under VO) · **VO:** yes, English, calm, measured male narrator, ~2.4 words/s

**Style tags:** kinetic-type, handwritten-accent, word-by-word-captions, 3d-render, ui-mockup, device-mockup, notification-ui, chat-ui, cutout-photo, halftone, text-on-path, text-behind-subject, motion-blur, glow, vignette, slow-push, slide-push, shape-wipe, morph-transition, documentary-vo, cta-endcard, comment-keyword-cta, antithesis-world-swap, object-pre-lap

**One-line essence:** a 14-line "X is A / Y is B" script where every sentence gets its own scene, and the two sides of the contrast always look different: **"selling"** lines get bright, commercial worlds (wood desk with cash, white phone with payment notifications, chat keyboard, "just bought $99" toasts). **"Storytelling"** lines get dark, cinematic worlds (blind-shadow wall, film-still board, glowing chess king, clapperboard disc, green radial glow). One typographic pairing runs through all of them: a tight bold grotesk plus a thin halftone handwritten word.

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** marketing-philosophy micro-explainer built on antithesis, with the brand CTA at the end (an After Effects project-file store).
- **Beats:**
  - 0.00–2.56 **Hook:** "Here's the difference between selling and storytelling." Opens on a fake Instagram **search "Recent" list** (earn.edits, earnera.co, S_tier content). The VO line types itself as the subtitle under the earn.edits handle, which works as a native-looking "someone posted this" frame.
  - 1.48–3.3 **Definitions:** "selling" (cash on a desk) / "Storytelling" (clapperboards on a dark wall), one word each.
  - 3.3–21.5 **Five antithesis pairs** (transactions/transformation, offer/why it matters, price & features/meaning & emotion, customer today/loyalty for years, product/person buying it). Each half gets its own scene and the halves alternate light ↔ dark.
  - 23.9–27.3 **Payoff:** "Storytelling turns products into symbols, buyers into believers" (hero car shot).
  - 27.6–31.1 **Moral:** "So don't just focus on selling. Tell stories worth caring about" (white paper, B/W halftone cut-out hand with camera).
  - 31.3–38.0 **CTA:** "Just for editors…" website hero on a tilted dark screen → EARNEDITS.com logo → Instagram comment UI with "Comment link and will send it to you".
- **Hook mechanics:** the first frame is near-black (luma 9). UI elements **blur-focus in** over f2–f14 (0.08–0.56 s) while the camera pushes in and rotates slightly (net_zoom +12.5, rot +22). The VO is already in the subtitle line by 0.5 s. The familiar platform UI and the brand's own handle in the first 0.5 s stop the scroll; at 1.04 s the whole list slides out left.
- **Script → visual rule:** the subject word of each sentence ("Selling" / "Storytelling") is always the first on-screen token, and the sentence's object is the hero prop (cash stack, phone + payment notifications, chat keyboard, money app-icon, clapperboard, chess king, Transformers still, sports car). The closing noun ("Transactions", "Transformation", "symbols", "for years", "selling") is set in the handwritten face as the punchline.

## 2. Visual language

Sizes: large statement lines 55–60 % of frame width ("selling is about" ≈ 420 px of 720; "storytelling turns" ≈ 430 px); emphasis word one step larger on its own line ("believers" ≈ 1.25× "buyers into"; "matters" ≈ 2× "shows them why it"); quiet lines small (≈ 3 % frame height, "so don't just focus on" ≈ 300 px wide). Used for the single key noun: "selling", "Storytelling", "Transactions", "Transformation", "Price", "Features", "symbols", "for years". White on dark, black on white, ≈ 55–60 % of frame width. It has a **halftone dot screen** inside the strokes (clearly visible on key_shot02 and key_shot09) and a **blurred offset "ghost" copy** below it (same word, ~40 % opacity, ~25 px Gaussian blur, offset +40–60 px down), which reads as a soft cast shadow.

- **Layout:** hero prop centred at y ≈ 45–60 %. Statement text goes in the top third (y ≈ 20–35 %) on dark scenes, or overlaps the prop at y ≈ 60–65 % on bright scenes ("selling is about" crosses the phone). Captions never sit in the bottom 20 %. Several scenes deliberately **bleed type off-frame** ("storytelling turns" sliding in, "just for editors" at ≈ 140 % width).

- **Finishing / FX:** heavy **defocus/motion blur** on everything that moves; soft vignette on most scenes; glow/bloom on the white chess king and the green-glow scenes; halftone on the handwritten type and pills; dust specks on the concrete; light sweeps (window stripes, top-right spill). The DM, website and comment screens get a **tilted-screen look** (perspective + rotation + edge defocus), as if the screen were filmed with a shallow-DOF camera.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** ~19 scenes in 38 s ≈ 0.5 scene changes/s. Scene length tracks sentence length: 0.84–1.0 s for single-word definitions, 1.5–2.5 s for most clauses, 3.2–3.6 s for the two-beat pairs (meaning+emotion, loyalty+for years, symbols+believers, CTA site). In the middle stretch, 10.5–21.5 s, the cuts disappear and scenes **morph into each other**: icon→dot→disc, fade-to-white, black slab wipe, disc flip. The densest information stretch therefore feels like one continuous move.
- **Easing signature:** smooth, decelerating moves with **no overshoot**. Pan/zoom never changes sign during an entrance (checked on the phone rise f82–f98, green-board push f129–f150 and website slide f777–f800). Typical shape: a 4–6 frame ramp up to the peak, then exponential decay with a half-life of ~6–8 frames, settling in 12–18 frames. Example: green board flow 0.2 → 7.4 over f125–f135, then 7.4 → 2.8 by f150. Exits **accelerate** (ease-in) into a hard cut on the blurriest frame: IG list f26–f36; the desk re-accelerates from pan_x −1.9 to −7.6 over f52–f57.
- **Entrance / exit vocab:**
  - Blur-focus-in per element or word (8–12 fr, Gaussian 20 px → 0, opacity 0 → 1). This is the default for every sans word.
  - Slide-in with directional blur (8–10 fr, ease-out) for props, handwritten words and toasts.
  - Rise from the bottom edge (phone, camera cut-out, website screen): 10 fr ease-out.
  - Scale-from-0 disc behind the hero (green disc, white disc): ~8 fr ease-out.
  - Callout hairline draw-on with its label (~6 fr).
  - Typing into a UI field (chat, IG subtitle), one word per VO word.
  - Karaoke colour highlight on existing text (website subtitle grey → green per word).
  - 3D flip from edge-on (Product disc, ~10 fr) and 3D tilt-away to flat ellipse (avatar, ~8 fr).
  - Collapse-to-centre (avatar ring, ~1.2 s staggered).
- **Camera:** an almost constant **slow virtual-camera drift** in every scene: push-ins (+12 to +23 net zoom), pull-backs (−10 to −23), small rotations (rot up to +68 net across the 6 s avatar sequence), and lateral tracking with typing (pan_x −52 in the chat). There are no shakes. The UI screens are always tilted 5–15° in perspective with edge defocus.
- **Secondary motion:** dark chess pieces tumbling past in the foreground; clapperboard slowly rotating and clapping; avatars orbiting; notification cards drifting; tyre smoke on the car; the light sweep on the concrete brightening over ~1.5 s.
- **Sync:**
  - **VO-driven, not beat-driven.** Scene changes fall within −0.26…+0.42 s of the sentence's first word (median ≈ 0; e.g. 3.32 vs "Selling" 3.30; 7.08 vs 7.16; 27.60 vs "So" 27.60). The mean distance from cut to the nearest beat (0.13 s at 123 BPM, 0.49 s period) is no better than chance.
  - Sans words appear **on** the spoken word (0–0.15 s after word start).
  - The handwritten punchline word often lands **0.2–0.35 s after** it is spoken ("selling" 1.14 → visible ~1.64; "Transactions" 3.90 → 4.3; "selling" 28.72 → 29.0), so it reads as a visual echo.
  - Pills can arrive ~0.3 s early (meaning, emotion).

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Accelerating slide-out + hard cut** (1.48, 2.32 s): the outgoing layer accelerates horizontally (ease-in, 6–10 fr), gaining blur, and the cut lands on the peak-blur frame. The incoming scene's props enter from the opposite edge with matching blur. Rebuild: outgoing `translateX` 0 → −60 % with `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)` over 8 fr plus SVG blur stdDeviation 0 → 12 on X; the next scene's props start at +40 % and ease out.
2. **Rise-over + background crossfade** (3.32 s): a new hero (phone) rises from the bottom edge over the old scene in 10 fr while the old background cross-fades to the new colour (dark → white) over 16 fr. The old type fades with it, and the new type starts typing during the fade.
3. **Push-in + dip to black** (6.72–7.08 s): fast zoom (+6 %/fr) into a picture inside the scene, then luma to 0 in 4 fr, 3 black frames, hard cut in. Rebuild: scale 1 → 1.35 over 6 fr ease-in, opacity of a black solid 0 → 1 over 4 fr, hold 3 fr.
4. **Object pre-lap** (8.80 s): the next scene's hero object (chess king) appears **over** the outgoing scene 4 frames before the cut, so the eye is already locked on it.
5. **Shrink-to-dot → grow-to-disc morph** (12.56–13.3 s): the hero icon scales down to a ~12 px white dot, the dot travels and **scales up into the next scene's white disc container** (~19 fr), and the new background fades in behind it. Rebuild: one `<circle>` whose r and position interpolate between two layouts.
6. **Fade-to-white with match-dissolve of a persistent element** (15.92–16.44 s, 13 fr): the background luma ramps to white while the "meaning" pill cross-dissolves into the "selling might win…" pill at nearly the same position.
7. **Black-slab wipe** (17.88–18.48 s, 15 fr; the optical-flow peak 47.5 at f451 is this move): a full-height black slab enters from the left edge and sweeps across in 8 fr (ease-in-out), fully covering the frame; then the black lifts upward like a shutter in 7 fr, revealing the new white scene. The persistent pill stays visible on top of the slab, and its text swaps from "selling…" to "storytelling" while covered.
8. **Disc flip (3D Y-rotation)** (20.40 and 21.52 s): an ellipse grows from a vertical line (edge-on) to a full circle (10 fr), carrying new text. At 21.52 the reverse happens: the disc goes edge-on (text breaks into strips) and comes back with a new label and a new background.
9. **3D tilt-away to type** (24.2–24.6 s): the hero rotates on X until it is a flat ellipse, while a giant headline slides through the frame from the right with blur, and the background changes underneath.
10. **Cross-dissolve to white with type continuity** (27.48–28.2 s, ~19 fr): a long, soft dissolve; the next line is already typing during it.
11. **Vertical scroll push** (29.7–30.1 s, ~10 fr): the whole layout moves up and the next one rises from the bottom, like scrolling a page.
12. **Perspective screen slide-in** (31.0–31.4 s, 10 fr): a dark tilted screen enters diagonally from the bottom-right and fills the frame (luma 226 → 41 in 15 fr).
13. **Tilt/zoom whip to logo** (34.3–34.8 s, ~12 fr): the screen rotates/zooms away with heavy blur and the logo resolves in focus.
14. **Side panel slide** (36.0–36.5 s, 12 fr): the final UI slides in from the right over the logo with blur.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Antithesis = two visual worlds.** Assign each side of a comparison a fixed look (bright/commercial vs. dark/cinematic) and alternate every sentence. The viewer reads the argument from the luma flip before reading any words.
2. **One typographic pair, used with discipline:** a tight bold grotesk for the sentence and a thin halftone handwritten word for the punchline noun, with a blurred ghost copy under the handwriting as a soft shadow.
3. **Every scene has a slow camera.** Constant gentle push/pull/rotate with ease-out and no overshoot makes static 3D props feel filmed.
4. **Blur is the transition glue.** Elements arrive and leave through blur (focus-in, motion blur) instead of opacity alone, and hard cuts land on the blurriest frame.
5. **Morph between scenes in the dense middle.** When the script lists many contrasts quickly, chain scenes with shape morphs, wipes and flips instead of cuts so it reads as one continuous thought.
6. **UI as a prop, filmed in perspective.** Chat, website and comments screens are tilted, defocused at the edges and vignetted, which makes them feel photographed rather than screen-recorded.
7. **Pre-lap the next hero** (the king appears 4 fr before its cut) to steer the eye across hard cuts.
8. **Karaoke-highlight real UI copy** (the website subtitle turns green word by word with the VO) so the CTA feels as narrated as the rest of the video.
9. **A single brand-green accent** in otherwise neutral scenes (glows, discs, buttons, prices) carries brand recall without a logo until the end.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `fps: 30` (the reference is 25; scale frame counts ×1.2), duration = VO length + ~0.5 s (≈ 1140 frames for 38 s).
- **Scene data model:** `scenes[] = {startWord, side: "sell"|"story"|"neutral", bg, hero, lines:[{text, style:"sans"|"script"|"pill", words:[{w, t}]}], transitionOut}`. The scene start is the first word's timestamp minus 2 frames.
- **Layer stack (bottom → top):** `<SceneBackground variant="wood|blinds|felt|concrete|white|void|greenGlow|brushed">` → `<LightSweep>` (radial/linear white gradient, opacity 0 → 0.35 over 40 fr) → `<HeroProp src depth>` (3D-render PNG/WebM, entrance by direction) → `<DiscBacker color size>` → `<SansLine>` / `<ScriptWord>` / `<GradientPill>` → `<Callout path>` → `<Vignette strength={0.45}>` → `<CameraDrift>` wrapper around everything (scale + rotate + translate).
- **Components & timings (30 fps values):**
  - `CameraDrift`: `scale: interpolate(f,[0,dur],[1,1.08])`, `rotate: interpolate(f,[0,dur],[0,1.5deg])`, plus an optional `translateX` for typing scenes. Use `Easing.out(Easing.quad)` so motion is strongest at scene start.
  - `BlurInWord`: per word, start at the word's `t*fps - 1`; over 10 fr, `filter: blur(18px → 0)`, `opacity 0 → 1`, `translateY 12px → 0`, `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`. Words not yet spoken can pre-render in `#9a9a9a` at 40 % opacity when the line should be visible in full.
  - `ScriptWord`: handwritten font, `mask-image` with a halftone dot pattern (radial-gradient tile, 4 px pitch) multiplied over the fill; ghost copy = the same text at `translateY(0.35em)`, `blur(10px)`, opacity 0.35. Entrance: slide from +25 % X with a horizontal motion blur (SVG `feGaussianBlur stdDeviation="14 0"` → 0) over 10 fr, `spring({damping: 200, stiffness: 120})` (critically damped, no overshoot). Delay ≈ +6–9 fr after the spoken word.
  - `GradientPill`: `border-radius: 999px`, `background: linear-gradient(90deg,#1b1b1b 0%,#7a7a7a 60%,#cfcfcf 100%)`, `box-shadow: 0 18px 30px rgba(0,0,0,.35)`, white Bold label plus an icon slot; slides in from −30 % X with blur over 10 fr.
  - `PurchaseToast`: avatar 64 px with a 4 px white border, "Name just bought on…" + green `#43a047` "$99.00"; spawn 4–6 of them at the screen edges, staggered by 5 fr, each sliding 120 px inward with blur, then drifting 0.3 px/fr.
  - `DiscFlip`: `transform: rotateY(90deg → 0)` with `perspective: 1200px` over 10 fr, `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`; swap the label at 90°.
  - `TextOnCircle`: SVG `<textPath>` on a circle of r = 0.22 W; reveal with `startOffset` animation or a per-glyph opacity stagger of 1 fr/glyph; keyword glyphs in `#7CFF4F` with `drop-shadow(0 0 12px #3f8f2a)`.
  - `AvatarRing`: 8 avatars on r = 0.28 W, scale-in stagger 1 fr each; ring rotates 0.4°/fr; collapse = per-avatar `interpolate` of radius r → 0 with 6 fr stagger and `Easing.in(Easing.quad)`.
  - `DotMorph`: one SVG circle interpolating `{cx, cy, r}` from the icon centre (r = 6) to the disc layout (r = 0.3 W) over 20 fr with `Easing.bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)`; cross-fade the background underneath.
  - `SlabWipe`: black rect `x: -W → 0` over 10 fr (`bezier(0.7,0,0.3,1)`), hold 1 fr, then `y: 0 → -H` over 8 fr (ease-in). Swap the content under it at full cover, and keep persistent elements (the pill) above the slab with `zIndex`.
  - `PerspectiveScreen`: `rotateX(12deg) rotateZ(-6deg) translateZ`; enters from `translate(40%, 60%)` over 12 fr ease-out; edge defocus via a radial mask applied to a blurred duplicate layer.
  - `KaraokeHighlight`: website subtitle spans change colour from `#bdbdbd` to `#5cbf4a` at each word's timestamp, with a 3 fr colour interpolate.
  - `DipToBlack`: scale 1 → 1.35 over 7 fr (`Easing.in(Easing.cubic)`) + black overlay 0 → 1 over 5 fr, then 3–4 fr of pure black.
- **Tricky effects:** halftone inside text (SVG `<pattern>` of dots used as the text `fill`, or CSS `background-clip: text` with a radial-gradient dot background); tilted-screen DOF (render the UI twice, the sharp one masked by a radial gradient centred on the active line and the blurred one underneath); light-sweep backgrounds (an animated `linear-gradient` angled 35° at 20 % opacity with `mix-blend-mode: screen`).

## 9. Variation ideas
1. **Personal finance "Saving vs. Investing"**: saving = bright, clinical bank-app worlds (white, piggy bank, balance notifications); investing = dark, glowing worlds (chart line with green glow, growing tree, compound-interest disc). Keep the sans + halftone-script pairing; swap the green accent for gold.
2. **Fitness "Motivation vs. Discipline"**: motivation = bright, loud scenes (confetti, alarm-clock notifications, story-ring UI), discipline = dark gym with a single rim-lit dumbbell. Same alternating rhythm, with workout-log UI typing word by word.
3. **SaaS product page "Feature vs. Outcome"**: feature lines on a tilted product-UI screen with karaoke-highlighted copy; outcome lines on cinematic dark scenes with glowing 3D icons and avatar rings. End with the website-hero perspective slide and a comment-keyword CTA.
