## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** tool tutorial and lead magnet ("how to make X → you don't need the hard way → one plugin → step-by-step → result → share/comment CTA"). It is the long-form sibling of the v38 Grid-plugin promo.
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–4.6 **Hook/promise:** "This is how you create this trendy smart grid animation in under a minute." It shows the finished grid on a laptop and makes a time claim.
  - 4.6–8.3 **Pain removal:** you don't need multiple layers, keyframes or expressions. Each item gets its own retro-window gag.
  - 8.5–12.9 **Solution:** "just one After Effects plugin" (mascot reveal) → "Let me show you how?"
  - 12.9–81.4 **Tutorial body**, 9 steps: new comp → import media → open plugin → Create Grid → settings (media count/format/rows/cols/duration/FPS) → Choose Layout → place and size blocks → Add Media → level control → Explore Motion presets / manual target / extras (Focus Blur, Colour Falloff, Parallax, colours) → Generate.
  - 81.6–89.4 **Result:** a new comp in a new folder → "Just drag that composition" → the effect is ready (final grid plays).
  - 89.6–95.7 **CTA:** "send it to an editor who needs it" (phone share sheet) → "Comment Grid and we'll send you the plugin for free."
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** frame 0 is a full-screen white grid card ("CREATE. SMART INSPIRE.", a red SMART label), already zooming out fast (−4.7 zoom units/frame, decaying to −1 by 0.6 s). It reveals the teal cutting mat, a lime swoosh band and "This Is How" sliding in from the left. A giant ghost "How" rises behind the card. At 1.0–1.33 a tree trunk sweeps across L→R as a foreground occluder (pan_x peak −11.5 = ~46 px/frame at 720 px) and wipes into the countryside set. Three layers move in different directions within 1.3 s.
- **Script → visual rule:** each instruction gets (a) a camera reframe onto the exact UI control *before* it is named, (b) a cursor-halo click on it, and (c) for key nouns, a glowing lime type echo or a highlighter on the UI label. Abstract claims ("you don't need keyframes") become retro window props that morph from one to the next.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** only ~15 real scenes in 95.7 s (0.16 cuts/s). The energy comes from **≈43 camera/UI events** (flow peaks > 1.6) at a median spacing of ~2.0 s, which matches the VO's sentence rhythm. The hook (0–12.9 s) is dense: 5 scenes and ~10 motion events in 13 s. The tutorial body (31.5–81.6) is one 50-s shot kept alive by reframes, highlights and cursor clicks. The outro returns to 1.8–3 s scenes.
- **Easing signature (measured):** reframes are **fast-in, long ease-out with no overshoot**. Flow reaches its peak 2–8 frames after motion starts (median ~4 f ≈ 0.13 s) and then decays over 9–24 frames (median ~13 f ≈ 0.43 s). No pan/zoom sign flips occur in any move, so they are critically damped. Example 33.90–34.57: zoom per frame −5.1, −9.4, −4.9, −1.4… −0.3 (peak at f2, 18-f tail). Example 45.37: pan_y −2, −5.5, −1.7, −4.6, −6.6, 0 (a short scroll with a hard stop). Rebuild with `Easing.bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` over 14–20 frames, or `spring({damping: 200, stiffness: 120, mass: 1})`.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:**
  - *Line-to-window scale-up* (Win95 dialog, 5 f): scaleY 0.02→1 and scaleX 0.3→1, ease-out.
  - *In-place window morph*: same window frame, new content per spoken noun. Hard swaps every 0.6–1.3 s.
  - *Glow-type pop*: word appears blurred (8–12 px) and over-bright with a strong glow, then resolves sharp in 2–3 f. The glow decays over ~10 f.
  - *Stacked word-by-word build*: one word per spoken word, appearing on its spoken frame or up to +0.3 s after.
  - *Tooltip spring*: scale 0.8→1 with ~5° rotation correcting to 0°, 5–8 f.
  - *Bubble stretch from tail*: the pill grows horizontally from the tail anchor, ~6 f.
  - *Rise-in with motion blur* (mascot, phone, clock sign): translateY +40%→0 in ~10 f, peak speed at f2.
  - *Pixel-tile dissolve* (18.4): the lime-glowing square tiles scatter outward from the clicked button over ~6 f.
  - *Highlighter block pop* (UI labels): a lime rectangle appears behind the label in 1–2 f with a glow.
  - *Outline draw-on* (level control): a rounded-rect stroke draws around the group in ~8 f.
- **Camera:** a virtual 2D camera over a flat set. Net zooms per move range from ±5 to ±50 and pans from 20 to 90 analysis-px (×4 = real px). Pull-backs reveal context (33.9, 51.4, 75.4, 90.8, 92.7); push-ins frame a control just before it is named. Continuous slow drift between moves is near zero, so holds are truly still and the UI stays readable.
- **Secondary motion:** the grid previews animate continuously (cells re-layout every ~0.6 s, close to the beat), the mascot sparkle twinkles, the cursor halo glides between fields with a 6–10 f ease, and the ring ripples expand on clicks.
- **Sync:**
  - Hard cuts are **both VO-led and beat-snapped**: 4.60 (VO "And" 4.58, beat 4.644), 10.57 (beat 10.612), 12.90 ("First" 12.90, beat 12.91), 20.10 (beat 20.085, +0.22 s after "Grid"), 81.60 ("The plugin" 81.56, beat 81.688), 88.17 ("your" 88.18, beat 88.236), 90.77 (beat 90.72, −0.13 s before "send"), 92.60 (beat 92.508, −0.12 s before "Comment"). ## 6. Sound design
A quiet, steady ~99 BPM electronic bed sits well under the VO (RMS 0.12). The VO runs almost continuously, with pauses of at most ~0.5 s. Strong onsets cluster at the green flashes (16.74/16.95, 20.09), the pixel dissolve (18.44), the burns (81.57/81.69, 92.81) and the 60 FPS slam (30.56). ## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Never show a raw screen recording.** Put the real UI inside a device bezel on a textured "workbench" set, and move a virtual camera over it.
2. **Reframe every ~2 s with an expo-out move (peak at f2–4, 12–18 f settle, motion blur, no overshoot)** so a 50-s single-UI section never feels static.
3. **Arrive before you name it.** The camera lands on the control 0.3–0.6 s before the VO says it, and the highlight appears at the word.
4. **One accent colour does all the pointing**: lime for the cursor halo, click ripples, label highlighters, outlines and keyword type. Everything else is teal, charcoal and white.
5. **Visualise what you're not doing.** "No layers / keyframes / expressions" becomes one retro window that morphs per spoken noun, which is funny and fast.
6. **Two-weight stacked captions:** small white lead-in plus a huge lime glowing payoff, built word by word and landing ≤0.3 s after the spoken word.
7. **Designed transitions only at chapter boundaries**: green click-flash for "the plugin reacts", logo film-burn for "result/outro", and hard cuts on the beat for everything else.
8. **Bookend with AI-photoreal sets and a mascot.** The tutorial core is utilitarian, but the hook and CTA feel like a brand world.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps` (or 60 fps for smoother UI blur), duration = VO + 0.4 s (≈ 2880 frames for this script). Drive everything from a Whisper word-timestamp JSON.
- **Scene stack (tutorial body), bottom → top:** `<CuttingMatBG gridColor="#65c4a2" base="#053e33" vignette={0.35}/>` → `<VirtualCamera keyframes=[{t, x, y, scale}] ease={Easing.bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)} dur={16} motionBlur>` wrapping `<DeviceBezel radius={28} border={10} color="#fbfbfb" shadow="0 30px 60px rgba(0,0,0,.45)"><UICapture src/></DeviceBezel>` → `<CursorHalo path color="#e9f7a6" size={56} glow={24}/>` → `<UIHighlighter rects=[{x,y,w,h,at}] color="#d7ea02" glow/>` → `<GlowKeywordStack lines=[{text, color, size, at}]/>` → `<ClickRipple at x y/>`.
- **Components & timings:**
  - `VirtualCamera`: interpolate `translate/scale` between keyframes over 14–20 f with `Easing.bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)`. Add `<CameraMotionBlur samples={6} shutterAngle={180}>` (from `@remotion/motion-blur`) only while moving.
  - `GlowKeywordStack`: per word `at = wordStart + 3 f`. Entrance: blur 10→0 px, opacity 0→1, scale 1.08→1 over 3 f. The glow `text-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(215,234,2,.7), 3px 3px 0 #364a0f` decays from 1.6× to 1× intensity over 10 f. Font: Barlow Semi Condensed 600. Lead-in size 0.38× hero size, white.
  - `RetroDialog` (Win95 chrome: navy title bar `#0a0aa8`, bevelled grey buttons): open with scaleY 0.02→1 over 5 f `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`, then `content` swaps keyed to words.
  - `ClickRipple`: 2 rings, staggered 3 f, `r` 20→600 px, stroke 8 px lime with an 18 px glow, plus a full-frame `#39e639` wash (opacity 0.7→0 over 18 f, `Easing.out(Easing.exp)`).
  - `PixelTileDissolve`: a 16×16 tile grid over the button with random outward velocity and opacity 1→0 over 7 f.
  - `FilmBurnTransition`: 4 f. A logo-shaped mask scales 0.6→2.5, with turbulence displacement (`feTurbulence baseFrequency=0.02` + `feDisplacementMap scale=40`) on the edge, an amber gradient, grain overlay and `screen` blend. Swap the scenes at f2.
  - `Tooltip` / `SpeechBubble`: `spring({damping: 12, stiffness: 200, mass: 0.6})` on scale 0.8→1, with rotate 5°→0°. The bubble scaleX grows from the tail origin.
  - `MascotRiseIn`: translateY 45%→0 over 10 f `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)` with a vertical blur of 12 px for the first 4 f. Sparkle: a 4-point star scaling 0→1→0 every 1.2 s.
  - `DefocusBackdrop`: background `filter: blur(0→12px)` over 8 f when a keyword stack enters.
- **Tricky bits:** the cropped-UI framing (the camera deliberately puts half the bezel off-frame) needs the UI capture at ≥2× resolution so 2–2.5× pushes stay crisp. Render UI captures as 2160-px-wide PNG/MP4.
