## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** founder-myth / business-psychology micro-doc with a motivational moral and a product CTA (same channel template as v05).
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–2.64 **Hook:** "$20 billion. On the table. He said no." Three 1-second fragments, each with its own visual (TWENTY BILLION type → money envelope → table → "HE SAID NO").
  - 2.88–5.34 **Context:** "That was Intel's offer for Nvidia in 2008" (red "OFFER" room, gloved hands trading cash for the Nvidia logo, "2008").
  - 5.7–8.46 **Protagonist + denial:** "Jensen Huang turned it down. Not because it wasn't enough" (Jensen cut-out rises; yellow badge).
  - 8.68–11.8 **Vision:** "…because he could see something nobody else had language for yet" (red glowing eyes, then a 4-clip montage of Nvidia's future tech).
  - 11.8–18.44 **Payoff:** "15 years later Nvidia crossed $3 trillion, the most valuable company on earth. Built on a single no."
  - 18.74–22.6 **Turn:** "Here's what nobody says. The people who took the safe exit, you don't know their names."
  - 22.88–26.66 **Moral:** "Conviction looks exactly like stubbornness until the timeline catches up."
  - 26.84–29.56 **Question to viewer:** "So what's the $20 billion offer you need to say no to?"
  - 29.56–34.24 **CTA:** project files on earnedits.com.
- **Hook mechanics:** frame 0 is a blank light-grey vignette, so the viewer gets one beat of nothing. At 0.17 s (frame 5) the lime word TWENTY pops in. At 0.47 s BILLION slams in from the right with motion blur while the background drops from light grey to near black in ~4 frames. At 0.87 s the background flashes lime green and a money-stuffed envelope rockets up from the bottom. That is four visual state changes in the first 1.3 s, all on the first three VO words.
- **Script → visual rule:** every VO clause becomes one surreal tableau built from a literal noun (offer → hands trading cash; "see" → glowing eyes; "most valuable on earth" → HQ standing on the globe; "safe exit" → avatars dropping out of a funnel; "timeline" → clock). Only the punchline noun phrase is set as type. Connective clauses get no type and are carried by footage or props.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** photoreal surreal collage. AI-generated or stock-composited "product shot" props (crumpled green-paper envelope full of $100 bills, a walnut side table, black-gloved hands, Nvidia HQ, a photoreal globe, a glossy 3D spiral cone) sit on painted-sky, wood or flat-colour sets. These are mixed with real keynote footage and press portraits. The look is cleaner and more "premium 3D" than v05's vintage paper; the collage is in the composition, not the texture.
- **Backgrounds** change with each beat and each carries meaning: neutral light grey `#dbdbdb` (blank slate) → near-black `#1e1e1e` → acid lime `#5e890a`/`#79a711` (money) → pale grey sky with green hills → **full B/W** (the refusal) → deep blood red `#560909` with faint isometric building shapes and a giant blurred "OFFER." watermark (the deal) → dark red vignette `#160404` (Jensen) → real keynote footage (cool blue/teal) → dark oak wood `#38251c` (history/time) → saturated blue sky `#218fcc` (success) → black `#0b0b0b` for the reflective second half (nobody says / funnel / sunburst / question / CTA).
- **Colour:** each scene is **one saturated field colour + white/black + one accent**. Accent logic: lime/Nvidia green (`#79a711`, `#92be29`, `#6f9f06`) = money/Nvidia; red (`#b5141a`, `#a51719`) = refusal/deal/danger; gold-yellow (`#d6b607`, `#e0b40a`) = value and wisdom (badge, "most valuable" type, sunburst); royal blue `#2a4bd8` appears only inside the difference-blend text. The emotional grade shift is at 1.9–2.2 s: saturation falls 35 → 7 (×0.2) over ~6 frames exactly as "He said no" begins, so the refusal is literally colour draining out.
- **Typography:**
  - **Hero keyword type:** ultra-heavy, slightly extended grotesk caps (Archivo Black / Montserrat Black / Druk Wide-like). TWENTY BILLION is lime `#79a711`, 60–70% frame width.   - **Numerals:** "2008" in a clean geometric bold sans (Poppins SemiBold / Manrope Bold), off-white `#e0e0e0`, ~65% width, y ≈ 20%.
  - **Whisper captions:** lowercase geometric grotesk at medium/regular weight (Google Sans / Product Sans / Inter Tight-like) with tight tracking.   - **Badge:** "not because / it wasn't enough": a rounded gold-yellow `#e0b40a` rectangle with a 1 px **dashed inner stitch border** (same stitched-badge device as v05), with white lowercase text (line 1 semibold, line 2 light). It sits at the chest of the cut-out at y ≈ 68%, ~40% width.
  - **Difference-blend text:** "conviction looks…" is white where it sits over black and royal blue where it crosses the yellow disc. This is exactly what a Difference blend of white over `#d6b607` produces, so the type is set in blend-mode difference.

- **Layout:** hero props are centred at y ≈ 55–75%. Keywords sit in the upper third (y ≈ 15–30%), and whisper captions sit at mid-frame (y ≈ 45–55%) on black scenes. Text and props overlap in depth: Jensen's head occludes the "00" of 2008, and the globe slides **behind** the building's base, which reads as 2.5D layering instead of stickers.
- **Assets:** cut-out press portrait in a **torn-paper frame** (white ragged edge, dark backing), stage photos of Jensen, Nvidia keynote B-roll with its original lower-third captions kept, AI-generated product props, a 3D spiral cone, generic user-avatar glyphs, flying crows, a clock face, a fanned stack of $100 bills, and a real website screenshot of earnedits.com.
- **Finishing / FX:** fine monochrome grain on every frame (visible as "stars" in the black scenes); shallow depth-of-field blur on secondary elements ("OFFER." watermark, distant clouds); radial vignettes on the red and black scenes; a red emissive glow on Jensen's eyes; neon glow on CTA type; motion blur on every fast entrance. The ~20 fps cadence (one duplicate every 3 frames) gives a slight stop-motion staccato.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** 0.6 real cuts/s overall, but strongly bimodal. The hook (0–3 s) has four visual states per second. The montage (9.67–11.9) cuts every 0.5–0.6 s, exactly one beat apart. The reflective second half (18.9–34) uses holds of 1.3–3.9 s on black backgrounds. **Fast = facts, slow = philosophy.**
- **Easing signatures (measured):**
  - Prop and cut-out entrances (envelope, Jensen, table, globe) are **expo ease-out with no overshoot**. Velocity peaks 1–2 unique frames after motion starts, then decays roughly ×0.85 per frame (Jensen: 5.9 → 4.4 → 3.1 → 2.4 → 1.9 → 1.5 → 1.1 → 0.6 → 0.25 px/f across 0.83 s). That suggests `Easing.out(Easing.exp)` or a spring `{damping: 30, stiffness: 120, mass: 1}` over 20–25 frames at 30 fps.
  - The camera reveal move (12.87–14.1) is **asymmetric ease-in-out**: ~8 frames of acceleration, ~25 frames of deceleration. Use `bezier(0.6, 0, 0.1, 1)` over 36 frames.
  - The spin-out exit (14.9–15.27) is **pure ease-in**: rotation velocity ramps linearly ×14 over 9 frames, then the cut lands at peak velocity. Use `bezier(0.7, 0, 1, 0.5)`, ~10 frames, 0 → 25°, plus a growing rotational blur.
  - Type is mostly **blur-in + fade** (3–4 frames, 8 px → 0 blur) for whisper captions and **hard pop** (1–2 frames, scale 1.15 → 1) for heavy keywords. The badge uses a **scaleX-from-centre reveal** (~8 frames, ease-out).
- **Entrance / exit vocab:** keyword pop (HE / SAID / NO); squash-and-blur handoff between words (TWENTY → BILLION); rise-from-bottom with motion blur (envelope, table, Jensen, globe, cone, bill fan); slide-in from the side (gloved hand, torn-paper portrait); glyph-by-glyph number build (2008); scaleX badge reveal; word-by-word blur-in captions; random letter flicker-on (EDITORS); RGB-split glitch-in (website card); dashed-line draw-on (arc, stock line, CTA arrow); neon underline write-on; marquee background type; drop-in avatars.
- **Camera:** layered 2.5D. Slow zoom-outs on reveal shots (net_zoom −14, −18, −62) and one big diagonal travel (shot 12). Spin transitions use rotation (net_rot +18 into shot 13, +49 on the sunburst). There is no handheld shake.
- **Secondary motion:** clouds drifting apart, rotating wire polygons around the hands, crows crossing, slow sunburst rotation, the sparkle twinkle, marquee rows, clock hands, and a constant ~0.5 px/f drift on most holds so nothing is ever frozen.
- **Sync (measured):**
  - **Beats:** 15 of 20 real cuts land within ±0.11 s of a detected beat (99.4 BPM grid). Examples: 13.00 (beat 13.00), 15.27 (15.26), 18.90 (18.86), 26.90 (26.86), 32.97 (32.95). The montage cuts sit 0.06–0.13 s **after** the beat (9.67/9.54, 10.27/10.17, 10.77/10.68, 11.30/11.24, 11.90/11.84).
  - **VO:** visual hits sit 0 to +0.2 s after the word. Cut 3.07 on "Intel's" 3.08; Jensen rise 5.87 on "Jensen" 5.70; badge 7.47 on "Not" 7.36; camera move 12.87 on "Nvidia" 12.96 (−0.09); globe on "earth" 16.74; sunburst 23.00 on "Conviction" 22.88; EDITORS 29.75 on "Editors" 29.56.
  - **Beat over word:** the one place the cut waits for the beat instead of the word is "Built on a single no" (word 17.30, cut 17.77 on beat 17.69).

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Invert flash** (3.07 = 1 frame; 8.97–9.03 = 3 frames; 15.30–15.33 = 2 frames): the first frame(s) of the incoming shot (or the last of the outgoing one) are shown colour-inverted (red → cyan, sky → dark clouds, portrait → negative). It reads as a camera-flash or "shock" cut. Rebuild: on the cut frame, apply `filter: invert(1)` (optionally with `hue-rotate(180deg)` to keep hues) to the incoming scene for 1–3 frames.
2. **White-frame flash cut** (10.27, 10.77, 11.30): one fully white or over-exposed frame between montage clips, landing ~0.1 s after the beat. Rebuild: a white `AbsoluteFill` with opacity 1 for 1 frame (2 for an accent), or `brightness(4)` on the incoming clip's first frame.
3. **Background luma/colour step without a cut** (0.43–0.57 grey → black; 0.77–0.87 black → lime; 1.27–1.50 lime → grey): the set changes colour under persistent type or props in 3–7 frames, so the scene "re-lights" instead of cutting. Rebuild: interpolate the background colour over 4 frames with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`.
4. **Grade drain to B/W** (1.90–2.20): saturation 1 → 0.15 over ~9 frames, synced to the turn in the story.
5. **Spin-out / spin-in with rotational blur** (14.9–15.27 out; 23.0 in; 32.97 whip-rotate): accelerating rotation of the whole comp, 8–10 frames, with radial/rotational blur and a hot-colour flare on the last frame. Rebuild: rotate the outgoing scene 0 → 25° with `bezier(0.7, 0, 1, 0.5)`. Fake the motion blur by stacking 6 copies at −1° steps with decreasing opacity, or use an SVG radial blur. The incoming scene starts at −15° and spins to 0 with ease-out over 8 frames.
6. **Foreground object wipe** (crow at 16.3–16.6): a large dark element crosses close to camera, covering ~40% of the frame for 4–6 frames. In this video it is used inside a shot, but it is a ready-made cut point.
7. **Layer entrance as a transition** (5.90 Jensen; 16.5 globe): a new hero cut-out rises in front of the existing set, changing the subject without a cut.
8. **Morph crossfade** (25.5–25.9): the sunburst's disc dissolves into a clock face of the same size and position (a shape match-cut), 12 frames.
9. **Dip to black** (18.9, 20.2, 29.7): 2–3 frames down to black, used only in the reflective second half.
10. **Glitch-in** (31.4–31.7): RGB channel offset of ±8 px plus horizontal slice jitter for ~6 frames, then clean.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **One surreal tableau per clause.** Combine two literal nouns into one image (HQ on top of the globe, eyes that literally "see"), so the metaphor reads in under a second.
2. **Change the set's colour rather than cutting.** Background luma and colour steps under persistent type (3–7 frames) give four "scenes" in one second without visual whiplash.
3. **Grade carries the story.** Drain to B/W on the refusal, red for the deal, gold for value, black for reflection. Each colour has one job.
4. **Flash punctuation:** 1–3 frame invert or white flashes on the beat make ordinary hard cuts feel percussive and expensive.
5. **Depth, not stickers:** cut-outs occlude text ("2008" behind Jensen), and the globe slides behind the building. The type lives inside the scene.
6. **Two type voices:** heavy caps for facts and numbers; small, lowercase, light grotesk for reflective lines, set alone on black.
7. **Ease-out everything that arrives; ease-in everything that leaves.** Entrances decay over ~20 frames; exits accelerate into the cut and hand off at peak velocity.
8. **Montage cuts land 2–4 frames after the beat,** so the hit is felt before it is seen.
9. **The CTA reuses the brand's own UI** (a real website screenshot with glitch-in and a dotted arrow) instead of a generic end card.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`. Optionally render at 30 and drop every 3rd frame to mimic the 20 fps staccato. Duration = VO + 0.3 s (≈1040 frames).
- **Layer stack per tableau (bottom → top):** `<SetBackground color|image grade>` → `<DepthBlurLayer>` (blurred watermark word or far clouds) → `<Prop>` cut-outs, each with `<RiseIn>` → `<HeroType>` placed **between** prop layers for occlusion → `<Caption>` → `<Doodle>` (dotted arc, dashed stock line, neon underline) → `<GrainOverlay opacity={0.06}>` → `<Vignette strength={0.5}>`.
- **Components:**
  - `RiseIn({from: 'bottom', distance: 0.6*H, durationInFrames: 22})`: `translateY` driven by `spring({damping: 30, stiffness: 120, mass: 1})` (no overshoot), plus `filter: blur()` mapped from velocity (max 12 px vertical blur via SVG `feGaussianBlur stdDeviation="0 12"`).
  - `KeywordPop({words, timings})`: each word appears at VO word start + 0–6 frames, scale 1.15 → 1 over 3 frames, `Easing.out(Easing.back(1.2))`; give the last word scale ×2.
  - `WordBlurCaption`: per word `opacity 0→1` and `blur 8→0 px` over 4 frames, starting at the word timestamp; line 2 uses `font-weight: 300` and `color: #b9b7b8`.
  - `StitchBadge`: `scaleX 0.05 → 1` from centre over 8 frames, `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`; `border: 1.5px dashed rgba(255,255,255,.7)` inset 6 px; `background: #e0b40a`; `border-radius: 10px`.
  - `GlyphBuild("2008")`: digits appear in order every 3 frames, each scale 0.6 → 1 with a 6-frame blur-out.
  - `InvertFlash({frames: 1|2|3})`: wrap the incoming `<Sequence>` and apply `filter: invert(1) hue-rotate(180deg)` while `frame < n`.
  - `WhiteFlash({frames: 1})`: a white `AbsoluteFill` placed on the cut frame.
  - `GradeShift({sat: [1, 0.15], frames: 9})`: CSS `saturate()` interpolation.
  - `SpinTransition({out: {deg: 25, frames: 10, ease: bezier(.7,0,1,.5)}, in: {deg: -15→0, frames: 8, ease: Easing.out(Easing.exp)}})`: rotational blur from 5 ghost copies at decreasing opacity.
  - `CameraTravel({to: {x, y}, frames: 36, ease: bezier(.6,0,.1,1)})`: translates a scene larger than the frame (the wood board holding the portrait and NVIDIA).
  - `DashedDrawOn` (SVG path, `strokeDasharray="12 10"`, animate `strokeDashoffset` over 10–15 frames) for the stock line and arrows.
  - `MarqueeRows({text: 'NOBODY SAYS', rows: 3, speed: ±3 px/f, opacity: .2, blur: 4})`.
  - `DifferenceText`: `mix-blend-mode: difference; color: #fff` over a `#d6b607` disc (it turns blue automatically).
  - `FlickerOn("EDITORS")`: each letter gets a random on-frame within 0–18 frames plus 1–2 off-blinks; `text-shadow: 0 0 24px #c6e02b, 0 0 60px #c6e02b80`.
  - `GlitchIn` (6 frames of RGB offset with `clip-path` slice jitter).
- **Tricky effects:**
  - The glowing eyes are a red radial-gradient blob with `mix-blend-mode: screen`, placed over the eye positions, plus a duotone grade (`grayscale(1)` then a red multiply layer).
  - The torn-paper frame is a PNG alpha mask (ragged edge) with a 6 px white stroke made by dilating the mask.
  - For the globe sliding under the building, render the building base as a separate top layer, or animate a `clip-path` on the globe.
