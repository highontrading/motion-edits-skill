## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** business-mindset micro-explainer (rhetorical question → thesis → 3 brand proofs → lesson → maxim → footage payoff → creator CTA).
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–4.8 **Hook:** a confrontational question ("be honest… obsessed with your customers' problems, or just pushing your solution?").
  - 5.4–8.2 **Thesis:** winners "don't sell harder, they solve better" (shown as the ChatGPT #1 App Store listing subtitle).
  - 8.6–16.9 **Proof ×3:** Amazon → convenience/fast delivery; Uber → waiting/unreliable taxis; Netflix → friction.
  - 17.3–20.8 **Lesson:** "didn't start with products / started with pain" (iMessage) → "That's the real lesson".
  - 21.3–25.8 **Maxim:** great brands aren't built by pushing solutions but by obsessing over problems (sticker desk + calculator).
  - 26.3–29.2 **Payoff:** "Solve the right problem deeply and growth follows naturally" over moody real footage.
  - 29.2–34.6 **CTA:** project file for editors on earnedits.com, comment "Link".
- **Hook mechanics:** frame 0 is a blown-out white, blurred frame; over 15 frames (0–0.5 s) saturation climbs 7→61 and luma drops 130→94 as a phone mockup rises with **"BE HONEST"** in purple halftone condensed type and 4 floating coloured UI chips (Zoom, Weekly, 12:00–13:00, address pill). At 1.30–1.43 s the camera **zooms through the phone screen** (zoom peak +3.9/frame) into an orange halftone anime boxer with giant "are you / Really". That gives three visual layers and a zoom-through in under 1.5 s.
- **Script → visual rule:** the subject noun picks the *UI world* (Amazon = icon grid, Uber = map + driver notification, Netflix = red cinema, "start with products" = text message). The 1–3 VO keywords become chips, pills, tags or typed text inside that UI.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** 17 real cuts in 34.6 s (≈0.49/s). Shots range from 0.1 s (flash) to 4.9 s (desk). The hook runs ~1.4 s per shot; the brand proofs ~1.7–3.0 s; the maxim desk scene is a single 4.9 s camera move with 5 internal "beats". **Pacing is carried by camera bumps, not cuts:** there are ~25 discrete zoom/pan impulses in the video, roughly one every 1.4 s.
- **Easing signatures (measured):**
  - *Camera bump*: asymmetric. Zoom/frame rises over 3–4 frames, peaks, then decays over 5–8 frames (e.g. 9.13→9.20→9.47: 0.09→0.52→0.0). It reads like `Easing.bezier(0.5,0,0.2,1)` over 10–12 frames with a short motion-blur smear at the peak.
  - *Ease-in exit before a cut*: the last 8–20 frames before a cut accelerate monotonically (11.20–11.43: zoom/f 0.09, 0.2, 0.34, 0.52, 0.76, 1.09, 1.21, 1.43; 20.70–21.20: 0.00→0.58). The cut lands at maximum speed.
  - *Ease-out entry after a cut*: the next shot starts at speed and decays exponentially (21.27: 3.55 → 2.44 → 2.31 → 2.04 → 1.84 … → 0.01 at 21.9 s ≈ 20 frames, half-life ~5 frames). There is no overshoot (no sign flip). The pair forms an **invisible "speed-matched" cut**.
  - *Desk camera moves*: 8–12 frame pans with a bell-shaped velocity (22.0→22.2→22.4: 0.2→3.6→0.9 px/f); the 24.4–25.4 descent combines zoom +2.3/f peak with rotation −3°/f peak.
- **Entrance / exit vocab:**
  - exposure/blur reveal from white (15 f)
  - zoom-through into a device screen (6 f in + 6 f out)
  - word-synced pop of words and pills (~4–6 f scale 0.9→1 + fade)
  - letter-by-letter typing with caret (iMessage ~2 letters/frame; desk stickers ~1 letter/2 f)
  - typewriter on a paper label
  - hand-drawn ellipse write-on (~6 f)
  - cursor-driven actions: slide-to-answer push, grab-and-drag a tag away, a click on a toggle
  - notification drop-in (~5 f with blur)
  - a dot flying in with motion blur and settling (6 f)
  - letter tiles scattering (word falls apart)
  - 3-frame invert flash
  - fade/blur-in captions on footage (~5 f per word)
- **Camera:** a virtual 3D camera with depth of field throughout. Net zoom per shot: +11, +12, −4, +31, −6, +9, +23, +38 (desk). There are notable rotations: Amazon grid tilts −14° net, the desk descent −39°, the CTA +33°. No camera shake.
- **Secondary motion:** floating UI chips at different parallax depths, spinning clock hands, a blinking text caret, cursor wander, orbiting Ae icons, dust specks in the CTA, a slow drift on every hold.
- **Sync:**
  - **Cuts are on the beat:** 3.27 (beat 3.18, +0.09), 5.37 (5.34, +0.03), 8.47 (8.52, −0.05), 11.50 (11.47, +0.03), 13.23 (13.28, −0.05), 17.33 (17.34, −0.01), 19.87 (19.95, −0.08), 21.23 (21.29, −0.06), 26.13 (26.19, −0.06). Typical offset is −0.06…+0.09 s.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Speed-matched zoom cut (ease-in → hard cut → ease-out)** at 8.47, 11.50, 19.87, 21.23 s. Shot A accelerates its push over 8–20 f, the cut lands at peak velocity, and shot B starts at the same velocity and decays over ~20 f. Rebuild: A `scale = interpolate(f, [end-15, end], [1, 1.12], {easing: Easing.in(Easing.cubic)})`; B `scale = interpolate(f, [0, 20], [1.15, 1], {easing: Easing.out(Easing.exp)})`; add 1–2 frames of `blur(4px)` at the joint.
2. **Zoom-through screen** at 1.30–1.43 s: the camera scales into the phone display until the screen content fills the window (6 f in, 6 f decay). Rebuild: one scene nested inside the device screen; scale the device 1→~4 about the screen centre with ease-in, then crossfade the nested comp to full-frame in the last 2 frames.
3. **Object wipe (send-icon swoop)** at 3.17–3.27 s: a giant blurred Telegram-blue icon sweeps up from the bottom-right across ~40% of the frame in 3 f, and the cut hides behind it.
4. **Invert/negative flash** at 4.63–4.73 s: 1 over-exposed frame + 2 colour-inverted frames that preview the next word ("solution"). Rebuild: `filter: invert(1) hue-rotate(180deg)` on the incoming scene for 2 frames after 1 white frame.
5. **Anchor dissolve (location-dot match)** at 13.20–13.53 s: the blue dot keeps its screen position while the white canvas cross-dissolves to a map over ~10 f, and "Waiting" blurs out (blur 0→12 px, 3 f). Rebuild: keep the dot layer above both scenes; crossfade the backgrounds underneath.
6. **Letterbox collapse** at 16.47 s: the full frame cuts to a cinemascope strip (~200 px tall) on charcoal, a "cinema" cue for the Netflix line. Rebuild: animate `clipPath: inset(42% 0 42% 0)` over 3–4 f, or hard-switch.
7. **Lens-blur whip inside one scene** at 22.2, 23.1, 23.9 s: an 8–12 f pan with a 2–3 f DOF-blur peak, so the camera "re-frames" without cutting. Rebuild: translate the desk group with a bell-velocity curve and set `filter: blur()` to `4 * normalisedSpeed` px.
8. **Zoom-out reveal into the card** at 31.6–31.73 s: the centre Ae icon resolves into the website card as the camera pulls back.
9. **Hard cut to footage** at 26.13 s: the only live-action shot, used for the emotional payoff, with no transition effect.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Tell each example inside its native UI.** Rebuilding the brand's own interface (map dot, App Store rank, chat bubble) explains the point faster than any icon and feels "designed".
2. **Put the camera in 3D with real depth of field.** Tilt the UI plane 15–40°, blur the near and far elements, and the flat recreations look like product photography.
3. **Hide cuts in speed:** ease-in the outgoing push, cut at peak velocity, then ease-out the incoming one. Over 4 such cuts the video feels like one continuous move.
4. **Give every beat an impulse:** a 10-frame zoom/pan bump with motion blur on each musical beat or keyword. Holds never feel static.
5. **Wrap keywords in containers:** pills with coloured dots, a red tag, a lime highlighter, a drawn ellipse, an LCD screen. Each keyword gets a different container, so repetition never bores.
6. **Alternate light and dark scenes** (orange → dark → white → dark → white → dark-red → white → white → green → night footage → dark) for constant contrast resets.
7. **Act the verb with a cursor:** "pushing" = a cursor pushing the call slider; "removed friction" = a cursor dragging the friction tag away; "solution falls apart" = tiles scattering.
8. **Save the single real footage shot for the emotional payoff**, with plain word-synced captions and a locked camera.
9. **Change aspect as a chapter marker:** a square letterbox for the hook and thesis, full 9:16 from the first proof onward, and a cinemascope strip for "entertainment".

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, 1040 frames (34.6 s). Scenes 1–7 render into a `1080×1056` centred `<AbsoluteFill>` window with black bars (`top: 432px`).
- **Layer stack per UI scene** (bottom → top):
  1. `<SceneBg variant="dark-vignette|white|icon-grid|cutting-mat">`
  2. a `<Camera3D>` wrapper (`perspective: 1400px`; animated `rotateX/rotateZ/scale/translate`)
  3. UI layers at z-depths with `<DepthBlur z>` (blur = |z − focusZ| × 0.02 px)
  4. `<KeywordPill>`/`<TagChip>`
  5. `<Cursor>`
  6. a `<GrainDust opacity={0.06}>` overlay
- **Components & timings:**
  - `CameraBump({at, axis:'zoom'|'x'|'y', amount})`: a 12-frame impulse with velocity profile `[0, .35, 1, .7, .45, .28, .16, .09, .05, .02, 0, 0]` × amount, plus `blur(velocity*3px)`. Place one on every beat in `beats[]` that coincides with a keyword.
  - `SpeedMatchedCut({outFrames: 15, inFrames: 20, scaleOut: 1.12, scaleIn: 1.15})`: `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)` out, `Easing.out(Easing.exp)` in.
  - `ZoomThroughScreen({device, inner, frames: 12})`.
  - `KeywordPill({text, dotColor})`: glassy `rgba(200,220,240,.55)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` and radius 14 px. Enter with `spring({damping: 14, stiffness: 220, mass: 0.6})` scale 0.8→1 + opacity over ~6 f, at word start − 2 f.
  - `ChatThread({messages:[{side, text, typeSpeed: 2 /*chars per frame*/}]})`: caret blink every 15 f; the send animation translates the bubble 40 px with 3 f of blur.
  - `MapScene({dotPos})` with `AnchorDissolve` (10 f).
  - `CursorDrag({path, grabAt, releaseAt})` for the friction tag; the tag follows with a 2-frame lag and rotates ±15°.
  - `TypewriterLabel({font: 'Courier Prime', bg: '#f2c94c', cps: 20})`.
  - `EllipseWriteOn`: SVG ellipse with `strokeDashoffset` 1→0 over 6 f and a slightly open end.
  - `StickerText({font: 'Lilita One', fill, stroke: 10px #fff, shadow: '0 6px 0 rgba(0,0,0,.35)'})` typed per letter every 2 f.
  - `HalftoneFill`: SVG pattern of 3 px dots at 45°, used as a text fill via `background-clip:text`.
