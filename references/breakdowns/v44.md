## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** pop-culture lore explainer / theory short (MCU *Avengers: Doomsday*), followed by a 5 s creator CTA for project files.
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–5.0 **Hook (claim):** the Fantastic Four may matter more in *Doomsday* than people think. Doom + the Avengers logo, then the FF line-up rising into frame.
  - 5.5–11.2 **Setup (misdirect → correction):** Doom's key comics relationship isn't the Avengers, it's the FF. Shown as a tarot card that flips twice: THE DOOM → THE HEROES → THE ELEMENTS.
  - 11.5–17.9 **Rivalry:** Doom and Reed as rivals, "genius against genius, ego against ego". Shown as playing cards dealt on felt and then merged into one double-king card, with handwritten captions.
  - 18.2–21.7 **Now:** they meet in the MCU (two rod-puppet kings in a theatre).
  - 22.2–27.0 **Turn / escalation:** "But this time…" Doom isn't only after Reed but everyone. Four fast photoreal Doom shots, ~1 s each.
  - 27.0–31.9 **Payoff:** Reed may be the only one who understands Doom (cosmic tableau: Reed in front of a burning Earth, Doom's back in the foreground).
  - 32.1–37.3 **CTA:** "Editors who level up fast study the project file. It's on earnedits.com." (AE file icons, amber end card).
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** frame 0 is already a complete poster: Doom, arms spread, centred in a night sky with engraved halftone clouds and gold 8-point stars. Within 0.07–0.4 s the *Avengers: Doomsday* logo ghost-fades in behind him while the camera pulls back with an ease-out (zoom −2.2 on the first moving frame, then −0.8 → −0.3/frame). At 0.87 s the camera starts tilting down, accelerating (pan_y −0.8 → −5.5 px/frame by 1.33 s), and Doom scrolls up out of frame. Two recognisable IPs and a camera move all land inside 1.5 s.
- **Script → visual rule:** every relationship in the VO becomes a **physical object manipulation**: a card flip = "isn't X, it's Y"; dealing two cards side by side = "rivals"; a hand merging the two cards into one = "genius against genius"; two rod puppets on one stage = "brought together". Proper nouns appear as logos or card titles, and only short emphatic clauses appear as type.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Frame rate:** the whole piece is animated **on twos (15 drawings/s)**. Every move therefore has a slightly stepped, stop-motion feel that matches the puppet-theatre art direction. It also makes AI stills feel "animated" rather than "slid". Rebuild by quantising time to 2-frame steps.
- **Pacing:** 0.35 cuts/s overall, but the structure is **slow → fast → slow**: diorama scenes last 4–7 s with in-scene builds (shots 2–5), the Doom montage cuts every **~1.0 s** (shots 7–10, 4 cuts in 4.4 s), then the payoff (4.4 s) and CTA (3.2 s) are long holds.
- **Easing signatures:**
  - Camera pull-backs on cuts are **exponential ease-out**: a big first-frame jump (zoom −2.2 to −5.8) followed by geometric decay over 6–20 frames. This is how each new scene "breathes in".
  - The vertical scroll transition is an **ease-in-out** over ~1.5 s: accelerate 0.87–1.33 s (pan_y −0.8 → −5.5 px/f at 180 px = −22 px/f at 720), travel through empty sky, then decelerate 1.87–2.4 s from −7.7 → −1.6 px/f.
  - Thrown/flipped cards take 6–13 f for the main move, with a 6–10 f overshoot-free settle (zoom decays −2.9 → −0.4) plus a tiny rotational wobble. Flips put the edge-on moment at the midpoint (~f5 of 10), and the content swaps there.
  - Badge pops are 2–3 drawings (4–6 f) with a ~5% overshoot and a small rotation (the MCU pill) that settles in ~8 f.
  - Hand and arm inserts are fast in, hold, fast out, with blur: arms cross the frame in ~4–6 f each way.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:** ghost fade (logo, 10 f); rise-from-bottom with ease-out (cast, 16 f); stars dropping on strings with a pendulum sway; empty pill → word-by-word fill; card thrown at the lens and falling back (13 f); Y-axis card flip (10 f); hand deal (9 f); hand-of-god swipe merge (10 f); handwriting write-on (~1 letter/frame); curtain-part reveal (6 f); scale-up title pop with depth sorting (8 f); stacked word drops (BUT/THIS/TIME); blur-fade word labels (~6 f each, grey → white); orbit-ring draw-on (6 f); staggered icon pops (2 f stagger).
- **Camera:** almost every shot has a slow virtual push or pull of 7–20 net zoom units over 1–6 s, so nothing is ever static except the end card. There is one large vertical camera move (the hook transition) and parallax in the space tableau. No shakes.
- **Secondary motion:** swaying hanging stars and moon, gull heads bobbing at the frame edges, flickering fire glow, drifting clouds.
- **Sync (measured):**
  - Cuts sit within ±0.05 s of the VO onset of the new sentence: 5.47 vs "Dr." 5.52 (−0.05); 18.20 vs "And" 18.18 (+0.02); 22.27 vs "But" 22.22 (+0.05); 32.13 vs "Editors" 32.12 (+0.01); 35.33 vs "It's" 35.34 (−0.01). The exception is 11.40 vs "Doom" 11.52 (−0.12, on onset 11.54).
  - Inside the montage, cuts follow the music, not the words: 25.27 = beat 25.24; the slide-up lands 23.33 vs onset 23.38.
  - Physical "hits" land on music onsets: flip 1 at 8.47 = onset 8.45; flip 2 at ~10.0 = onset 10.08; the cast rise settles on beat 2.37.
  - Text: **containers lead** (empty pill 0.04 s before "far", MCU pill 0.08 s before "MCU"), **words trail** the voice by 0.1–0.25 s (FAR +0.16, THIS +0.21, TIME +0.16, EDITORS +0.15). The script captions start on the first word ("Genius" 14.84 → write-on from 14.93).

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Vertical scroll / tilt-down through the set** (0.87–2.4 s): one continuous sky. The outgoing hero exits up as the camera accelerates; there are ~5 frames of empty sky at peak speed; the incoming hero rises with an ease-out. Rebuild: put both scenes in one tall `AbsoluteFill` (height 2 × 1280 + 300 gap) and animate `translateY` from 0 to −(1280 + 300) with `Easing.bezier(0.55, 0, 0.2, 1)` over 46 frames, plus a ~6 px vertical motion blur at peak.
2. **Cut + exponential pull-back** (5.47, 11.40, 18.20): a hard cut where the new scene starts at scale ~1.12 and decays to 1.0 (`1 + 0.12·e^(−t/5f)`). The cut feels like the camera "lands".
3. **Card flip as a "not X, but Y" device** (8.2–8.5, 9.7–10.0): `rotateY 0 → 180°` with `perspective: 900px` over 10 f, swapping the face texture at 90°, plus a ~4 px horizontal blur near edge-on. Land it on a music onset.
4. **Hand-of-god insert** (14.6–14.93, 22.57–23.3): a body part enters from the frame top with heavy motion blur, performs the change (merges cards / covers text), and exits. The change happens under the occlusion, so no transition FX are needed.
5. **Slide-up panel push** (23.13–23.33): the next shot rises from the bottom as a hard-edged rectangle over ~6 f (`Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`), while the jester arm retracts upward. The next shot starts defocused and racks sharp in 2 f.
6. **Hard cuts on the beat** in the montage (24.27, 25.27, 26.27): plain cuts, ~1 s apart, with each shot slowly pushing in.
7. **Micro-dissolve** (27.63–27.73, 3 f): a crossfade used once to move from ground to space.
8. **Brightness-slam end card** (35.33): a dark starfield (luma 30) hard-cuts to an amber full frame (luma 154).

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Stage the argument as a physical performance.** Cards flip, get dealt and get merged by hands, and the logic of the script becomes something you can watch.
2. **One cohesive world for the explanation, a contrasting world for the stakes.** The storybook theatre carries the setup, and photoreal darkness arrives exactly on "But". The art-direction switch is itself a plot point.
3. **Animate on twos** (15 fps) when the assets are illustrated stills. It adds handmade texture and hides the "Ken Burns" look.
4. **Every scene breathes:** cut in slightly zoomed and decay out (exponential ease-out), then keep a slow push. Nothing is ever fully static until the CTA.
5. **Containers lead, words trail.** Pop the empty badge just before the phrase, then fill word by word 0.1–0.2 s after each spoken word.
6. **Depth-sort type behind the subject** (crowns over TOGETHER, hood over WHAT DOOM IS) so the type sits inside the scene, not on top of it.
7. **Hierarchy through size, not colour:** one white heavy grotesk, a pill badge for "quotes", a thin script for poetic parallels (genius/ego) placed diagonally opposite each other.
8. **Use frame-edge "peekers"** (gull heads, hands, arms, curtain swags) with heavy DOF blur to create foreground depth on 2D sets.
9. **Montage rhythm:** in the escalation, cut on the music every ~1 s with four different photoreal angles of the same character, then release into a long, wide tableau for the payoff.
10. **Hard CTA reset:** a single flat brand colour after 35 s of dark frames makes the URL unmissable.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `fps: 30`. Quantise all animation to 2-frame steps: `const f = Math.floor(frame/2)*2`. Duration = VO + 0.3 s (≈1120 frames for 37.3 s).
- **Typical diorama scene stack (bottom → top):** `<SkyBackdrop texture="quilt|night" grain>` → `<StageProp src="moon|cloud|star" hangFrom={y} sway={{amp:3, period:60}}>` → hero cut-outs (`<RodPuppet>` / `<TarotCard>` / `<PlayingCard>`) → `<DepthSortedTitle>` (text layer z-index between the background and a masked copy of the subject's head) → `<Curtains side="left|right|top" open={progress}>` → `<EdgePeeker src="gull|hand" blur={18}>` → `<Grain opacity={0.06}/>` + `<Vignette strength={0.45}/>`.
- **Components & timings (30 fps frames):**
  - `<CutBreath>`: scale `1.12 → 1.0` with `1 + 0.12*Math.exp(-f/5)`, plus a continuous `slowPush` of +0.6%/s.
  - `<VerticalScrollTransition from={A} to={B}>`: 46 f, `Easing.bezier(0.55,0,0.2,1)`, with vertical blur `feGaussianBlur stdDeviation="0 6"` when velocity > threshold.
  - `<TarotFlip front back at={frame}>`: 10 f `rotateY(0→180deg)`, `perspective(900px)`, swap at f5, settle with `spring({damping: 14, stiffness: 160, mass: 0.7})` on rotateZ ±2°.
  - `<CardThrowIn>`: start `scale 2.6, rotateX 35deg, blur 12px`, end `scale 1, rotateX 0`, over 13 f with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`, then a 10 f settle.
  - `<PillBadge words timings>`: box pops with `spring({damping: 10, stiffness: 220, mass: 0.6})` 2 f before the first word; each word appears at `wordStart + 4 f` (opacity 0 → 1, y 6 → 0 px, 4 f).
  - `<HandwritingWriteOn text font="Allura">`: SVG path `strokeDashoffset` or a clip-path wipe L→R at ~1 glyph/frame, starting on the first word.
  - `<StackedWords lines={['BUT','THIS','TIME']}>`: each line appears at `wordStart + 5 f` with scale 1.08 → 1 over 4 f, leading 0.85.
  - `<BlurInLabel>`: per word `filter: blur(8px) → 0`, opacity 0 → 1, x +12 → 0 px over 6 f, colour `#8a8a8a → #fff` over 10 f.
  - `<OrbitEllipse>`: SVG ellipse rx = 1.1 × text width, ry = 0.35 × text height, stroke `#a59a55` 1.5 px, draw on over 6 f, rotated −4°.
  - `<IconPopStagger count={3} stagger={2}>`: `spring({damping: 9, stiffness: 260})` scale 0 → 1.
  - `<SlideUpPanelPush>`: incoming `translateY(100% → 0)` over 6 f `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`, plus `filter: blur(10px → 0)` over the next 2 f (rack focus).
  - `<ParallaxTableau layers=[{src:bg, drift:0}, {src:earth, drift:-0.3}, {src:foreground, drift:-1}]>`: 4.4 s pull-out (scale 1.08 → 1.0).
  - `<EndCard bg="radial-gradient(circle, #f6b70a 0%, #a87203 100%)" text="EARNEDITS.COM" extrude={{dx:4, dy:4, color:'#211317'}}/>`.
- **Tricky bits:** text behind the subject needs a cut-out (alpha) copy of the subject's head/crown layered above the title. Generate it with a segmentation pass or design the assets in layers.
