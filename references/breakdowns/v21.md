# v21 — "Banned in 72 hours": AI-news mystery explainer (mixed-media poster montage)

**Source creator handle:** earn.edits · **Format:** 720×1280, 9:16, 30 fps container (a ~20 fps master: every 3rd frame is a duplicate, 390/1129 frames have zero diff), 37.6 s · **Shots:** ~22 real (24 detected; the 2.97–3.07 "shot" is a 3-frame flash, 13.6 is a logo fade-in, 16.07/16.23/16.33 are a desaturation; missed cuts at 7.35 (slide), 11.6 (whip) and 17.23 (hard cut)) · **Avg shot:** ~1.7 s (median ~1.4 s) · **Music BPM:** ~108 (dark pulse bed under VO) · **VO:** yes, English, urgent "breaking news" narrator

**Style tags:** collage-explainer, cutout-photo, 3d-render, ui-mockup, kinetic-type, giant-keyword, stacked-type, word-by-word-captions, typewriter, highlighter-marker, hand-drawn-doodle, grain, depth-of-field, motion-blur, whip-pan, zoom-through, slide-push, beat-synced-cuts (montage section only), documentary-vo, cta-endcard, logo-montage*, symbol-motif*

**One-line essence:** one brand symbol (the orange 10-spoke asterisk) is recast as a new literal prop in almost every scene (glowing idol, chick hatching from an egg, rocket badge, mousetrap bait, starburst halo, circuit chip), so a 37 s news story reads as one visual thread while every ~1.5 s the art style, palette and typeface change completely.

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** tech-news mystery micro-documentary ("shock claim → twist → background → collapse → 'the wild part' → open question → CTA"). Note: the on-screen newspaper is literally titled "The Fake Times", so the piece is framed as satire/speculation.
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–4.9 **Hook:** "the most powerful AI ... banned in just 72 hours". Superlative + ban + number in the first sentence.
  - 4.9–7.3 **Twist:** not by a competitor, by its own government.
  - 7.3–14.4 **Background:** the model was reportedly too dangerous to release, so a "safer version" (Fable 5) shipped.
  - 14.5–21.2 **Collapse:** three days later "everything collapsed" → one jailbreak, one letter → access shut down globally.
  - 21.2–25.1 **Turn:** "The wild part?" → other free models can already do the same (a rapid logo montage).
  - 25.1–32.6 **Open question:** what really happened? Were they punished for honesty, or was the model too powerful to stay online?
  - 32.9–37.6 **CTA:** "Editors. Project files on earnedits.com. Visit now."
- **Hook mechanics:** 2 frames of black, then a glowing peach asterisk over a crowd of reaching hands (a "worship" image), and the first word "The" fades in by f5. At 2.97 s a 3-frame red/negative flash hits and a wooden "BANNED" placard is shoved into frame from the bottom right. The orange/cream circus-stripe wall then snaps in behind it (3.5 s). The calm → violent contrast lands inside 3.5 s.
- **Script → visual rule:** every clause gets a new "poster", and the clause's noun becomes a literal prop (ban → placard, release → hatching egg, launch → rocket, jailbreak → boot-failure terminal, letter → paper sheets, shut down → road sign + hazard tape, models → their real logos, "what really happened" → a word-search puzzle, punished → mousetrap, government → a politician's head in a starburst, powerful → glowing chip). Only the keyword phrase is set as type.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** a mixed-media montage. There is no single style; it alternates between soft-focus photographic/3D renders (hands, egg, mousetrap, chip), flat-graphic type posters (colour bars, "The wild part?"), real UI/logos (model picker dropdown, Gemini, Jasper, Copilot) and paper props (newspaper, letters, word search). What holds it together is the recurring asterisk symbol, film grain and shallow depth of field on everything.
- **Backgrounds & textures:** warm-brown studio dark with vignette (hook) → orange/cream vertical stripes, 8 stripes across the frame, soft-focused (BANNED) → off-white paper with horizontal pastel bar "data" strips → newsprint ("The Fake Times", typewriter body copy) → beige paper with **ghosted translucent collage images** (camera, mushroom, butterfly at ~15–25% opacity dissolving into each other) → starfield → CRT boot log with barrel distortion → blue halftone grid → black + yellow/black hazard tape → light grey (wild part) → light UI grey / Gemini navy gradient → torn-poster grunge wall (word search) → deep navy `#08050e` (mousetrap, starburst) → near-black `#0e0e0e` (chip, CTA).
- **Colour:** each scene is one dominant field + one accent, and the brand orange recurs throughout:
  - Brand orange/peach: `#bf733e`, `#c5773e`, `#e8935a` (asterisk, stripes); glow peach `#bf9078`.
  - Hook browns: `#1c1615`, `#30211f`, `#4f352e`.
  - Paper neutrals: `#aea394`, `#c3b9b7`, `#d1d0d1`.
  - Alarm red for keywords: `#b40d10` (EVERYTHING COLLAPSED), `#bf2a2e`/`#c8202a` (question mark).
  - Navy `#1f2a4a` (wild part type), blue `#134280` (letter scene).
  - Hazard yellow `#d2c338`; gold CTA `#cfb24f`/`#c9a54a`; highlighter yellow `#e4d33a`.
  - **Grade-as-story:** at "collapsed" (16.18 s) the rocket desaturates from orange flame to grey/white over ~6 frames, and the following terminal scene has saturation ≈0 (sat 0.2).
- **Typography:** a different face per scene, chosen to match its prop:
  - Hook/captions: light geometric grotesk (Inter Light / Söhne Leicht / Styrene-like), sentence case, ~5% of frame width per cap height. Line 1 is off-white `#d8d0cc`, line 2 peach `#e0a080`. Words fade in with a blur, one per spoken word.
  - "72 HOURS", "NOT BY A COMPETITOR", "EVERYTHING COLLAPSED", "DID THEY GET PUNISHED", "WHAT THEY BUILT": heavy condensed grotesk (Bebas Neue / Oswald Bold / Anton), ALL CAPS. The hero word spans 55–65% of frame width (COMPETITOR ≈ 60%).
  - Newspaper: blackletter masthead (Old English / UnifrakturCook-like) + typewriter mono body (Courier Prime / American Typewriter).
  - "Fable 5": regular geometric sans (Product Sans / Google Sans-like) at ~40% width, typed letter by letter.
  - "ONE JAILBREAK": blocky pixel/terminal display face (VT323 / "Press Start"-like but chunkier), white with a soft glow.
  - "EVENING / LETTER": wide heavy grotesk (Archivo Black Expanded-like), beige `#c9c0aa` with a dark drop shadow, ~75% width.
  - "SHUT DOWN": condensed bold (Bebas/League Gothic) set inside a yellow diamond road sign. The caption above it is ultra-thin grey sans.
  - "The wild part?": very heavy geometric sans (Futura Extra Bold / Gilroy Black) in navy, stacked 3 lines at ~65% width, over a giant red "?". Where a letter overlaps the "?", it flips to white (**knockout/difference colouring**).
  - "TOO POWERFUL TO STAY ONLINE": heavy extended grotesk (Archivo Black / Helvetica Black Extended), white, with the payoff word ONLINE in gold `#d9b03a`.
  - CTA: EDITORS in gold heavy extended sans (~45% width); "project files on earnedits.com" in light sans; "visit now and download it" in light sans with a thin hand-drawn curved line.
- **Emphasis rules:** one word per caption gets the accent colour (HONEST in yellow, ONLINE in gold). Mid-sentence keywords are highlighted with a yellow marker sweep (newspaper) or a red hand-drawn ellipse (word search, "HAPPENED").
- **Layout:** hero props sit centred or lower-centre (y 45–80%). Type sits in the upper third (y 10–35%), or is right-aligned and stacked on the right edge when a prop fills the left (mousetrap). Nothing important goes below y ≈ 85%.
- **Assets:** 3D/AI-rendered props (asterisk idol, cracked egg + asterisk chick, chrome retro rocket with asterisk porthole, mousetrap with an orange 3D asterisk as bait, circuit-board chip with an asterisk core), a cut-out politician head, real logos (Gemini, Jasper, Copilot, a black geometric model logo), a ChatGPT-style model-picker dropdown, letters, a word-search sheet, road sign, hazard tape, and the earnedits.com mobile site mockup.
- **Finishing:** constant fine grain, heavy vignette on dark scenes, very shallow DOF (foreground/background elements blurred at 8–20 px), directional motion blur on every move, a soft bloom on light sources (asterisk, chip), and luma flicker of ±5–10 on the paper scenes (texture boil).

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** ~0.6 real cuts/s, but strongly variable.
  - Setup scenes hold 2.5–4.3 s (newspaper 4.25 s, Fable 2.8 s).
  - The collapse section cuts every 0.9–1.8 s.
  - The "other models" **logo montage runs 5 shots in 2.7 s (0.43–0.73 s each)**, the fastest moment in the piece.
  - The open question relaxes back to 1.5–2.4 s.
- **Frame cadence:** the master is ~20 fps inside a 30 fps file (2 unique frames + 1 duplicate), which gives every move a slight stutter. Rebuild at 30 fps but consider `steps`/hold-frames on fast moves for the same crunchy feel.
- **Easing signatures (from per_frame.csv):**
  - *Whip exits are ease-in:* newspaper exit pan_x −3.6, −5.5, −8.1, −11.9 over 4 frames, then cut. Placard exit pan_y −2.5, −4.8, −6.3 over 3 frames. The camera accelerates out and the cut happens at peak speed.
  - *Entrances are ease-out:* the starburst contraction peaks at frame 2–3 after the cut (flow 28) and holds high for ~16 frames, then drops to 4. The rocket rise and sign drop settle in 4–6 frames.
  - *Decaying spin:* starburst rotation decays nearly linearly from 4.1 to 1.6 over 36 frames (an ease-out rotation that never fully stops before the cut).
  - Overshoot: small on the sign drop (scale dips below 1 for 1–2 frames). No overshoot on type; type only blur-fades.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:**
  - word-by-word blur-fade (each word 4–6 frames, blur ~8 px → 0, opacity 0 → 1)
  - letter-by-letter typing (Fable 5, jasper, ONE JAILBREAK, EVENING/LETTER: ~1 letter per frame)
  - highlighter sweep (≈0.8 s for 4 words)
  - hand-drawn ellipse write-on (≈4–6 frames)
  - drop-in from oversized (sign, starburst)
  - thrust-in prop with rotation (placard)
  - UI dropdown expand (≈15 frames)
  - logo wipe (Gemini)
  - CRT power-off squash (2–3 frames)
  - zoom-through toward camera (1–2 frames)
  - ghost cross-dissolve of background images (~0.5 s each)
- **Camera:** almost every shot has a slow push or drift (net_zoom +9 to +25 over 2–4 s), plus decisive whips at exits. Measured big moves: shot 5 net pan_x −107 (long leftward drift + whip), shot 19 net_rot +58 (spin), shot 18 pan_x −21.7 (reveal slide).
- **Secondary motion:** the ghost images morph behind "safer version", the rocket hovers, the lens-flare glint on the chip, hazard tapes slide continuously, and paper texture boils (luma ±10).
- **Sync:**
  - Visuals generally lead or match the VO: newspaper highlight lands on "dangerous" (+0.0 s), "Fab" types +0.1 s after "Fable", the red ellipse lands ~0.3 s after "happened", and the dropdown expands on "other" (22.40 vs 22.44).
  - The BANNED flash comes 0.15 s after "banned" (2.82).
  - Shut-down cut 19.43 sits between "Access" (19.18) and "shut" (19.76), so the sign is already there when the word lands.
  - "Did" (26.18) arrives 0.4 s before its scene (26.6); the cut lands on "punished".
  - **Beat sync:** across the whole video, cut-to-beat offsets average 0.13 s on a 0.557 s beat, which is effectively random. **The logo montage is the exception:** 22.40 (−0.01), 22.97 (0.00), 23.50 (−0.05), 25.13 (+0.01) all land within 2 frames of a beat. Snap montage cuts to the beat grid even when the rest of the edit follows the VO.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Flash hit (red → negative → back)** at 2.97 s, 3 frames: frame 1 saturation spikes (sat 231, red tint), frames 2–3 are bright and inverted (luma 162), then the new layout arrives with the placard. Rebuild: a 1-frame `<AbsoluteFill style={{background:'#c0201a', mixBlendMode:'screen'}}>` and then 2 frames of `filter: invert(1) brightness(1.3)`. Pair it with an impact SFX.
2. **Slide push** at 7.35 s (colour bars → newspaper): the layout translates left while the next poster enters from the right, ~10 frames, ease-in-out.
3. **Accelerating whip-pan** at 11.6 and 28.9 s: 3–4 frames of ease-in translation (up to ~12 px/f at 180 px ≈ 48 px/f at 720 px) with horizontal blur, then a cut on the blurred peak. The new scene starts slightly blurred and resolves in 2–3 frames.
4. **Dip to black** at 14.3–14.5 s (6 frames) into the space scene: a "chapter break" before the collapse.
5. **Grade-shift (colour → grey)** at 16.0–16.4 s: a story beat, not a cut. Saturation goes from 1 to ~0.1 on the hero over ~6 frames, synced to "collapsed".
6. **Brightness-pop hard cut** at 17.23 s: luma +20 over 3 frames, then cut.
7. **CRT power-off** at 18.5 s: content squashes to a horizontal line (scaleY 1 → 0.05, scaleX 1 → 1.1) with a white glow, 2–3 frames.
8. **Zoom-through** at 21.2 and 22.4 s: the current layer scales ×1.3–1.5 toward the camera with radial/zoom blur in 1–2 frames, then a hard cut.
9. **Beat-cut logo montage** at 22.4–25.1 s: hard cuts on beats, each logo with its own micro-reveal (expand, wipe, rush, type, type).
10. **Shake-whip into a prop** at 25.13 s: 3–4 frames of chaotic smear (letters motion-blurred diagonally), then settle.
11. **Desaturate-dissolve to black** at 32.7–33.0 s into the CTA.
12. **Slide-in UI card** at 34.9 s plus a **scroll-up** at 36.4 s inside the CTA.

## 6. Sound design
A low, ominous pulse bed (~108 BPM, RMS 0.12) under a fast VO with short pauses (0.2–0.4 s) between clauses. Strong onsets cluster on visual hits (7.06/7.17 slide push, 11.61 whip, 20.11/20.62 tape, 22.41 dropdown, 24.1 jasper, 25.12 word search, 26.24/26.73 mousetrap words, 28.79 whip, 30.0/30.42 spin, 33.6–35.4 CTA), so expect whoosh SFX on whips, impact on the BANNED flash, typing clicks on the letter-by-letter reveals, and a CRT "power-down" blip at 18.5 s. The bed carries through the CTA; there is no silence moment.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **One recurring symbol, reinvented per scene.** The asterisk becomes an idol, a chick, a porthole, bait, a halo and a chip. The viewer tracks the protagonist even though the style changes every shot.
2. **Change the typeface with the prop.** Blackletter + typewriter on the newspaper, terminal font on the CRT, road-sign condensed on SHUT DOWN, real brand faces in the logo montage. Type feels "found in the world", not overlaid.
3. **Exits accelerate, entrances decelerate.** Whips ease in to the cut at peak speed, and the next shot starts already moving and settles.
4. **Grade as narrative.** Colour drains at "collapsed" and the next scene is fully monochrome.
5. **Montage the proof on the beat.** When the script lists examples, cut 0.4–0.7 s per item exactly on the music grid; elsewhere follow the VO.
6. **Literal visual puns for abstract lines** ("what really happened" → word search; "punished" → mousetrap; "too dangerous to release" → hatching egg).
7. **Highlight in place** (marker sweep, hand-drawn ellipses) instead of adding new captions when the prop already contains the words.
8. **Shallow DOF + grain + vignette on everything** to unify AI renders, UI screenshots and logos.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, duration = VO + 0.5 s (≈1140 frames). Optional `useStutter(3)` hold to mimic the 20 fps cadence on fast moves.
- **Scene stack (bottom → top):**
  1. `<SceneBG variant="stripes|paper|starfield|crt|halftoneGrid|navy">`
  2. `<GhostCollage images opacity={0.2} crossfade={15}>` (optional)
  3. `<HeroProp src depthBlur={0..12} enter="rise|thrust|dropIn" />`
  4. `<KeywordStack words fontFamily accentWord accentColor align="right|center" />`
  5. `<Doodle type="ellipse|swoosh" color stroke={5} drawOn={6} />`
  6. `<Highlighter from to duration={24} color="#e4d33a" />`
  7. `<Grain opacity={0.07} />` + `<Vignette strength={0.45} />`
- **Key components & timings:**
  - `WordBlurIn`: per word at `wordStart − 2f`; opacity 0 → 1, `filter: blur(8px) → 0`, scale 1.06 → 1 over 5 frames, `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`.
  - `TypeOn`: 1 char/frame, optional caret.
  - `FlashHit`: frames [0] red screen-blend, [1–2] `invert(1) brightness(1.3)`.
  - `WhipExit`: translateX `interpolate(f,[0,4],[0,-900],{easing:Easing.in(Easing.quad)})` + SVG `feGaussianBlur stdDeviation="24 0"`, with the cut at the last frame.
  - `WhipEnter`: mirror of the exit with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)` over 5 frames.
  - `DropInSign`: `spring({damping: 11, stiffness: 160, mass: 0.7})` mapping scale 1.4 → 1 and rotate −8° → 0.
  - `DecayingSpin`: `rotate = base + 200 * (1 - Math.exp(-f/25))` for the starburst; scale 1.6 → 1 over 16 frames with `Easing.out(Easing.exp)`.
  - `DesatBeat`: `filter: saturate(interpolate(f,[0,6],[1,0.1]))` at the keyword frame.
  - `CRTOff`: scaleY 1 → 0.04 over 3 frames with `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)`, a white `box-shadow` glow, then black.
  - `ZoomThrough`: scale 1 → 1.5 + radial blur (stacked scaled copies at decreasing opacity) over 2 frames.
  - `DropdownExpand`: list height 1 → N items over 15 frames, each row fading in staggered by 2 frames.
  - `LogoMontage`: an array of `{logo, reveal}` with cut frames snapped to `beats[]` (round to the nearest beat frame).
- **Knockout type over a shape** ("The wild part?"): render the navy text, then a second white copy clipped by the "?" path (`clipPath: url(#q)`), or use `mix-blend-mode: difference` on a white layer over a red "?".
