# v65 — "Comment TUT": free-tutorial lead magnet (self-promo, process reel + product CTA)

**Source creator handle:** earn.edits · **Format:** 720×1280, 9:16, 16 fps (delivered file; likely a re-encode of a 24/30 fps master), 29.8 s · **Shots:** ~17 real (24 detected; 1.94–2.31 is one strobe, 10.69/10.81 and 15.94/16.06 are in-recording scroll/toggle jumps; the 21.3–23.0 flip-book is one shot with ~10 card swaps inside it) · **Avg shot:** ~1.75 s (median ~1.6 s) · **Music BPM:** ~89–92 (beat spacing 0.65–0.70 s) · **VO:** yes, English, calm conversational male, studio-dry

**Style tags:** kinetic-type, ui-mockup, 3d-render, product-showcase, label-badge, serif-editorial, neon-glow, glow, vignette, frame-in-frame, zoom-through, cursor-ui, search-bar-cta, cta-endcard, grain, arc-carousel*, screen-recording-broll*, strobe-flicker*, blur-in-words*

**One-line essence:** a 30-second "here's how we do it" funnel that alternates three registers (polished 3D/kinetic-type title cards, raw-but-graded screen recordings of the actual workflow, and a fast flip-book of portfolio work), all on near-black with one glowing accent per scene, ending on a bright studio product shot with a comment-keyword CTA.

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** creator-business promo / lead magnet ("comment a keyword, get the free tutorial"). It is an ad for the channel's own course, told as an answer to a viewer question.
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–2.4 **Hook (social proof + question):** "A lot of editors ask us the same question." A crosshair grid with the brand mascot avatar in the centre cell, then an 8 Hz invert strobe on "question".
  - 2.4–5.1 **The question:** "How do you come up with these ideas?" set between two curved rotating arcs of the channel's own thumbnails (proof of output).
  - 5.1–7.4 **The gift:** "So we put together a gift for you guys / We made a tutorial…" A 3D gift box drops onto a spotlit stage, then bursts open into a floating "BEGINNER COURSE" box with orange god-rays.
  - 7.4–19.7 **What's inside (3 steps):** (a) inspiration sources, shown as a Pinterest-style moodboard scroll; (b) feeding references into AI, shown as a chat + image-gen screen recording; (c) bringing it into After Effects, shown as a keying, roto and puppet-rig screen recording.
  - 19.7–23.9 **Promise:** "The kind of motion that turns a scene into something that actually goes viral." A glowing serif "motion" title, then a flip-book of ~10 finished viral pieces in viewfinder brackets, the last one zooming to full screen.
  - 23.9–29.8 **Offer + CTA:** "And the best part, this tutorial is completely free. Comment TUT…" A search-bar-style bar, a cursor clicking an orange search button, the course box rising into frame, and a blur-in caption. Fade to black.
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** f0 is a glowing orange square on black (a 1-frame "ember"). f1 is a full white flash with the square inverted to blue. f2 shows a black frame with four thin white guide lines (2 vertical, 2 horizontal) sliding in from the edges to frame a 120 px orange tile holding the 3D mascot. "A lot of" types word by word to the left of the tile and "editors" to the right. At 1.0 s the grid snaps in 2 frames: the tile scales ~1.6× and the mascot swaps to a sunglasses variant (flow peak 19.7, the biggest motion spike of the opening). A flash, a grid and a character swap all happen in 1 s, with near-zero colour except one orange cell.
- **Script → visual rule:** each VO sentence gets one scene. Abstract nouns ("question", "gift", "tutorial", "magic", "motion", "best part") get designed title cards. Process verbs ("find", "feed into AI", "bring into After Effects") get the real screen recording of that step. Claims ("goes viral") get proof via portfolio footage.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** mixed. (1) Kinetic-type title cards on black with a single neon/glow accent. (2) Pre-rendered 3D product objects (gift box, course box) on a spotlit cyclorama. (3) Raw screen recordings (Pinterest, a French-locale ChatGPT, an image-gen grid, After Effects with Keylight / Motion Tools / Bounce / JerryFlow panels), shown full-bleed and darkened. (4) Portfolio thumbnails as framed cards. The polish comes from grading everything into the same near-black world. Even the screen recordings sit on #020202 with the UI chrome dimmed.
- **Backgrounds & textures:** pure black with a large soft radial glow behind the hero (orange in the hook, amber in the badges scene, blue on "motion"); a green spotlit cyc for the gift (#212e06 → #535b1c, hard vignette); orange radial god-rays for the course reveal; a light-grey studio sweep (#c6c6c6–#e0e0e0) with a heavy corner vignette and fine grain for the CTA. The only bright scene is the last one, which is the payoff.
- **Colour:** near-black base (`#020202`–`#0b0400`) on every scene except the CTA. **One accent per scene:** hook orange `#f1bb70`/`#c45e28` (with a cool inverted twin `#4f90ce` for the strobe); gift green `#535b1c` + ribbon red `#d55635`; course orange `#a13c07`; badge amber `#e0a24a` (palette `#e0af5c`/`#d09c50`); "motion" neon blue ~`#6a9cf0` with a `#1f3a80` glow; Buffett crimson `#6e1618`; CTA orange search chip `#d9782a` on a charcoal bar `#2b2b2b`. Brand orange recurs as the thread (mascot tile → badges → course box → search button).
- **Typography:**
  - **Primary UI sans:** light geometric grotesk (Sora Light / Plus Jakarta Sans Light / Gilroy Light-like), weight 300–400, sentence case, tight letter-spacing (≈ −2%) but **wide word gaps** in the hook ("A   lot   of", ~1.5 em between words). Size: hook words ~5% of frame height in cap-height terms (≈ 40 px at 720 w); the question "How do you come up / with these ideas?" is 2 lines centred, line width ~60% of frame width, white `#f0f0f0` at ~90% opacity.
  - **Editorial italic serif:** high-contrast display italic (Instrument Serif Italic / DM Serif Display Italic / Playfair Italic-like) for the emotional words: "Exactly", "that" (white, ~7% of width) and the hero "motion" (lowercase, **~78% of frame width**, neon blue fill with ~20 px outer glow and a 1 px lighter inner edge). "The Kind" and "of" sit above it in the light sans at ~1/4 its x-height. "of" is pushed far right to make room, so the line is justified to the serif word's width.
  - **Label badges:** words sit on flat amber rectangles (`#e0a24a`, ~160×50 px each, 0 radius, 10–12 px gaps), white light sans inside with a faint dark shadow. They are three separate chips, one per word: "that | breaks | down".
  - **Search-bar CTA:** a charcoal parallelogram bar (~66% of frame width × 60 px, skewed ≈ −6°, 3D drop shadow down-right) with "The   best   part" in white light sans and wide word spacing, plus a separate orange skewed chip holding a white magnifier icon.
  - **CTA caption:** "Comment "TUT" and / we'll send you the link" in dark-grey (`#3a3a3a`) light sans, 2 lines, ~65% width, bottom third.
  - **In-content captions:** the portfolio pieces carry their own type (heavy grotesk "300", serif "her beauty", "One Insult" pill), which shows range.
- **Layout & composition:** strictly centred. Hero type sits at y ≈ 45–50% (hook, badges) or y ≈ 25–30% (carousel scenes, where the thumbnail arcs fill the top and bottom 30%). In the CTA the bar sits at y ≈ 32% with the product box below at y ≈ 45–85% and the caption at y ≈ 88%. Lots of negative black space: the hero rarely exceeds 35% of the frame area except the "motion" word and the product box.
- **Assets:** 3D mascot avatars (Pixar-style cubs/teen in a bear hat), a pre-rendered 3D gift box with an "EARNEDITS" tag, a 3D software-box mockup ("BEGINNER COURSE BUNDLE"), real screen captures, portfolio video thumbnails (Avengers parody, Lay's aisle, Fantastic 4, Richard Sears, Vikings, Listerine, Warren Buffett), a single-line drawing of two face profiles (glowing white stroke), viewfinder corner brackets, and a cursor arrow.
- **Finishing / FX:** large radial glows and heavy vignettes on every dark scene; outer-glow neon on "motion"; soft directional drop shadows in the CTA; fine luminance grain in the grey studio; motion blur on the gift drop and lid burst; a fisheye/bulge warp on the exit from Buffett; a gaussian blur-to-sharp on the CTA caption words.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** ~0.57 real cuts/s, but very uneven. Title cards run 1.5–2.7 s; the screen-recording section runs long (2.3, 4.1, 3.25 s) yet feels fast because of internal jump-cuts every ~0.5 s. The flip-book at 21.3–23.0 hits ~6 swaps/s, the fastest moment, and lands on the word "viral". The CTA slows right down: 5.2 s of almost static layout.
- **Easing signatures (from per_frame.csv):**
  - *Zoom-through (shot 13):* rises over 3 frames to a peak (11.4), then decays geometrically at ×0.6/frame for 5 frames, then a mild negative drift (overshoot) for 5 frames. That is a spring with light damping: `spring({damping: 14, stiffness: 120, mass: 0.8})` or `Easing.bezier(0.2, 0.9, 0.3, 1)` over 10–12 frames at 30 fps.
  - *Product box rise (shot 16):* a symmetric-start, long-tail ease. Velocity ramps 0.08 → 0.52 → 1.03 → 1.77 → 2.61 → 3.73 → 4.72 (6 frames), then falls 4.23 → 2.98 → 2.52 → 1.97 → 1.56 → 1.0 → 0.77 → 0.63 → 0.31 → 0.12 (≈ 12 frames). In 30 fps terms: ~11 frames in, ~22 frames out ≈ `Easing.bezier(0.45, 0, 0.15, 1)` over ~32 frames. No overshoot.
  - *Gift drop (shot 4):* a pre-rendered 3D fall, 4 frames from top of frame to contact (≈ 0.25 s, gravity-like ease-in), a 1-frame squash and then a dead stop.
  - *Course-box exit (shot 5):* a pure ease-in camera tilt (pan_y 1.4 → 4.6 → 8.6 over 3 frames), cut at peak velocity, so the next black frame reads as a whip.
  - *Fade-out:* linear, 8 frames.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:**
  - *Word blur-fade:* each word goes from opacity 0 / blur ~8 px to sharp over ~3 frames (0.19 s), timed to the spoken word. Used for the hook, question, "The Kind of" and the CTA caption. The CTA caption words start visibly blurry (~12 px) and resolve over ~4 frames.
  - *Chip split:* a single amber bar that subdivides into word chips as each word is spoken.
  - *Line-art draw-on:* a single SVG stroke revealed over ~10 frames, with a glow.
  - *Glow bloom:* the hero serif word fades in while its glow radius and saturation grow over ~5 frames.
  - *Snap zoom:* a 2-frame scale jump with a content swap (hook mascot).
  - *Flip-book:* hard-cut card swaps every 2–5 frames inside fixed brackets.
  - *Rise-and-settle:* the product box rises from the bottom with scale.
  - *UI type-on + button fill:* the search bar.
- **Camera:** mostly locked-off. Movement comes from (a) the continuously rotating thumbnail arcs (~14°/s), (b) digital push-ins on screen recordings (the AE zoom to 300–400%), (c) one zoom-through (Buffett) and (d) one accelerating tilt exit (course box). No shakes.
- **Secondary motion:** the arcs keep turning under static type; the viewfinder brackets breathe; the god-rays and particles shimmer behind the course box; the cursor drifts in the CTA; the glow on "motion" pulses slightly.
- **Sync (VO word timestamps vs picture):**
  - Scene cuts land on or just before the sentence: "where" 9.30 / cut 9.31 (+0.01); "and" 11.58 / cut 11.62 (+0.04); "Then" 15.74 / cut 15.69 (−0.05); "The" 19.86 / cut 19.69 (−0.17); "into" 21.52 / flip-book 21.31 (−0.21); "made" 6.74 / lid burst 6.69 (−0.05).
  - Hits land on the keyword: gift box contacts at 5.31 vs "gift" 5.38 (−0.07); invert strobe starts 1.94, inside "question." (1.70–2.08); the zoom-through to full frame at 23.0 vs "viral" 22.96 (+0.04).
  - On-screen words appear within ±0.1 s of the spoken word (How 2.42/2.50, Kind 19.98/19.88, motion 20.28/20.25, Comment 26.88/26.75).
  - Music: cuts are not beat-locked (offsets to the nearest beat range −0.36 to +0.30 s). The edit follows the VO. Only 21.31 (beat 21.36, onset 21.36) and 24.0 (beat 24.01, onset 24.01) coincide with the music; both are section starts (flip-book, CTA).

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Flash-in from ember (0.00–0.12):** 1 frame of an orange glowing square, 1 frame of full white with the inverted square, then the scene. Rebuild: frames 0–1 a solid with a radial-glow square; frame 2 white; cut in. At 30 fps use 2 + 2 frames.
2. **Invert strobe (1.94–2.38):** the scene alternates every 2 frames between its normal and a colour-inverted + hue-rotated version (orange → blue, black → white), 4 swaps, with a small zoom pulse on each. Rebuild: `filter: invert(1) hue-rotate(180deg)` toggled on `Math.floor(frame/4)%2` at 30 fps for 13 frames, with scale 1 → 1.03 on each "on" frame.
3. **Object burst (6.69):** the gift lid explodes upward (1 dark frame), revealing the product. It is a 3D render match, but a 2D rebuild works: the lid sprite translates y −400 px with rotation 25° over 3 frames with motion blur; flash the frame to 20% luma for 1 frame; the new product springs up from below.
4. **Accelerating tilt-out → black (7.12–7.44):** the camera moves up with ease-in and cuts at peak velocity to a near-black scene. It works because the next scene is also dark.
5. **Flip-book inside brackets (21.31–23.0):** the brackets stay as a persistent frame while content hard-swaps; the swap interval shortens from 5 to 2 frames toward the word "viral". Rebuild: an array of clips, durations `[5,3,2,2,2,2,2,1,4,4]` frames at 16 fps (≈ ×1.9 at 30 fps).
6. **Frame-in-frame zoom-through (23.0–23.3):** the last card scales from ~45% width to full-bleed (≈ ×2.3) with a spring ease; the brackets fly out past the edges.
7. **Warp → white → black-with-element (23.88–24.06):** a bulge/fisheye distortion on the outgoing frame (centre magnified ~1.4× with barrel edges), then 1 white frame, then 1 black frame where only the next scene's hero element (the bar) glows white, then the light scene resolves. This works as a "light switch" into the bright CTA. Rebuild: SVG `feDisplacementMap` with a radial map ramp on frame 1, white solid on frame 2, black + a blurred white rect on frame 3.
8. **Hard cuts** between the screen recordings, each aligned to the start of a VO sentence.
9. **Fade to black** (8 frames, linear) at the end.

## 6. Sound design
- A soft, mid-tempo (~90 BPM) cinematic/lo-fi bed at low level under a dry VO (rms 0.13). Strong onsets cluster in the hook (0.07, 0.46, 0.81, 0.95, 1.60), which suggests a riser/whoosh and an impact on the 1.0 s snap-zoom, and in the 20.2–21.4 window (the "motion" bloom and the start of the flip-book, ticks for the card swaps).
- An onset at 24.01 coincides with the white/black flash into the CTA (an impact or "switch" SFX). Onsets at 25.68 and 27.35 sit on the search-button click and the box rise (UI click and whoosh-up).
- The VO never pauses more than ~0.5 s. Natural gaps (2.08–2.42, 4.0–4.28, 19.32–19.86) are where the hard scene changes happen.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **One accent per scene on near-black.** Every dark scene has exactly one saturated hue plus a big radial glow behind the hero. The brand orange is the recurring thread.
2. **Show the real process, but grade it into the brand world.** Raw screen recordings feel premium when they are darkened, full-bleed, jump-cut every ~0.5 s and bracketed by designed title cards.
3. **Light sans for the sentence, a glowing italic serif for the one emotional word.** "motion" is set at ~78% of frame width while the rest of the line stays tiny.
4. **Proof by velocity.** A flip-book of 10 finished pieces in 1.7 s, accelerating into the claim word ("viral"), then one piece zooms to full screen.
5. **Persistent frame, changing content.** Viewfinder brackets (and the hook's crosshair grid) stay still while the content swaps, so rapid swaps read as one deliberate shot.
6. **Light-switch into the CTA.** After 24 s of dark scenes, a white/black flash drops the viewer into a bright studio, which marks the offer as a new section.
7. **Physical product for a digital offer.** The free tutorial becomes a 3D gift box and a retail software box with a shadow, which makes "free" feel valuable.
8. **Long-tail ease on the hero reveal** (~1:2 in:out ratio, no overshoot) for the product; springy overshoot only on the zoom-through.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, ~900 frames (VO length + 0.5 s). Keep all dark scenes at `#030303` with a radial glow layer.
- **Layer stack (dark title card):** `<SolidBG color="#030303">` → `<RadialGlow color={accent} radius="45%" opacity={0.35}>` → `<ArcCarousel>` or `<LineArtDrawOn>` → `<WordBlurIn>` / `<ChipSplitCaption>` / `<GlowSerifWord>` → `<Vignette strength={0.6}>` → `<GrainOverlay opacity={0.05}>`.
- **Components:**
  - `ArcCarousel({cards, radius: 1400, arc: 70deg, speedDegPerSec: 14, position: 'top'|'bottom'})`: place cards on a circle centred far off-screen (`transform: rotate(θ) translateY(-R) rotate(-θ*0.3)`), rotate the parent with `frame * speed / fps`, darken the cards toward the arc ends with a linear-gradient mask.
  - `WordBlurIn({words, timings})`: per word, `opacity: interpolate(f, [t-2, t+4], [0,1])` and `filter: blur(interpolate(f,[t-2,t+4],[10,0])px)`, with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`; start 2 frames before the whisper word start.
  - `ChipSplitCaption({words, color:'#e0a24a'})`: render one full-width bar at t0; at each word time, the bar width animates to the measured chip widths with 12 px gaps (`spring({damping: 20, stiffness: 200})`), and the word fades in inside its chip.
  - `GlowSerifWord({text:'motion', font:'Instrument Serif Italic', color:'#6a9cf0', widthPct: 78})`: opacity 0 → 1 over 5 frames; `text-shadow: 0 0 ${g}px #3d6fe0, 0 0 ${2g}px #1f3a80` with g: 0 → 18 over 8 frames.
  - `LineArtDrawOn({path, durationInFrames: 18})`: SVG `strokeDasharray` / `strokeDashoffset` with `filter: drop-shadow(0 0 6px #fff)`.
  - `InvertStrobe({frames: 13, period: 4})`: CSS `invert(1) hue-rotate(180deg)` toggle.
  - `BracketFlipbook({clips, durations, bracketInset: 24})`: `<Series>` of `<OffthreadVideo>`/`<Img>`, with the bracket corners as a separate layer animated by `scale: 1 + 0.04*Math.sin(f/6)`.
  - `ZoomThrough({from: 0.45, to: 1})`: `spring({damping: 14, stiffness: 120, mass: 0.8})` on scale; the brackets scale 1 → 2.5 and fade.
  - `WarpFlashTransition`: frame 0 `feDisplacementMap` scale 60, frame 1 white, frame 2 black + glow rect, then the next scene.
  - `SearchBarCTA({text, chipColor:'#d9782a', skew:-6})`: a skewed charcoal bar with `box-shadow: 12px 18px 24px rgba(0,0,0,.35)`, words typed per VO, and a chip fading from 30% → 100% opacity over 4 frames when the cursor arrives; `<Cursor path>` with a click scale-pulse 1 → 0.85 → 1 over 4 frames.
  - `ProductRise({src, fromScale: 0.5, fromY: 400})`: `Easing.bezier(0.45, 0, 0.15, 1)` over 32 frames; the other layers translate y −8% with the same curve.
  - `ScreenRecordingPanel({src, playbackRate: 2–4, jumpCuts: [...]})`: darken with `brightness(0.85) contrast(1.1)`, letterbox on black, optional digital push via `scale` keyframes (`Easing.inOut(Easing.cubic)`, 12 frames).
- **Tricky bits:** the 3D gift and product boxes are pre-rendered. In Remotion, use a PNG/WebM with alpha, or `@remotion/three` with a simple box mesh and a spotlight. The arc carousel reads best with a slight `perspective(1200px) rotateX(10deg)` on the parent.
