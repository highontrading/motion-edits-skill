## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** pop-culture trivia micro-documentary ("crazy behind-the-scenes fact → escalation → twist → rhetorical question → CTA"), creator-promo CTA for a project-file shop.
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–4.6 **Hook (cold open with the payoff first):** fake cocaine put him in hospital AND the FBI seized the profits. Two shocking claims in 4.6 s, each with its own image: a hospital exterior, then Jordan holding a dollar bill with FBI agents around him.
  - 5.5–6.9 **Title drop:** "The Wolf of Wall Street" title card behind the actor.
  - 6.9–18.8 **Story 1:** Jonah Hill as the best friend → snorted crushed vitamin B → hospital with severe bronchitis.
  - 19.3–20.3 **Turn:** "But it gets crazier" on a full-screen serif text card.
  - 20.7–29.4 **Story 2:** the film was partly funded with money stolen from Malaysia's state fund → the FBI uncovered it and seized the profits (newspaper proof).
  - 29.4–35.9 **Twist / question:** a movie about illegal money made with illegal money — coincidence, or the ultimate crime?
  - 36.4–41.5 **CTA:** "The editors making work like this…" + phone mockup with the shop URL.
- **Hook mechanics:** frame 0–4 is a blurred cloud close-up. At 0.17 it hard-cuts on the beat to a sky, and the camera **tilts down** at ~38 px/frame onto a hospital façade carrying a huge 3D "FAKE / COCAINE" title, as if it were the building's signage. The first spoken word ("Fake") and the first big type land within 0.5 s. At 0.9 s the camera accelerates into a truck right to reveal a nurse pushing a stretcher.
- **Script → visual rule:** each clause gets its own **staged location** (an AI-generated cinematic scene or a film still) with one graphic that names the noun: a 3D title, a tracked name tag, a torn-paper label, an anatomy callout, a relief map, a newspaper. Abstract turns ("But it gets crazier", "Coincidence… or the ultimate crime?") get **plain textured text cards** with serif type.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** cinematic, footage-led 2.5D. The material is film stills and AI-recreated scenes animated with camera moves: tilts, trucks, push-ins, rack focus, foreground parallax (blurred crowd heads, a viewer's shoulder). The graphics are composited *into* the scenes (tracked, occluded, perspective-matched), not floated on top.
- **Backgrounds & textures:**
  - Scenes: hospital exterior (cyan sky), dark trading-floor office, blue stage, a golden-hour penthouse couch, an orange desk from above, a teal hospital room, a dark gallery with a white wall, a dark wood floor with window-light shadows, a warm living room with a TV.

  - CTA: a light-grey scratched film/acetate plate (`#dfe0de`) with dust, hairline scratches and faint horizontal scan banding.
- **Colour:** each scene owns one dominant temperature, alternating warm and cool every shot: cyan `#35caed` → warm brown `#866449` → navy `#161728`/`#395684` → orange `#c06130` → teal `#186772`/`#399ca4` → navy card → cool grey `#c1c5c7` → oxblood wood `#592513` → warm amber → oxblood card → light grey. Accents: signal red `#d71a24` (FAKE COCAINE, Jordan tag, CTA headline), electric blue `#1f6fe8` (Jonah tag), lemon yellow `#f2d71b` (Vitamin-B paper, newspaper highlighter, film-title yellow `#e3c31b`), terracotta map `#c0582f`. The grade is soft and filmic: lifted blacks, halation/bloom on highlights, a strong vignette (~30% corners) and heavy fine grain on every shot.
- **Typography:**

"FAKE" is white with a chiselled grey bevel; "COCAINE" is red `#d71a24` with a dark-red `#7a0c10` extrude of ~6 px, plus a soft shadow. Perspective-matched to the façade and moving with it.

  - **Name tags:** bold italic geometric sans (Poppins Bold Italic / Gilroy ExtraBold Italic), white, ~5% of frame height, on a solid rectangle (red `#e8202a` for Jordan, blue `#1f6fe8` for Jonah), rotated −3° to −8°, with an inner light sweep and soft outer glow.

- **Assets:**

  - AI-generated photoreal re-creations: hospital exterior, hospital bed and X-ray version, gallery with the movie poster, a 3D relief map of Malaysia, the "Washington Post" front page with a yellow-highlighted headline, a living room with a viewer.
  - UI: a medical anatomy illustration and a phone mockup with a website screen recording.
- **Finishing:** film grain (strong, ~10% opacity), bloom, vignette, depth-of-field foregrounds, motion blur on every camera move, and a stepped ~18 fps cadence in the first 8 s (a stop-motion/AI-video feel). Flash frames are used as transitions.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** ~14 scenes in 41.5 s ≈ 0.34 scene changes/s, but motion never stops. Every shot has a camera move plus at least one graphic event, and graphic beats land every ~0.8–1.2 s. Scene length is 1.5–5.1 s; the text cards are the shortest (1.5 s, 2.9 s).
- **Frame cadence as texture:** the first ~8 s is on 2s/3s (36–41% duplicated frames ≈ 18 fps effective), which gives the AI/film b-roll a stepped, tactile feel. From 8 s on it plays at full 30 fps; the CTA returns to ~23% duplicates.
- **Easing signatures (measured):**
  - **Tilt-down reveal (0.4–0.6 s):** fast start, ease-out: 38 → 24 px/frame over 6 f, with an initial zoom/roll kick (rot +2.2°/f for 2 f).
  - **Truck (0.9–1.3 s):** linear acceleration from 30 to 49 px/frame. These are ease-in camera moves that hand into a transition.
  - **Spin-zoom whip out (7.63–8.00 s):** quadratic ease-in on zoom (+1.65 → +5.2 %/f) and roll (−1.8 → −6.2 °/f) over 11 f, then a cut. The incoming shot decelerates the opposite zoom in ~4 f (−6.9, −4.5, …).
  - **Zoom-through (29.77–30.00 s):** constant-velocity zoom-in for 8 f, then a cut. The incoming shot has a zoom-out ease-out over 8 f (−5.3 → −1.0) with an upward tilt (pan_y up to −2.9/f).
  - **Roll-match (33.3–33.47 s):** roll accelerates −0.8 → −1.6 °/f over 5 f; the new card starts rotated and settles with a long ease-out (~20 f).
  - **Flash transitions:** luma 64 → 134 → 199 → 132 → 63 over 4 frames, a symmetric triangle peaking at ~80% white.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:**
  - Fly-in tag with rotation settle: 11 f, −12° → −4°, motion blur.
  - Bar-to-rectangle tag expand: 5 f, then text de-blur over 6 f.
  - Torn-paper scrap + colour wipe: 2 f + 6 f.
  - Grow-from-chest anatomy overlay: 6 f.
  - Magnifier circle scale-in with ring stroke: 8 f.
  - Line-by-line title slide-in behind the subject: ~8 f per line.
  - Redaction-box → text: ~4 f.
  - Serif blur-in word: ~5 f, 4 px rise.
  - Big keyword pop (CRAZIER): scale 1.08 → 1 over 5 f.
  - Newspaper slide-in with unfold: 13 f.
  - Phone rise: 15 f, ease-out.
  - Dynamic-island → search-bar morph: 8 f.
- **Camera:** always moving, never static. Moves include tilt-down openers (shots 2 and 3), trucks that reveal the second half of a two-part fact (nurse + stretcher; Jordan → Jonah), slow pull-backs that add foreground parallax (FBI agents, crowd, viewer's shoulder), rack focus (gallery), a snap-zoom punch-in (desk) and 3D perspective tilts of graphic planes (map).
- **Secondary motion:** the pink radar pulse ring around Jonah, the label wobble, the TV content changing in the background, newspaper float, the X-ray flicker and the continuous website scroll in the CTA.
- **Sync:**
  - **Cuts vs music (beat ≈ 0.465 s):** 0.17 (+0.03), 5.50 flash (0.00), 11.00 (−0.01), 15.15 portal start (−0.06), 26.8 newspaper landing (−0.06), 28.20 strobe (−0.01), 30.00 (+0.02), 33.47 (−0.04) and 37.9 phone rise (+0.03). About two-thirds of the major events sit within ±2 frames of a beat. The others (8.03, 20.50) follow the VO instead.
  - **Graphics vs VO words:**
    - Map words: 0 to +0.05 s.
    - Serif card words: +0.06…+0.3 s, always after the word.
    - Name tags: Jonah −0.15 s; Jordan's tag lands +0.5 s after his name, because it waits for the cut.
    - Vitamin-B: +0.2 s after "vitamin".
    - Anatomy overlay: +0.1 s after "severe".
    - Invert strobe: +0.1 s after "seized".
    - CTA headline words: +0.3 s on the first words, catching up to −0.15 s by "THIS". The graphics accelerate to meet the VO.
  - **Twist structure:** each story hook is restated visually (hospital, FBI), so the payoff scenes (bed, newspaper) feel like callbacks.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Slide-up cover wipe** at 2.43–2.77: the next scene (dark office, heavily blurred) rises from the bottom over the current one in ~10 f with a stepped cadence, then the camera tilts down inside it. Rebuild: incoming layer `translateY 100% → 0` with `Easing.inOut(Easing.cubic)` over 10 f, `blur(12px → 0)`, rendered on 2s.
2. **White exposure flash** at 5.47 and 25.97: a 4-frame triangle to ~80% white (a cross-dissolve hidden inside overexposure). Rebuild: a white solid with opacity 0 → 0.8 → 0 over 4 f, with the cut underneath at frame 2, plus `brightness(1.6)` on both clips.
3. **Spin-zoom whip** at 8.03: the outgoing shot zooms in and rolls with a quadratic ease-in over 11 f, adding radial/motion blur; the cut happens at peak; the incoming shot counter-rotates and decelerates in 4 f. Rebuild: `scale(1 → 1.6)`, `rotate(0 → -40deg)`, radial blur via a stacked scaled-copies technique; incoming `scale(1.3 → 1)`, `rotate(15deg → 0)`.
4. **Glowing portal / window grow** at 15.15–15.55: a small square inset of the next scene appears at the action point (the straw), surrounded by a white bloom, and scales up to full frame in 12 f. The outgoing scene blows out to white around it. Rebuild: the next scene in a `clipPath inset()` rectangle centred on the point, growing with `Easing.in(Easing.quad)`; a `drop-shadow(0 0 40px white)` glow; the outgoing layer `brightness 1 → 2.5`.
5. **X-ray flash** at 15.70–15.83: a 4-frame swap to an X-ray render of the same composition. Rebuild: a matched second image, hard-swapped for 4 f with a slight glow.
6. **Continuous-world pan** at 22.4–23.1: no cut. The camera trucks past a pillar from the gallery onto a white wall where the map lives, so two story beats share one physical space.
7. **Invert / colour strobe** at 28.20–28.30: 3 frames alternating cyan-negative → sepia → cyan-negative on the impact word. Rebuild: `filter: invert(1) hue-rotate(180deg)` on frames 0 and 2, `sepia(1)` on frame 1.

9. **Roll-match** at 33.47: the outgoing camera roll accelerates clockwise; the incoming text card starts rotated ~30° and rolls back to ~10° over ~20 f. The rotation reads as one continuous move.
10. **Hard cuts to text cards** (19.00, 36.33): a hard cut from a busy photographic scene to a flat textured card, where the type does the work.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Graphics live in the world.** Pin titles to buildings, track name tags to chests, put the title behind the actor's head, place the map on a gallery wall, and let a newspaper land on the floor. Integration (tracking, occlusion, perspective) beats overlay.
2. **Every scene is a camera move.** Tilt-down openers, trucks to reveal the second half of a fact, pull-backs with blurred foreground parallax, rack focus. Stills never sit still.
3. **Alternate warm and cool scenes** and give each one a single dominant hue. Accent graphics use red/blue/yellow so they always pop against the grade.
4. **Use flat textured text cards for narrative turns** ("But it gets crazier", the rhetorical question). The sudden drop in visual complexity tells the viewer a turn is coming.
5. **Match graphic style to meaning:** 3D bevel title for the sensational hook, TV-lower-third tags for people, torn paper for the "fake" substance, a medical magnifier for the diagnosis, redaction boxes for stolen money, an editorial serif for the moral question.
6. **Make transitions physical and motivated:** a portal opening at the straw, an X-ray at "hospital", a camera-flash strobe at "seized", a roll continuing into the next card. Each transition matches the story event it sits on.
7. **Land big events on the beat** (flashes, strobes, cuts within ±2 f) and let small type follow the VO by 0.1–0.3 s.
8. **Film finishing on everything** (grain, bloom, vignette, DoF, a stepped cadence in the hook) makes mixed-source and AI imagery feel like one film.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, duration = VO + 0.4 s (≈ 1245 f). Optionally render the hook section on 2s (`Math.floor(frame/2)*2`) for the stepped feel.
- **Scene stack (bottom → top):** `<KenBurnsPlate src move={{tilt, truck, zoom, roll}} dof>` (image or `<OffthreadVideo>`) → `<TrackedGraphic track={keyframes}>` (follows the plate transform) → `<ForegroundParallax src blur={16}>` (optional) → `<FilmFinish grain={0.1} vignette={0.3} bloom>`.
- **Components:**
  - `CameraPlate`: wraps a still in a scaled container (1.3×) and animates `translate/scale/rotate` with `interpolate` + `Easing`. Opening tilt: `translateY` over 6 f with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`; truck: `Easing.in(Easing.quad)`.
  - `Pinned3DTitle({lines, extrude: 6, colors})`: CSS `transform: perspective(900px) rotateY(-8deg)`, a stacked `text-shadow` extrude, and a bevel via `background-clip:text` with a linear-gradient. Parent it inside the `CameraPlate` so it moves with the façade.
  - `TextBehindSubject`: three layers (plate, title, roto matte of the subject as a PNG/alpha video on top). The title slides in per line with `translateX(-60% → 0)` over 8 f, `Easing.out(Easing.exp)`.
  - `NameTag({text, color, rotate: -4, mode: 'fly'|'expand'})`:
    - `fly`: `translateX(-200px → 0)`, `rotate(-12 → -4)`, `spring({damping: 14, stiffness: 160, mass: 0.6})`.
    - `expand`: `scaleX(0.02 → 1)` over 5 f, then text `blur(8 → 0)` over 6 f.
    - Inner light sweep: a moving linear-gradient overlay.
  - `RadarPulse({cx, cy, color: '#ff4d8d'})`: 2 concentric rings, scale 0.6 → 1.4 and opacity 0.6 → 0 over 18 f, looping.
  - `TornPaperLabel({text})`: a white SVG torn path translateX in from the right over 2 f, then a yellow fill `clipPath inset(0 100% 0 0 → 0)` over 6 f.
  - `PortalTransition({point, dur: 12})`: the incoming scene in a growing `clipPath: inset()` rectangle centred on `point`, a white glow and an outgoing `brightness()` ramp.
  - `FlashCut({dur: 4, peak: 0.8})`, `InvertStrobe({frames: ['invert','sepia','invert']})`.
  - `SpinZoomWhip({outFrames: 11, inFrames: 4, maxScale: 1.6, maxRot: -40})`.
  - `RollMatch({deg: 30, settle: 20})`.
  - `AnatomyCallout`: an overlay PNG that scales 0.6 → 1 + opacity over 6 f with `mix-blend-mode: screen`; a `Magnifier` circle (`clipPath: circle()` of a zoomed image, white 2 px ring, scale 0 → 1 over 8 f with `spring({damping: 12, stiffness: 200})`).
  - `RedactionWord({word, at})`: a dark rectangle (`#2b2e31`) for 2 f, then cross-fades to the text with `blur(4 → 1px)`. Place the words inside a CSS 3D plane with the map so `rotateX/rotateY` tilts them together.
  - `SerifWordCard({words, bg: 'navyPlaid'|'oxbloodPlaid', italic})`: one word at a time (replace or append), blur-in 5 f, 4 px rise; giant keyword pop `scale 1.08 → 1`.
  - `PhoneCTA`: the headline built word by word (`blur(10 → 0)`, opacity, 6 f); the phone `translateY(110% → 35%)` over 15 f with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`; the dynamic island morphs to a search pill (`width 30% → 90%`, `height 3% → 5%`, 8 f) and types the URL.
- **Tricky bits:**
  - **Tracking:** for still plates, a graphic placed inside the same transformed container is "tracked" for free. For real video, use keyframed positions from an AE/Mocha export (JSON) or Remotion `interpolate` over hand-picked keyframes.
  - **Roto behind-subject:** pre-cut a matte (e.g. SAM/rembg) and render it as the top layer.
