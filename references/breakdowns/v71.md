## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** spec/brand commercial (unofficial Durex ad) built as a motion-design showcase: problem → product → benefit → tagline → logo.
- **Script (reconstructed from on-screen type; the Whisper transcript is garbled after 7 s):** "Things can get messy. Sometimes being close is a risk that can end up in [various problems]. What if you could change that? [Durex] Protection is key! A simple solution for your needs. Release your inner animal. [Durex] Play it safe."
- **Beats:**
  - 0.00–2.83 **Hook:** "Things can get messy", typed on white, flipped to black, then MESSY spelled huge among flying 3D product stands.
  - 2.83–7.40 **Problem:** "Sometimes being close / is a Risk / that can end up in…" plus an emoji rebus (baby, angry face, money with wings, pregnant woman) to show the consequences without saying them.
  - 7.40–8.73 **Turn:** "What if you could change that?"
  - 8.73–10.10 **First brand hit:** a blue liquid fill washes up and the Durex wordmark settles out of the liquid.
  - 10.10–14.93 **Benefits:** "Protection is key!" (ring of orbiting condom wrappers) → "A Simple" (blue glow) → "Solution for your needs" → "Release your inner…"
  - 15.0–16.0 **Payoff gag:** "Animal" with tiger emoji.
  - 16.0–19.97 **Endcard:** the real Durex pill logo on royal blue with ripple rings → "Play it Safe" → fade to black (2.1 s).
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** frame 0 is already busy, with two blurred condom wrappers drifting and rotating in (zoom −1.9/frame decaying to 0 by f8). "Things" types on behind an underscore caret at 0.07, "can" and a blue iOS-style caret follow at 0.30–0.47, and "get" lands with a 2-frame horizontal squash at 0.50. At 0.567 the whole frame **inverts to black** (luma 237→18 in one frame) while the layout stays put. A yellow/blue flash-glitch at 1.37 throws you into MESSY. That makes four visual events in 1.5 s, all using one 18-px-high line of type.
- **Script → visual rule:** each clause is its own scene, and one word per scene gets a treatment (colour sweep, giant scale, letter-by-letter spelling, emoji). Nouns that are awkward to show (pregnancy, money, anger) become **Apple emoji**, which keeps a sensitive brand light and literal.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** Apple-keynote minimalism (white or black field, one centred line of system-font type) mixed with a few **3D product renders** (condom wrappers, Durex retail display stands) and **Apple emoji as illustration**. There is no texture and no grain. "Premium" comes from glow, motion blur and transitions.
- **Backgrounds:** flat off-white `#f3f5f2`/`#f9fafa` ↔ pure black `#000000`, alternating almost every scene (white 0–0.57, black 0.57–2.83, white 2.83–8.73, liquid blue 8.73–10.1, white→black fade 10.2–11.5, white 11.5–14.0, black 14.0–16.0, blue 16.0–19.5). There are three exceptions: a **blue→white radial gradient** behind the first logo (`#455efd` corners → `#dce0fc` centre), a **linear luma fade from white to black** behind "Protection is key!" (luma 239→7 over 31 frames, ≈−7.5/frame, so the scene is a fade, not a cut), and the **flat royal blue `#0049ac`** endcard, which fades linearly to black over 63 frames (17.5→19.6).
- **Colour:** neutrals carry 85–97% of every frame. The accents are one hue per keyword. **Red-orange `#fc3819`** for "Risk" and the "S" of "Sometimes" (with soft red glow), **electric blue `#148ef9`** for "Simple", **brand blues** `#4c6afd` (liquid) and `#0049ac` (endcard), and multicolour wrappers (yellow, teal, purple, pink) in the "Protection" ring. In the invert strobe the blue "Simple" becomes its complement, orange `#eb7106` with an orange glow on black.
- **Typography:**
  - A single family throughout: **SF Pro Rounded** (rounded terminals visible on R, k, y, f in key frames 10/19/23), Semibold–Bold, sentence case. MESSY is the only all-caps word.
  - Scale range: body lines ("Things can get", "That can end up in", "Solution for your needs") are small, with cap height ≈2.5–3.5% of frame height and a line ≈18–35% of frame width. Keyword scenes jump to **giant**: "A Simple" ≈85% of width (cap height ≈27% of height), "…s a Risk" bleeds off the left edge (~65% visible), and "Play it Safe" is ≈50% of width.
  - Colour: black `#000000`-ish on white, white `#ffffff` on black, white on blue. The endcard text dims to light blue (`#a6c4ec`, ~60% opacity) before vanishing.
  - **Glow:** keyword glows are wide Gaussian halos (radius ≈ 0.8× cap height, opacity ~60%) in the keyword's own colour. MESSY gets a white glow plus a **giant blurred echo**: the same word at ~3.5× scale, grey `#3b3e3f`, blur ~12 px, sitting behind it and cropped by the frame.
  - Emphasis rules: only one word per scene is coloured, and the colour arrives by a sweep (see §4). Everything else stays neutral.
- **Layout:** the type line is always centred on the frame centre (y ≈ 50%) except where it intentionally drifts (the "Release your inner" line slides from centred to left-third; "What if…" drops ~10% lower before the liquid wipe). Props sit in symmetrical arrangements around the text: wrappers top and bottom (shot 1), four stands at the compass points (MESSY), an 8-item ring (Protection), and tigers at opposite corners (Animal).
- **Assets:** 3D condom wrappers (plain silver with blue logo; multicolour patterned versions), 3D Durex retail display stands ("World's No.1 condom brand"), Apple emoji (😣, 💦, 👶, 😠, 💸, 🤰, 🐅, 🐯), the black outline Durex wordmark, and the official white-pill Durex logo.
- **Finishing / FX:** colour glows, heavy directional motion blur on every fast move, a liquid displacement wobble on the logo, and pixelation/mosaic on the "Protection" exit. No grain, vignette or shadows except a soft dark shadow under the outline wordmark.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** 15 scenes in 17.5 s of content (≈0.86 scenes/s; avg 1.15 s, range 0.57–1.43 s). Then a 3 s endcard and fade. Pacing is almost metronomic: nearly every scene is 0.9–1.4 s, about two beats at 103 BPM (2 × 0.58 = 1.16 s).
- **Easing signatures:**
  - **Prop entrances** are exponential ease-outs of ~6 frames (wrappers: zoom −1.87 → −0.05 by ×0.55/frame; emoji converge: 6 frames to peak and 6 to settle). Neither shows overshoot (no sign flips in zoom or rot).
  - **Camera roll and pull-back on MESSY** is a long ease-out of ~16 frames (zoom −0.72 → −0.22/frame) with roll up to −1.6°/frame.
  - **Type entrances** are either blur-ins (blur ~10 px → 0 and opacity 0 → 1 over 4–8 frames) or 1–3-frame motion-blurred slides. Nothing springs or bounces. The type is calm and the transitions are violent.
  - **Colour sweeps** take 12 frames (Risk, fast ease-out) and 20 frames (Simple, near-linear), both moving right-to-left.
  - **Transitions** are 1–5 frames with single-frame peaks: zoom-snap at 3.70 (flow 27.7 ≈ 196 px/frame at full res), spin at 10.13 (rot 48.8° in one frame, flow 65 ≈ 460 px/frame), strobe at 12.53 (flow 39 alternating).
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:** underscore-caret typewriter (0.07), blue block caret (0.37), squash-pop (0.50, 2 frames), invert (1 frame), letter-by-letter spelling (MESSY, about every 5 frames; Animal, about every 3 frames), leading-edge red letters (Sometimes), word blur-in (4–8 frames), motion-blur slide-in (Simple, 3 frames), fly-in plus tracking-relax (Solution…, ~25 frames), slide-to-left exit (Release…, ~8 frames), emoji burst-and-converge (12 frames), ring orbit (continuous), logo scale-up plus ripple rings (10 frames), dim-out (8 frames).
- **Camera:** essentially locked. The only "camera" moves are the MESSY roll and pull-back (−15° roll), the zoom-snap at 3.70 and the slow push in "That can end up in" (+0.6/frame). Everything else is object motion.
- **Secondary motion:** continuous 3D rotation of the stands and wrappers, the ring orbit, emoji float, ripple rings around the endcard logo, and the liquid wobble on the wordmark.
- **Sync:**
  - **Beats (103 BPM, 0.58 s period):** 7 of ~17 transitions land within ±0.1 s of a beat: the glitch into MESSY 1.367 (beat 1.370, −3 ms), zoom-snap 3.70 (beat/strong onset 3.738, −38 ms), cut 4.933 (beat 4.923, +10 ms), invert-flash 7.30 (beat 7.268, +32 ms), spin 10.10 (beat 10.194, −94 ms), invert strobe 12.533 (beat + strong onset 12.539, −6 ms), and logo 16.0 (beat + strong onset 16.068, −68 ms). The others fall roughly on off-beats (+0.13 to +0.28 s), which is consistent with cutting on half-beats.
  - **VO:** first-sentence word times are reliable: "Things" 0.00 → on screen 0.07 (+0.07); "can" 0.38 → 0.30 (−0.08); "get" 0.64 → popped 0.50, settled 0.70; "close" 3.18 → 3.20. Keywords **lead** the word: "Risk" giant at 3.70 vs. spoken 4.12 (−0.42 s), which gives the colour sweep time to land on the word. MESSY spells out 1.53–2.07, after "messy" (0.86–1.62), so the giant keyword acts as an echo. After 7 s the Whisper timestamps are unusable (it misheard the VO), so later sync can't be measured. The tagline appears at 17.0 and the last transcribed word ("safe") is at 17.41–17.83, so the on-screen tagline leads by ~0.4 s.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Hard invert (same layout)** (0.567): a 1-frame swap from black-on-white to white-on-black with the props kept in place. Rebuild: a `<Sequence>` switch of the theme, with no animation.
2. **Flash-glitch colour frames** (1.37–1.50, 4 frames): yellow-on-white → black with a blue light streak → solid blue → blue with dark blocks, then the new scene. Rebuild: four 1-frame `<AbsoluteFill>`s with pre-designed colour plates.
3. **Drop-frame + smear exit** (2.73–2.83, 3 frames): 1 frame with the hero text removed, 1 frame normal, 1 frame with `blur(24px 0)` horizontal smear, then cut.
4. **Zoom-snap into giant type** (3.70, 1 frame + 2-frame settle): the small line is scaled ×4–5 in one frame with motion blur, then settles. Rebuild: interpolate scale 1 → 4.5 over frames [0,1] plus a radial/zoom blur on that frame.
5. **Block-censor wipe** (5.70–5.87, 5 frames): each word is replaced in turn by a solid rectangle the size of its bounding box (red `#fc3819`, black, blue `#148ef9`), then one black bar covers the line, then the bar collapses horizontally to a sliver and vanishes. Rebuild: measure word boxes, render `<div>` blocks with 1-frame stagger, then `scaleX 1→0.05` over 2 frames.
6. **Invert-flash + desat + split** (7.30–7.40, 3 frames): CSS `filter: invert(1)` for 1 frame → `grayscale(1)` for 1 frame → horizontal blur split (two copies offset ±60 px with blur) → blank.
7. **Liquid fill wipe** (8.53–8.73, 6 frames) **+ liquid logo emergence** (8.73–8.87): a blue SVG wave path (2–3 sine bumps, amplitude ~4% of height) whose y animates from 100% to −10%; the prior text gets a vertical displacement smear; the logo enters through `feTurbulence` + `feDisplacementMap` with scale 40 → 0 over 4 frames.
8. **Shape-flash + spin** (10.10–10.23, 4 frames): a black square → a white horizontal bar → a pale-blue bar → a diagonal split, with a 48° rotation in one frame. Rebuild: four 1-frame shape plates, the middle one rotated, with the outgoing logo motion-blurred.
9. **Mosaic glitch out** (11.43–11.53, 3 frames): RGB offset → pixelation (`image-rendering: pixelated` on a 1/16-scale render, upscaled) → cut.
10. **Invert strobe on the beat** (12.53–12.67, 5 frames): alternate `invert(1)` every frame (the blue glow becomes its complementary orange), landing exactly on the strong onset at 12.539.
11. **Black slab + smear** (13.93–14.00, 3 frames): a black rectangle covers the lower half, the text smears horizontally, then full black.
12. **Linear luma fades** used as scene "lighting" (10.37–11.40 white→black under live content; 17.5–19.6 endcard to black).

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **One typeface, one line, one coloured word.** SF Pro Rounded only. Each scene shows one short phrase, and only its keyword gets colour and glow. That restraint lets the transitions carry the energy.
2. **Glow as a highlighter.** The keyword colour fills the word by a 12–20-frame sweep, and the glow halo is wide (≈0.8× cap height) and soft. It reads as "lit from inside" rather than as a text-shadow.
3. **Transitions are 1–5 frame events, never crossfades.** Invert, strobe, block-censor, liquid fill, shape-flash spin and mosaic. Each is used once, so the viewer never sees the same trick twice.
4. **Alternate white and black scenes** to create rhythm without changing the layout. The hard invert at 0.57 shows the trick in its purest form.
5. **Emoji as a polite rebus** for sensitive or abstract consequences (baby, money with wings, angry face). They are cheap, instantly legible and on-brand for social.
6. **Symmetric prop orbits** (ring of 8 wrappers, 4 stands at compass points, 2 tigers in opposite corners) frame the centred type and add 3D motion without any camera move.
7. **Scenes ≈ 2 beats long, with major transitions on beats or strong onsets** (7 of 17 within ±0.1 s).
8. **The endcard decelerates:** ripple-ring logo → tagline → a long linear 2 s fade to black. The calm ending contrasts with the busy body.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1920×1080`, `30 fps`, ~600 frames (20 s). Also works at `1080×1920` if the type scale is changed from frame width to frame height.
- **Font:** `SF Pro Rounded` Semibold/Bold (fallbacks: `Nunito` 700/800, `Varela Round`, `M PLUS Rounded 1c` 700). Body line `fontSize: 0.03 * height`; keyword scenes `0.27 * height`.
- **Scene stack:** `<ThemeFill color="#f3f5f2"|"#000">` → `<PropOrbit items radius rotationSpeed>` (3D renders/PNGs, with `rotateY` driven by frame for faux-3D) → `<SentenceLine words timing>` → `<KeywordSweep>` → `<TransitionPlate>` (1–5-frame overlays).
- **Components:**
  - `<BlurInWord delay>`: opacity `interpolate(f,[0,6],[0,1])`, `filter: blur(${interpolate(f,[0,6],[10,0])}px)`, ease `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`.
  - `<TypewriterCaret variant="underscore"|"block">`: one char per 1–2 frames, caret colour `#148ef9`, blink every 6 frames.
  - `<LetterSpell text stagger={5}>`: per-letter blur-in plus a white glow `text-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(255,255,255,.7)`, and a `<GhostEcho scale={3.5} blur={12} color="#3b3e3f">` behind.
  - `<KeywordSweep color="#fc3819" frames={12} direction="rtl">`: two stacked copies of the word; the coloured copy is clipped with `clipPath: inset(0 0 0 ${100 - p}%)` running right-to-left; glow = `filter: drop-shadow(0 0 30px color) drop-shadow(0 0 60px color)` at 60% opacity. Use `Easing.out(Easing.quad)` for 12 frames, or linear for 20.
  - `<ZoomSnap from={1} to={4.5} frames={1}>` plus `<MotionBlur>` (Remotion `@remotion/motion-blur` `<CameraMotionBlur samples={8} shutterAngle={360}>`).
  - `<BlockCensor words colors={["#fc3819","#000","#148ef9"]} stagger={1}>`, then a full-line bar `scaleX 1→0.05` over 2 frames.
  - `<InvertStrobe start frames={5}>`: `filter: f % 2 ? 'invert(1) hue-rotate(180deg)' : 'none'`. The hue-rotate keeps the "complementary" orange look.
  - `<LiquidFill color="#3e5cf5" frames={6}>`: SVG path `M0,y Q…` with 3 sine bumps whose phase shifts 20°/frame; y eases `Easing.in(Easing.quad)` 100%→−10%.
  - `<LiquidReveal>` for the logo: `<feTurbulence baseFrequency="0.02 0.06" numOctaves={2}>` + `<feDisplacementMap scale={interpolate(f,[0,4],[40,0])}>`.
  - `<EmojiConverge items>`: start at the frame edges at scale 1.8, `spring({damping: 20, stiffness: 160, mass: 0.6})` to cluster positions (≈12 frames, no overshoot), plus ±3° idle rotation.
  - `<RippleLogo>`: logo `spring({damping: 18, stiffness: 140})` scale 0.6→1 (≈10 frames); 3 rings drawn as SVG paths with a small `feTurbulence` wobble, scale 0.8→1.6 and opacity 1→0 over 25 frames, staggered 4 frames.
  - `<LumaFade from to frames>`: linear background colour interpolation (31 frames white→black under the Protection ring; 63 frames blue→black at the end).

## 9. Variation ideas
1. **Fintech or insurance spec ad** ("Markets get messy… Protection is key"): the same white/black alternation, with the keyword sweep in green for gains and red for risk, coins or cards as the orbiting props, and the brand mark rising from a liquid "money" fill.
2. **App launch teaser:** replace emoji and 3D props with app icons and UI chips; keep the one-line SF Pro sentences, the blue caret typewriter, the invert strobe on the drop and a ripple-ring app-icon endcard.
3. **Health or PSA vertical (9:16):** the same script grammar (problem → "What if you could change that?" → solution → tagline), emoji as the rebus for sensitive topics, giant keyword scenes rotated to fill vertical height, and a slower 90 BPM bed with transitions only on downbeats.
