**One-line essence:** a historical story told through photoreal AI-generated "diorama" stills, each given a real 2.5D camera move, and stitched together almost entirely with **circular iris/portal transitions that grow out of a point already in the picture** (a ceiling lamp, a red dot, joined palms, a glowing chart endpoint). ## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** history micro-documentary / "forgotten hero" story with a moral, then a creator-product CTA.
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–3.0 **Hook:** "Doctors were unknowingly killing their own patients." A storm-lit hospital with blood-red windows, then a surgeon at work.
  - 3.0–7.7 **Setup:** "until one man discovered a simple habit that could save lives." A bright sky and field (tonal flip from dread to hope), the hero's portrait card, then a panning type line.
  - 7.4–12.7 **Character + context:** name card "Ignaz Semmelweis", then "1840s", an archival photo of women, "dying from childbirth fever".
  - 12.7–20.2 **Problem:** a chart line climbs, then a doctor walks from the "Autopsy Room" door to the "Patient Room" door, without washing his hands.
  - 20.5–25.0 **Turn:** handwashing at a sink, then "the death rate dropped dramatically" (falling green area chart).
  - 25.2–27.0 **Conflict:** "doctors rejected his idea" on a clipboard.
  - 27.0–35.4 **Payoff/vindication:** a newspaper reads "Scientific evidence confirms microbes cause disease", a microscope view of bacteria, then the moral one word at a time, "…clean hands could save lives."
  - 35.5–40.9 **CTA:** "THE EDITORS MAKING WORK LIKE THIS", a laptop showing the site, and a search bar typing "EarnEdits.com".
- **Hook mechanics:** frame 0 is an extreme close-up of a glowing red-cross sign against storm clouds. Over the first 1.4 s the camera pulls back and counter-rotates (net rot −25°, pan_y −58) to reveal the whole hospital with red-lit windows, and an ambulance drives in at 1.0 s. At 1.73 s it is already zooming through a window. That gives three camera events in under 2 s, and the red-on-navy palette signals danger before any word lands.
- **Script → visual rule:** one diorama per sentence. The scene is a **literal staging** of the sentence: "going from autopsies to patients" is a man walking between two labelled doors, and "rejected his idea" is the phrase written on a clipboard. Only some sentences get on-screen type, and when they do it is the full clause, not one keyword.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** ~0.42 transitions/s; scenes are 1.0–5.0 s, median ~2.5 s. That is slow for the channel, and it works because the camera never stops moving: mean flow stays 1.2–2.9 px/f analysis in every narrative scene. Only the final moral (shot 15, mean 0.56) and CTA (0.66) are calm. Pace is fastest in the hook and setup (≈1.2 s per visual change) and slows into the reflective moral.
- **Easing signatures (measured):**
  - Opening pull-back: velocity 0 → peak in **14 f**, then an exponential tail of **~27 f** (velocity halves roughly every 6–7 f). Approximate as `Easing.bezier(0.3, 0, 0.1, 1)` over ~40 f.
  - Zoom-through push: accelerates over **~9 f** (1.43→1.73 s), peaks, then decays over **~15 f** to a slow drift. Approximate as `bezier(0.6, 0, 0.2, 1)`.
  - Type-line truck: a long symmetric ease-in (36 f) → hold → ease-out (42 f), i.e. `bezier(0.45, 0, 0.55, 1)` across ~3 s.
  - Exit spin (6 → 7) and whip (12 → 13): **pure ease-in over 10–13 f** right up to the cut. The incoming shot continues the same motion and decays over 15–25 f. This is the classic "ease-in out of A / ease-out into B" match.
  - Prop drops (photo, clipboard, newspaper): ease-out over 15–25 f with rotation settling the last 3–5°. There is no visible overshoot; this channel's props land softly, not springy.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:** flutter-in paper card with curl (~12 f); slide-in with rotation settle (15–25 f); drop-in from above (photo, ~25 f); tape-strip stick (~4 f); focus-pull word reveal at frame edge (~6 f); blur-pop word reveal (~4–5 f); typewriter (21–42 char/s); underline grow tied to the typed text; chart line draw-on (2.7 s); highlighter sweep (~16 f per line); single-word replace (cut + 3 f fade); hand slide-up (~30 f); pill → bar stretch (~8 f); zoom-blur text exit (~6 f).
- **Camera:** every still has a move. Pulls with rotation (shot 1: zoom +102 net, rot −25°), zoom-through (shot 2), long trucks (shot 5: pan_x −272; shot 9: −148), a follow-the-pen track (shot 8), rack focus (shots 7, 10), and slow push-ins of +1–3%/s on holds. Rotation is used a lot (net rot −46 to +39 per shot), giving a hand-held "tabletop camera" feel.
- **Secondary motion:** the sheen sweep on the portrait, the ambulance driving, dust specks in the slate light beam, the shifting window shadow on the grid paper, the website auto-scroll in the laptop, and the walk cycle.
- **Frame rate trick:** the 3.2–6.7 s sky/type section has every other frame duplicated (diff ≈ 0), i.e. it is **animated on 2s (15 fps)** inside a 30 fps timeline. This is likely a precomp with a lower frame rate, and it gives the type pan a slightly tactile, stop-motion texture.
- **Sync:**
  - **Hard cuts are beat-locked:** 10.00 (+0.015 s from beat), 12.50 (+0.008), 20.50 (−0.003), 32.50 (−0.008). All are within 1 frame of a beat at 123 BPM. Soft transitions (irises, whips) are placed on the VO instead and drift ±0.1–0.22 s off the grid.
  - **Cuts vs VO:** hard cuts lead or meet the phrase ("1840s" cut 0.22 s before the word; "So…" sink cut 0.04 s before; CTA cut 0.20 s before "The"). The iris into "the death rate" completes at 23.33 while "death" is spoken at 23.06–23.34. The lens closes on "bacteria" (31.34) at 31.27.
  - **Type trails the VO:** the sky line words arrive ~0.5–0.7 s after they are spoken (because their reveal is driven by the camera truck); typewriter text trails by 0.3–0.45 s; clipboard words trail by 0.57 → 0.45 → 0.14 → 0.09 s (catching up); single moral words trail by 0.10–0.25 s; CTA words trail by ~0.1–0.3 s. This is the opposite of v05: type confirms what was just heard rather than anticipating it.
  - Visual actions land on their noun: the hand rises on "hands" (33.74), the man reaches the "Patient Room" door on "patients" (18.18), and the highlighter starts ~0.2 s before "germ theory" (28.22).

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Generate the scene, then shoot it.** AI-photoreal stills only look premium because each gets a deliberate camera move (pull + rotate, truck, rack focus) plus one or two separately animated props. A still is never left static.
2. **Transitions grow from something already in frame.** Every iris starts at a meaningful point (lamp, dot, palms, chart endpoint), so the cut reads as the story "opening a window" rather than an effect.
3. **A unified print finish:** a halftone screen + grain + soft vignette over all scenes makes mismatched sources (AI renders, archival photos, UI, charts) feel like one printed object.
4. **The desk as evidence board:** grid paper, aged photo cards, tape, clipboards and newspapers make data and history feel tangible. Props land tilted 5–15° with soft shadows.
5. **Literal staging of the sentence** (a man walking from the "Autopsy Room" door to the "Patient Room" door) explains the mechanism faster than any caption.
6. **Colour encodes valence:** red = death/rejection, green = improvement, with a sky-blue palette flip at "until one man" marking the turn.
7. **Hard cuts sit exactly on beats; soft transitions follow the VO.** The edit feels musical without looking chopped.
8. **Slow the type down for the moral.** Rapid caption pops early on, then one serif-italic word at a time in a spotlight for the closing line, so the takeaway gets a ceremonial beat.
9. **Typography family per register:** heavy rounded caps for narrative claims, typewriter mono for statistics, serif italic for dates and the moral.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, duration = VO + 0.5 s (≈ 1230 f for 41 s).
- **Typical scene stack (bottom → top):** `<StillScene src camera={{zoom:[1,1.12], rot:[-6,0], x, y}} focus={[blurStart, 0]}>` → animated props (`<PropDrop>`, `<CutoutWalker>`) → caption layer → `<HalftoneOverlay pitch={4} opacity={0.18}>` → `<GrainOverlay opacity={0.07}>` → `<Vignette strength={0.35}>`.
- **Components:**
  - `KenBurns3`: interpolates scale, rotateZ, translate and blur with `Easing.bezier(0.3,0,0.1,1)` for ~40-f settles; for long trucks use `bezier(0.45,0,0.55,1)`.
  - `IrisTransition({originX, originY, from:0.02, to:1.5, frames:10, bloom:true, spin:0})`: next scene in `clipPath: circle(${r*100}% at ${x}% ${y}%)`, r driven by `interpolate(f,[0,10],[from,to],{easing: Easing.in(Easing.cubic)})`; optional bloom ring = a radial-gradient div with a transparent centre at radius r and white at r..r+0.15, `filter: blur(30px)`; `spin` rotates the outgoing scene by up to 40° over the same frames.
  - `SpinBlurCut`: A rotates `interpolate(f,[0,13],[0,25],{easing:Easing.in(Easing.quad)})`° with blur 0→12 px; B starts at −15° and 12 px blur and settles over 15 f with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`.
  - `WhipVertical`: A translateY 0→+60% over 10 f (`Easing.in(Easing.cubic)`), SVG `feGaussianBlur stdDeviation="0 24"`; B enters from −40% → 0 over 12 f ease-out.
  - `PropDrop({from:{x:-0.3,y:-0.4,rot:-25,scale:1.3}, to:{rot:-8,scale:1}, frames:22})` with `spring({damping: 26, stiffness: 120, mass: 1})` (critically damped, no overshoot) + `box-shadow: 0 12px 30px rgba(0,0,0,.35)`.
  - `FocusInWord`: for words along a long line, opacity 0→1 and blur 10→0 px over 6 f, triggered when the word's x crosses 85% of the frame width during the camera truck.
  - `BlurPopWord`: opacity 0→1, blur 8→0, scale 1.06→1 over 5 f; trigger = word start + 4 f (this video's type trails the VO).
  - `Typewriter({text, cps:42|21, caret:false})` + `UnderlineGrow` whose width = measured width of the typed substring.
  - `ChartDrawOn`: SVG path `strokeDashoffset` over 80 f, with the camera (parent transform) tracking the path head: translate = −(head − frameCenter) × 0.6.
  - `HighlighterSweep`: per line, a yellow `#f7c21a` rect with `mix-blend-mode: multiply` scaling X 0→1 from the left over 16 f, lines staggered.
  - `SingleWordSpotlight`: one word visible at a time (cut on at word.start + 3 f, 3 f fade), over a background with a radial/conic light-cone mask.
  - `SearchBarCTA`: pill width 15% → 70% over 8 f with `spring({damping: 18, stiffness: 200})`, then a typewriter at 16 cps with key-click SFX.
- **Tricky bits:** for the on-2s look, wrap the section in `<Freeze>`-style frame quantisation: `const f2 = Math.floor(frame/2)*2` and drive all of that section's animations with `f2`. For the halftone, use a full-frame SVG `<pattern>` of dots at 4 px pitch in `mix-blend-mode: overlay` at 15–20% opacity, or a pre-rendered PNG tile. For the walking figure, use a looped PNG sequence or video with alpha, kept centred while the background layer translates X at constant speed (`-148/180*1080 ≈ -890 px` over 5 s).
