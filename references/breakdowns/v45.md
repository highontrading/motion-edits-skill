**One-line essence:** a news story told as a relay of fully different "worlds" (logo stage, article page, stage footage, blueprint, cutting mat, AI-render dioramas, slot machine, cream end card), held together by one thin white word-by-word caption and one exit move: every scene accelerates into the camera and hands over on a 1–3-frame flash.

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** business/tech news micro-explainer ("deal headline → numbers → what the company is → who uses it → valuation logic → thesis → CTA"). It doubles as an ad for the editor's project files (the meta line "So does this edit").
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–2.7 **Hook:** "Stripe just made the biggest AI deal this year." Stripe logo build, then the real news headline with the CEO photo.
  - 2.8–6.7 **Subject + number 1:** "OpenRouter, worth $1.3 billion this May." Logo reveal, then four stage/podcast clips of the founder under a growing caption.
  - 6.7–10.3 **Number 2 (the turn):** "Just 82 days later, worth over $8 billion." Office-building mosaic, then a CEO cut-out with a giant "$8 Billion" behind his head.
  - 10.1–14.5 **What it is:** "Same company. Routes AI requests across 400 models." Photo pile on a cutting mat, then logo tiles scattered on a dark grid.
  - 14.5–17.7 **Social proof:** NVIDIA, Zoom, Lovable each get a 0.7–0.9 s card with the CEO cut-out and a giant brand word, then "all use it" over a group of lit faces.
  - 17.7–24.7 **Valuation logic + thesis:** $50M revenue (building on a cloud full of cash) → $8B price tag ("Companies for sale" diorama) → "not a valuation" (dollar-bill cubes) → "a bet on the entire AI economy" (slot machine 777).
  - 25.2–31.4 **CTA:** "The AI economy moves fast. So does this edit. Grab the project file on earnedits.com." Cream card, phone mockup rises, fade to black.
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** frame 0 is a glowing purple square. Frames 1–4 alternate with **inverted/negative flash frames** (lime-green and white), the Stripe mark assembles from sliding squares with a white bloom burst (f5), and "Stripe" types in at top. At 0.87 s another bloom + negative flash cuts to the real headline page "Stripe is buying OpenRouter for $7.5 billion" with a CEO photo. In 1.5 s you get 6 flashes, a logo and a real headline, which reads as "breaking news".
- **Script → visual rule:** one world per sentence and one caption word per spoken word. Proper nouns (company names) get a **logo or giant brand word**; numbers get either a giant keyword ("$8 Billion") or a **literal 3D metaphor** (building with cash = revenue, "for sale" sign = price tag, slot machine = bet).

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** mixed media. Real press photos and stage footage (graded flat and muted), rotoscoped CEO cut-outs on studio-white gradients, AI-generated photoreal 3D dioramas (isometric building on a cloud, western "Companies for sale" storefront, dollar-bill origami cubes, chrome slot machine), top-down desk collage, and logo/UI elements. Each world is a different medium; the caption system is the constant.
- **Backgrounds & textures:** near-black with purple light leak (Stripe) → greyscale article page with heavy vignette → charcoal `#111111` with lime confetti (OpenRouter) → graded footage → navy blueprint grid with cloud shadows → orange self-healing cutting mat `#8e4425` with grid lines and a phone and pen → perspective dark grid floor → white radial "studio" gradient (CEO cards) → black void → teal sky → desert sand → cream `#faf6e6` vignette (two times). **A horizontal scanline overlay** (1 px lines every ~3 px at ~10–15% opacity, visible on every key frame) and **strong oval vignettes** (corners ~40–60% darker) sit on everything.
- **Colour:** muted, low-saturation grade on footage (skin pushed warm-grey, blacks lifted to ~`#141113`). The colour comes from brand accents only: Stripe blurple `#6d51f8`/`#3b2699`, OpenRouter lime `#bbdc0a`, NVIDIA green, Zoom blue, a Lovable magenta→orange gradient, gold `#e0a81c` for "$50 MILLION", and a red arc `#9b1c1c` behind the slot machine. The end card is a warm cream `#f3eede`→`#faf6e6` with black type, a deliberate calm after the colour run.
- **Typography:**
  - **Running caption (the spine):** neo-grotesk, light/regular (SF Pro Display Light / Inter Light), lowercase as spoken ("worth $1.3 Billion this May"), white `#f2f2f2` with a faint soft shadow. Small: cap height ~2.2% of frame height, line ~35–60% of frame width. It sits at the **frame centre or lower-middle (y ≈ 55–75%)**, often over the subject's chest. Words are spaced wider than normal (~1.5 em gaps in "across 400 models", "worth over $8 Billion"). The newest word appears in **lighter grey `#a0a0a0` and turns white** when the next word lands (a karaoke trail).
  - **Selection-handle box:** on the article page the caption "Biggest / Biggest AI / Biggest AI Deal" sits inside a design-tool transform box: a dashed 1 px white rectangle with 4 white square handles (~22 px at 720 w) at the corners. The box grows to fit each new word.
  - **Giant keyword:** "$8 Billion" in a heavy grotesk (SF Pro Display Heavy / Inter Black), black `#0a0a0a` with a 2 px light inner outline and a soft offset drop shadow, set **≈100% of frame width** (it bleeds off both edges) at y ≈ 25%, **behind the CEO's head** (text-behind-subject).
  - **Brand-word walls:** "NVIDIA", "ZOOM", "LOVABLE" in ultra-condensed heavy caps at the top 15% of frame, ~100% wide, coloured in the brand hue, with a vertical fade/blur at the bottom edge, again behind the cut-out.
  - **Editorial serif:** "this" / "this year" (white, on the photo) and the end-card headline "The AI economy moves fast" in a **condensed high-contrast italic serif** (Instrument Serif Italic / PP Editorial New Italic), black, ~70% width. "Router" in the OpenRouter lockup uses the same italic serif in lime next to an upright grotesk "Open". The rule is **grotesk = information, italic serif = voice/attitude**.
  - **3D display type inside renders:** "$50 MILLION REVENUE" in gold heavy extended caps with a dark-brown extrude and drop shadow (Archivo Black-like), and "8 BILLION / DOLLAR" white heavy caps with a dark outline on a wooden sign. Both **scramble-type in** letter by letter.
  - **Thesis line:** "that is not a / valuation" in Inter Regular/Medium dark grey `#333`, with "valuation" ~1.3× bigger and a cube passing in front of it. "that's the bet on the / entire AI economy" in white-grey medium grotesk at ~100% width, **defocused** (depth-of-field) behind the slot machine.
- **Layout & composition:** the hero (logo, cut-out or 3D object) is centred, filling 50–70% of frame height. Big words sit in the top 10–30% and captions in the middle to lower-middle. The CTA stacks headline at y ≈ 25%, link line at y ≈ 53% and phone mockup in the bottom 30%.
- **Assets:** Stripe/OpenRouter logos, a real news-article screenshot, press photos of the Stripe founders, stage/podcast clips of the OpenRouter founder, roto cut-outs of four CEOs, AI-model logo tiles (Claude, Meta, Llama, NVIDIA, Alibaba, Zoom…), AI-generated 3D renders, a hand cut-out, and a phone mockup of the creator's shop page.
- **Finishing / FX:** scanlines, vignette, **RGB-split/chromatic aberration** on the OpenRouter logo slam and confetti (red/cyan fringes ~4 px), white **bloom bursts** on logo assembly, **inverted-colour flash frames**, directional motion blur on every exit push, depth-of-field blur on background type, soft glow on sparkles.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** first 10 s ≈ 1.5 cuts/s counting internal swaps (photo swaps every 3–16 frames, footage clips every ~0.7 s). After 10 s, real scenes run 0.7–2.5 s, with the three CEO cards as a fast 0.7–0.9 s triplet. The end card holds for 5.8 s. Pace is fast while listing facts and slow on the thesis and CTA.
- **Signature exit: accelerating push into the cut.** Almost every scene ends with a 4–6 frame **ease-in zoom** where per-frame zoom roughly doubles each step (measured 0.95 → 1.5 → 2.3 → 4.8 at 15.98–16.12; 1.1 → 1.5 → 2.5 → 6.6 at 10.11–10.24; 0.9 → 1.2 → 2.1 → 4.8 at 24.99–25.13; 0.2 → 0.3 → 0.5 → 1.3 → 3.2 on the logo at 3.60–3.77). Directional blur grows with it, and the next scene starts already settled (no matching zoom-in-from-big). Rebuild: scale 1 → 1.12 over 6 frames with `Easing.in(Easing.exp)` or `bezier(0.7, 0, 1, 0.5)` and blur 0 → 12 px.
- **Rotational variant:** at 12.55–12.75 the camera rotation ramps −2.4°, −3.1°, −3.9°, −5.2°, −6.9° per frame (≈ −22° total in 7 frames), then 3 frames of spin-blurred hue-shifted image bridge to the next scene.
- **Within-scene camera:** constant slow push on almost every scene (net zoom +3 to +22 over 0.7–2.5 s, i.e. ~1–1.5% scale per second). The 3D dioramas and the end card do the opposite: slow pull-backs (−6 to −18) to feel calm and "reveal the whole".
- **Entrances:** logo slam from ~3× scale with RGB split (4 frames, no visible bounce); scale-pop tiles (3–4 frames); white-silhouette → colour cut-out (5 frames); yellow-glow silhouette → photo (3 frames hold, then a 1-frame swap); giant keyword defocus-in (7 frames, blur → 0); 3D object rise from the bottom with motion blur (~15 frames); a lid lift (~8 frames); a phone mockup rising (~27 frames, ease-out cubic).
- **Caption mechanics:** each new word appears in **1 frame**, sometimes preceded by a **white block "caret"** the size of the coming word for 1–3 frames (5.81, 6.07, 1.33 s), lighter grey for ~0.2 s, then white. No fades or slides on the running caption. It is a pure cut-on.
- **Posterised animation:** in many graphic sections every 3rd frame is a duplicate (the diff curve shows a hi-hi-low pattern), so the motion graphics run at ~20 fps inside the 29.97 fps file. This gives a slightly stepped, "edited" feel against the smooth footage.
- **Sync (VO):** cuts are locked to the word, not the beat. Scene cuts land within −0.10…+0.20 s of the key word onset: NVIDIA cut 14.55 vs word 14.52 (+0.03), Zoom 15.48 vs 15.44 (+0.04), Lovable 16.15 vs 16.12 (+0.03), "all" 16.88 vs 16.86 (+0.02), "$50" 17.68 vs 17.78 (−0.10), "$8 billion price tag" 19.52 vs 19.50, end card 25.16 vs 25.22 (−0.06). Caption words **trail** the voice by 0.1–0.4 s ("Billion" +0.20, "May" +0.19, "just" +0.15, "across" +0.48, "models" +0.18), so the ear leads and the eye confirms.
- **Sync (music):** the mean |cut − nearest beat| is 0.13 s against a 0.465 s beat period, which is no better than chance. The music is a bed only, and the edit follows the voice.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Zoom-through (accelerating push + hard cut)**: 3.84, 8.24, 10.31, 16.15, 16.88, 25.16 s. 4–6 frames of exponential zoom-in with blur on the outgoing scene, then a hard cut to a static incoming scene. Rebuild: on the outgoing `<Sequence>`'s last 6 frames, `scale: interpolate(f, [0, 6], [1, 1.12], {easing: Easing.in(Easing.exp)})` plus `filter: blur()` 0 → 10 px.
2. **Flash-frame cut**: 0.07–0.17 and 0.87 s (inverted/negative frames and white bloom), 2.20–2.27 (inverted RGB-split frames), 2.74 (white → black), 3.07 (white → charcoal). 1–3 frames of an inverted copy (`filter: invert(1) hue-rotate(180deg)`) or a white bloom between scenes.
3. **Glow-silhouette reveal**: 14.55, 15.48, 16.15 s (yellow) and 8.24 s (white). The incoming cut-out first shows as a flat fill of its alpha (yellow `#f5e04a` or white) with an outer glow for 3 frames, then the real pixels. Rebuild: render the PNG twice, with the top copy using `filter: brightness(0) invert(1) sepia(1) saturate(5)` plus `drop-shadow(0 0 30px)`; its opacity goes 1 → 0 between frames 3 and 5.
4. **Block-wipe swap**: 1.43–2.17 s in the article montage. A flat white rectangle covering ~30–50% of the photo slot appears for 1–2 frames, then the new photo. Rebuild: `<div>` rect at random quadrant and `opacity` 1 for 2 frames, image src swap underneath.
5. **Tile mosaic build**: 6.74–7.5 s. White squares appear on a grid and each one fills with a crop of the next image, until the full picture is assembled. Rebuild: N clip-path rects on the image, each revealed on a staggered frame (stagger 3 frames), first as a white flat for 2 frames.
6. **Spin whip**: 12.55–12.88 s. Rotational ease-in (−22° in 7 frames) then 3 blurred/hue-shifted frames. Rebuild: `rotate` with `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)`, a radial or rotational blur approximated by 3 stacked rotated copies at 30% opacity.
7. **Fade to black** at 30.8 s: 8 frames, linear.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Change the medium, keep the caption.** Every sentence gets a different world (footage, article, render, desk collage, logo stage), and one small, consistent white word-by-word caption keeps it readable.
2. **One exit grammar.** An accelerating push with blur on nearly every scene end makes 20 different worlds feel like one camera diving forward.
3. **Proper nouns get a wall of type behind a cut-out.** Brand word ~100% wide, brand colour, behind the rotoscoped person, on a white radial gradient. Repeating the same template three times quickly (0.7–0.9 s each) makes a list feel rhythmic.
4. **Numbers become literal metaphors.** Revenue is a building full of cash, price is a "for sale" sign, a valuation is stacked money cubes, a bet is a slot machine. Use 3D/AI renders for the abstract parts of the story.
5. **Flash frames as punctuation.** 1–3 frames of inverted colour, a white bloom or a glowing silhouette sell the energy of a cut without a long transition.
6. **Two-voice type system.** Light grotesk for facts, condensed italic serif for attitude lines ("this year", "The AI economy moves fast"), and a heavy black grotesk only for the single biggest number.
7. **A unified finishing layer** (scanlines, heavy vignette, muted grade) hides how mismatched the sources are.
8. **Cuts on the word, captions just after it.** Picture changes within ±0.1 s of the noun, and the caption word trails by ~0.2 s.
9. **Calm the CTA.** Drop to cream, serif and a slow pull-back after 25 s of energy, so the link reads clearly.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, duration = VO + 0.6 s (31.4 s → 942 frames). Optionally posterise graphic layers to 20 fps (`Math.floor(frame / 1.5) * 1.5`) for the stepped feel.
- **Scene stack (bottom → top):** `<SceneBackground kind="footage|render|gradient|grid">` → `<Cutout src glowIn="white|yellow">` → `<BrandWall text color>` (placed **between** background and cut-out) → `<WordCaption words y="62%">` → `<ScanlineOverlay spacing={3} opacity={0.12}>` → `<Vignette strength={0.55}>`.
- **Components:**
  - `ZoomThroughExit` (last 6 frames of each scene): `scale 1 → 1.12`, `Easing.in(Easing.exp)`, blur 0 → 10 px.
  - `ScenePush`: scale `1 → 1 + 0.012 * seconds`, linear.
  - `WordCaption`: Inter Light / SF Pro Display 300, 34 px at 1080 w, letter-spacing −0.01 em, word gap 0.6 em. Each word appears at `wordStart + 5 frames` (≈ +0.17 s). Optionally a 2-frame white block `<div>` of the word's measured width precedes it. The newest word is `#a0a0a0`, and the previous ones are `#f2f2f2`.
  - `SelectionBoxCaption`: the caption wrapped in a `1px dashed #fff` rect with 4 × 22 px white squares at the corners. Its width interpolates to the text width over 2 frames per new word.
  - `GiantKeyword`: Inter Black 260 px, `#0a0a0a`, `-webkit-text-stroke: 2px #e8e8e8`, `text-shadow: 6px 8px 0 rgba(0,0,0,.35)`. Entrance: blur 20 → 0 px and scale 1.08 → 1 over 7 frames, `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`.
  - `BrandWall`: condensed heavy caps (Anton / Druk Wide alt), fill = brand colour or gradient, `mask-image: linear-gradient(#000 60%, transparent)`. Letters reveal in a shuffled order, 2 frames apart, each blur 8 → 0 px.
  - `GlowSilhouetteIn`: 3 frames of solid-fill alpha with `drop-shadow(0 0 40px #fff6a0)`, then cut to the real image.
  - `FlashFrame`: a 1–2 frame `filter: invert(1)` copy of the incoming frame, or a white radial bloom.
  - `ScrambleType` (gold 3D title, sign text, slot display): random glyphs per letter for 4–8 frames, resolving left → right, 1 letter per frame. Extrude = 6 stacked `text-shadow` layers in `#5a3a00`.
  - `LogoSlam`: scale 3 → 1 over 4 frames with `spring({damping: 20, stiffness: 400, mass: 0.5})`, plus an RGB split (two tinted copies offset ±4 px that collapse to 0 over the same 4 frames).
  - `TileMosaicReveal`: a grid of `clip-path: inset()` tiles, white flat for 2 frames, then the image crop, staggered by 3 frames.
  - `PhoneMockupRise`: translateY 100% → 0 over 27 frames, `bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.
- **Tricky bits:** text-behind-subject needs a cut-out PNG (or a segmentation matte for user footage, e.g. MediaPipe/Robust Video Matting pre-rendered to a WebM with alpha) layered over the type. For user footage, the same stack works: graded clip → brand wall → matte cut-out of the speaker → caption.
