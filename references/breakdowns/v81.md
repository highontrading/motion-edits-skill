**One-line essence:** a brand-philosophy monologue where every sentence is literally *typed, selected or posted inside a phone UI* (old Instagram grid, iOS-6 Facebook composer, Story, Notes, text selection menu, Photos albums), with typographic depth-of-field cards and two movie-footage beats. Familiar UI becomes the typography.

## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** brand-strategy manifesto ("not X, not Y → it's Z" → evidence → advice ×3 → maxim → creator CTA).
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–3.9 **Hook (negation triple):** your brand isn't your logo, colours or product.
  - 4.3–6.7 **Redefinition:** a brand is how people feel about you.
  - 7.4–14.4 **Expansion:** it lives in message, story, emotions, and how clearly you help people win.
  - 15.1–19.6 **Turn:** you can't buy that with ads; you build it through every action and experience.
  - 20.1–23.5 **Proof:** happy customers tell friends and family (real bowling-alley footage).
  - 23.6–30.9 **Advice ×3:** be clear what you stand for, tell a real story, show how you help people move forward consistently (film footage for the last line).
  - 31.8–35.4 **Maxim:** branding isn't what you say, it's what people feel when they think of you.
  - 35.8–40.8 **CTA:** project file on earnedits.com, comment "Link".
- **Hook mechanics:** frame 0 is a top-down still of a wooden desk with an iPhone 4, an iPod Classic and earbuds. An ease-in push (zoom/frame 0.07→3.57 over 5 f) snaps at 0.17 s into an **iPhone camera-app viewfinder** (PHOTO mode, shutter button) framing that phone. The old Instagram profile grid then fills tile by tile with the spoken words "YOUR / BRAND / IS / Not / YOUR LOGO". There is a fake autofocus "hunt" blur at 0.70 s on the word "not". So a nostalgia object, a camera UI and word-synced type all land inside 1 s.
- **Script → visual rule:** each sentence gets a *different phone surface*, chosen for how the text would naturally be produced (typed in a composer, posted as a story, written in Notes, selected in a document, shown as album names). The verbs "tell" and "show" switch to cinematic footage.

## 2. Visual language

These alternate with flat typographic "spec cards" on white or grid paper and two real-footage inserts (a stock bowling-alley group, and racing-film close-ups in the style of *Ford v Ferrari*). The CTA reuses the creator's standard dark Ae-file outro (identical to v80).

  - The handwritten accent is always a **colour script** (green "Colors", green "every experience", red "story", red "branding", red "about you") against neutral grotesk.
- **Typography:**
  - **Primary:** neo-grotesk bold (Helvetica Neue Bold / SF Pro Display Bold), sentence case, tight tracking. Sizes: card headlines ~55–60% frame width ("Every experience", "Every Happy Customer"); footage captions ~60% width, 2 lines, centre-left.
  - **Script accent:** thin monoline signature script (Amsterdam / Monsieur La Doulaise / "Thesignature" class), placed as a **small echo line** right-aligned under the grotesk headline (e.g. "every experience" in green under "Every experience", at ~45% of headline size). It is also used as a brush-marker script for hero words ("Colors" written by a green crayon, "story", "branding").
  - **DOF typography:** on the Portra and grid-paper cards, the same phrase is repeated as huge (~90% width) blurred grotesk in the foreground and background (blur ~6–10 px), while the sharp small headline sits in the middle. This gives three depth planes of the same words.
  - **UI-native type:** SF/Helvetica in iOS screens, Times New Roman for the selected document sentence, "Instagram" Billabong logo, iOS-6 Helvetica bold with a bevel, Notes SF in yellow-accented dark.
  - **Retro tile type:** chunky Cooper/Bauhaus-style mixed fonts per Instagram tile ("YO UR", "BRAND BRAND", "IS", "Not", "YOUR LOGO"), white or green on black.
  - **Emphasis rules:** negation is shown by a **red prohibition sign stamped over the "o" of "not"**; colour words get hex codes and swatches; the key verb gets an orange colour ("Show"); a single word ("Consistently") gets a letter-scramble assemble.
- **Layout & composition:** the hero line sits at y≈45–55%. Card scenes are centred with a green "earnera.co" wordmark at top centre (y≈7%) and a tiny grey thesis tagline at the bottom (y≈87%), like a printed spec sheet. UI scenes are shot at an angle with DOF so a third of the frame is soft.
- **Assets:** iPhone 4/6 hardware, iPod Classic, earbuds, iOS camera UI, old Instagram profile, an IG dark feed post, iOS Contacts card with a pixel-art cat sticker, iOS-6 Facebook composer, IG Story frame, iOS app switcher, Notes app, a document with selection handles and the copy/paste callout, Photos album list, 😎 emojis, a binary-digit circular text ring, a camera-HUD frame (red corner brackets), Facebook reaction emoji column, a curled sticky-note paper, real footage, Ae .AEP icons, website cards.
- **Finishing / FX:** heavy DOF, light-leak/bloom flashes (white burst from the top-left), a 3-frame black-and-white pixel-sort glitch, a vertical frame-roll, film-edge markings, window gobos, and fine grain in the CTA. The film footage is stepped (duplicate frames every other frame, as from a 50% slow-down).

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** ~0.56 cuts/s. Scenes are 1.2–3.8 s. Intro scenes are short (1.4 s); the explanatory UI scenes (contact card, Story, Photos, maxim desk) take 2.5–3.8 s and carry their pace with internal camera moves.
- **Easing signatures (measured):**
  - *Ease-in exits into a cut* at 0.17 (0.07→3.57 over 5 f) and 1.37 (0.3→10.7 over 9 f). The cut lands at peak velocity, like v80.
  - *Shrink/app-switch*: a −5.45 zoom/f peak, then an exponential decay over 8 f (−2.98, −1.75, −1.11, −0.86, −0.56…), i.e. ease-out with a ~2.5 f half-life. Then a 4-frame lateral swipe with a bell-shaped velocity (1.8 → 4.8 → 8.9 → peak), and a zoom-in that decays over 6 f.
  - *Whip-rotate* (3.20–3.27): 3 frames at −8.5/−23.9/−5.6°/f with −4.3…−4.5 zoom/f. It is a very short, violent move that settles in 6 f.
  - *Camera bumps on beats*: a 3 f rise and 5–8 f decay (e.g. 32.7→32.8→33.2: 1.58→5.35→0.31).
  - No overshoot anywhere (no sign flip after peaks). Everything is critically damped.
- **Entrance / exit vocab:**
  - word-per-tile fill (Instagram grid)
  - prohibition-sign stamp on a letter (~3 f, scale 1.3→1)
  - crayon script write-on (~9 f)
  - typewriter with caret (~15 chars/s)
  - karaoke grey→white line highlight (~6 f fade per line)
  - text-selection expansion word by word
  - iOS callout pop (3 f)
  - list items typed into a native list
  - letter-scramble assemble (random order, ~6 f)
  - word-synced caption build on footage (~4 f fade per word)
  - handwritten write-on lines
  - light-leak bloom out, 3 f white, bloom in
- **Camera:** a virtual camera on UIs at 15–30° tilt with DOF. Net zoom per scene: +23, +14, −16, +15, +20, +22, +89 (the maxim desk push from wide), and net pans up to −164 (desk truck). The footage is nearly locked (≤0.25 px/f drift).
- **Secondary motion:** rotating binary ring, parallax type planes, floating emojis, caret blink, a pixel cat sticker, Ae icons orbiting, and an autofocus blur pulse.
- **Sync:**
  - **Cuts are on the beat (±0.1 s):** 1.37 (beat 1.39), 13.00 (12.96), 14.77 (14.81), 18.73 (18.81), 20.10 (20.09), 21.77 (21.73), 23.63 (23.71), 26.17 (26.12), 30.43 (30.47), 31.83 (31.88), 35.67 (35.78).
  - **Cuts lead or coincide with VO sentence starts:** 4.23 vs "Your" 4.32 (−0.09); 7.37 vs 7.42 (−0.05); 18.73 vs "every" 18.78 (−0.05); 20.10 vs 20.10 (0.00); 23.63 vs 23.64 (−0.01); 26.17 vs 26.22 (−0.05); 31.83 vs 31.80 (+0.03); 35.67 vs 35.76 (−0.09).
  - On-screen words appear 0–0.1 s after the spoken word ("not" stamp 1.87 vs 1.80; "Colors" 2.1 vs 2.22; "Tell" 26.3 vs 26.22). The exception is "Consistently", which builds ~0.7 s after it is spoken, as a held button word.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Zoom-through into the cut** at 0.17 and 1.37 s: an ease-in push over 5–9 frames that cuts at peak speed. Rebuild: `scale: interpolate(f,[end-9,end],[1,1.6],{easing: Easing.in(Easing.quad)})` plus a 2 f `blur(6px)`.
2. **Autofocus hunt pulse** at 0.70 s: a 2-frame full-frame blur (~10 px) with a slight scale (1.02) and a yellow focus bracket. It is a "camera" accent on a stressed word.
3. **B/W pixel-sort glitch** at 2.77–2.83 s: 3 frames of desaturated horizontal smear bars that bridge the spec card to the same card as a post. Rebuild: a horizontal displacement map (SVG `feDisplacementMap` with a striped noise) + `grayscale(1)` for 3 f.
4. **Whip-rotate re-centre** at 3.20 s: −24°/f rotation + zoom in 3 f with motion blur.
5. **Vertical frame-roll** at 9.27–9.40 s: the outgoing image tiles vertically with black gaps and scrolls up ~1 frame-height in 4 frames (a film-gate slip), and the incoming scene lands. Rebuild: render the outgoing scene twice stacked with an 8% black gap and `translateY` −100% over 4 f, then switch.
6. **App-switcher shrink + swipe** at 13.00–13.37 s: the full-screen scene scales to an iOS multitasking card (radius 40 px, 8 f ease-out), swipes sideways (4 f), and the next app card zooms to full screen.
7. **Inverse match cut** at 18.73 s: the same layout, black↔white swapped, on the beat. Rebuild: the same component with a `theme` prop flipped at the cut frame.
8. **Light-leak bloom flash** at 21.60 and 27.73 s: a warm white radial bloom from the top-left grows over 2 f, then 3 frames of pure white, then the footage fades out of white with a lingering lens flare on the left (2–3 f). Rebuild: a radial-gradient `#fff8e8` with `mix-blend-mode: screen`, opacity 0→1 over 2 f, a solid white hold for 3 f, and the incoming footage starting at brightness 1.8→1 over 4 f.
9. **Letterbox window** (4.23–7.37 s): one scene is shown inside a framed window on black, as a chapter-like change in format.
10. **Zoom-out reveal into the CTA card** (~37.9 s): the shared outro move.

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **Let the UI produce the type.** Instead of captions, the words are typed in a composer, posted in a Story, selected in a document, or listed as album names. The viewer reads the script *through familiar gestures*.
2. **Use nostalgic hardware and old UI** (iPhone 4, iOS 6, 2012 Instagram) to make a generic topic feel like a memory.
3. **Turn negation into a graphic:** a prohibition sign stamped on the "o" of "not" says "not" twice.
4. **Use three-plane DOF typography:** the same phrase huge and blurred in front and behind, sharp and small in the middle. It creates depth with no imagery at all.
5. **Pair a grotesk headline with a coloured script echo** for warmth. Keep the script small, right-aligned and in a single accent colour.
6. **Use karaoke on a paragraph**, not single words: show the whole thought in grey and light up the spoken line in white while the camera pans to it.
7. **Reserve real footage for "tell" and "show"**, entered via a light-leak flash so the jump from UI to film feels like an exposure, not a cut.
8. **Make every cut land on the beat** and within 0.1 s before the sentence. Give long scenes beat-locked camera steps instead of cuts.
9. **Use a repeatable branded outro** (identical across videos) to build recognition.

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1080×1920`, `30 fps`, 1226 frames (40.9 s). Make a `beats[]` array from the music (152 BPM → 11.84 frames/beat) and snap scene starts to the nearest beat ≤ the VO sentence start.
- **Layer stack** (bottom → top):
  1. `<Surface variant="white|black|gridPaper|portraFrame|blobGradient">`
  2. `<Camera3D tilt focusZ>`
  3. the UI component
  4. `<DepthType>` planes
  5. `<ScriptEcho>`
  6. a `<LightLeak>`/`<Grain>` overlay
- **Components & timings:**
  - `InstaGridFill({words, tileStagger: 'word-synced'})`: each tile pops with scale 0.85→1 over 4 f.
  - `CameraViewfinder({children})` with `FocusHunt({at, frames: 2, blur: 10})`.
  - `SpecCard({hexes, swatches, headline})` + `NotStamp({letterIndex})`: an SVG ⃠ at scale 1.4→1 via `spring({damping: 12, stiffness: 260, mass: 0.5})`.
  - `ScriptWriteOn({text, color, durationFrames: 9})`: SVG path of the script with `strokeDashoffset`, or a mask-wipe L→R.
  - `FeedPost` + `WhipRotate({deg: 30, frames: 3})`.
  - `RetroComposer({text, cps: 15})` with a caret blink every 15 f.
  - `KaraokeParagraph({lines, activeColor: '#fff', idleColor: 'rgba(255,255,255,.45)', fade: 6})` + a camera pan to the active line's y.
  - `AppSwitcher({from, to, shrinkFrames: 8, swipeFrames: 4, growFrames: 6})` with `Easing.out(Easing.exp)`.
  - `TextSelection({sentence, wordTimes})`: the selection rect width interpolates to the right edge of the current word, with handles as 3 px blue lines and 10 px dots.
  - `CalloutMenu({items})` with a 3 f pop.
  - `DepthType({text, front: {scale: 3, blur: 8, drift: -1}, back: {scale: 2.5, blur: 6, drift: +1}})`.
  - `PortraFrame({invert})` for the inverse match cut.
  - `BinaryRing({radius: 220, speedDegPerFrame: 2})` via SVG `textPath`.
  - `FootageCaption({lines, accentWordColor: '#ff7a1a', scriptTail})`.
  - `ScrambleAssemble({word, frames: 6})`, with letters revealed in a random permutation.
  - `LightLeakFlash({bloomFrames: 2, whiteFrames: 3, recoverFrames: 4})`.
  - `FrameRoll({frames: 4, gapPct: 8})`.
  - `StickyNote({curl: true, emojis: fbReactions})` + `HandwrittenLine`.
- **Tricky bits:**
  - The photographed retro screen: use a real photo of an old iPhone with the composer area masked, and overlay live typed text with a slight perspective transform (`matrix3d`) and `blur(0.6px)` to match the photo's softness.
  - Stepped footage: render at `playbackRate={0.5}` and hold frames on 2s (`Math.floor(frame/2)*2`) to copy the film-clip feel.
