## 1. Concept & script structure
- **Genre:** sponsored SaaS feature promo (AI tool: extend a clip with matching visuals and audio), 16:9 for YouTube/LinkedIn.
- **Beats:**
  - 0.0–2.7 **Hook / thesis:** "The footage ends, but the scene doesn't have to." The footage literally ends on screen: the clip shrinks into a card, shows a replay icon, then collapses into a text caret.
  - 3.3–7.3 **Pain:** the perfect shot that runs out of footage → reshoot. Shown as a real NLE timeline with a red "out of media" marker.
  - 7.3–13.1 **Product intro:** "With InVideo AI you can extend an existing scene…" Product homepage, then an editor window where the clip end glows red and stretches.
  - 13.1–17.8 **Feature list:** "matching visuals, dialogue, music and sound effects" as a 4-item list with a moving highlight.
  - 17.8–21.5 **Proof:** Before / After cards side by side ("feel completely natural").
  - 21.5–26.2 **How-to:** a cursor drags the clip into the prompt, the prompt types itself, submit → the generated continuation renders in.
  - 26.2–32.9 **CTA:** "Comment INVIDEO" → logo endcard with the offer line (up to 50% off + project timeline).
- **Hook mechanics (first 1.5 s):** frame 0 is the raw full-bleed footage; at frame 1 it snaps to an 86% card with a video-player scrubber, then eases down to ~55% width with Figma-style selection handles and guide lines extending to the frame edges. "The footage" letter-reveals to the left and "ends" to the right, a replay icon appears, and at 1.22 s the card squeezes horizontally into a 1-px purple caret. The viewer sees the metaphor ("footage ends") acted out before the sentence finishes.
- **Script → visual rule:** abstract clauses become **white-canvas kinetic type** (one big word, then the full line small); concrete product claims become **real UI recordings** on the meadow backdrop. The two modes alternate every 2–5 s: type, UI, UI, UI, type, UI, UI, UI, type, endcard.

## 2. Visual language
- **Art direction:** mixed **minimal-swiss kinetic type** (pure white `#fdfdfe`, one ink colour, one accent) + **UI mockups** (DaVinci/Premiere-style dark editor, macOS window with dock, the InVideo web prompt). One flat-vector illustration (a 3-track timeline icon) bridges the two.
- **Backgrounds:**
  - Type scenes: flat white, no texture or grain.
  - UI scenes: a single **blurred alpine-meadow photo** (Seceda-like ridge, wildflowers, Gaussian blur ≈ 8–12 px at 1276 w) with a **white dot-grid overlay** (dots ~3 px, pitch ~32 px, ~70% opacity). The same backdrop is reused in every UI scene, which ties the screen recordings together as one "product world".
  - Endcard: an app-gallery screenshot washed with ~70% white (luma 218), blurred slightly.
- **Colour:** white + near-black ink `#111014` + **one purple family**: caret/clip `#8340df`, gradient bar `#7b3ff2 → #cdb6fa`, pink video clips `#ee8ffb`, lavender letter-tint `#c49be0`. Timeline icon adds dark teal `#2e645b` with mint waveform `#6cc0b0` and an iOS-blue playhead `#0a84ff`. Inactive list items are light grey `#c8c8cc`. The red in the editor scenes (`#e5243b` out-of-media marker and glow) is the only warm accent and means "problem".
- **Typography:**
  - **Primary:** neo-grotesk (Helvetica Now Display / Neue Haas Grotesk / SF Pro Display-like), **Regular–Medium weight**, sentence case, tight default tracking (−1 to −2%). Two scale states:
    - **Hero word:** "Ever", "It can", "but the", cap height ≈ 22–25% of frame height, word ≈ 40–46% of frame width, centred.
    - **Line:** the full sentence ("Ever had the perfect shot") at cap height ≈ 3.5% of height, ≈ 45–58% of width, centred.
  - **List:** same family, cap height ≈ 8% of height; active item purple with a left-to-right gradient fill on the glyphs (`#8340df → #cdb6fa`), inactive items `#c8c8cc`, and a **thin-stroke arrow →** in purple at the left.
  - **Labels:** "Before" / "After" in white Medium, cap height ≈ 5% of height, centred above each card, no badge.
  - **CTA keyword:** "INVIDEO" in ALL CAPS in a lighter lavender that darkens to ink, next to sentence-case "Comment".
  - **Letter entrance colour:** every new letter is born lavender `#c49be0` at ~20% opacity and settles to ink `#111` over ~8 frames, so a word being typed shows a purple-to-black gradient across its letters (newest = palest).
  - **Endcard:** the brand wordmark (InVideo, heavy rounded geometric) + a 2-line subtitle in the same neo-grotesk Regular, cap height ≈ 2.7% of height, black, centred.
- **Layout:** everything on the centre axis. Type is optically centred (y ≈ 50%). UI windows fill 65–95% of the width and are deliberately cropped by the frame edges when the "camera" pushes in. Before/After cards: two 40%-wide cards with a 4% gutter, top edge at y ≈ 30%.
- **Assets:** stock lifestyle footage (four hikers walking toward camera), real NLE screen recording, macOS dock (Finder, Mail, WhatsApp, Tasks icons), the InVideo homepage prompt box, a macOS grabbing-hand cursor, app gallery tiles (Seedance 2.5, Gemini Omni…), a vector timeline icon.
- **Finishing:** soft large drop shadows under UI windows (blur ~40 px, 25% black), 20–24 px corner radius on all cards and windows, background depth-of-field, no grain. Generation moment: a **false-colour (solarised pink/orange) pass** on the new footage that resolves to the natural grade.

## 4. Motion & timing analysis
- **Pacing:** 12 transitions in 32.9 s ≈ 0.37 cuts/s; shots 0.55–5.4 s, median ~2.0 s. Calmer than vertical Shorts: the energy comes from motion inside each shot (letters, list steps, camera drifts), not from cut rate. The fastest stretch is the hook (3 shots in 3.3 s); the longest holds are the feature list (4.7 s) and the endcard (5.4 s).
- **Easing signatures (measured):**
  - **Card shrink (shot 1):** an exponential-decay ease-out. Zoom per frame −3.1, −3.6, −1.9, −1.2, −0.83, −0.60, −0.53, −0.38 … with a half-life of ~2 frames and settled by ~15 f; no overshoot (no sign flip). Equivalent to `Easing.out(Easing.exp)` over 15 f, or `spring({damping: 200, stiffness: 120})`.
  - **Before/After rise:** peak velocity at frame 2, then a long tail (half-life ~4 f, still moving at 15 f, fully settled at ~25 f). This is the "Apple" ease-out: `Easing.bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` over 24 f.
  - **Whip-pan transition (21.2–21.75):** an ease-in over 8 f (pan_x −1.45 → −6.04 px/f at analysis size, i.e. 10 → 43 real px/f), a cut at peak, then an ease-out over 8 f on the incoming scene (−5.3 → −1.5). Total 16 f, symmetric.
  - **Hero → line snap:** not animated. It is a 1-frame swap (diff drops from 8 to 2 between 3.80 and 3.83, and between 13.567 and 13.60). The size change reads as a cut on the next word.
  - **List step:** a 4-frame ease-in-out scroll (zoom spikes −1.35/−1.29 at 15.13–15.17), one row per step.
- **Entrance / exit vocabulary:**
  - Per-letter fade + tint settle: 2 f stagger, 8 f per letter, opacity 0→1, colour `#c49be0`→`#111`, translateY 10→0 px.
  - Word fade-on (small line): 6 f, grey→ink.
  - Caret typing with a block caret.
  - Caret inflate (bar → square): 2 f, then a linear grow over 3 f.
  - Horizontal squeeze-to-caret: 9 f, scaleX 1 → 0.02, ease-in.
  - Card rise with expo ease-out: 24 f.
  - Logo lockup: mark appears, wordmark wipe + slide-to-centre, 4 f.
  - Prompt type-on: ~30 chars/s.
  - Pixel dissolve: 4 f.
  - False-colour resolve: 20 f.
- **Camera:** every UI scene has a virtual camera. It drifts slowly (≈ 80–90 px/s), pushes in on the action (shot 7: window 65%→95% width in ~0.5 s), and uses fast follow-pans to keep the typed text and cursor in frame (shot 10). Type scenes have no camera move at all; the stillness of the white canvas is the contrast.
- **Secondary motion:** the cursor always moves on curved paths with ease-in-out. The NLE playhead runs linearly. Both clips in Before/After play in sync. The out-of-media marker pulses red.
- **Sync:**
  - **Text reveals vs VO:** the first letter of a hero word lands −0.19 s ("It"), −0.05 s ("Ever") and +0.12 s ("Comment") relative to the spoken word, so within ±0.2 s. The hero→line snap lands on the next spoken word (+0.05…+0.17 s).
  - **Feature-list highlight steps vs VO:** −0.09 s ("dialogue"), −0.05 s ("music"), +0.06 s ("sound effects"). This is word-locked to within 3 frames.
  - **Cuts vs music (beat ≈ 0.51 s):** 2.07 (+0.00), 5.07 (−0.01), 8.83 (+0.03), 24.40 (+0.09) and 27.47 (−0.07) sit on beats. The rest (7.27, 13.07, 17.77, 21.47) lead the next VO phrase by 0.1–0.2 s instead. Rule: cut on the beat when the VO allows it; otherwise cut ~4 frames before the phrase.

## 5. Transitions catalogue
1. **Footage → caret squeeze (morph)** at 1.22–1.52: the footage card keeps its height and scaleX collapses with an ease-in over 9 f. During the last 4 f, a purple fill fades up over the footage, so the sliver becomes the text caret, which then starts typing. Rebuild: animate `scaleX` 1→0.02 with `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)` over 9 f, cross-fade a `#8340df` overlay 0→1 over the last 4 f, then swap to the `<Caret>` at the same x.
2. **Accent-shape match cut** at 2.07: the caret inflates into a purple square (2 f bar→square, 3 f grow), then a hard cut to the timeline icon whose purple clip sits at a similar screen position and colour. Rebuild: the same colour + similar size + a hard cut is enough; no tweening needed.
3. **Whip pan with cut at peak velocity** at 21.47: the outgoing scene accelerates horizontally over 8 f, the cut happens at peak, and the incoming scene starts already moving and decelerates over 8 f. Rebuild: outgoing `translateX` with `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)` 0→−300 px plus `blur(0→12px)` horizontal; incoming `translateX` +300→0 with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`, blur 12→0.
4. **Pixel / block dissolve** at 24.27–24.40: square blocks (~1.5% of width) in purple and cyan, some outlined and some filled, bloom from the submit button over the prompt card for 4 f, then a hard cut to full-frame generated footage. Rebuild: a grid of 20×20 px squares with random delays (0–3 f), opacity and scale pops, masked to the card; an outline/fill mix with `#8340df` and `#5ac8fa`.
5. **Generate-in resolve** at 24.40–25.05: the new footage starts full frame in a solarised pink/orange false colour and simultaneously shrinks into a selection frame (10 f, expo out) and de-solarises over 20 f. Rebuild: two `<OffthreadVideo>` layers (an SVG `feComponentTransfer` false-colour version and the clean version), cross-fade over 20 f.
6. **Hard cut white ↔ UI**: every switch between the white type canvas and the meadow UI world is a plain hard cut, landed on a beat where possible. The contrast in luma (248 vs 80–140) is the transition.
7. **Hero-to-line snap**: a 1-frame scale swap inside the type scenes (see §4).

## 7. What makes it high-end (the transferable lessons)
1. **One accent colour that physically travels.** The purple object morphs footage → caret → square → timeline clip → list bar → the tint of new letters. A single hue gives brand cohesion without a logo on screen.
2. **Act out the metaphor in the first second.** "The footage ends" is shown as footage literally shrinking and collapsing into a cursor.
3. **Two visual modes, strictly alternated:** a silent white type canvas (no camera, no texture) vs. a rich photographic UI world (blurred photo, dot grid, camera moves). The contrast resets attention every 2–5 s.
4. **Hero word, then line.** Show the first word huge, then snap to the full sentence small at the next spoken word. This gives both impact and readability.
5. **New letters are born in the accent colour and settle to ink.** It reads as "being written" without a typewriter cliché.
6. **Word-locked list highlighting** (±3 f) makes a boring feature list feel choreographed.
7. **Put screen recordings on a designed stage:** a blurred lifestyle photo + dot grid + rounded window + soft shadow + a moving virtual camera. Never show a raw, static screen capture.
8. **Selection handles and guide lines** around footage borrow designer-tool UI to say "this is editable".
9. **Apple-style easing everywhere:** long expo ease-outs with no overshoot. Premium comes from restraint (no bounce).

## 8. Remotion rebuild recipe
- **Composition:** `1920×1080`, `30 fps`, duration = VO + 0.6 s (≈ 990 f here).
- **Scene stacks:**
  - Type scenes: `<AbsoluteFill bg="#fdfdfe">` → `<HeroThenLine>`.
  - UI scenes: `<BlurredPhotoBG src blur={10}>` → `<DotGrid pitch={32} dot={3} opacity={0.7}>` → `<VirtualCamera keyframes>` wrapping `<UIWindow radius={22} shadow="0 30px 80px rgba(0,0,0,.25)">` (an `<OffthreadVideo>` screen recording or a rebuilt HTML UI) → `<Cursor path>`.
- **Components:**
  - `HeroThenLine({words, timings})`: renders `words[0]` at hero size (cap height 24% of H) via `<LetterSettle>`, then at the frame of `words[1].start` swaps to the full line at 3.5% cap height (no tween), where each subsequent word fades in over 6 f.
  - `LetterSettle({text, startFrame, stagger: 2, dur: 8, from: '#c49be0', to: '#111014'})`: per letter, `opacity: interpolate(f, [s, s+8], [0.2, 1])` and colour interpolated in OKLCH. Set `translateY` 10→0 with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`.
  - `FootageCardIntro`: full-bleed on f0, scale 0.86 at f1, then `interpolate(f, [1, 15], [0.86, 0.55], {easing: Easing.out(Easing.exp)})`. Add `<SelectionHandles>` (4 × 8 px purple dots + 1 px `#d0d0d4` guides extending to the frame edges) and a `<SqueezeToCaret startFrame={37} dur={9}>`.
  - `Caret({mode: 'bar'|'block', color: '#8340df'})`: types the text at ~12 chars/s synced to the word timestamps. For `inflate`, interpolate width 6→H*0.25 over 5 f.
  - `FeatureList({items, activeAt: [frames], rowH: 0.08*H})`: the container `translateY = -activeIndex*rowH`, with each step taking 4 f via `Easing.inOut(Easing.cubic)`. The active row gets the gradient text (`background-clip: text`, `linear-gradient(90deg,#8340df,#cdb6fa)`), a fixed arrow at the left and a gradient bar at the right; inactive rows are `#c8c8cc`.
  - `RiseIn({dist: 130, dur: 24, easing: Easing.bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)})` for the Before/After cards.
  - `WhipPan({outFrames: 8, inFrames: 8, dist: 300, maxBlur: 12})`, using an SVG `feGaussianBlur stdDeviation="12 0"` for directional blur.
  - `PixelDissolve({origin, cell: 20, colors: ['#8340df','#5ac8fa'], dur: 4})`.
  - `FalseColorResolve({dur: 20})`: an SVG filter `feComponentTransfer` with a table mapping to pink/orange on the top layer, opacity 1→0.
  - `LogoLockupEndcard`: the mark at x+12%, the wordmark revealed by a `clipPath inset(0 100%→0 0)` over 4 f while the group translates −12%→0. Subtitle via `WordFade`. Background `scale 1→1.04` over the whole hold.
- **Sync data:** drive every reveal from the Whisper word JSON; offsets: hero first letter = word.start − 4 f, list step = word.start − 2 f, cuts = phrase.start − 4 f or the nearest beat if within ±3 f.

## 9. Variation ideas
1. **Any SaaS feature launch** (e.g. a "smart reply" email feature): keep the white-canvas hero→line type and the one-accent morph (an email card squeezing into the caret). Put product recordings on a blurred lifestyle photo matching the product's audience, with the dot grid kept.
2. **Personal-finance explainer** ("Your savings run out, your plan doesn't have to"): replace the footage card with a bank-balance card that drains and collapses into a caret. Use a green accent instead of purple, a highlighted list for "budget / invest / insure", and Before/After charts in place of footage.
3. **Creator-tool tutorial series:** turn the structure into a reusable template (hook metaphor → pain in the old tool → new tool UI with camera push → word-locked feature list → before/after → prompt type-on → "Comment KEYWORD" CTA), recolouring the accent per sponsor brand.
