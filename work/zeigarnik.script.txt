Your brain hates unfinished business. In 1927, a psychologist noticed something strange. Waiters remembered every open order, then forgot it the second the bill was paid. She called it the Zeigarnik effect. Unfinished tasks stay loaded in your memory, like tabs you never closed. That's why cliffhangers work. Why writing a to-do list calms you down. And why the best stories stop right before the answer. Follow for part two.
